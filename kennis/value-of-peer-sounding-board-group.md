# A senior peer group is the best sounding board

Writing in late 2018, after ~7 months in the Dutch 'Technical Fellows' group, Bas frames a high-signal peer network as the perfect sounding board: ask a question, technical or not, and within minutes you get one or more answers. The collective spans every vendor and layer (workplace, network, storage, app/virtualization, cloud, on-prem, software-defined), together more than 180 years of experience. His view: the value is not just technical knowledge but openness and honesty. Everyone is open, nothing is taboo, people help each other find assignments, and even unpleasant past mistakes get shared because that is how you learn. For a self-employed consultant, this kind of group also exchanges tips on running your own business.
