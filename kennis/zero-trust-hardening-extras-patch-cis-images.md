# Zero Trust extras: patching cadence and CIS-hardened images

Beyond the ten-principle mapping, Bas's "what else" closing points for hardening AVD and W365 estates: keep machines up to date and honour Patch Tuesday, because automation of patching lowers human error; and consider CIS-hardened images as the base for golden images / Cloud PCs. These are durable hardening habits that sit underneath the formal Zero Trust principles.
