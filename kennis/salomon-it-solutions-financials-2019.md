# Salomon IT Solutions financials 2019

Concept annual accounts (jaarrekening, CONCEPT) for Salomon IT Solutions B.V., reporting period 1 Jan - 31 Dec 2019. This is the turnaround year.

Key figures (EUR):
- Revenue (omzet): 855,484 (2018: 610,318), up 40.2%.
- Cost of sales: 99,961; gross margin 755,523 (88.3%).
- Total costs: 751,824, of which personnel costs 633,316 (74.0% of revenue) and car costs 96,503 (11.3%).
- Operating result: +3,699 (first positive, vs -15,127 in 2018); net result: +3,224 (first net profit, vs -15,547).

Balance sheet 31-12-2019:
- Total assets: 275,382 (mostly receivables 247,423 + cash 24,565).
- Equity (eigen vermogen): -47,656 (still negative, but improving from -50,881); accumulated other reserves -207,034 weigh it down.
- Short-term liabilities: 323,039.

Multi-year ratios: working capital -51,051; quick/current ratio 0.84; solvency (EV/TV) -17.3% (improving from -46.4% in 2018, but still negative).

Reading: 2019 is the inflection point - first profitable year and rapid revenue growth, yet the balance sheet stays thin (negative equity, sub-1 liquidity ratios). Growth was funded largely through short-term liabilities and receivables. Private company-history financial data, calibration only.
