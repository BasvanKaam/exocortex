# Ready 4 Growth Assessment (Salomon method)

A Salomon IT Solutions service offering / propositie for organizations facing a forced or planned change in their IT infrastructure (economic end-of-life of current kit, an acquisition forcing sudden growth, or new policy/stricter requirements). It answers: what does the current infrastructure look like, what should the future one look like, and what is the gap between them.

Method, in essence:
- Take a 'photo' (snapshot) of the current IT infrastructure: landscape, assets, resource load, technological currency.
- Establish the starting point (vertrekpunt): current infrastructure load, dependencies, the organization's IT policy, current licensing.
- Translate the organization's challenges and requirements into a high-level picture of the target infrastructure and a transition plan.
- Surface bottlenecks, including quick wins achievable short-term.
- Deliver a roadmap and the handvatten to build a sound budget so the client can make concrete choices.

Salomon developed a model to make an independent, founded choice. Input is primarily the client's wishes and requirements (often gathered via a workshop/meeting), translated into prioritized IT topics. Per topic, IT-infrastructure variables are defined and scored 'where are you now / where do you want to go'. The assessed domains are: Datacenter Management & Virtualization; Server Security; Networking; Storage; Device Deployment and Management; Device Security; Identity and Access Services; Information Protection & Control; IT Process & Compliance.

The report uses clear graphical overviews per infrastructure layer, presents scenarios with the best-fit solution, identifies the key choices for a qualitative business case, and ends with concrete conclusions and recommendations. Positioning line: no surprises, no unexpected costs. Tagline throughout: 'wij geven het inzicht, u maakt de juiste keuzes!'

Note: corporate Salomon marketing material (contact Eric Kuiper), not BvK personal voice. Captured as a method/propositie for reference.
