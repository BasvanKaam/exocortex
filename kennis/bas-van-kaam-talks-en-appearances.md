# Bas van Kaam: talks, sessions and media appearances

Dated, sourced record of his public speaking and podcast/interview appearances, gathered from a web sweep. Not exhaustive (his About page notes "dozens" of sessions), but every item below is tied to a real source. Use as reference for bios, speaker intros and timeline context.

## Conference and community sessions
- **DuCUG (Dutch Citrix User Group)**, "Why we don't need Citrix Support (all the time): Troubleshooting the FMA", 6 Mar 2015, Amsterdam. His public-speaking debut, around 150 attendees.
- **E2EVC**, "FMA Power Management" (with Mick Glover), 13 Jun 2015, Berlin.
- **DuCUG (7th)**, "Nutanix and Citrix, a match made in heaven" (with Kees Baggerman), 25 Sep 2015, Amsterdam.
- **Virtual Expo (first ever)**, "Houdini's guide to Citrix printing", 30 Sep 2015, online.
- **E2EVC**, "The XenDesktop 7.6 HA and Failover (im)possibilities" (with Wilco van Bragt), Nov 2015, Lisbon.
- **Citrix Synergy 2016 (SYN508)**, "Houdini's guide to Citrix printing", May 2016, Las Vegas. Plus a second accepted FMA breakout with Wilco van Bragt.
- **VCNRW**, FMA session, Mar 2016, Cologne.
- **Norwegian Citrix User Group**, two sessions, Oct 2016, Oslo to Kiel cruise.
- **Avensus MCS event**, Windows Virtual Desktop (Dutch), 11 Apr 2019.
- **SBC-Solutions**, Windows Virtual Desktop (Dutch, ~75 min), 19 Jun 2019.
- **Microsoft Meet Community event** (with Christiaan Brinkhoff), Windows Virtual Desktop, 28 Apr 2020, online.
- **IGEL Digital DISRUPT 2020**, "Nerdio Manager for WVD", 25 Jun 2020, online.
- **AVD TechFest 2022**, "6 Cost Reduction Strategies for Azure Virtual Desktop" (with Neil McLoughlin), online.
- **NerdioCon 2025**, "How to become BFFs with your CISO: Next-Level Security for AVD and Windows 365", 9 Apr 2025, Palm Springs.

## Podcast and interview appearances
- **THRIVEcast 052**, "Bas van Kaam has forgotten more about EUC than we've ever learned", 18 Jun 2021 (guest).
- **Technado Ep. 171**, "Nerdio's Bas van Kaam", 1 Oct 2020 (guest).
- **WVD Community Podcast**, guest episode on community and the new Nerdio Field CTO role, Jun 2020.
- **Login TechCast Extra 06**, "Dennis Smith en Bas van Kaam over VDI Drones" (Dutch, guest).
- **Login TechCast Extra 11**, "Bas van Kaam en Christiaan Brinkhoff over Byte-Sized" (Dutch, guest).
- **AzureCrazy**, written Q&A interview (career, WVD, MVP, Nerdio, ProByteSized, EUCdigest), Jan 2021.

For the podcast he co-founded and hosts, see the EUCdigest notes; for his own AI-narrated microlearning podcast see the EUC News Nuggets notes.
