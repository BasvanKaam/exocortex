# From cost model to business case

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

To develop the cost analysis into a business case, add an extra level: investments (transition costs), the new operating costs, and the expected revenues. In other words, how much must I invest in the transition and what are my costs afterward. Only then can you judge whether a cloud migration is financially interesting in the short and longer term.

The cloud strategy may also contain objectives that cannot be quantified immediately but are major drivers for the transition. the framework's mechanism for these is to use weights in the financial model to represent them.

Cost rules of thumb from the framework: for IaaS and PaaS the new costs are reasonably easy to calculate using the online calculators from AWS and Azure. Transition (migration) costs are much harder; the bandwidth estimate lies between 2,000 and 3,500 per server to be migrated.
