# Nerdio innovating on top of WVD is 'a perfect marriage'

April 2020, his first hands-on Nerdio Manager for WVD post. Bas's stance: Microsoft is doing an excellent job on the WVD core (he praises the 2020 spring release and MSIX AppAttach progress), and Nerdio takes it 'a few steps further even, innovating on top of WVD - a perfect marriage.' He singles out deployment options, image and ongoing management, cost optimization, automation and ease of use as the areas where Nerdio is 'the one to watch.'

Context: this predates his later employment at Nerdio, so it's an early, organically positive read from an MVP. The note worth tracking is how clearly he separates the platform vendor (Microsoft) from the value-add layer (Nerdio) and credits both.
