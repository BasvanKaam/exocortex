# Prefer ProPlus for its licensing model and flexibility

Both Office scenarios have pros and cons, but Bas's preference in most cases goes to the Office 365 ProPlus variant. The licensing model (Opex subscription rather than a Capex one-off) plays a big role, as does the flexibility the software brings and the continuous stream of new features, which many businesses see as a major plus. He frames the choice as one that demands a solid plan or roadmap looking 5 to 10 years ahead, grounded in the right information rather than picked ad hoc.
