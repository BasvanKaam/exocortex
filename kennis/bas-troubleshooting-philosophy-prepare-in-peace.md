# Bas's troubleshooting philosophy: prepare in times of peace

Beyond the many free tools, Bas argues IT administrators too often forget the basics: you must know and understand the products you work with before anything else. These bullets, he notes, apply to all technologies, not just Citrix:

- Understand the architecture you're dealing with: the FMA, its main components and services, communication paths, including the printing pathways and the UPS/UPD setup and traffic flow.
- Understand expected behaviour and interaction: how it all works under normal circumstances (one of his main reasons for writing the paper).
- Assemble a personal tool kit: research tools beforehand so you know each one's exact purpose and operation, saving valuable time when things go wrong.

His memorable summary: 'prepare at times of peace,' because things will go wrong sooner or later.
