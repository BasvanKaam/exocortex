# SCAF Engagement: From First Conversation to Kick-off

Before any SCAF work starts, the handbook prescribes a deliberate commercial onboarding flow that doubles as a fit check:

1. Initiation phase: one or more meetings to explore whether and how the SCAF applies. After mutual introductions, both sides go deeper into the current way of working, wishes, challenges and any up-front questions. A central point: the "gut feeling" between both parties must also be right, alongside short lines of communication and clarity.
2. Proposal: a written proposal explaining step by step how Salomon will work, the results, the scope, and what is expected back from the client.
3. Order confirmation (opdrachtbevestiging): a follow-up meeting to discuss details and jointly draft and sign an order confirmation that summarises the assignment, the work to be done, an estimated lead time and the expected financial investment.
4. Kick-off: once the signed confirmation is back, an "official" kick-off with all stakeholders present, explaining the approach and the plan for the first days to two weeks, immediately scheduling a first follow-up and leaving room for questions, so there is clarity from day one.

This is the practical, client-facing front end that turns the SCAF model into a billable, scoped engagement.
