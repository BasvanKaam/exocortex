# We Give the Insight, You Make the Choice

A recurring stance that runs through Bas's consulting work: the consultant's role is to surface facts and insight, not to make the decision for the client. He states it plainly in the context of an independent audit. The client's underlying reasons for wanting a review are often not, or less, relevant to him. What matters is delivering clear insight so the client can make the right call.

This pairs with his preference for facts over assumptions and for not pushing the client toward any predetermined answer. The motive is the client's; the insight is his contribution. Quality comes before speed: enough preparation and time go into the work because quality is paramount.
