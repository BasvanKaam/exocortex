# If you have to give a discount, you were already too expensive

A sales-philosophy idea Bas floated for a Salomon internal session. The core argument: price is not always the most important factor in a deal, and the moment you have to discount, you have effectively priced yourself wrong from the start. The framing pushes engineers and consultants to sell on value and to anticipate and counter the customer's objections ("weerleggen of slim beantwoorden") rather than reflexively dropping price.

Proposed session format alongside this: walk through the three most common customer situations together, look at what goes wrong and how to fix it, and what goes well so colleagues can reuse it. The underlying aim he names is becoming more successful with your own existing customers by learning from each other's mistakes and successes.
