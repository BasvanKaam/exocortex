# The Technology / Innovation Board

Bas presents a Technology / Innovation Board as the next step building on the low-hanging fruit: keep in regular contact, keep the dialogue going, discuss new ideas and possibilities. Speaking more often shrinks the distance and creates an innovation process. Exploring how the cloud can help further is not a one-off action — you keep asking: is what we do still right, are we doing it well, what could be different or better? 'De kracht van vragen (blijven) stellen' (the power of (keeping on) asking questions).

Definition: A Technology or Innovation Board is a strategic body responsible for monitoring and steering technological innovations and developments. It evaluates new technologies, identifies opportunities for innovation, and partly determines the organisation's technological course. By consulting regularly with stakeholders such as IT experts, business leaders, and innovation managers, it ensures technology investments align with business objectives and the organisation stays agile.
