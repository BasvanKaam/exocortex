# Run a CDF trace before you contact Citrix Support

Best-practice stance (March 2015): 'Consider it to be a good practice to run a CDF trace on the components you suspect, making sure to include all modules... together with a collect and upload also run from Scout, before contacting CTX Support. It will at least safe you a day or two.'

Broader ethos he repeats: 'I personally consider it to be a good practice to understand what happens underneath the covers whenever possible.' He's clear-eyed about limits, though - parsing .etl files is relatively easy, but actually reading them to find the fault 'is something not to be taken lightly' and needs special skills. His framing positions the admin as an active partner to support, not a ticket-filer, while respecting where deep CTX expertise (and private TMF files) is genuinely required.
