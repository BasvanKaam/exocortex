# Pick a stable foundation to write about, not a moving target

Bas reveals (March 2016) that this is actually his second serious attempt at a book. The first, on Citrix XenMobile, he abandoned because the product moved too fast: "whenever I finished a section or chapter something had already changed or Citrix would have at least announced a new release within the coming weeks." He found that frustrating and counter-productive, called it a day, and recycled the material into detailed blog posts instead. The FMA was different precisely because, although constantly improved, "the basics remain." His framing: choose durable fundamentals over the latest-and-greatest when you want work to last. A lesson that ages well into the AI era too.
