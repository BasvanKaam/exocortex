# Remote Work Is a Talent Requirement and an Opportunity

Bas argues that for many younger employees, particularly Generation Y (Millennials), being able to work mobile and from home is a hard requirement; employers who cannot facilitate it may miss out on talent. He reframes the crisis as an opportunity to ready the organization for what is coming, and predicts current employees will be grateful for it. He also gives result-oriented working a central role: it has never been as relevant as now, and where good result-driven agreements exist, employees should decide when they are most productive and work then.

Personally, he values 'office time' and says 100% home working would not suit him, while stressing this differs per person ('waar het voor de een past als een handschoen zit het voor de ander te krap'). Human contact remains indispensable no matter how digital the world becomes.

Source: Bas van Kaam, Salomon IT blog, 2020-03-18.
