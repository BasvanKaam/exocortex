# Cloud business-case template (eleven dimensions)

The business-case template Bas hands learners, framed to push back on the idea that a business case is just a financial comparison (for cloud it is usually far more). Eleven points of attention, each with a description column to fill in: Background (how it arose); Added value (what it delivers/improves); Ease of use (training, qualified staff, responsibility, management effort); Technology/alternatives (proven, reliable, mature, flexible); Administrative impact (on work processes and procedures); Risks and impact (identify and record); Lead time (gross and net, what happens when); Legal consequences (law, policy, privacy); ROI and TCO (migration/transition effort, final/temporary/one-off costs); Supplier qualifications (experience, references, security, contract terms); and Organization (governance, control, data ownership, support). The same template is reused for a DaaS-specific business case, with a worked filled-in example.
