# The Avensus group, Salomon IT and Come Get IT (CGIT): how they fit together

Company-history context Bas wrote to clear up confusion among clients, partners and colleagues during a fast growth phase (multiple acquisitions, new colleagues, partnerships).

The Avensus genealogy:
- Avensus started as 'de Computerij Groep' and has operated under the name Avensus since 2000. It consists of two business units: HGS (High Grade Security) and MCS (Managed Cloud Services).
- End of November 2016 (per a computable.nl article 'Investeerders stappen in bij Avensus'), investment firm Holland Venture and entrepreneur Abram Schermer invested in Avensus.
- Abram Schermer sold his earlier company Qwise to the Vision IT Group in 2010, and from 2015 worked as an independent investor and entrepreneur.
- The 'Avensus group' grew through acquisitions: ICT Vision (Feb 2018), AuditConnect (Jul 2018), Client ICT (Dec 2018). So the so-called Avensus group = Avensus (HGS + MCS), ICT Vision, AuditConnect, Client ICT. Bas notes 'Avensus group' was never a deliberate marketing term; it arose organically and was picked up by (social) media. The entities operate partly independently.

Separate investment vehicle: Salomon IT, TIEN-IT and SBC-Solutions come from Savoy & Meadow Investments, also owned by Abram Schermer. TIEN-IT focuses almost exclusively on cloud technologies and developments; SBC-Solutions (acquired mid-2017) does consultancy and detachering around virtualization and Application Delivery. Salomon IT (founded roughly three years before this post) differentiates on assessments and detailed roadmap designs, slogan 'wij geven het inzicht, jij maakt de juiste keuzes'. All operate independently but collaborate, including with Avensus.

Come Get IT (CGIT): an independent central IT knowledge-sharing platform, open to contributions from everyone, not just the above companies. Multiple colleagues from each organization/business unit contribute via podcasts, blogs and live events. CGIT is the overarching umbrella.
