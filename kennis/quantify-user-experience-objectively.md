# Quantify user experience objectively

Bas's stance: the universally recognised pain of slow logins and sluggish apps (the user who 'drinks two cups of coffee' before they can work) is usually hard or impossible for IT teams to root-cause, and that has to change. His position is that end-user experience should be expressed in objective numbers rather than left to anecdote and guesswork.

Two connected convictions: (1) the whole chain counts, since experience is only as strong as the weakest link, so you must look end-to-end; and (2) the role of the consultant is to give insight, not to dictate the choice — 'wij geven het inzicht, en maak jij vervolgens de juiste keuze'. Prevention beats cure, so monitoring should be continuous, not a one-off snapshot.
