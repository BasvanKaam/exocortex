# SCAF Adoption-phase template as a consulting instrument

The Adoption-phase working template (instrument layer beyond the book). In this phase a large part of the work started in the Execution phase is finished and taken 'into production', after which daily operations take over. What the template drives:

- Supporting documents: a combined PDCA/DMAIC cycle, a progress / skill-readiness training plan, a cost-awareness strategy overview (tooling used), and a communication model (schema plus names and contacts).
- Migration/adoption: wave-3 applications/systems/datasets are migrated and completed in this phase, finishing what started in Execution.
- Process fine-tuning: where Execution introduced and adjusted processes, Adoption mainly fine-tunes them - change, incident and problem management shift further, especially under a more agile way of working (faster changes, faster decommissioning).
- Skill gaps: the earlier skill-readiness plan is fully worked off so the knowledge can be applied immediately; per-department or per-team training plans may be written.
- Progress and project management: although teams, roles and responsibilities sit in the RACI model, the work itself is tracked separately; regular progress communication goes to user organizations and management.
- End-state: a fully operational (hybrid) cloud platform - all cloud services built, tested, delivered and taken into management.
- Documentation and reporting: agree with the client whether and in what form system/management documentation is delivered (their template or his); this is the moment of the final report, optionally with a separate management summary per phase via the Management Summary Palette.
