# Bas opens the COVID chapter with a Nostradamus quatrain

A characteristic Bas flourish: he prefaces the COVID-19 section with the remark that no cloud book is complete without a piece on COVID/corona, which we may now regard as a fixed part of the "nieuwe normaal" (new normal), "wat dat ook precies mag betekenen."

He then quotes a Nostradamus quatrain about a great plague in the sea of the great city that does not cease for seven months, spreading death and disease, arriving like a lightning flash and fading just as fast. He notes that many interpret such quatrains as describing a worldwide pandemic, with the "grote stad" read symbolically as the world-city, a metaphor for globalisation and international connectedness, and invites readers to research more examples themselves.
