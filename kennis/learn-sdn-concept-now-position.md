# Learn the virtual-network concept now, even if you won't deploy it soon

Writing in October 2013, Bas predicts multi-tenant virtual networking will spread: "As time progresses, we'll probably see more and more of these kinds of infrastructures being implemented." His recommendation is not to implement SDN immediately but to understand the concept and its consequences early, because it will become common. He admits he is "no networking specialist" but still rates it a great technology. A recurring move: flag the durable concept over the perishable product.
