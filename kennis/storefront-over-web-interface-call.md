# StoreFront over Web Interface is a good thing (April 2013)

Bas comes down clearly on the StoreFront side: "For me the replacement of Web Interface by StoreFront is a good thing, although it is a nice product StoreFront is just more robust and I like its modularity. One interface connects you to all your internal resources including AppController and provides you with Single Sign-on along the way."

*Bron: blogpost 'Post Excalibur remarks' (2013-04-10), basvankaam.com.*
