# Office 365 subscription families and plans

The licensing map Bas uses in the whitepaper. Office 365 splits into subscription families, then plans:
- Business (max 300 users): Business Essentials, Business, Business Premium.
- Enterprise (unlimited): E1, E3, E5, F1.
- Education (unlimited): Education, Education E5.
- Government (unlimited): mirrors the Business plans.

ProPlus can be bought standalone or as part of an Office 365 subscription (monthly), which is the most common route. Licenses are managed in the Admin Center; subscriptions of 25 licenses or fewer can be cancelled self-service, more require phoning Microsoft. Dated circa 2019 plan naming.
