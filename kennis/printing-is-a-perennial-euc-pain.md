# Printing is the perennial pain admins shouldn't have to focus on

Bas openly admits a long personal interest in printing and a history of failures with it in Citrix/RDSH/VDI ('who hasn't?'), Feb 2018. His framing is in the title: an admin's job is to focus on the platform (Citrix, VMware, Microsoft), not to fight printing. His take on universal print drivers is balanced rather than cheerleading: they 'work ok until you need a specific, more advanced print feature, which is only supported by the native print driver', so the ideal is a universal driver that retains full native feature parity, which he says Tricerat delivers. Reflects his pattern of championing point solutions that remove a known operational headache from the EUC stack.

*Bron: blogpost 'Focus on Citrix (or VMware, or Microsoft) - not printing!' (2018-02-21), basvankaam.com.*
