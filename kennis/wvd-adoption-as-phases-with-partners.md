# Framing WVD rollout as phases, mapping partners onto each step

How he approached the WVD partner landscape in April 2020: instead of trying to keep an exhaustive, always-out-of-date partner cheat sheet, he reframed it as a journey. Like any new digital workspace concept, implementing and adopting WVD goes through a few phases (from A to Z), and you can map the main third-party partners onto each step. In the talk he highlighted 4-5 partners more specifically rather than covering all of them.

Dated as to the specifics (WVD branding, the 2020 partner set), but the durable move is the framing: present a sprawling, fast-moving ecosystem as a staged adoption path so the audience gets a mental map instead of a flat list.

*Bron: blogpost 'Previewing his WVD partner cheat sheet talk at the MS Community event' (2020-04-08), basvankaam.com.*
