# A complete, current WVD partner cheat sheet is near impossible to maintain

April 2020. Bas, the cheat-sheet guy, openly admits the limits of his own format: keeping a WVD partner cheat sheet up to date is 'near to impossible.' The technological partner landscape around WVD was diverse and growing 'by the week/month.' His response wasn't to give up but to change approach (phased journey, highlight a handful of partners). He's candid that he can't cover every partner and that '100% satisfaction is never guaranteed.'

This shows his self-aware stance on his own content brand: the sheets are useful, but he won't pretend they're complete or evergreen.

*Bron: blogpost 'Previewing his WVD partner cheat sheet talk at the MS Community event' (2020-04-08), basvankaam.com.*
