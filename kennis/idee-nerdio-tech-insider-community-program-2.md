# Idea: a sales-free tech community feedback program

Bas's model for the Nerdio Technology Insider Program, a reusable community-building blueprint:

- **Sales/marketing free by design.** The promise is zero sales or marketing material; give tech/community folks what they actually want to see, hear, and play with.
- **Quality over quantity.** Start small (a Meetup page), do not chase member counts. If it grows, great; if not, fine. The aim is genuine contact, not scale.
- **Feedback into an agile loop.** Bas had already been collecting feedback informally for months before naming the program. Community input (from partners, customers, colleagues, peers, interested parties) feeds the roadmap. Monthly software releases make the loop visible: contributors see their suggestions ship.
- **What it offers members:** regular portfolio updates, early/hands-on product access, online webinars and technical sessions, in-person dinner/fun events when possible, swag, and a rebate/referral program.
- **Tone of the pitch:** "come 'Nerd' with us", getting to know members, valuing their feedback, helping in any way (demos, support with prospects).

Reusable takeaway: name and formalize the community you already have, anchor it on quality contact and a visible feedback-to-release loop, and keep it explicitly free of sales pressure.
