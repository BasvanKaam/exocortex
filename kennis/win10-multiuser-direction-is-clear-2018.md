# Bas: the direction is already clear, enough has been said

Writing in August 2018, Bas deliberately steps back from adding fresh opinion. He had shared his thoughts back on 26 March 2018 (alongside Brian Madden, Rob Beekmans and others), and now says "I think enough has been said and shared already and based on what we know I think it's pretty clear where this is heading." His read: RDSH on Windows Server is not going away (server will keep offering it), and Windows 10 multi-session will be an additional/alternative option rather than a replacement. He is cautious, repeatedly warning the picture could shift ("don't get your hopes up just yet"). This positions him as a community curator-aggregator at this moment rather than a hot-take generator, and his restraint proved well-calibrated: Windows 10 multi-session did ship and coexisted with traditional RDSH.
