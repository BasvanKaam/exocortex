# The rapid Current Release cadence makes sense

December 2016. He pushes back on a common complaint that Citrix's rapid XA/XD release pace is excessive, that admins can't keep up, that it's 'crazy.'

His argument: not much actually changed. Before CR/LTSR, releases were further apart but there were multiple Feature Packs every 4-6 months (7.6 had three Feature Packs before becoming LTSR). Now CR delivers the same incremental value as new versions, and LTSR gets Cumulative Updates instead of Rollup Packs. CR support is 'basically a name change, nothing more.' You don't have to upgrade or keep up, it's a choice, just as before, and installing Feature/Rollup Packs all over the place was never a walk in the park either.

He credits the cadence: Citrix has been far more innovative, focused and on top of their game than in previous years, having rebuilt the XenApp features lost in the 7.x platform versus 6.5, plus added new capability. He also grants the critics a point: going from ~4 to 2-3 releases a year would make staying current easier for some companies. And he notes the real new wins are the stable LTSR (5-10 years support) and the Citrix Cloud, even if he knows almost no customers using the latter yet.

*Bron: blogpost 'What's new in XenApp & XenDesktop 7.12, and why the rapid release cycle makes sense' (2016-12-06), basvankaam.com.*
