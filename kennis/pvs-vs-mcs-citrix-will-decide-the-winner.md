# PVS vs MCS is a race, and Citrix will pick the survivor

In June 2013, ahead of the XenDesktop 7 launch, Bas framed PVS and MCS as competitors inside Citrix's own portfolio rather than complementary tools. His take: PVS is mature and growing but MCS keeps getting better release after release, and with Windows XP's EOL nearing 'PVS will have to dig deep to keep up.' He explicitly says 'it's up to Citrix which one will come out on top eventually,' reading the two products as headed for a reckoning. He cheers each PVS improvement (BDM, synthetic NIC switch) almost like a fan rooting for an underdog: 'PVS battling back!' Honest about his own gap: he had done the PVS exams but never run it in anger, and hoped it would 'be around long enough for me to get a taste.'
