# 2013 mobile management vendor landscape

How the field looked in mid-2013:
- **Citrix XenMobile** (after acquiring Zenprise): MDM + MAM via Worx/MDX containerization, micro VPN, ShareFile for MIM. Broadest device support (iOS, Android, Symbian, BlackBerry, Windows Phone).
- **VMware Horizon Mobile**: dual persona via on-device hypervisor for two specific Verizon Android phones (LG Intuition, Motorola RAZR M), and MAM/app-wrapping on iOS; no other devices.
- **BlackBerry**: Secure Work Space (MAM/wrapping) for Android and iOS as part of BlackBerry Enterprise Service 10; BlackBerry devices use BlackBerry Balance.
- **Samsung KNOX**: Android-only dual-persona framework built into the OS for deeper integration; partnered with Citrix, and KNOX container/security policies were integrated into XenMobile 8.5.

All mature solutions offered containerization, remote lock/wipe, app/URL black-and-whitelisting, and large policy sets.
