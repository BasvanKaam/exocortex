# Persistence beats everything: Bas's career and life advice

Bas's stock advice for starting (and succeeding at) anything, not just an IT career: be persistent, keep at it, and don't give up — that's it. He stresses it applies to everything: life, friends, family, being a dad, a husband, a friend, your career, sports, writing, learning. Learn from your mistakes. While pushing, take breaks and watch your health, however tempting it is to keep going.

His closing wisdom in the same interview: stay close to yourself and don't copy others — people will like you because of you (or not). Being inspired by others is fine, but don't go overboard; develop your own identity. Get a mentor if you can. Treat others the way you want to be treated, with respect and kindness. And again: it's all about being persistent — keep it up, do your best, and your time will come.

He also frames his greatest achievement not as a technical one but as personal growth: overcoming fears like public speaking to advance his career, and knowing when to step up versus when to back down and leave others to it.
