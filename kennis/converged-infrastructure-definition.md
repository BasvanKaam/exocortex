# What a converged infrastructure is

A converged infrastructure combines or integrates multiple infrastructural components (server compute, storage, networking and virtualization) into a single platform or appliance. Resources are aggregated and pooled, improving performance, flexibility and efficiency. Management is centralized and meant to be handled as a single entity no matter how big the system grows; initial setup is fast and maintenance minimal, lowering operational costs (ROI). These are also called converged or software-defined (virtualized) data centers. The durable point: it is not that hardware disappears (you still need cooling, power, disks, flash, networking) but that the way components are put together (converged) and managed (software) changes.
