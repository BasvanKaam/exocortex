# Cloud is a potential solution, not a silver bullet

In his own contributed 'general recommendation' (January 2019), Bas positions himself as a 'whatever fits my customers use-case best' kind of guy. He likes Cloud, AWS, Azure and the rest, and particularly the flexibility they potentially offer, but treats Cloud as a potential solution to a problem, not a default. 'It’s no silver bullet.' In fact, used incorrectly, Cloud often introduces new challenges. At the same time he calls it undeniable that (hybrid) Cloud services in general are slowly taking over; it will take time but it is a fact. This is a measured, vendor-agnostic, pragmatist stance from the EUC/Citrix era, framing him as deliberately non-evangelical about Cloud.

*Bron: blogpost 'Byte sized Cloud design principles and architectural recommendations' (2019-01-30), basvankaam.com.*
