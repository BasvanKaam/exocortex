# Simple doesn't mean easy (cloud ops and finances)

Bas's recurring stance, drawn from the Dave Ramsey analogy: whether you're paying off debt or eliminating cloud waste, building savings or building cloud resiliency, the concepts are the same and the desired outcomes are clear, but the path to get there is personal, messy, underestimated and often overlooked. Everyone knows they should track costs, optimize resources, build buffers, prepare for failure and invest in skills. In practice most teams still struggle to do it consistently, or at all. The reason is not knowledge, it's behavior and discipline. "Simple doesn't mean easy." Execution is the differentiator, not the plan.
