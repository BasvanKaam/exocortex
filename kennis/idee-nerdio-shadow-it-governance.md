# Idea: Nerdio vs shadow IT

Content concept positioning Nerdio as a governance enabler, not just a deployment tool.

Define shadow IT in a Nerdio context: disconnected Intune configs, unmanaged groups, rogue app assignments. Detection tools: tenant monitoring (compare expected vs actual), Intune policy baselines (identify drift), audit logs (who changed what, when). Remediation: baseline templates to re-enforce standards, scheduled scripted actions to detect and fix.

Narrative hook: how a single missed setting caused a compliance breach and how Nerdio caught it. CTA: downloadable 'Shadow IT Risk Audit' script or checklist.
