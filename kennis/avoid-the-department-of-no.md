# Don't Become the Department of No

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

His stance: governance over the vendor portfolio is about alignment between business and IT and making deliberate commissioning decisions, not about blocking. He is blunt that it is not always possible to block parts of a provider's portfolio or otherwise prevent service commissioning, and that trying to is the wrong instinct.

The explicit warning is to avoid becoming the "Department of No". The alternative he advocates is communication: inform your customers well, then let them make the decisions. Governance creates business value through good portfolio choices and informed customers, not through veto power.
