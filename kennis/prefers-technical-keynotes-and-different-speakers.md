# Prefers the technical keynotes, but seeks out 'different' speakers

A small but telling self-disclosure from the Synergy 2017 schedule post: across both Synergy and Summit, Bas says he is 'always looking forward to the vision and more technical Keynotes' and from experience 'slightly prefer[s] the more technical Keynotes, on average that is.' Yet he places the non-technical Malcolm Gladwell 'Innovation super session' high on his list precisely because 'I'm always inspired by experienced speakers who take a different approach.' The combination, a default pull toward deep technical content plus a deliberate appetite for outside-the-bubble thinking, is a consistent feature of how he learns and, later, how he creates content.
