# Bas leans into hyper-convergence and web-scale as the future

Framing (March 2015): Bas is visibly enthusiastic about the NPX, treating it as a serious milestone ('not just another IT certification'). He approvingly quotes Nutanix's Mark Brunstad that the program answers 'the global shortage of enterprise architects who really understand... software defined storage and hyper-convergence... that's the future of IT.' His own admiration is for the breadth required: you 'really require a high level of proficiency in all related software stacks that might run on top of the Nutanix web-scale orientated platform.'

Context for his evolution: this is a Citrix/EUC blogger in 2015 already orienting toward hyper-converged infrastructure and hypervisor-agnostic, web-scale thinking - an early signal of his move beyond pure Citrix toward broader datacenter/cloud topics. He frames the difficulty as a feature: 'The harder the fight the sweeter the victory.'

*Bron: blogpost 'Sh*t just got real - the Nutanix Platform Expert program' (2015-03-24), basvankaam.com.*
