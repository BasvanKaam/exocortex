# Bas's troubleshooting and sizing philosophy

Bas's recurring methodology: successful troubleshooting starts with understanding the environment, architecture and components you work with — knowing how traffic flows is the only way to troubleshoot the FMA effectively. In times of 'peace', get to know the various tools and methodologies, and assemble your own toolkit and approach.

On sizing: there is no 'one size fits all'. Understand your workloads and size accordingly, and remember it's more than compute — don't forget the underlying storage platform. Don't overdo it; real-world testing is always needed. Ask peers, consult the Citrix XenDesktop Handbook, use Project Accelerator, and test, test and test some more. Load testing only indicates what might be possible because users are very unpredictable — always include any exotic applications in load tests, since those are the ones to be most curious about.
