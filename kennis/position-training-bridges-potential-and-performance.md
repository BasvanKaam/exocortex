# Training Bridges the Gap Between Potential and Performance

Bas's argument for why training is core to realizing a tool's value: knowing what to do, how to do it, and the most efficient way to get it done is essential to maximize any IT solution. Without proper training, even the most advanced systems lead to frustration and missed opportunity. With it, teams take full control, optimize workflows, and focus on what matters — innovation or personal time. Training 'bridges the gap between potential and performance.' This is how he ties his L&D role directly to customer ROI.
