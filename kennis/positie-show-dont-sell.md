# Show, don't sell

Bas's stance on technical selling: a live, partially pre-configured environment that the prospect can log into and interact with beats any slide deck or RFP response. Traditional RFPs are 'long, generic, and full of assumptions'. A working setup is 'fast, focused, and makes an immediate impact'. It complements the formal process rather than replacing it: you still submit the forms, but you bring 'proof, not promises'. He leans on the psychology: once a prospect uses something that reflects their own world, walking away feels like losing progress ('future lock-in').
