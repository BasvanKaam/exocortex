# Define what an agent outcome is worth before you open a dashboard

Leadership asks the same thing about every agent and Copilot project: we paid for this, what are we getting back? Rightfully so, says Bas. Real value-adds are still rare, and agents are "like the Oprah show, everyone gets one." His advice: do not wait for tooling or GA. Pick one agent you already run and write down this week what a good outcome is worth to the business, in real numbers, before you touch any dashboard.

## The catch he warns about
ROI tooling (such as Foundry's agent ROI preview) makes you set what a passing result is worth. A soft definition hands you a flattering number. It forces you to answer the outcome question, but also lets you answer it in your own favor. Read every early ROI screenshot with that in mind, your own included.

## Why it matters
"The teams that pull ahead are the ones who can say, on paper, what they paid and what they got back." Define value honestly, measure it against cost, and you walk into the leadership meeting with an answer instead of a feeling. Start with the end in mind.

This is his cloud stance (business before technology, cost is not the main driver, the business case first) applied to agents.
