# Continuous improvement is a mindset every stakeholder must adopt

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

The framework's stance is that it is very easy to just go through the motions, doing work because it is expected. His counter is that everything done should ultimately contribute to the organization's goals, and that continuous improvement is a mindset every stakeholder must adopt within the scope of their own responsibilities, not a separate process bolted on. He also insists the organization must actively enable it: determine incentives that stimulate improvement and design work structures that genuinely give teams time to do it. Without incentives and protected time, the mindset will not survive day-to-day pressure.
