# Bas's general troubleshooting tips (facts, not guesses)

A collection of practical troubleshooting tips Bas picked up along the way:

- Never guess the solution: always base actions on facts.
- Don't assume anything, no matter how obvious it seems.
- Isolate the issue, make it smaller, take it step by step.
- Inventorise all information found; categorise and prioritise it. Try the option with the least impact first, and always have a plan B.
- Come up with a morning ritual: a 5-to-10-minute daily stand-up so everyone knows the (attack) plan for the day and can discuss progress and setbacks.
- Assign a specific issue to a specific person, giving a single point of reference.
- Keep track of what you and the team have done, so you don't re-run the same tests or install a hotfix for the third time unknowingly. Bas thinks this point is often underestimated.
- Think out loud and share information.
- Don't forget social media: even a single retweet can do the trick.
- A BIG one: ask for help. Don't think you know it all, because you don't. When in doubt, ask, rather than making a big problem worse.
