# XenMobile is hard to deploy even when you know what you're doing

Bas's candid 2014 take on XenMobile: despite a year-plus of mass deployment by 'hundreds, if not thousands' of consultants, 'I have still to come across the first summary stating that it all went smoothly.' His framing: 'it can be very complex to implement and manage if you don't exactly know what you're doing and even if you do it's no walk in the park.' He calls XenMobile one of Citrix's most comprehensive products to date and matured to enterprise-ready, but is honest that real-world deployments are painful. Shows his from-the-field pragmatism: he aggregated others' pain into a survival guide rather than pretending the product was smooth.
