# Never skip the basics: start troubleshooting at the event logs

A small but characteristic methodological stance from Bas (Nov 2018). When troubleshooting slow user logins, application or networking issues, or storage bottlenecks, his answer to "where do you start?" is the Windows event logs, "never skip the basics." Even while promoting a sophisticated monitoring product (Stratusphere UX), he grounds the value in a fundamentals-first mindset: the tooling matters because it makes the basics (like event logs) more visual, navigable and historically groupable, not because it replaces them. He also signals a personal taste that shapes his tool preferences: "I just like it when things are handy and manageable from a single interface." This single-pane-of-glass preference recurs across his product write-ups.
