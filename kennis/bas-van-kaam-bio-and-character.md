# Bas van Kaam: experience, character and the people behind the book

Bas describes himself as a (Field) CTO, Principal Technical Trainer, and technical/business consultant with over 25 years in IT (working since 1998). He has helped hundreds of companies move to the cloud, from small businesses to large multinationals, from 5 to 50,000 users. He has worn many "petten" (hats), from junior to heavy-senior roles, and is in the fortunate position of having witnessed the very start of computer virtualisation and cloud computing, so "er is geen projecttype dat ik niet eerder heb gezien."

Update (2026): his Nerdio role has since evolved from Field CTO EMEA into Learning & Development. He was Principal Technical Trainer (2025 to 2026) and is now Principal Learning Architect (2026 to present). See the career timeline note for the full arc.

On character, he describes himself as a real "streber": all-in, you do it well or not at all, someone who always wants to win and is a poor loser (though he hides the latter well now, "meestal dan"). These traits brought him to where he is: grateful, happy, healthy, successful. Another trait he enjoys is making others happy, giving away things he no longer uses, buying small gifts spontaneously, and helping people. After 25 years it felt like time to share his knowledge with a bigger audience.

The book is dedicated to his late mother-in-law Loes Schellekens (a "powervrouw," taken too early at 65), and he credits his wife Tineke van Kaam-Schellekens: "dit boek er niet was geweest zonder mijn waanzinnig lieve, leuke en begripvolle vrouw ... Love you babe X."
