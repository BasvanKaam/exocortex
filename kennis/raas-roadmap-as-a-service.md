# Idea: RaaS, Roadmap as a Service

Package roadmap-building as a productised advisory service. The premise: clients struggle to choose between cloud options, and a today's choice has a large effect on the rest of the year and the years after, across hardware, software, and organisation.

A related design principle he proposes: design an environment with the year 2023 in mind. Everyone is already doing 'something with 2020 or 2025', so naming a closer, more specific horizon stands out, and forces the real question: how do you build today while accounting for plans two to three years out?

This sits alongside productising health checks and assessments (he notes competitors charge ~18,000 euro for an extensive health check), turning advisory deliverables into named, sellable offerings.
