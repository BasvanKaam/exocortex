# SCAF Strategy-phase intake template (strategie-samenvatting)

A supporting document used in the Strategy phase of SCAF to capture an organization's starting position on a single page. It is a structured intake/interview form, not a deliverable in itself, that feeds the later business case and Go/No-Go.

Fields it captures:
- Primary drivers for moving to cloud, plus specific requirements, wishes and strategic objectives.
- Mission, vision and core values.
- Challenges, risks, concerns and other particulars.
- Running projects of any significance (multiple rows), each with their own risks/concerns.
- Desired outcomes and the KPIs that define "satisfied".
- IT and security policy, plus a free "other" field.
- A concluding summary.

This is one of the SCAF supporting artifacts a consultant fills in with the client at the front of an engagement; it operationalizes the abstract Strategy phase into concrete questions.
