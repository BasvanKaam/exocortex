# Standardized pre-built templates over starting from scratch

Bas's stance behind the SCAF toolkit: practical, pre-built, standardized templates save organisations significant time and money during cloud implementation and adoption, while still leaving room to apply your own creativity and insights to refine the process. He favours giving people a ready-to-use, accessible starting point rather than a blank page.
