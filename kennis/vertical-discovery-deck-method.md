# Vertical-tailored discovery deck for smaller opportunities

Bas's lightweight alternative to a full success-criteria document for smaller opportunities where a heavy doc doesn't make sense. He built a single interactive slide deck in multiple vertical versions (Government, Healthcare, Education, plus a general one).

How it's designed:

- The first slide (agenda) sets up the rest of the discovery conversation, letting you steer toward specific topics or a demo.
- It opens by naming the main challenges for that vertical/sector — used as a resonance check ("does this land?"), which it usually does. Explaining how you help is the easy part; the line-of-business context you bring beforehand.
- It's navigable (arrows to jump between sections); icons and text on a content slide are clickable and link out to relevant info, including the Nerdio website.
- It also carries install/prerequisites info (link to install guide), firewall ports, an FAQ section to start the conversation or to help the prospect discuss internally, and case studies.
- Even if it's never opened in a meeting, it stands alone as a summary of who you are, what you do, and where to find more.

The reusable idea: a single self-contained, interactive, vertical-aware deck that works both as a live discovery aid and as a leave-behind.
