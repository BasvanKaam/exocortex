# Framehawk and the Lightweight Framebuffer Protocol (LFP)

How it worked then (2014): Citrix acquired Framehawk, whose **Lightweight Framebuffer Protocol (LFP)** was designed for long-distance, high-latency networks, with origins (per Citrix) in the founders' experience with spacecraft communications at NASA. It aimed to maintain quality and security over poor links and provide a strong experience on mobile devices (touch, gesture mapping, offset mouse, keyboard popup).

Framehawk later became part of the Citrix HDX protocol family (the HDX/Framehawk graphics mode), positioned for lossy/high-latency conditions alongside Thinwire. At announcement time Bas flagged it as potentially a competitive differentiator if it landed in XenApp or XenMobile.
