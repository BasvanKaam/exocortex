# Framehawk and the Lightweight Framebuffer Protocol (LFP)

How it worked then (2014): Citrix acquired Framehawk, whose value was its **Lightweight Framebuffer Protocol (LFP)**, a display protocol designed for long-distance, high-latency networks, with origins (per Citrix) in the founders' experience with spacecraft communications at NASA. LFP aimed to communicate over poor links while maintaining security with no or minimal loss of quality, and to deliver a strong user experience on mobile devices (touch, gesture mapping, offset mouse, keyboard popup).

Framehawk later became part of the Citrix HDX protocol family (the HDX/Framehawk graphics mode), positioned for lossy/high-latency conditions alongside Thinwire. The bet was that blending it into XenApp/XenDesktop/XenMobile would put Citrix ahead on user experience over bad networks. At announcement time Bas flagged it as potentially a competitive differentiator if it landed in XenApp or XenMobile.
