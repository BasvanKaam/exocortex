# Self-publishing via Create Space and KDP: separate the setup from the writing

How it worked then (2016): Bas self-published through Amazon Create Space (paperback) and, separately, Kindle Direct Publishing (e-book). Create Space offered free services worth using: interior reviewer, cover creator, standard and expanded distribution, and a free ISBN (with restrictions). Paid services covered design, editing and marketing, none of them cheap. Key lesson: get to know Create Space and KDP up front, separately from the writing, because learning the platforms takes days and you don't want surprises about layout once the book is nearly done and your head is full of ideas. He also warns self-published quality is close to but visibly below professionally printed books (cover DPI, paper, interior design).

*Bron: blogpost 'Inside Citrix - The making of & lessons learned!' (2016-08-15), basvankaam.com.*
