# myCUGC: the Citrix User Group Community

myCUGC (Citrix User Group Community, www.mycugc.org) is a Citrix community initiative launched the year before the book, with over 8000 registered members since May 2015 (Bas guessed it would near or surpass 10,000 by publication). The main forum is active all week; CTPs (Citrix Technology Professionals) and members contribute via a community roundtable, blog posts, SIGs (Special Interest Groups), and webinars.

It is also where new CUGs (Citrix User Groups) are formed: starting a thread in the dedicated 'Group starting' forum section is enough, and groups typically went from forum request to an official in-person meeting (often 20-25 attendees) within a few weeks. Around 45 new groups had formed at the time of writing. CUGC describes itself as 'for users, by users', a source of high-value content, an online and in-person hub for connections, and a voice of influence with Citrix.
