# Excalibur is XenDesktop-centric and steals XenApp's thunder (April 2013)

Prompted by a reader's comment, Bas concedes a real concern: the Tech Preview is "primarily XenDesktop being referenced, stealing XenApp's thunder." He lists XenApp 6.5 features absent from Studio - hotfix management, application folders, load balance policies, worker groups, zones, the streamed/streamed-to-server app types, Lingering and Pre-Launch. His framing is cautiously optimistic but conditional: "I'm pretty sure we'll see some of the above features back in one of the later Excalibur (XD7) releases." In comment replies he sharpens it: if the XenApp integration ships this thin, it would be a problem; he expects "a whole other product in a few months time" and reads admin resistance as people who "spent the past 4 years or more behind your AppCenter console" struggling to accept change. A snapshot of his thinking mid-transition - excited about the architecture, wary of the feature gap.
