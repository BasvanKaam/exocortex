# Linking infrastructure performance to employee productivity

From the March 2021 EUCdigest episode with Barry Schiffer (who co-created RDanalyzer with Bram Wolfs and joined eG Innovations during COVID): the central theme is monitoring the performance of an IT infrastructure and how that relates to the productivity of employees. The durable EUC idea here is that infrastructure performance is not an end in itself but a lever on end-user productivity and experience, a thread that runs through Bas's interest in benchmarking and monitoring (see VDI Drones).
