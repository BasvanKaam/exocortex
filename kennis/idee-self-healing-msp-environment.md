# Idea: The self-healing MSP environment

Content concept: Nerdio as the nervous system of a responsive IT service. Use automation to detect, report, and remediate without human intervention.

Self-healing scenarios:
- VM stuck in 'Failed' -> auto-restart script.
- Drift detected in Defender settings -> baseline re-applied.
- Unused VMs running outside business hours -> scheduled auto-stop.

Technical components: scripted actions, alerts plus webhooks, policy monitoring plus logging. Setup guidance: use tags, thresholds, and scheduling logic to reduce false positives. CTA: three ready-to-import self-healing actions for download.
