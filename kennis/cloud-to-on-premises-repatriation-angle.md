# The 'Cloud -> On-premises' counter-trend (draft angle)

In an unfinished, deliberately more-technical Salomon blog draft, Bas signals that no tech-flavoured article escapes the cloud topic, but he chooses to lead with a reversal heading: 'Cloud -> On-premises'. The intent is to discuss technological developments that he and Salomon's customers will deal with in the coming years, framed 'hoog over' (high level).

The draft cuts off before the argument is made, so this is an angle rather than a finished position: the notion that the one-directional cloud narrative on his social timelines deserves a counterpoint, with workloads potentially moving back from cloud to on-premises. Pairs with his broader 'don't stress about the cloud' and vendor-independent stances.
