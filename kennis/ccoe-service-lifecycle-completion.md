# CCoE owns service-lifecycle completion and currency

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

Additional continuous-improvement activities for the CCoE:

- Make sure the service lifecycle is completed, meaning unused or obsolete cloud services are properly decommissioned.
- Confirm currency of cloud services.

Governance to-do list for this domain: determine incentives to stimulate continuous improvement; determine work structures that actually let teams spend time on it; and ensure the business is informed of the latest developments.
