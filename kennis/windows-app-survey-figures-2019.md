# Survey figures on Windows-app persistence (2019)

Bas's paper cites surveys showing the use of traditional Windows applications barely declines.

- A survey by van Kaam & Brinkhoff (2019), answered by 176 IT experts/architects/decision-makers worldwide, on the statement "Traditional (Windows) applications will stay with us for at least xx more years."
- End User Computing 2019 (Spruijt, Plettenberg & Brinkhoff, 2019), 580 participants: 29.97% use 100% Windows applications; 33.33% say 80-99% of their apps are Windows; for 17.43% it is 60-79% Windows applications. (No image reproduced due to copyright.)
