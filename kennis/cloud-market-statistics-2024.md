# Cloud Market Statistics (2024)

Key figures Bas cites in Chapter 3:
- Per Hostingtribunal.com, ~90% of organisations start with Azure or AWS, depending on use and business case.
- 2023 global cloud infrastructure spend ~$623 billion; 2024 expected to grow ~20% toward $727.9 billion. Source: Canalys and Marketstandards.
- 2024 global AI market estimated at nearly $235 billion, projected to exceed $631 billion by 2028. Source: IDC.
- 96% of companies use at least one public cloud service; 92% have adopted a multi-cloud strategy. Source: Spacelift and Techopedia.
- Hybrid cloud adoption rose from ~58% (2020) to ~73% (2024) — nearly three-quarters of companies combine public and private cloud. Source: Flexera.
- DaaS (Desktop-as-a-Service) market: projected CAGR of 13.9% from 2024-2031, reaching USD 14.6 billion by 2031. Source: Business Research Insights and Technavio.
- Microsoft 365 had ~345 million paying users worldwide in 2024, up from ~200 million in 2020. Source: DMR and Signhouse.
