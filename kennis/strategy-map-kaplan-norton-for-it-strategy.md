# Strategy Map (Kaplan & Norton) for IT Strategy Communication

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

The framework cites research that 95% of employees are not aware of their organization's strategic objectives, and recommends a Strategy Map (Kaplan & Norton) as the remedy.

A Strategy Map is a method to map the relations between resources and strategic objectives while facilitating communication about those objectives. Kaplan & Norton's research showed it significantly contributes to goal orientation and focus in realizing strategic objectives.

In this framework the Strategy Map doubles as a visual representation and summary of the IT strategy, used to communicate to the entire organization. By mapping the relations between strategic objectives and IT capabilities, the IT strategy significantly contributes to realizing the organization's strategic objectives.
