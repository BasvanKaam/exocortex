# Opdracht 3: Beheer-to-Regie Self-Assessment

Bas's third exercise ("Hoe Ver Zijn Jullie?") holds up a mirror to the organisation, assessing where it stands in the transition from Beheer to Regie. He notes the 12 statements look obvious at first glance, but in practice few organisations score an 8 or higher, and too few people actually ask whether they meet these conditions.

**Method:** score each of 12 statements from 1 to 5, for both Beheer and Regie. The 1-5 scale runs:
1. Fully Beheer — still wholly dependent on traditional IT processes; IT controls all decisions, little collaboration with the business.
2. Mainly Beheer — some attempts toward regie, but IT keeps most control; business not yet fully involved.
3. Mixed — good balance; IT and business collaborate, but steps remain.
4. Mainly Regie — strongly leaning to a regie model; IT mainly advisor/facilitator, with improvement points.
5. Fully Regie — IT fully integrated into business strategy and processes; innovation and tech decisions taken jointly with focus on business results.

Compute the average per column (total points / 12 statements) and read the result using the interpretation table.

Bas insists the assessment is not only about the organisation: you must also reflect on your own personal contribution to the transition, write down concrete actions to better align your way of working with regie principles, and consider how to act more as a strategic partner of the business rather than just an executor of technology.
