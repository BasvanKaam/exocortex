# There is no such thing as a cloud strategy

A sharp position: the term "Cloud-strategie" sounds good and rolls off the tongue, but in reality cloud is nothing more than a delivery or operating model. It is absolutely part of your IT strategy, but it is not a strategy in itself.

He leans on Paul Maritz to make the point: "Cloud is about how you do computing, not where you do computing" (Bas explicitly endorses the first half as fitting). Technology is supporting, not a goal; a real strategy describes how technology serves business-driven needs, not the other way around.

The consequence he presses: in a world where solutions are increasingly cloud-based, you still need a proper plan. A "day to day" mentality where you occasionally glance at how you work, with no solid plan behind it, will eventually backfire ("de deksel op je neus"). Without IT, he claims, 90%+ of companies would close within weeks. Roadmapping turns the strategy into something executable; smaller organizations especially lose overview, and outsourcing the direction (regie) on projects and strategy is a legitimate remedy.
