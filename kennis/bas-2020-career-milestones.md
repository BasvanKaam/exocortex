# Bas van Kaam 2020 career milestones

Key biographical facts from his 2020 year-in-review (useful for bio/voice grounding):

- Joined Nerdio in June 2020 as Field CTO for the EMEA/UK region, responsible for building out the business. Says he's never enjoyed his work as much.
- Co-authored the book 'Byte-Sized Cloud Design Principles and Architectural Recommendations' with Christiaan Brinkhoff; 6500+ downloads in 2020.
- Wrote 45 blog posts and 1 WVD whitepaper in 2020.
- Co-founded EUCdigest (one of three board members); the EUCdigest Podcast crew published 22 episodes - he notes he didn't attend a single recording.
- Founded the Dutch WVD User Group (with Stefan Dingemanse, later Esther Barthel and Niels Kok); ~240 Meetup members.
- Sparked a global WVD User Group movement: tweeted the idea, 6 groups within 24 hours, 14+ worldwide by year end.
- Founded the Nerdio Tech Insider Community Program; launched NMW Community Edition.
- Became a Microsoft Azure Certified Solution Architect; MVP for Enterprise Mobility (status nuance: his Oct 2019 award already covered 2020-2021, so no 2020 renewal needed).
- Personal: moved into a new home in March 2020 with a private office; turning 42 in a month; still runs 10K in under 45 minutes; attended 0 physical events all year (COVID).

*Bron: blogpost '2020 - my year in review' (2020-12-21), basvankaam.com.*
