# Repackaging existing capability as a focused new brand

The go-to-market idea behind De Cloud Professionals: it does not need to become a standalone company. It can be a collective name for 5 to 10 professionals already employed across the various business units (Avensus, Tien, SBC, Client ICT, Salomon). Bas frames this explicitly as "oude wijn in nieuwe zakken" (old wine in new bottles): a different way of presenting and packaging capability you already have in-house.

The only effort required: a separate website, a separate logo, a separate clear-and-short message, and pulling in the right people. The core insight is repackaging-as-strategy: "it's all about perception." Cloud workspace (a desktop from the cloud) is treated as trivial to deliver for them ('een fluitje van een cent'); the value of the new brand is in focus, naming, and presentation rather than new capability.

The service set behind it: AWS and Azure (primarily DaaS plus Office/Microsoft 365), WVD, Citrix, Amazon Workspaces, plus DRaaS, backup (including Office 365-specific), archiving, secure datashare, classic Citrix/RDS from the cloud, standalone Office 365, and workspace aggregation from a single portal.
