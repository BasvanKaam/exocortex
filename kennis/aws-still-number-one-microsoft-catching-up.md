# AWS is still No. 1 in cloud, Microsoft is catching up fast

In December 2018 Bas states his read of the market plainly: 'AWS has always been and still is the Nr 1 in Cloud services and solutions worldwide. Of course, Microsoft is catching up quickly.' Notable because Bas is fundamentally an EUC/Microsoft-ecosystem person, yet he gives AWS the crown here. On Outposts he is measured rather than hyped: 'Earth chocking? I don't think so, but a very welcome (and pretty huge) addition to their portfolio.' His framing throughout is that the major players now all accept a hybrid world as the reality ('I guess that's old news by now').
