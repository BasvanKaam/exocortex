# Against Jargon-Heavy, 100-Page Deliverables

Bas positions the SCAF against the consulting habit of producing 100+ page end reports full of jargon. His stance: only what actually matters to the client should be examined, discussed and tested, yielding tailored, to-the-point, independent advice every time. This is both a methodological and a brand position, advice should be lean, relevant and substantiated rather than voluminous.

Source: Bas van Kaam, Salomon IT blog, 2019-09-17.
