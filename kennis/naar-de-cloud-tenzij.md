# Position-led idea: 'to the cloud, unless'

A deliberately contrarian content angle: 'Bekijk het eens anders, naar de cloud, tenzij! On-prem is zo gek nog niet.' Invert the default cloud-first reflex and make the case that on-premises is not as crazy as it sounds, and that hybrid environments will grow large in the coming years.

The companion angle, 'De cloud, het is niet alles goud wat er blinkt' (the cloud, it's not all that glitters), is meant to surface the pitfalls people route around: where your data sits (GDPR), connectivity, and the partial loss of control that comes with the cloud. Pitfalls are usually avoided in marketing; leading with them is the differentiator.

This is both a content hook and a genuine advisory stance: not anti-cloud, but anti-reflex, push for the honest trade-off.
