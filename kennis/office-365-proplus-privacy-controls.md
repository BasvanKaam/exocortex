# ProPlus diagnostic data and privacy controls

ProPlus collects and sends diagnostic data about Office client usage to Microsoft to keep it secure, current, and improving, plus connected online services tied to Word/Excel/PowerPoint/Outlook (co-authoring, slide suggestions, communication). For privacy-sensitive organizations, Microsoft offers options to limit these services and the data collected, configurable via Group Policy and/or the Office Cloud Policy Service. Some services Microsoft marks as essential cannot be restricted, which is worth investigating or taking advice on.
