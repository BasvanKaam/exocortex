# Atomic Habits as a Lens on the Strategy Phase

A characteristic Bas aside: he recommends James Clear's book *Atomic Habits* ("aanrader"). Clear writes about forming habits and why people often fail at it. One of his tools is an "Implementation Intention plan" — essentially a strategy: what you do, when, under which circumstances, and so on.

Clear's key insight, which Bas finds fitting for the strategy phase of cloud adoption: you don't lack motivation, you lack clarity ("het ontbreekt je niet aan motivatie, maar aan duidelijkheid"). Bas found this an apt framing for the SCAF strategy phase.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> Kort gezegd, het ontbreekt je niet aan motivatie, maar aan duidelijkheid.
