# Quiz as Community Engagement

Idea: run a light history/trivia quiz (WVD launch dates, product precursors, founding year) as an icebreaker or engagement hook in a technical community webinar. Low-stakes nostalgia/history questions get an audience participating before the heavy technical content starts. Cheap to produce, reusable per session. Pair with the webinar onboarding arc.
