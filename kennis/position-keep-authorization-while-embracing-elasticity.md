# Position: Embrace Cloud Elasticity Without Losing Purchasing Authorization

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

The framework holds that the existing procurement and approval flow, often complex or bureaucratic, must be re-engineered to handle the elastic nature of cloud. Fluid scale-up and scale-down is a genuine cloud benefit and should be exploited, but it cannot come at the cost of authorization control.

The stance is that good governance here eliminates bureaucracy, or at least carves a smooth path through it, while still preventing unauthorized IT purchases. He explicitly cautions against automating monthly spend monitoring too early: you want human reflection in the loop before you hand it to a machine. Cost discipline and agility are framed as compatible, not opposing, goals.
