# Bas reads to learn, not to escape

In this 2018 book list Bas states plainly that he does not read fiction and does not read dozens of books a year. His filter for a book is simple: he wants to learn something he can apply both personally and professionally. The list itself reveals his interests at the time: leadership and the senior technical executive (the CTO book, The First 90 Days), public speaking and presentation craft (Berkun, Duarte's slide:ology, Reynolds' Presentation Zen), entrepreneurial inspiration (Branson's Screw It Let's Do It, Zuckerberg), cycling (David Millar), the no-nonsense self-help genre (Mark Manson), and deep technical reference (Windows Internals). The framing shows a man deliberately building himself toward a more senior, more public-facing, more business-minded role around 2018, not just staying a hands-on engineer.
