# DaaS provider takes roughly 60% of the management off the IT department

Per the Chapter 6 takeaways, a DaaS provider generally takes over around 60% of the management. This relieves the IT department and frees up time to invest elsewhere.

Also noted: the technology behind multi-user sessions has a rich history, beginning with Citrix, eventually leading to innovations such as Microsoft's Remote Desktop Services (RDS), which now forms the basis for many VDI and DaaS solutions and of course AVD.
