# Use health-check tooling proactively, not only when things fall apart

A conviction Bas repeats explicitly in the Smart Check post (May 2017): 'I have said this multiple times already and I'll say it again: don't just use Insight Services and/or Smart Check when things start to fall apart, use it wisely, use it proactively!' His framing is that Smart Check 'goes way beyond monitoring' and is about catching problems before they hit end users. It is an operational-maturity stance, the value of diagnostics is realised by running them on a schedule as prevention, not as a panicked reaction. The post is also notable as a personal milestone: he discloses he was actively involved in helping build Smart Check ('I take pride in being part of the team', 'Makes me kinda proud'), an early sign of him crossing from independent blogger toward working alongside the vendor's product team.
