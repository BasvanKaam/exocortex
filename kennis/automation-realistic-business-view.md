# Be realistic about automation as a business

Bas encourages technically-oriented IT pros to get into automation, even deep-dive it. But he insists on a business-perspective reality check.

- Companies that have automation "down" often lack the time to keep it updated and running smoothly, because they're busy with day-to-day operations and putting out fires. That won't change overnight, it's the nature of the profession.
- Getting real hands-on automation/DevOps experience takes a lot of time, and it's tricky to maintain. "If it were easy everybody would do it," and from personal experience that is definitely not the case.
- You can only spend your time once.

The argument supports a tooling-over-DIY-scripting stance for most organisations, without dismissing automation skills.
