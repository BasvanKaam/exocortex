# Community Scripts Should Be Free and Vendor-Neutral

When Bas designed the Nerdio Hackathon, the stance was explicit: all scripted actions are made publicly available and free for everyone to use in their own environment, with or without Nerdio installed, and scripts do not have to be Nerdio-specific. The argument is that a vendor-run community event earns more goodwill and adoption by giving away genuinely portable value than by locking output to its own product. "This way we all benefit."
