# Begin at the beginning

Bas's stance: 'begin at the beginning' sounds obvious but in practice often isn't, because people skip the question of what the beginning even is. You cannot choose the right route to a destination without knowing where you are coming from. Good preparation is half the work ('een goede voorbereiding is het halve werk').

The promise attached to this position is predictability: 'weet je waar je aan toe bent, geen verrassingen, en geen onverwachte kosten.' He sells certainty and gap-clarity rather than a predetermined technical answer, the route depends on where the organisation is heading and why.
