# Structure for a 45-minute 'getting started' onboarding session

Reusable instructional pattern Bas uses for a live A-to-Z product-onboarding session (here NMW Community Edition, but the shape is portable). The method: take a learner from first install to onboarding their existing environment inside one short, time-boxed session, telling them what to do, where to go, and what to look out for.

Agenda template:
1. Quick recap of what the product is.
2. Where to get it, prerequisites, and how to install.
3. Basic settings and integration options.
4. What to be aware of and what to do about it (the gotchas).
5. Onboarding existing environments.
6. Intermediate settings and use-cases.
7. Helpful resources.

Note the recurring 'what to look out for / what to be aware of and what we can do about it' beat: he always builds the failure modes and their fixes into the walk-through rather than only the happy path.
