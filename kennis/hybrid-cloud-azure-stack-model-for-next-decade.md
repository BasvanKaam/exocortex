# Hybrid/private on-prem (Azure Stack-style) is the model for the next 5-10 years

In his 2017 Detron proposition portfolio, Bas takes a clear position on Azure Stack and the hybrid/private-cloud model. Microsoft recognises the rise of hybrid (private) cloud and on-prem environments and is responding big with Azure Stack, which offers many (not all) cloud-computing benefits without going all-in on the cloud for your base infrastructure and data, while staying on-prem in your own datacenter, which also benefits speed and user experience. Whatever the driver (gut feel, legislation, technical restrictions), Bas's stance is that this is going to be the model for the coming 5 to 10 years, and that all leading analysts agree.

He pairs this with a related argument under hyperconverged infrastructure: continuing to invest in existing legacy three-tier environments is not the smartest choice today; HCI lets you get 'cloud economics' benefits without becoming dependent on a cloud provider, keeping data in your own hands and close to the user.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> Wat de reden ook is of gaat zijn: gevoel, wetgeving, technische restricties enzovoorts, dit gaat het model worden voor de komende 5 tot 10 jaar. Daar zijn alle toonaangevende analisten het wel over eens.

> Investeren in bestaande, legacy three tier omgevingen is vandaag de dag niet de meest verstandige keuze.
