# Active Learning Beats Passive: Design Training for Retention, Not Coverage

Bas's stance: hearing or seeing information isn't enough to build lasting expertise. Active methods — hands-on activities, group discussions, teaching others — are far more effective than passive ones like listening to lectures or reading docs. Training should be measured by what participants retain and apply, not by content delivered. 'Training isn't just about transferring information — it's about enabling meaningful transformation.' He treats it as continuous trial and error: all you can do is keep improving.
