# Cloud Mastery - self-positioning and book intent

In the 'About the Author' page of 'Cloud Mastery', Bas positions himself as a Principal Technical Trainer, (Field) CTO, and technical/business consultant with 25+ years in IT and cloud, having helped hundreds of organizations (5 to 50,000 users) move to the cloud. He frames the book as for anyone who wants to go beyond cloud theory and history and actually put it into practice effectively and efficiently, serving both IT pros wanting technical depth and business leaders curious about transformation.

Characteristic stances and asides he owns here:
- Self-described perfectionist: 'you either do it right or don't do it at all', tends to go all in.
- Competitive by nature, not a fan of losing (admits he has gotten better at hiding it, mostly).
- Takes joy in making others happy: gives away things he no longer uses, surprises people with small gifts, always tries to help others move forward personally and professionally.
- Notes he witnessed the early rise of both virtualization and cloud computing firsthand.

*Bron: Inside Citrix (2016), 'About the Author (Cloud Mastery)'.*
