# SCAF final-report management summary palette

A supporting-documentation template for the SCAF (Step-Cloud Adoptie Framework) final report (Eindrapportage). A one-page management summary "palette" tying the engagement back to strategy and capturing closing state against fixed checkpoints:

- Primary drivers for cloud: specific requirements, wishes, strategic objectives.
- Mission, vision, core values.
- Risks, identified GAPS and other notable points.
- Financial and/or operational impact.
- Implementation / transition progress.
- Teams involved or to be formed.
- Modernization: what, how, impact, duration.
- Conclusion / summary.

This artifact closes the SCAF loop by reconnecting outcomes to the organization's mission, vision and primary cloud drivers, and reporting financial/operational impact and modernization scope. The framework itself is covered in the book.
