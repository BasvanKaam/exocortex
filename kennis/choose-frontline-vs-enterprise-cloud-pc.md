# Always-on Cloud PCs are wrong for intermittent users; Frontline fills the gap

Bas argues that paying for an always-on Enterprise Cloud PC makes little sense when a user only needs it occasionally or a few hours a day. Even though Frontline SKUs carry a higher per-unit list price than Enterprise Cloud PCs, the concurrency model wins on total cost for intermittent populations: in his worked example, 500 Enterprise Cloud PCs cost far more monthly than 150 Frontline licenses covering the same workforce. His broader stance: there is no silver bullet across Cloud PC, Frontline, and AVD; combining solutions is often the right answer, and the real questions are which to choose per use case, the pros/cons of each, and how to get the most out of it.
