# SCAF Workshop Startpunt Questions

Bas provides a 'Workshop Startpunt' — how a SCAF strategy workshop can begin and which questions a group can ask to get a clearer picture of 'how do we proceed?' All gathered information is documented in the SCAF template forms. Beyond the foundation questions, additional workshop questions include:

- How does cloud support the organisation's current mission and vision?
- When does a cloud implementation meet expectations and count as successful — any KPIs?
- When adopting cloud, who are the most important stakeholders and why? Who should we absolutely speak to, and why?
- How do you view a more agile way of working?
- What can you tell about current IT partners/suppliers — who are they and what do they do for you?
- What are the strengths and weaknesses, where is improvement needed? Where lie the opportunities and biggest threats?
- Do you have any experience with cloud services/products/technologies?
- Which primary IT-related processes are in place and do they work well?
- What are you (very) satisfied or dissatisfied about — anything specific?
- Do business objectives steer or contribute to the strategic IT plan (or should they)?
- Do IT and the business collaborate well? Often IT doesn't know what the business is doing or expects, and vice versa.
- Who composes a Cloud Center of Excellence?
