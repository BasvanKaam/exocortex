# Inside Citrix: technical reviewers and IGEL as sponsor

The 2016 first edition (v1.0, ISBN-13 978-1530428526) of *Inside Citrix - The FlexCast Management Architecture* was reviewed by a roster of well-known Citrix community names: Erik Bakker (@Bakker_Erik), Esther Barthel (@virtuEs_IT), Kees Baggerman (@KBaggerman), Tobias Kreidl (@t_kreidl), Marius Sandbu (@msandbu), Geert Braakhekke (@Easi123), Martin Zugec (@MartinZugec) and Mick Glover (@XDtipster). Bas singled out Martin, Erik and Tobias as the most detailed reviewers, each taking on hundreds of pages. Copy-editing/proofreading was done by ProofProfessor. The project was supported by sponsor IGEL.

Why it matters: this is the concrete community network behind the book, and the start of the IGEL relationship that recurs elsewhere in the brain.

*Bron: Inside Citrix (2016), 'Inside Citrix prologue - copyrights and acknowledgements'.*
