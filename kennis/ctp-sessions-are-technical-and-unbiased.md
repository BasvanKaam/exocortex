# CTP-hosted sessions are the technical, unbiased ones

Writing ahead of Synergy 2017, Bas argues the CTP-hosted sessions are among the highest rated at the conference 'and with good reason': almost without exception highly technical and giving an unbiased view 'in every way possible'. He is careful and self-aware about the bias in his own recommendation, noting he is a CTP himself and likes to 'advertise the program (and its members) from time to time', and explicitly says he is not claiming other sessions are lesser. This captures both his pride in and loyalty to the CTP community and his instinct to flag his own potential bias rather than hide it, a fairness reflex that recurs in his writing.
