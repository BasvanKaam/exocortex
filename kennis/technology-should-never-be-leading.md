# Technology Should Never Be Leading

Bas's recurring principle, applied here to collaboration tools: choose the right tool based on the goal and what matters for your organization, and not the other way around. 'Techniek zou nooit leidend moeten zijn.' He extends this across the whole work-from-home piece by repeatedly framing technology as 'bijzaak' (a side matter) and putting routine, communication and well-being first. The position is consistent with his cloud-strategy writing where the business, not the technology, leads.

Source: Bas van Kaam, Salomon IT blog, 2020-03-18.
