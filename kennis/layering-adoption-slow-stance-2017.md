# Surprised by slow application-layering adoption (2017)

Reacting to the 2017 Login VSI survey, Bas was genuinely surprised that almost 75% used no application layering at all, 'With all that has been going on in this space.' He attributed it to adoption simply taking time rather than a lack of benefit ('which I doubt'). On vendors he made a contrarian call: while Unidesk (then newly part of Citrix) and App Volumes (VMware) got the attention, 'don't rule out Liquidware Labs with their Flexapp solution. In fact, they are just about the only ones that can back it up with a complete suite of integrated products and services (ProfileUnity and Stratusphere).' He also doubted the survey's claim that GPU/flash/SDS had 'solved' historical Windows-application challenges, especially around GPUs.
