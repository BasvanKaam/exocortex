# SCAF Pillars and Design Principles

Design principles and pillars of the Salomon Cloud Adoptie Framework (independent cloud adoption framework), as described in 2019:

- **Independence first.** Does not steer toward Microsoft Azure, AWS, Google Cloud or any other provider. Built on years of experience and a fresh, realistic view of the market.
- **Business at the center.** Besides technological components, 'the business' is central (still too often overlooked or forgotten).
- **Supporting pillars.** (Cloud) governance, cultural shifts, security, privacy, and service/project management (processes).
- **Scale-invariant.** Applicable to small and large organizations, a few workloads or hundreds; the worked-out principles stay the same.
- **Structured method.** A structured way of working, inventorying and deciding, with solid substantiation, ideally consuming everything 'as a service' (rarely fully achievable, but the gains remain large).
- **Adoption is human.** Provides practical (visual and written) handvatten for landing the new way of working and thinking inside a company. Key words: involvement, communication, and ambassadors.
- **Anti-bloat.** Its setup and flexibility avoid 100+ page end reports and piles of jargon; only what matters to the client is examined, discussed and tested. Tailored, to-the-point advice every time.

Note: the possibilities of cloud services go far beyond 'just' virtual machines and Office 365.

Source: Bas van Kaam, Salomon IT blog, 2019-09-17.
