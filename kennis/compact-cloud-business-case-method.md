# Building a Compact Cloud Business Case

A lightweight, iterative method to decide what should move to the cloud and whether it is worth it, part of the SCAF.

**Make it smaller.** Rather than pouring time, effort and money into something whose value you cannot yet judge, break the whole into smaller steps. This keeps overview and focus and stops you from losing the forest for the trees.

**Steps:**
1. Map the current situation: applications, systems, data, processes, teams, governance, any growth ambitions, depreciation, and so on, including current costs (TCO).
2. From a smart inventory, create a base from which a compact business case can be written.
3. Determine whether it makes sense to take a next step. This quickly clarifies what could (and could not) move to the cloud and how: migrate (lift & shift), modernize, and/or innovate, possibly a combination, on the basis of IaaS, PaaS, SaaS, DaaS, XaaS (refactor/rationalization).
4. Iterate. Such an inventory pass is rarely complete in one go; running multiple iterations prevents surprises afterward. A more extensive analysis can follow later if needed, and earlier choices get reviewed. Stay flexible.

**Depth discipline.** Because this is a 'first impression', going into extreme detail has little point; do it later only if the gain is not clearly visible (usually unnecessary). Do not forget organizational and strategic expectations and objectives in the cut.

Source: Bas van Kaam, Salomon IT blog, 2019-10-23.
