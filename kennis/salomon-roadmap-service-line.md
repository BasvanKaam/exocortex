# Salomon's IT Roadmap service line (assessment to Roadmap-as-a-Service)

The service model Bas/Salomon offers, as a staged journey:

1. Assessment — measure both 'in IT' (infrastructure, physical/virtual workplace, cloud and XaaS) and 'around IT' (GDPR/AVG, IT policy and strategy). Done from the perspective of the end-user experience.
2. Roadmap design — built on two foundations: the client's business objectives and the jointly established status quo. It spans the long-term vision, concrete short-term projects, and quick wins. Technology choices go hand-in-hand with organizational change.
3. Roadmap management — Salomon guards progress on the client's behalf, combining project & program management with technical depth, working alongside the internal IT team and existing IT partners, 'without sitting in the chair of the IT suppliers.'
4. Roadmap as a Service — recurring check-ins through the year to verify goals are met and course-correct with small sprints, keeping momentum.

The recurring tagline anchoring the whole line: 'Wij geven het inzicht, jij maakt de juiste keuzes' (we give the insight, you make the right choices).
