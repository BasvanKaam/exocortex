# Vendor Portfolio Alignment

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

As cloud services become more standardized and providers offer ever-growing functionality, the provider landscape spreads out. Vendor Portfolio Alignment is the knowledge domain for managing which services each contracted provider delivers and under what conditions you use them.

Large organizations are likely to have multiple large cloud vendors with overlapping portfolios but differing service details. Very few organizations are large enough to dictate terms to large cloud providers, so understanding each provider's terms is critical. Subjects to track per provider include: contract details, the data processing agreement, the service level agreement, existing pricing plans and volume agreements, and security baselining.

The core activity is offering and maintaining a catalog of supported cloud services. The goal is to make deliberate decisions when commissioning cloud services and to avoid doubling up on services or purchasing services without a proper landing zone in place.
