# Position: Business Objectives Must Drive Cloud Investments

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

The framework holds that cloud governance must aim higher than the IT-governance minimum of "adequate and relevant" technology. Because digital transformation touches business models, organizational structure and processes, cloud governance has to be as closely aligned with the business as possible. His core claim: business objectives drive digital transformation and should therefore drive cloud investments.

This is a stance against IT making cloud decisions in isolation. He further argues continuous improvement must be embedded in the organization's DNA, not bolted on, precisely because cloud services innovate so fast that a static operating model falls behind.
