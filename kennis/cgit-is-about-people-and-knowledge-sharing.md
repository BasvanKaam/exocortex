# CGIT is about people and knowledge sharing, not just the brand

In the internal promo plan Bas argues that CGIT should not be put solely at the centre: the goal is also getting to know colleagues better and sharing knowledge among each other ('Niet alleen CGIT staat hierbij centraal, ook het beter leren kennen van je collega's, kennis delen onder elkaar'). His stance: an internal community/initiative wins adoption by leading with people and shared learning, not by hard-selling the brand. He also values keeping sessions tight ('we willen het ook niet onnodig rekken').

*Bron: Salomon-IT 'CGIT intern promo plan' (CGIT Event).*
