# Bas: frame the business case with an executive summary and added-value story

Bas advises that a first mini business case for the 'modern workplace' should include an introduction (executive summary) to make context and objectives clear. Beyond that, he recommends saying something about the added value of the new workplace concept: how it moves the organisation forward, how it makes the business more future-proof, and what concrete advantages it brings to the user organisation. The point is to sell the why, not just list the technical swaps.
