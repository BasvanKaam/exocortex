# Mapping IT Capabilities to Strategic Business Goals

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

Understanding how IT capabilities and activities contribute to strategic business goals is vital to the success of the IT strategy. Strategy choice changes IT needs: an organization pursuing an Operational Excellence model has different IT needs than one based on Product Leadership. Determining the strategic objectives and how IT and cloud contribute is an essential step in realizing the strategy.

The framework offers a checklist for building this view: watch market trends and developments; analyze strategic drivers for cloud; study how other organizations handle it; relate it to existing strategy and business goals; decide how and where to start with cloud; assess impact on the operating model and customer experience; validate with stakeholders; and include the impact of digital trends like IoT and Machine Learning. The artifact should be readable without extensive explanation.
