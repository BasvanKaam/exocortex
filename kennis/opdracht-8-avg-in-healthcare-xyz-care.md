# Opdracht 8: AVG in healthcare (XYZ-Care)

Bas's assignment 'AVG In De Zorg: Inzicht, Grondslagen En Beveiliging.' His standing instruction for all exercises: do extra research where needed; the goal is to drive you to action, beyond merely reading the book. Missing details in the case you simply invent — it doesn't matter much for the exercise.

**Case:** XYZ-Care Health Group, a care organization, processes large amounts of sensitive data daily (medical records, identity data, financial information). Given strict AVG norms and the sensitivity of patient data, it must align its processes with AVG requirements and ensure optimal security.

**The assignment:**
- **Identify personal data:** map the personal data processed at XYZ-Care, using data types from the case (NAW/name-address-city data, financial data, special categories of personal data), including data stored or processed via the Electronic Patient Record (EPD), the planning tool and other applications.
- **Determine the legal basis:** analyze the grondslag per data type, making a reasoned choice from the six legal bases, and explain the risks of a wrong choice.
- **Map AVG obligations:** accountability duty (register, DPIA, etc.), appropriate technical and organizational security measures, and the rights of data subjects (erasure, access).
- **Perform a risk analysis:** on current practices, e.g. unencrypted laptops, manual updates, lack of multi-factor authentication; name the biggest risks and propose improvements.

**The method behind it (Uitwerking):** the exercise gives a structured way to make data protection concrete by identifying personal data and choosing the right legal basis; the risk analysis exposes current vulnerabilities (outdated hardware, missing baseline controls). Drafting a proposal and preparing a presentation helps formulate a clear vision and build management buy-in (draagvlak), emphasizing measures like MFA, processing registers and DPIAs. The outcome is greater awareness plus concrete steps to make IT processes safer and more efficient, while developing strategic-choice and presentation skills.
