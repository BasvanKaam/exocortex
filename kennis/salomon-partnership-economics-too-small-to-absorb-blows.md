# Why the Salomon Partnership Doesn't Add Up: Too Small to Absorb the Blows

Bas's argued conclusion on the partnership offer. Salomon makes no profit; the profit it does make is just enough to fill the holes and stay alive. He doesn't see that changing fast. Even if Abram joins in, it would take at least a year and a half before reasonable profit appears. Two percent of a Ton (100k) is 2,000 euro — and a Ton of profit is still very far off, and that assumes nothing is spent on marketing, training, exams, events, expenses. One person gets sick and there you go: too small, can't absorb the blows.

He weighs his alternatives (RAW, Lemontree, a vendor, freelance, writing a book, working for AWS/Microsoft or Workspace 365 as Field CTO / Technical Evangelist) and concludes the straight partnership is a "No." The fallback he leans toward: possibly become partner for just 1 or 2 percent and give it a year so he has at least been a partner once and can still go any direction — or keep building basvankaam.com and stay at Salomon as a non-partner for now.
