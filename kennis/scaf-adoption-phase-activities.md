# SCAF Adoption phase: activities and deliverables

In Bas's SCAF Adoption phase, earlier documentation again plays a central role, and work started in earlier phases is completed or further optimised. All links of the Cloud transition are connected together. Key activities Bas lists:

- **Completed migrations**: the first migrations of low-hanging-fruit applications, datasets and systems started in execution are now finished for all relevant core systems.
- **Execution of the migration and implementation plan** together with technical and functional designs, using lessons learned in execution for an ever more efficient process.
- **Security and modernisation**: all security matters followed up; outdated resources adjusted/modernised to stay secure and up-to-date.
- **Active teams and governance**: teams fully operational, the Cloud governance model put into practice with all coupled processes, with a renewed check on how those processes function.
- **Communication and progress reporting** to the various parts of the user organisation(s).
- **Execution of the skill-readiness plan** so staff have the right knowledge and skills.
- **Ambassadors and training**: ambassadors actively go into the organisation to promote, answer questions and gather feedback; workshops and trainings are organised.
- **Optimisation of agile working and lifecycle management** based on the Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle, embracing both a new way of working and a mindset of flexibility and continuous improvement.

Supporting templates for this phase: the SCAF-Adoptie template and the SCAF Eindrapportage (final report) template. Supporting documentation includes a combined PDCA/DMAIC cycle, skill-readiness progress, a cost-awareness strategy overview (tooling), and a communication model with names and contact details.
