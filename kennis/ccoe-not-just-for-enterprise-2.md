# You're Never Too Small for a CCoE

Bas pushes back hard on a common misconception: that a Cloud Center of Excellence (CCoE) sounds big and is only applicable to large enterprise environments. His verdict is blunt: this is WRONG ("ONJUIST"). Whether you're a large multinational or a smaller organisation, a CCoE can play a key role in managing and implementing cloud solutions. His memorable framing: "Je bent nooit te klein voor een CCoE" (you're never too small for a CCoE).

In the SCAF model, setting up a CCoE is an optional part of the strategy phase. The CCoE is a multidisciplinary team that functions as the engine ("de motor") behind the cloud project.

Why the roles matter (Bas's four reasons): collaboration of IT with the business (technical AND business stakeholders ensure the strategy is both technically feasible and aligned with business goals); solid governance (clear guidelines, policy, compliance, cost-efficiency); expertise and knowledge sharing (the CCoE acts as a knowledge hub/spil where cloud expertise is consolidated and shared); and managing complexity (keeping project management, security, governance and technical aspects seamlessly aligned).
