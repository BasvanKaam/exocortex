# NIS 2 vs the rest of the world

The EU NIS 2 directive shares many concepts with international standards like NIST and ISO 27001, both worldwide references for an effective cybersecurity program. NIS 2 is officially an EU directive applying only within EU member states; countries outside the EU are not obliged to adopt it, but it can have indirect influence:
- Multinationals active in and outside the EU must comply with NIS 2 within the EU, which may push them to harmonize their global cybersecurity strategy.
- Companies outside the EU that supply EU organizations (e.g. cloud providers, IT service providers) may face pressure to adopt comparable security measures, because EU organizations must manage supply-chain risks.

Comparable initiatives elsewhere: the US uses the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and Critical Infrastructure Security Directives; the UK's regulation partly aligns with NIS (implemented during EU membership); Australia has its own Security of Critical Infrastructure Act (SOCI).
