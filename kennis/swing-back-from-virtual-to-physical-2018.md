# Bas: companies are swinging back from virtual/cloud to physical

A notable counter-cyclical observation Bas makes in September 2018, embedded in a printing article. Despite being deep in the VDI/EUC world, he states that over the previous few years more and more companies have been switching back to physical, coming from virtual or even cloud. His reasons: modern management has made maintaining physical devices much easier and less complex, while the maturity of cloud (specifically IaaS and DaaS) "isn't quite there yet" though getting closer by the day. He notes the early adopters of cloud-based desktops/workspaces may have had a disappointing experience, while acknowledging things have improved greatly since. He also reminds readers that physical is still by far the most-used machine type worldwide, and even cloud desktops always need a physical endpoint to work from. This is an honest, non-dogmatic take from someone whose content is largely virtualization-focused, a willingness to call the pendulum swing as he sees it rather than cheerlead VDI unconditionally.
