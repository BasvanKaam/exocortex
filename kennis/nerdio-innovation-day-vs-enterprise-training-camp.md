# Nerdio Innovation Day vs Enterprise Training Camp

Bas's working framework for how these two Nerdio event types differ, useful when designing or pitching either.

Innovation Day:
- Goal: inspire, inform, and connect with new prospects through thought leadership and strategic/technical content.
- Audience: new business contacts, IT leaders, decision-makers, potential partners.
- Content: vision keynotes, roadmap overviews, customer stories, live demos, with light hands-on labs.
- Structure: half- to full-day, conference-style, with some labs and networking near the end.
- Commercial focus: strong, designed to spark interest and accelerate adoption. Sales typically not present.
- Collaboration: co-hosted with Microsoft, emphasizing joint value.
- Outcome: awareness, excitement, a pathway to new opportunities.

Enterprise Training Camp (ETC):
- Goal: help existing customers and partners master the platform through guided, hands-on training.
- Audience: current customers, partners, IT admins, engineers, cloud architects.
- Content: advanced topics (auto-scaling, FSLogix, image management, MSIX, scripting) with extensive full hands-on labs and real-world deployment walkthroughs.
- Structure: full-day, classroom-style with interactive guidance, plus networking and an evening prior.
- Commercial focus: minimal, it's about enablement not sales. Sales/BD teams typically involved.
- Collaboration: first-party, run independently by Nerdio (not co-hosted).
- Outcome: practical skills, configuration know-how, operational confidence.

The distinction in one line: Innovation Day is top-of-funnel inspiration co-hosted with Microsoft; ETC is bottom-of-funnel enablement run by Nerdio.
