# Bas: Pragmatism on Governance and Process Documentation

In the SCAF Insight phase, alongside the technical side, Bas wants to map existing processes and governance. Upfront questions include: which processes are currently in use and who owns them; which processes are described but barely followed (what can go?); what a clear objective for the process descriptions is and who's responsible; how processes are recorded (specific software? what detail level? - high-level is often enough, details live in work instructions; visual recording preferred); what the most important processes are and whether a risk matrix is needed; and how documented processes will be maintained (a test/monitoring plan agreed with process owners, kept up via periodic review with functional administrators / risk-process manager).

Bas's characteristic pragmatic aside: **processes are hard**. When asking these questions, think carefully how far you want to go. The answer to "do we need a risk matrix, must we write out processes?" is quickly "yes," but how often is this actually revisited afterward? That valuable time can be spent (much) better on other things.
