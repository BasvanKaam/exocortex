# A lab is allowed to be boring (ease of use as a selling point)

Bas's stance on demos and hands-on sessions: "Labs are allowed to be boring" precisely because that boring-ness is the proof. If standing up a working AVD PoC in a couple of hours is uneventful, that lack of drama is the product story.

A companion line he uses to make the same point about safety: Nerdio is "monkey proof" because resources cannot accidentally be deleted. The closing lab deliberately has people try to tear down their resources to demonstrate it.

Stance: don't dazzle with complexity. Let the absence of friction do the selling. Ease of use, not feature theatrics, is the differentiator he leans on.
