# Nutanix is a mature product and here to stay

In April 2014, Bas was an early and enthusiastic backer of Nutanix. He framed traditional datacenters as simply not designed to handle today's data growth, user demand and virtualized workloads, and positioned Nutanix as the turnkey, scalable answer that gets you running in hours without a huge upfront SAN investment.

His take went a step beyond Nutanix's own marketing: Nutanix said NOS 4.0 'helps contribute to the maturity of their product', but Bas argued 'I think that we're dealing with a very mature and robust product already.' Citing the Best of VMworld Gold award, the Gigaom Top 50 Cloud Innovators listing, 500+ customers and record funding, he concluded 'it's safe to say that Nutanix is here to stay.' This is an early marker of his datacenter/web-scale conviction that would recur across 2014 posts.
