# If you want to go far, go together

Bas frames the Byte-Sized success around the proverb "If You Want To Go Fast, Go Alone. If You Want To Go Far, Go Together" and the line "The force of the community is strong." The whole project is his proof-point that distributed community contribution outperforms going solo, and that community work should give back (charity, not profit: "it's not for us, all the money goes to charity"). A consistent BvK community-first value.
