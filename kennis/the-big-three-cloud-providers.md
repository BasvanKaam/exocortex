# The Big Three Cloud Providers (AWS, Azure, GCP)

Chapter 3 of Bas's book covers the three largest cloud providers and their differences and similarities, drawing on Flexera's annual State of the Cloud Report (based on surveys of hundreds of IT professionals and leaders).

Approximate global market shares (end of 2024):
- AWS ~32% (market leader)
- Microsoft Azure ~23%
- Google Cloud Platform (GCP) ~10%

Bas notes these figures were nearly identical a few years earlier; the three grow in parallel. Beyond the Big Three there are dozens of providers, with strong growth especially in the (Middle) East from players like Alibaba, Tencent, and NTT. Whether they can threaten Microsoft and Amazon remains an open question. Bas recommends following Gartner's annual Magic Quadrants to track these developments. Source: Gartner and Amazon.
