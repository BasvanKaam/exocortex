# Put the Business Before the Technology

A core Bas position: in cloud adoption 'the business' must be central, something he says is still too often overlooked or simply forgotten. Technology should connect to the business objectives, wishes and requirements, not lead them. He pairs this with the human side of adoption: involvement, communication and ambassadors are the key words for landing a new way of working. He also pushes back on the narrow view that cloud equals virtual machines and Office 365, the possibilities go much further.

Source: Bas van Kaam, Salomon IT blog, 2019-09-17.
