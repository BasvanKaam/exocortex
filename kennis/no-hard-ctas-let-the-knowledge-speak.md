# No hard CTAs; let demonstrated expertise do the selling

His stance on marketing: drop the standard call-to-action machinery ('let's grab a coffee', etc.). It should simply be clear that you know your stuff, full stop. The audience is intelligent enough to find you on their own. Content should not feel like advertising even when it draws on your own Roadmap, training, and adoption framework; underline real, common challenges and show how you help, rather than pitching.

Verbatim: "Geen directe CTA als in, kopje koffie, etc. het moet duidelijk zijn dat we kennis van zaken hebben, that's it. Men weet ons te vinden, ons publiek is intelligent genoeg."
