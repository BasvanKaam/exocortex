# Excalibur drops Citrix Profiling (app streaming) for App-V (2013 Synergy news)

A notable piece of Synergy 2013 news Bas relayed: Citrix Profiling (application streaming) is no longer supported in Excalibur - "it's all App-V from here on." This marks the point where Citrix exited its own app-streaming technology and aligned application delivery on Microsoft App-V. Dated, but a real inflection in the EUC app-delivery story.
