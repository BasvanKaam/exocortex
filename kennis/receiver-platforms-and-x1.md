# Receiver platforms and the Receiver X1

There is a Citrix Receiver for almost every platform: Windows, Mac, iOS, Linux, Android, Chrome, HTML5, Windows 8/RT, Windows Phone and BlackBerry 10. There are also plug-ins like the HDX RealTime Media engine for Skype and Lync, Receiver for Desktop Lock, and the Offline plug-in.

Receiver for Windows Enterprise (vs. standard Receiver for Windows) supports only: repurposed PCs and thin clients with desktop lock, Fast Connect-enabled products, and 508-compliance applications.

Citrix was working on the X1 release of Receiver (X1 = eXperience 1st), which combined with the latest StoreFront offers: an enhanced StoreFront management console; grouping resources under self-managed categories; flexible branding (no more green bubble theme, custom logos and colour schemes, eventually extending to the NetScaler logon page); one Receiver for both XenDesktop/XenApp-hosted apps and XenMobile/App Controller mobile apps (replacing Worx Home); and a unified look and feel across devices including the HTML5 Receiver. Once X1 reaches GA, other Receiver editions (e.g. Receiver for Enterprise) become unnecessary.

FMA fact: Receiver X1 combined with StoreFront will greatly simplify management and improve the user experience on multiple levels.
