# FMA Power Management as a niche, not-for-everyone capability

How it worked then (2015): Power management in the Citrix FlexCast Management Architecture (FMA) was treated as a relatively obscure, less popular feature. Bas and Mick Glover chose it for their E2EVC Berlin talk precisely because it was not well known. The honest framing was that it can seem complicated or not straightforward at first; even once you grasp the basics it still needs careful planning and smart thinking to implement. But in some (not all) cases it offers real advantages, and once implemented it is relatively straightforward to manage. The session aimed to give an overview of the inner workings, terminology, pros and cons, and things to be aware of. A durable takeaway: not every feature deserves blanket adoption; some are situational and worth understanding before deciding whether they fit.
