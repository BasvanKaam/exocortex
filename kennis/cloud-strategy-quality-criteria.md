# What makes a good Cloud Strategy

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

The Cloud Strategy describes the organization's objectives regarding the use of cloud services. In the Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework a good Cloud Strategy is:

- Future proof
- Aligned with strategic business objectives
- Derived from the Digital Strategy
- Part of a Sourcing Strategy
- In alignment with the Security Strategy
- Contains a reversibility plan (Exit Strategy)
- Allows structured analysis of application functionality by providing a Decision Tree
- Provides a Roadmap for the existing landscape

It sits under the Digital Strategy and feeds the Sourcing and Security strategies rather than standing alone. A strategy is 'activated' the moment the Steering Committee approves it or the Board accepts it; from that point all purchasing and refactoring decisions are made in alignment with it.
