# We do it differently: culture over recruitment ritual

A reflective, personal piece that doubles as a manifesto for how Bas likes to work and hire. His stance: no standard recruitment ritual ("dit is geen vacature, en ik ben geen recruiter"); Salomon does it differently, the way he opened the blog years earlier with "Wij doen het anders".

What he values and signals: short lines with lots of room for own input, creativity and personal development. You should maybe be a bit eigenwijs (stubborn/headstrong) to fit the team. His colleagues are professionals, each with their own opinion, seniors in their field, energetic, sometimes a little weird, but always pleasant and ready to help. Interaction and synergy is key, they meet often, at the office, virtually, at a client, along the road, or at a Van der Valk. The firm grows strongly, delivers quality, and almost without exception enters long-term relationships with clients.

The invitation is deliberately informal: evenings, breaks, phone, Skype, a conference, over coffee or a cold beer, "ik ben niet zo moeilijk". The whole position: hire for fit and attitude, keep it human, no "13 in een dozijn" story.
