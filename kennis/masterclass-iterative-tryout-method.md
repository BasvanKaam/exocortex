# Method: Ship the Masterclass via Feedback Rounds and a Low-Fee Try-Out

Bas's development loop for the Cloud Masterclass, with content at 99% and in fine-tuning:

- Run colleague feedback sessions, polish again, then run another try-out, possibly public against a low fee.
- Bring in a guest speaker (he had an appointment with Jasper for a guest slot, and was already eyeing other speakers but chose to start with one).
- Separate the content work from the delivery work: he still had to go through every module on language and text, place the right notes, internalise the content, and find a coaching/presentation flow. He flagged that he did not yet have a 100% clear picture of the time the training needs.

The open commercial questions he kept separate from production: how and where to offer it, price, and discounting.
