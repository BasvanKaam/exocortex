# NVP Newsletter: Bas's Recurring Community Update Format

Bas writes a recurring NVP newsletter to keep the cohort engaged. His reusable section template:

1. **Channel / housekeeping** – where to talk (dedicated Slack), how to get an invite.
2. **Product updates / release notes** – latest NME/NMM versions with links, framed around pace of delivery (“our developers are not sitting still”).
3. **Program resources** – the public NVP landing page and registration form, framed as something members can share to recruit others.
4. **Feature-request / Idea form** – a structured channel for submitting ideas (one idea at a time) that feed into the product roadmap.
5. **Welcome new members** – named welcomes that highlight what each person contributes, with a nudge to connect on Slack.
6. **Welcome kit / perks** recap.
7. **Nerdio in the news** – a curated press roundup.
8. **Feedback ask** – explicitly invite suggestions on the newsletter itself.

The pattern: mix program news, product value, recognition and a two-way feedback loop in every issue, and openly let cadence be ‘weekly, monthly, or whatever makes sense.’
