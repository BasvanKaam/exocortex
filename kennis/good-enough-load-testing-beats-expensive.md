# Good-enough load testing at 10% of the price beats the gold-plated tool

Bas's framing of VDI Drones (Sept 2018) is a value-over-perfection argument aimed squarely at the unnamed "elephant in the room," Login VSI. His pitch: traditional load-testing tools are a hard sell to management, expensive licenses, complex setup, extra infrastructure, often a consultant required, especially painful for smaller environments of 500-2500 users. VDI Drones, he argues, gives you roughly 50-60% (or more) of the same results at about 10% of the per-seat cost, with almost no extra infrastructure. He is candid about the trade-offs: "Is it perfect? No. Do you get a shiny polished dashboard with a ton of bells and whistles? Also no, but who cares?" The deeper conviction: a synthetic load test never gives a 100% guarantee (no tool does, "the only real way is to test it with real users"), so what you actually want is a more-than-educated guess, and paying 10x for marginal polish is not worth it for most. He also takes pains to disclose he has no financial stake, he is helping a friend's product for community reasons, a recurring integrity move in his writing.
