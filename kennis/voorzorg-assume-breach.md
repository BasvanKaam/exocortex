# Voorzorg: Assume You Will Be Breached

Bas's framing: the question is not *if* you will ever face a cyber incident, but *when*. Absolute protection does not exist; 100% prevention is impossible. So it is wise to focus not only on keeping threats out, but also on what 'they' can do once they are inside: which parts of the network can they reach, and how much damage could they cause?

This means thinking about ways to limit the impact of a breach or ransomware: network segmentation, applying least privilege (users only get access to what they need), and extensive monitoring and logging. That way you are better prepared and can act quickly to minimise damage when preventive measures fail.
