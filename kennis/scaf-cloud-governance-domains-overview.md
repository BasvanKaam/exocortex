# SCAF Cloud Governance: The Nine Domains (Overview)

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

A single-slide taxonomy the framework uses to show, at a glance, what falls under the SCAF cloud-governance pillar. Governance is presented as nine domains rather than one undifferentiated topic:

1. Lifecycle & roadmap management
2. Communication & ambassadors
3. Innovation, build & management (bouw & beheer)
4. Compliance & best practices
5. Monitoring, reporting & optimization
6. Security (veiligheid)
7. Cost control (kostenbeheersing)
8. Policy, project and programme management, processes

(The slide frames governance as the surrounding ninth element binding these together.)

The value of this overview is the visual framing: it turns the broad, slippery word 'governance' into a concrete set of addressable areas, each of which maps onto specific roles, owners and templates elsewhere in SCAF. Use this as the map; the role/owner detail and the adoption-phase control pass are held in the related notes.
