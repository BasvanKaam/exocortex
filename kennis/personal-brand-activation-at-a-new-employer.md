# Method: activating your personal brand when you join a new employer

When joining a company, channel an existing personal network and reputation into the employer's pipeline without burning the network's trust.

The sequence: an interview primarily about 'why this company', then a blog in your own voice on basvankaam.com about the why and the partnership, then repeat moments on LinkedIn, then use a rolling set of topics to stage the company's proposition, working through first-degree connections. Announce availability on LinkedIn and lean on the power of repetition. Cross-post the personal blog on both the company site and the personal site. Profile through external outlets (e.g. Computable).

Key nuance on partnerships: emphasise the genuine, long-standing partner relationship (Liquidware) as authentic, not merely a consequence of the new job. Other IT firms will instinctively read you as a competitor; make it clear from the first contact that this is not the case.
