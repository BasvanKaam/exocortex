# Risk Analysis: Control Measures and Decision-Making

After identifying risks, you develop control measures (beheersmaatregelen) to limit or prevent each one. These can be preventive (stop the risk occurring) or reactive (limit impact if it occurs). Examples: making back-ups, adding extra security layers, or finding alternative suppliers.

Monitoring and reporting: document findings in a risk register, regularly monitor the status of identified risks and the effectiveness of the measures, and plan regular evaluations to keep risk management current and surface new risks.

Decision-making: based on the risk analysis results, management can decide on acceptance, reduction, transfer or elimination of risks, which feeds an action plan to manage them.

Example rows from the book's table: Data loss (likelihood High, impact 8/very high) → regular back-ups, owner IT department; Cyberattack (medium, 10/high) → enable firewall and antivirus, owner IT security; Compliance (low, 7/medium) → compliance trainings, owner Legal.
