# Opdracht 1 - Cloud promises critically examined (XYZ-Care)

Bas's first assignment, set against a fictional healthcare organisation XYZ-Care Health Group. A standing instruction applies to all his exercises: do extra research where needed; the goal is to drive you to action, going beyond merely reading the book.

The task asks the reader to: determine who should be involved at which step and why; analyse the Cloud promises and decide which are most relevant for XYZ-Care; document risks/pitfalls specific to a healthcare organisation; describe XYZ-Care's reason to migrate and how it aligns with strategic goals (part 2 can be finished in a later chapter); and discuss together which Cloud promises you agree or disagree with and why - since today we know not all promises are easily kept.

The worked answer (Uitwerking): the management team starts by analysing the promises, building a table of the nine primary promises and rating each on relevance to XYZ-Care's challenges. In a brainstorm they document risks like vendor lock-in and compliance challenges, categorised by likelihood and impact (an approach detailed later in the book). Bas's method principle: every point of doubt or criticism should have a follow-up action - points of disagreement get further research or a proof of concept to validate feasibility, while points of consensus are turned into concrete tasks to improve the plan.
