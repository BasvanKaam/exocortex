# Self-publishing a niche Citrix book: the numbers behind Inside Citrix

How it worked then (Feb 2017, roughly nine months after the May 2016 launch): Inside Citrix - The FlexCast Management Architecture had sold around 830 copies, a mix of paperback and Kindle (over 200 Kindle). That averaged about 90 books per month, with one or more orders coming in almost daily. Distribution was Amazon-only, both paperback and Kindle, with no public PDF version offered on principle. Notable constraint: no paperback edition was available on Amazon.in (India), but the free cross-platform Kindle reader was the workaround.

Context for a second brain: this is a reference point for what a deeply niche, non install-and-configure technical title on a single vendor's flagship architecture can realistically sell when self-published and marketed through a personal blog and social channels.
