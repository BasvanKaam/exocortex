# Publish the draft and let the community fact-check it

In April 2016 Bas did something unusual: he published an entire near-final chapter of his upcoming book (Citrix + Microsoft licensing) on the open blog and explicitly asked readers to fact-check it. His framing of the ask is precise: "I'm not looking for any textual and/or grammar faults or anything, I'm more interested in (technical) fact checking." The motive is threefold, stated openly: a sneak peek to promote the book, genuine quality improvement through crowd-sourced verification, and giving readers useful info regardless. This shows his working philosophy on hard-to-verify material like licensing, defer to the crowd and to vendor reps rather than overclaim: "Since licensing can be (very) tricky I would definitely advice talking to one of your Citrix / Microsoft representative when in doubt. It's not to be taken lightly, and there often is a lot money involved." An early, concrete instance of his never-invent / flag-don't-guess instinct around facts.
