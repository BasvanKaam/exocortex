# Selecting the First Migration Workload (Simple to Complex)

Bas's practical advice ('Punten Van Aandacht' / 'Samengevat Advies') for starting migration:
- The rationalisation process need not be fully finished before the next step, especially in larger, complex environments.
- For large amounts of data/systems/apps, consider limiting the choice to e.g. two refactor options (for example retire and rehost, in a primarily IaaS case). Then only one choice really has to be made and you reach a first result fast.
- A quick, working demo increases the chance of success: it enthuses users of the new workplace and can reduce or even remove concerns and doubts.
- Earlier choices can always be reconsidered (agile thinking).
- Prefer a first workload that has no complex dependencies, is well documented, easy to test, needs no access to protected data, and can optionally be split into smaller steps.
- Run rationalisation in parallel; distinguish workloads to migrate, modernise or innovate; select the first five to ten applications/systems by priority (in a table) for the adoption/migration/implementation backlog. Work from simple to complex, migrate in batches.
- Some teams, roles and responsibilities are temporary and disappear during or after the adoption phase.
