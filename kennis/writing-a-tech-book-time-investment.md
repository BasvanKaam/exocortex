# What writing a tech book actually costs in time

Durable lesson from Bas's own experience: his book took about 6 months of near-daily work at roughly 15 to 20 hours per week, on top of a full-time consulting job and preparing three conference presentations. The routine was one to two hours after getting home from a customer site, plus 3 to 5 hours each on Saturday and Sunday. Peak load fell in the middle and in the final 2-3 weeks before a hard deadline (Citrix Synergy). His honest verdict: rewarding and something he is proud of, but "something I wouldn't do again if given the choice." Proof-reading and technical editing are a separate hurdle he partly outsourced.

*Bron: blogpost 'Inside Citrix - The making of & lessons learned!' (2016-08-15), basvankaam.com.*
