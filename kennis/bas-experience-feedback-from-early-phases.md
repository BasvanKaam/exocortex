# Bas's experience: feedback from early phases builds tighter teams

In a 'Mijn ervaring' aside, Bas recounts that across his career he has seen how useful it is to take feedback seriously from the insight and execution phases. On several projects he worked closely with users and involved employees from the start. By giving them room to speak openly, even when it meant criticism, they could learn from those insights.

His claim: this approach not only delivered better results but also produced a tighter team and a product that genuinely added value. The process of learning and improving never stops; by distributing responsibility, making room for personal stories, and taking feedback seriously, the organisation keeps growing and adapting to users' needs, creating a product or service that not only meets but exceeds expectations.
