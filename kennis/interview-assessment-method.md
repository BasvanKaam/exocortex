# Interview and assessment intake method

How I run the discovery interviews that feed an advisory report.

## Approach
- Interviews are driven by a set of pre-defined questions, tuned to the agreed scope, plus input from the client's board/IT management, plus whatever else comes up during the conversation. Not everything can be planned, and that is fine: leave room for the unscripted.
- **Threshold rule:** when more than three interviews are taken, work from a fixed questionnaire (aligned to the scope) so the inputs stay comparable. Below that, a lighter touch is fine.
- Use a consistent set of guiding themes as the backbone of the questionnaire: scalability, mobility and flexibility, the digital workplace, general employee satisfaction, support and support requests, and collaboration (internal/external).

## How interviews are written up in the report
- Per interview, record name, role and date. That is enough for the header.
- Give a short summary in a few sentences or a paragraph.
- Summarize the discussed points as bullets under a "General" heading.
- For question-and-answer, write the question in italics and the answer in normal text directly after it.
- Scale the write-up to the number of interviews: terse when there are many, more elaborate (a fuller narrative) when there are only two or three people.
