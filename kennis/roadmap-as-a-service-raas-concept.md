# Roadmap as a Service (RaaS): Subscription Packaging of a Consulting Offer

A Salomon-era idea (Bas's own draft) to repackage the existing roadmap consulting service as an "as a service" subscription. The core insight: RaaS is not a new service, it is *a different way to market and sell the existing roadmap offering*, with the same end result. "We voegen het 'as a service' stuk toe aan een reeds bestaande dienst."

**Three variants (term-based)**
- Variant 1: runs 12 months, then renewable or cancelable monthly.
- Variant 2: runs 6 months, then renewable or cancelable monthly.
- Variant 3: runs 3 months, then renewable or cancelable monthly.
- Rule: **longer is cheaper, shorter is more expensive.** Preference is to stay engaged long (good for customer retention, upsell, influence, spotting other opportunities, getting own people in).

**End-of-term options**
- After term, bill monthly (unless renewed for 3/6/12 months).
- Renew for 3/6/12 months for 10% off the total price.
- Flexible monthly hours (more or less), adjustable in units of 2 hours.

**Package contents**
- *(Always)* the roadmap proposition itself, ~120 hours: workshops, orienting interviews with the customer across layers, a "stip aan de horizon," current work, challenges, ideas, requirements, business goals; "meten = weten" (measurement, the biggest time sink); writing an advisory report combining interviews and measurement; reviewing results and setting plans in motion.
- *(Optional)* progress measurement / interim advice / consultancy / regie, in two flavors: **Salomon regie** (e.g. 4 hours/month on-site or remote, scalable per 2 hours) or **Salomon regie + partial execution** (technical and process), at an agreed hours-per-month.
- The customer chooses the degree of involvement, which sets the monthly hours and thus a fixed price. Default suggestion: 4 hours/month.

**Indicative pricing (Bas's draft numbers)**
- Roadmap 120 hours at €135/hr (~3 weeks) = €16,200 base.
- Variant 1: 12 months × 4 hrs/month at €100/hr = €4,800 -> total €21,000.
- Variant 2: 6 months × 4 hrs/month at €125/hr = €3,000 -> total €19,200.
- Variant 3: 3 months × 4 hrs/month at €150/hr = €1,800 -> total €18,000.
Bas flags these numbers as provisional ("de prijs zou zo maar niet kunnen kloppen").

**Launch plan**
- A blog describing the service and re-explaining what a roadmap is and why it matters, with a visual and a clear call to action.
- Existing website roadmap copy already fits, especially the progress-monitoring angle.
- A press release ("lijkt me een must, dit is er nog niet!") with a quote from Abram.
- Repeat posts on LinkedIn; a piece on basvankaam.com.

**Power phrases collected for marketing**: pay for what you use, scalable, clear, no surprises afterward, fixed cost, Roadmap on demand, from A to Z, grows (or shrinks) with you, more or fewer resources.
