# Cost Saving Shouldn't Be the Main Reason to Go Cloud

Bas's consistent line: saving money should not be the main goal when you look into cloud services. He says he has made this point many times. The real value sits elsewhere (flexibility, ease of use, manageability), and cost is a side effect, not the headline.

He holds this even while selling autoscaling as a cost play: the autoscaling and one-click image-update features do make a genuine difference on Azure spend, but he frames that as a bonus on top of the operational win, not the reason to move.

Related tension he names openly: running a VDI estate is "always going to be a fine line between cost optimization and keeping your users happy." Both matter; you tune between them rather than maximizing one.
