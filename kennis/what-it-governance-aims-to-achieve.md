# What IT Governance Aims to Achieve

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

IT governance functions partly as an accountability system: it determines who is responsible and who is accountable, registers the provider-to-user relationship, and captures demand-and-supply relations, with the aim of establishing mutual respect for each party's decision-making authority.

Adding policies to guide decisions plus organization-wide adoption of standards facilitates consistency of management, strengthens compliance and reduces risk.

The higher aim is continuity of expectations: IT and reality often diverge, so IT governance seeks the ability to measure a return on IT investments and to ensure initiatives deliver the innovation and benefits they promised. The minimum bar is technology that is adequate and relevant, not technology that fails to contribute to the digital transformation.
