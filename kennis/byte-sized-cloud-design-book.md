# Byte-Sized: Cloud Design Principles and Architectural Recommendations book

A 335-page community paperback Bas co-authored with Christiaan Brinkhoff, started late 2018 and published June 2019. It pulled in 138 other contributors from around the globe. Officially launched at E2EVC Berlin with the first copies given away during their presentation, and offered as a free downloadable PDF on basvankaam.com. A follow-up Byte-Sized project was planned for 2020.

Note on his thinking even in a celebratory post: the work was framed as a "community masterpiece", reflecting his consistent community-driven, vendor-agnostic stance.

*Bron: blogpost 'A personal look in the rear-view mirror – what happened in 2019?!' (2019-12-10), basvankaam.com.*
