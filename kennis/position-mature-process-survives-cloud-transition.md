# Assess maturity, don't rebuild the incident process for cloud

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

The framework's stance is that moving to the cloud does not require tearing down and rebuilding the incident-management process. A mature process should be able to absorb the changes in event data flow, tooling, and service chains. The right move is therefore to evaluate process maturity, identify the maturity gap, and adapt just enough to 'Run in Control', rather than starting over. He also builds in pragmatism with a dispensation route: allow specific deviations from the process when the situation genuinely calls for it, so governance does not become rigid.
