# Method: a per-quarter themed content calendar

Run marketing content on a quarterly cadence: one theme every three months, with blogs, podcasts, whitepapers, and infographics all tuned to that theme. Two or three times a year, close out a quarter with a roundtable (or similar) in the theme's slipstream. Crucially, keep room for ad-hoc topics that surface, because nothing changes faster than IT.

The theme is the load-bearing element: 'Rode draad/thema is belangrijk, op basis daarvan de content bepalen', the through-line/theme is important, and content follows from it.
