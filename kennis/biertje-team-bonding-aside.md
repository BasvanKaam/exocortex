# Hey... Biiieeerrrrrtje? — Early Team Bonding

A classic Bas aside built on the famous ski-instructor catchphrase. Larger cloud projects are often supported by external, temporary, hired-in staff alongside internal employees.

Bas's hard-won experience: organise group activities OUTSIDE the office walls so people get to know each other better. A shared activity, a bite and a drink, make people interact more smoothly — inside jokes form ('from the night before, remember...'), that sort of thing.

Key method: don't wait until you're halfway through the project. The sooner the better — that's when the added value is greatest. Repeat it at suitable moments, but at least get it going early ('dan is in ieder geval de kop eraf'). The better a team is attuned, the nicer the collaboration, which benefits the whole project.
