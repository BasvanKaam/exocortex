# ShareFile is miles ahead of the competition (2017 take)

In his Synergy 2017 schedule post Bas drops an unhedged product opinion: addressing the 'Why should I use ShareFile if I already have Office365?' question, he says secure store/share/send/edit of critical company data is top of everyone's mind based on what he sees at customer sites, and flatly states 'I think ShareFile is miles ahead of the competition, let's see what they come up with next.' A datestamped, confident stance worth keeping as a marker of how he read the secure file-sharing market in 2017 (and a contrast point for how that bet aged).
