# Using AI voices to deliver his content

Bas takes the position that AI voices are a legitimate, well-working tool for producing clear, to-the-point content. On EUC News Nuggets he built an AI narrator persona ("Finn Morgan") who does "most of the heavy podcast lifting," while drawing a firm line on authorship: the research, writing and content remain his ("My content, he just delivers"). His stance is that AI handles delivery, not the thinking.

*Bron: Inside Citrix (2016), 'EUC News Nuggets platform launch'.*
