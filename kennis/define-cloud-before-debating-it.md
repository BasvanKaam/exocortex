# Bas's rule: define the Cloud before debating its pros and cons

Bas observes that the word "Cloud" is used te pas en te onpas (at every turn) and means wildly different things to different people: to some it is just online storage (Google Drive, Dropbox), to others a far broader concept of IaaS/PaaS/SaaS delivered over the internet. Add the word "hybride" and it gets even worse. This variety makes discussions vague and confusing when people fail to demarcate what they are actually talking about.

His personal first question whenever someone talks Cloud is always: what do we mean by Cloud in this context? Is it a specific platform (AWS, Azure), a type of service (e.g. SaaS), or infrastructure (e.g. IaaS)? Only by defining these terms clearly can a meaningful discussion happen and everyone get on the same line.

His prescribed order for any internal meeting or client conversation: first define what type of Cloud and/or service or solution you are talking about, only then discuss the pros and cons, possibly followed by what is and is not possible. He frames the goal sharply: we are not looking for a problem to fit our solution, but a solution for the problem.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> Een interne bespreking of klantgesprek? Dan eerst definiëren over wat voor type Cloud en/of-dienst of oplossing het gaat, daarna pas discussiëren over de voor- en nadelen, eventueel gevolgd door wat wel en niet mogelijk is.
>
> We zoeken namelijk geen probleem voor onze oplossing, maar een oplossing voor het probleem.
