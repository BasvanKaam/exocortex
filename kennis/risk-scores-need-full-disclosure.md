# Don't publish scores you can't fully back up in the open

In the April 2020 v3.0 of his conferencing-software cheat sheet, Bas pulled the security risk scores he had originally assigned to tools like Zoom, Teams and WebEx. He had the data to back the scores up, but he was not allowed to share it. His position: a score without full disclosure isn't fair to readers because they can't discuss or challenge it on the facts.

"Not without full disclosure to give my readers something to discuss, based on facts." He didn't get much negative feedback, he just decided it "didn't feel right." This is a clean example of his editorial-integrity reflex: if he can't show his sources, he'd rather remove the claim than ship an unverifiable judgement.
