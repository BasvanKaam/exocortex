# Containers hold great potential but aren't easy out of the box

April 2015. Bas was clearly excited by the Docker wave but stayed balanced, dedicating a full 'Some disadvantages' section to counter the hype. His framing: containers "hold great potential, it isn't easy out of the box," they bring complexity, offer less isolation than a traditional VM, and the shared Linux kernel is "a potential security threat." He specifically wondered how Microsoft would cope on the security side "since they tend to be a lot more prone to virus attacks and such," while noting the tiny Nano footprint and VMware's Photon/Lightwave as promising mitigations. A measured early take: enthusiastic about the direction, realistic about maturity and security in 2015.
