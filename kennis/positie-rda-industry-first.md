# RDA is an industry first that out-shipped Citrix itself

In Feb 2016, Bas enthusiastically backs the Remote Display Analyzer as a standout community contribution. His framing: the concept sounds simple but the technology is not, and the live codec-switching capability is 'huge' and 'an industry first, since even Citrix themselves have not been able to come up with something similar'. He champions community tooling that solves real admin pain (the confusing, hard-to-inspect HDX codec landscape) better than the vendor does, and points feature requests and support questions back to the authors. This reflects his consistent role as an amplifier and credit-giver for the Citrix community, not just a vendor mouthpiece.

*Bron: blogpost 'Presenting... The Remote Display Analyzer!' (2016-02-23), basvankaam.com.*
