# Governance Outcomes by Level and the Role of Communication

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

Governance outcomes differ across the hierarchy:
- Strategic level: knowing you are performing against set targets and leading requirements; that the transformation is managed, (IT) objectives are met, and a working governance framework is in place.
- Tactical level: the system of internal control. Does the framework work, are you in control of your control?
- Operational level: verifying that principles are applied and that policies, standards, laws, regulations and the requirements of service capabilities and contracts are met.

Communication closes the loop. Communicating results up the chain of command is key to good governance: without information there is no feeling of control, without control there is no trust, and trust is needed to empower an increasingly self-managed enterprise.
