# Bas on why he likes Citrix printing

Bas opens his printing chapter with a personal confession: 'I like printing. There, I've said it.' He hasn't built many complex print architectures, just a couple, but he likes the technology and processes behind it, especially since printing is business-critical at almost every customer he's visited. His characteristic aside: when a user gets an important email or finds an interesting whitepaper, the first thing they do is print it, then read it, annotate it, take it home. He admits he does it too, preferring pen and paper over a keyboard ('so perhaps I'm not the best reference').

His printing material grew out of his blog at basvankaam.com, started over four years before writing: his second article ever was on native Citrix printing and printing pathways, born from disappointing real-world experiences and what he could find on Citrix E-Docs. This led to more articles, version 1.0 of his 'ultimate Citrix printing internals cheat sheet,' and a Citrix Synergy presentation. He's proud the cheat sheet helped community members fix print issues (someone recently fixed one with it: 'how cool is that?!'). The book's printing chapter is a rewritten, expanded update of that cheat sheet (prompted by 'thanks, Jamie').
