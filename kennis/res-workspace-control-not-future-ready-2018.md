# RES/Workspace Control isn't a future-ready bet (post-Ivanti)

Bas's take after the E2EVC panel on the RES acquisition by Ivanti (June 2018): existing customers are slowly dropping out and not renewing, and he questions whether buying Workspace Control (former RES) makes sense at all given its state. The concerns: lack of vision and roadmap, no new features, the product in maintenance mode, a potential forced and far-from-ideal migration to UWM (AppSense), a rumoured end-of-life date, and mixed messaging from the company to customers and partners, all creating a negative reputation.

His nuanced conclusion: Ivanti's announced three-year Long-Term Maintenance Release (LTMR) could reassure existing customers enough to renew for a year or two. But 'as someone who is looking to invest in a future-ready (and steady) EUM platform, Workspace Control would probably not be high on my list.' This continues his 2018 thread (see the State-of-the-union post) that RES's future is 'cloudy' and customers are right to hesitate.
