# Summarize the features, spare people the marketing propaganda

Bas's framing of release coverage (Aug 2014): his explicit value-add is curation, "sparing you the marketing propaganda surrounding it." He invites Citrix employees and readers to correct or extend the list, and in the replies he does exactly that, owning two corrections (PVS edition availability, and "Central image management") from commenters Berry and Bart.

His upgrade advice in the comments is pragmatically conservative: "It offers some cool additional (new) features which can make life a little easier, but if you don't need them go with 7.5 for now and upgrade later." Features-don't-justify-an-upgrade-by-themselves is a recurring stance.

*Bron: blogpost 'Presenting Citrix XenApp 7.6 & Citrix XenDesktop 7.6... What's new?!' (2014-08-20), basvankaam.com.*
