# Platinum grows in worth, but unbundle the editions

November 2016. His stance on the long-running 'is Platinum worth it?' debate:

- He thinks Citrix did a good job returning value to Platinum (and Enterprise), especially over the prior ~2 years. Once you own Platinum it 'continues to grow in worth with each new feature added.' His sunk-cost logic: 'assuming you already paid, you have access, so you might as well take advantage of it.' Customers who came for one or two features now likely use half a dozen.
- The honest answer to whether it's worth it is 'it all depends': on budget, on what a given feature is worth to you, on infrastructure size, and on how Platinum compares to third-party monitoring/EUM/provisioning vendors.
- His structural critique: the gap between Platinum and Enterprise is too big. He floats merging Advanced + Enterprise into one base edition with optional add-ons customers choose by need/budget, and (added in an update) being able to purchase and share different editions/licenses within the same Site. He repeatedly suggests handing some Platinum-only features (e.g. some SCOM management packs) down to Enterprise.

Throughout he ends sections with open questions to readers ('Worth the investment? Yes? No? Why not?').
