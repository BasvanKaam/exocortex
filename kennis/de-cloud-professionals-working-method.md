# De Cloud Professionals: engagement working method

A step-by-step consulting engagement model ("eenvoudiger en completer kunnen we het niet maken"):

1. Get acquainted, find a click, discuss the client's challenges and wishes.
2. For larger/complex tracks, run a kick-off or workshop event to go deeper and capture first agreements.
3. Start work: clearly state how they will help and what the primary activities are; deliver a plan including cost estimate (quote) plus functional and technical designs and other documentation.
4. Depending on the solution, perform an inventory.
5. Optionally check with partner Salomon how the solution fits the bigger picture; a brief or extended roadmap is possible.
6. Report progress at pre-agreed moments and ask the right questions on time.
7. Preferably involve the client's own staff in designing and implementing, to avoid surprises afterward.
8. Deliver and hand over management to the client or to a managed services partner (Avensus MCS, ICT Vision, Client ICT).
9. Aftercare ("nazorg") which is often forgotten: on-the-floor support plus training and workshops.
10. Celebrate the successful, in-production delivery together.

The distinguishing thread is staying close to the client, involving their own people, and explicitly elevating aftercare and knowledge transfer rather than dropping the client at go-live.
