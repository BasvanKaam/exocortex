# Not WVD on-prem, but a pragmatic middle ground

April 2019 framing. Bas acknowledged that what most people actually wanted (WVD running on-premises) was not what he was offering. Instead he argued there's 'a ton of stuff that Microsoft Azure offers to extend what you have on-premises', and you could throw 'a little WVD in the mix'. His pragmatic angle: the Win10 multi-session image, usable outside the WVD control panel (e.g. with Citrix) as long as it stays in Azure, 'could be a nice step in between for some', even though Win10 multi-user would never run on-prem. The stance is the consultant's instinct to find a workable hybrid path rather than wait for the ideal feature.

*Bron: blogpost 'Combine WVD, IaaS RDS, on-prem RDS to scale, protect & migrate' (2019-04-29), basvankaam.com.*
