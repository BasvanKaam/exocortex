# Salomon IT Solutions financials 2017

Annual accounts (jaarrekening) for Salomon IT Solutions B.V., reporting period 1 Jan - 31 Dec 2017. This is the company's first real trading year; 2016 was effectively a startup stub.

Key figures (EUR):
- Revenue (omzet): 373,083 (2016: 21,708) - a near twenty-fold jump.
- Cost of sales: 39,730; gross margin 333,352 (89.4%).
- Total costs: 357,424, of which personnel costs 286,352 (76.8% of revenue) and car costs 48,301 (12.9%).
- Operating result: -24,072; net result: -24,244 (loss), but a strong recovery versus 2016's -167,197 loss.

Reading: heavy personnel and lease-car cost base; the business scaled revenue fast but was still loss-making in 2017. This is private company-history financial data, calibration only, not for output.
