# Christiaan Brinkhoff as 'Mister WVD' and the 2020 WVD framing

In a Come Get IT speaker spotlight, Bas characterised Christiaan Brinkhoff as 'Mister WVD himself' and traced his path: from Detron to FSLogix, and via FSLogix to a Global Black Belt role inside the Microsoft WVD team. Brinkhoff is described as travelling the world congress-to-congress 'om het WVD evangelie te verkondigen', supporting customer Proofs of Concept, running trainings/workshops, and co-driving community initiatives including Project Byte-Sized. Brinkhoff had presented at the first Come Get IT event in 2019 introducing WVD to the audience.

The accompanying framing of WVD in 2020: the beta/test/preview phases were passed and 2020 was positioned as the year WVD becomes usable in production worldwide, with a fast-growing feature set and roadmap.
