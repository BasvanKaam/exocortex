# Idea: build a flagship demo (F1 sim on VDI) to prove a technology's limits

A propositioning idea Bas highlights and clearly admires: rather than claim VDI can handle demanding graphical/peripheral workloads, ITQ (Johan van Amersfoort) built a working F1 race simulator on top of VMware Horizon combined with vGPUs and USB redirection — purely to show the world what VDI technology (and the firm behind it) is capable of.

The transferable idea: the most convincing proof of a platform's capability is an extreme, tangible, slightly playful showcase that stress-tests the very objections people raise (here: graphics-heavy, low-latency, peripheral-dependent). It doubles as technical-marketing and as a community talk ("the story behind it, the hardware and software used, configuration details"). Worth reusing as a pattern: pick the workload nobody thinks the platform can do, build it, then tell the build story.
