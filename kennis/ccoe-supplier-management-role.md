# CCoE Role in Supplier Management

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

In Vendor Portfolio Alignment, responsibilities are demarcated between the CCoE, the Portfolio Manager, and DevOps teams:

- The CCoE supports the Portfolio Manager in making choices, and helps with supplier management: service level harmonization, alignment on pricing models, terms of usage, and SLA tracking.
- The CCoE is responsible for tracking changes and developments on the cloud platform that may be relevant.
- DevOps teams are responsible for tracking changes and developments on the specific services they use.

Practical to-dos include tracking relevant SLA and Customer Agreement URLs to catch important changes, subscribing to periodic supplier updates (blogs, whitepapers, RSS feeds), and adjusting asset management and license management processes accordingly.
