# Bas on the future limits of self-hosting your own data growth

Bas notes that while a single company's data growth isn't comparable to worldwide growth, he can imagine a point in the next five to ten years where it becomes virtually impossible, or at least very hard, to keep up with these developments independently. His reasoning: how much physical space do you ultimately have for hardware (granting that form factors keep shrinking and systems get smaller and more powerful), and how much storage and compute can you realistically hold?

His sharper reframe is that even if it were technically and practically possible, the real question is: do you even *want* that? With the increase in data and required compute comes a lot of responsibility. That said, he frames this as 'opnieuw een gouden kans' (yet another golden opportunity) for hybrid infrastructures.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> Hoewel de groei en met name de snelle toename niet te vergelijken is met wat er wereldwijd plaatsvindt, kan ik me voorstellen dat we op een punt komen (in de komende vijf tot tien jaar) dat het vrijwel onmogelijk of op z'n minst erg lastig is om deze ontwikkelingen zelfstandig bij te houden.
>
> opnieuw een gouden kans voor hybride infrastructuren
