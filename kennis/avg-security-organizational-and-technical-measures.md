# AVG security: organizational and technical measures

The AVG requires organizations to take appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard personal data and prevent data breaches.

**Organizational measures:** assigning responsibilities, promoting security awareness, and reviewing log files, aimed at creating a culture of data security. The AVG requires that employees know how to handle personal data safely and that responsibilities are clear. Protocols for data breaches and incident management must be updated regularly to respond quickly and adequately.

**Technical measures:** access security, encryption and patch management protect data against unauthorized access and loss. Encryption of data both in transit and at rest is a key AVG requirement so that, in a breach, personal data is not directly accessible to unauthorized parties. Backups and automatic deletion of old data strengthen protection and meet data-minimization requirements.
