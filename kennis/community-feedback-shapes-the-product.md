# Community feedback should visibly shape the product

Bas's stance: a vendor community is only worth running if members can see their input shaping the product. He argues for taking feedback seriously enough that it shows up in the software, backed by an agile cadence (monthly releases, an active roadmap) so the loop is real, not performative. He explicitly credits the community as a reason the products are shaped the way they are. The corollary is quality over quantity: a small, engaged group beats a large passive one, and the program should never become a sales channel in disguise.
