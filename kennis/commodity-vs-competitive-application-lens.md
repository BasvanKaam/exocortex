# Commodity vs competitive applications as the core strategy lens

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

The organizing principle of a simple Cloud Strategy is the split between commodity and competitive functionality:

- **Commodity functionality** (low competitive advantage) is procured 'as-a-service', or alternatively hosted in the public cloud on PaaS or IaaS.
- **Competitive functionality** is developed cloud-native using PaaS capabilities.

The rationale: procuring commodity functionality from SaaS or BPaaS providers outsources non-core operational activities. This relieves the IT operations team from work that does not contribute to competitiveness and frees money and resources for functionality that does provide competitive advantage. A high-priority goal of a cloud strategy is therefore to relieve IT operations from maintaining commodity applications, whose 'keeping the lights on' costs are high while their competitive advantage is limited.
