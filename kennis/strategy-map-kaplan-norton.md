# Strategy Map (Kaplan & Norton) in SCAF

A strategy map is a visual overview of business objectives and how they interconnect, clarifying the steps and priorities needed to execute the business strategy. In SCAF it is created during the Strategie phase and becomes the reference frame for later steps, ensuring everyone shares the same picture of the main goals and their coherence. From a partner perspective it can be filled in jointly with the customer, or drafted first and then refined with them.

The model was devised by professors Robert S. Kaplan and David P. Norton (1992) and is popular with commercial companies for its structured approach. It is built around four perspectives:

- **Financiën (Financial):** financial goals such as increasing revenue and improving productivity.
- **Klanten (Customers):** how the company adds value for customers — product leadership, customer focus, operational excellence.
- **Interne processen (Internal processes):** efficiency and effectiveness of internal processes — innovation, customer relations, operational excellence, stakeholder relations.
- **Organisatie, leren en groei (Learning & growth):** developing the organisation, improving skills and growing knowledge so it has the right competencies.
