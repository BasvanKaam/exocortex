# Curated free-cloud-learning resource page as a colleague onboarding tool

A living, community-built resource page that helps colleagues broaden and deepen IT knowledge as the demand for cloud services and technologies becomes impossible to ignore. Deliberately a 'work in progress' that invites contributions (tips, knowledge sources, missing entries) rather than a finished list.

Key design choices that make it durable:
- Emphasis on FREE material. Exams cost money (clear with your manager first), training material is free.
- Structured by medium: podcasts (weekly Azure/AWS/GCP news in ~4 min, plus longer topical shows), free books, events, training/certification paths (AZ-900, MS-900, AWS Cloud Practitioner CLF-C01), YouTube channels, customer/startup success stories, paid+free training portals, and a large alphabetical list of community bloggers and influencers to follow.
- Foundational framing: cloud is not the answer to everything, but at least basic knowledge of the different cloud solutions and platforms is needed to have an informed opinion.
- Practical guardrail on events: don't all rush to attend; clear it with your manager because it costs (billable) hours.

The transferable idea: maintain a curated, contribution-driven, free-first learning hub and broadcast updates through an existing internal channel.
