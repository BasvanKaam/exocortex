# Start cheap, have fun, upgrade later: his take on entering sim racing

Writing in July 2021, Bas deliberately chose console-based sim racing (Xbox Series X) over the PC route, even though he acknowledges PC has the largest communities and the best drivers in the world. His framing: PC sim racing communities dominate and most info online is PC-centric, but for getting started and having fun, console is enough.

On spending he is self-aware and honest about the tension: he doesn't want to overspend in case he doesn't like it, but suspects he will like it and will want to upgrade ASAP, meaning he'd effectively pay twice. He still leans toward just getting started with a cheaper setup. He draws a parallel to real F1: the more money you spend, the better the equipment and the faster you can go, assuming you're a seasoned driver, which he jokes he is not.

*Bron: blogpost 'A new hobby - F1 sim racing, console style... on the Xbox Series X' (2021-07-28), basvankaam.com.*
