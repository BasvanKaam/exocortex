# If cost saving is your primary cloud driver, reconsider your strategy

A position Bas repeats across the Masterclass: if saving money is one of your primary motivations to look at cloud, you should reconsider your approach. He undercuts the "go to the cloud, it will be cheaper" cliche with his own worked comparison showing IaaS is not automatically the cheapest once variables are honest, and argues a cloud business case is much more than a financial justification. The real value sits in flexibility, speed, modernization and innovation, not a lower bill. The Capex-vs-Opex debate, he notes, does not even apply to many organizations, but is useful to understand.
