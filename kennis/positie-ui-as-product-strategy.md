# Good UI is a business strategy, not decoration

Bas argues that interface quality is a commercial lever, not cosmetics. UX/UI is about building flow, reducing friction, and creating trust through every click; when you get it right it doesn't just feel better, it works better. Concretely, good UI deflects support calls, shortens onboarding, and accelerates sales. He frames the interface as an onboarding coach, a support deflector, a sales accelerator, and a platform for growth all at once, and notes that while he loves training, 'the interface often speaks for itself.'
