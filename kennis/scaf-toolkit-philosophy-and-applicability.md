# SCAF Toolkit: Bas's 'keep it simple' philosophy and applicability

Throughout the book Bas references documents and overviews, and each SCAF phase lists its accompanying documentation. He keeps the base simple, using **Word, Excel and PowerPoint from Microsoft Office**; using more advanced software for project planning, agile working and document sharing is up to you, as the content and work are complex enough already.

**On applicability**: Bas is explicit that the book is not meant to convince you this is the *only* good approach or that you must do every step. His goal is to give you the handvatten (handholds) to make Cloud a success by pointing out best practices, pitfalls, pros and cons; his templates are one example. He says it's fine if you only use 30, 40 or 60 percent of the book, or read it purely for pleasure; in practice few organisations will walk through every step. He explicitly does not claim the book is complete ("dat zou toch knap zijn, vind je niet?"), welcomes feedback always, and hints a part 2 is in the making.
