# Bas's war story: writing book sections and key takeaways with ChatGPT

Bas openly demonstrates his AI workflow inside the book: he wrote a whole section using ChatGPT from a single carefully crafted prompt, and he reveals that *all the book's Key Takeaways were generated by GPT* and that he used AI here and there to analyse chapters. He benefits in four ways: efficiency (a good prompt generates relevant material fast), contextualisation (sketching context yields targeted, accurate answers), creative support (new angles, useful against writer's block), and accessibility (structuring complex ideas for a broad audience).

His actual prompt cast GPT as "een gerenommeerd Cloud expert" who explains technologies simply but in detail and keeps the tone light and pleasant to read. The generated text ends with GPT proudly declaring it was 100% AI-written.

Bas's honest verdict afterwards: "Niet slecht, toch?" but there is room for improvement and GPT is only a small part of the whole. He stresses you can iteratively refine the prompt mid-conversation — "keep the text mostly the same but use fewer bullets, explain LLM training in more detail," etc. — like chatting with someone; the text gets rewritten in 1-2 minutes, fast and nearly free of writing/language errors. Crucial caveat: **if you develop topics this way you must fact-check the generated content for factual accuracy.**
