# Don't be fooled: DaaS marketing sells a client desktop you aren't actually getting

In December 2013 Bas's stance on the DaaS wave (driven by Amazon WorkSpaces' big announcement) was: the technology is clever and cost-effective, but the marketing is misleading. Providers make it sound like you are leasing a one-to-one client OS desktop when in reality it is a server OS running Hosted Shared Desktops dressed up with Desktop Experience.

His advice: not a bad thing, but get IT involved before you commit, because there are legitimate reasons (per-user installs, admin rights, persistent storage) where HSD will not do and you genuinely need a one-on-one client OS machine. He frames it as inevitably a mix-and-match decision, and signs off characteristically inviting pushback: "Or am I wrong?!"

He reads Amazon's entry as trendsetting mainly because of who they are: "it's just that they're Amazon, they're big and they're known, and that's what matters."
