# Bulleted lists are one of the best ways to share knowledge

In April 2017, introducing his FMA-facts dump, Bas comes right out with a stance about how he teaches: 'I like, no wait, I love lists.' He thinks bulleted lists are one of the best ways to share facts and knowledge, which is why his book carries dozens of them, 116 'FMA facts' in total. The format was deliberate and evolved: he first tried Twitter-sized (144 char) quotes as the visually-different 'extra' in each chapter, then landed on the FMA facts. This is an early, explicit signal of his content philosophy, atomic, scannable, fact-per-bullet, the same instinct that later shows up across his whole body of work.

*Bron: blogpost '100+ Citrix XenApp & XenDesktop FMA facts listed' (2017-04-11), basvankaam.com.*
