# MSIX positioned to replace App-V (2019 transition)

Context as of 2019. MSIX was introduced at Microsoft Build the year prior and Microsoft signalled it would slowly move away from App-V, positioning MSIX (in conjunction with SCCM/InTune) as the main technology for application packaging and provisioning going forward. App-V was not under active development but remained supported in Windows 10. Existing MSIs, EXEs, and App-V packages could be converted to MSIX via the MSIX Packaging Tool. Many in the community did not see MSIX as a worthy App-V replacement yet and were worried about the transition.
