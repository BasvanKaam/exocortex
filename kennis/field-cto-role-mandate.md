# Bas's Field CTO mandate at Nerdio (the brief he set himself)

From his 2020 announcement of joining Nerdio as Field CTO, EMEA region. His self-described scope, useful as a reference for who he is and how he frames his own work:

- Talk with customers, partners, and interested parties about how Nerdio enables MSPs and companies of all sizes to build a Cloud practice on Microsoft Azure, with WVD as a leading actor.
- Explain what sets the Cloud-native software apart; accompany, support, and train organisations before, during, and after their move to Azure.
- Build the brand: onboard channel partners, write articles, do webinars, give presentations, attend events globally, run roundtables, record videos.
- Community involvement as a core pillar. He came in wanting to build a Nerdio-backed community program: "We'll start small, shape it together, and take it from there." This later became the Nerdio Tech Insider program.

Context: he came from Salomon IT and was an active Microsoft MVP in the Enterprise Mobility category (covering RDS and WVD). Career-context note, links to his community-builder identity.
