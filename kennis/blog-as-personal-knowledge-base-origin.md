# The blog began as a personal knowledge base, and sharing became the point

Framing (Feb 2015, ~2 years into blogging): Bas explains why he writes. He signed up to Wordpress.com, bought basvankaam.com, and 'just started writing' to create an online personal knowledge base and to learn about technologies as he went, primarily the Citrix portfolio. Over time the motivation shifted: 'sharing what I know, or have learned, with others is probably one of the main reasons I kept, and keep, doing what I do.' He calls it fun, educational and rewarding, and notes the site now also functions as a knowledge repository for others.

This is an early, explicit statement of the 'learn in public / second brain' philosophy that runs through his whole body of work - and a direct ancestor of the exocortex idea.

*Bron: blogpost 'My version of why the pen is mightier than the keyboard' (2015-02-09), basvankaam.com.*
