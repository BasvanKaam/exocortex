# Citrix Technology Professionals (CTP) and Citrix Technology Advocates (CTA)

The Citrix Technology Professionals (CTP) is an elite group of max 50 technology professionals elected annually by Citrix, who interact closely with internal Citrix product teams and share huge amounts of knowledge. Bas became part of this group as of February 2016.

The Citrix Technology Advocates (CTA) is a similar but newer group, more focused on spreading the word and less on interacting with internal Citrix product teams (though your opinion is still heard). Bas was part of the founding group that shaped the program (meeting online twice a month on benefits, application/ranking criteria, etc.). He proudly notes he came up with the 'CTA' abbreviation, which won the most votes from the team.

On a personal note, after three years as a CTP, Bas decided it was time to move on and make room for others: he voluntarily gave up his CTP seat and internally applied to become a CTA instead. (Note: the source text contains an apparent slip, writing 'hence the name Citrix Technology Professional' where CTA was meant.)
