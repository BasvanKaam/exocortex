# Moving VDI to the cloud shifts emissions from Scope 2 to Scope 3

Bas's sustainability explainer for DaaS, useful as a durable talking point:

- Data centre carbon emissions sit between roughly 2.5% and 3.7% of total worldwide emissions, which he points out is higher than aviation.
- Moving from on-premises VDI to cloud VDI changes the emissions classification from **Scope 2** (purchased energy, on-prem) to **Scope 3** (value-chain / supplier, cloud).
- A hyperscaler data centre is roughly 3x more efficient than a typical local data centre.
- Autoscale is the lever: by powering machines down when not needed you cut Scope 3 emissions, which he frames as saving 10-15% of overall emissions.

The argument: cloud VDI is not automatically greener, but cloud plus autoscaling is, and the savings are quantifiable for a business case. Nerdio surfaces unoptimized-vs-optimized CO2 in the Modeler as a Scope 3 savings calculator.

This is the conceptual core; the specific percentages are illustrative and version-dependent.
