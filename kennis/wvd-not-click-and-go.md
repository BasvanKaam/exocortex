# WVD is not a click-and-go product

Even with Microsoft's good documentation and step-by-step guidance, WVD (2019 preview) was not a click-and-go product. To get it running you needed working understanding of Azure networking, comfort with PowerShell, identity/Active Directory, and image management. This is where technology partners came in: at first WVD lacked certain enterprise features, both in preview and after global availability, and partners filled that gap.
