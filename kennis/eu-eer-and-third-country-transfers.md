# EU, EER and data transfers to third countries

Within the EU the same data-protection rules apply as under the AVG. Countries in the European Economic Area (Europese Economische Ruimte, EER) — Norway, Liechtenstein and Iceland — maintain an equivalent level of protection, so data can be freely exchanged between them and the EU.

For transfers to countries outside the EER ('third countries' / derde landen), personal data may only be passed on if the country offers an adequate level of protection comparable to AVG standards. This can be established via an adequacy decision (adequaatheidsbesluit) of the European Commission.
