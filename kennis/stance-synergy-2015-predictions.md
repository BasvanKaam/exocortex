# Synergy 2015 wish-list: Workspace Cloud, X1, fix the GUIs, better monitoring

May 2015, written ahead of his first-ever Citrix Synergy. Bas laid out expectations and personal hopes, useful as a snapshot of what the EUC community was wishing for. Highlights of his stance:

- Most excited about Citrix Workspace Cloud, based on enthusiastic CTP tweets after a briefing ("This should be good").
- Expected attention on Octoblu/IoT, XenMobile 10.x, ShareFile, the X1 Mouse + X1 Receiver, Framehawk HDX, Atlantis HyperScale/XenServer, and the Sanbolic/Melio acquisition (which he hoped would stay workload-, hypervisor- and storage-agnostic).
- A pointed UX critique: he predicted (and clearly wanted) Citrix to redesign its many slow graphical user interfaces, possibly consolidating them and moving to HTML5: "there has been a lot of debate, and complaints, on almost all current Citrix product user interfaces. They're too slow, there are too many." He noted it's an industry-wide struggle for big vendors.
- Wanted better integration of monitoring/troubleshooting tools (Director, TaaS/Insight Services, SCOUT), better IMA-to-FMA migration tooling, and Connection Leasing improvements reintroducing some old LHC capability.

The 'Or not' in the title signals he knows predictions are guesses. Captures his recurring concerns: end user experience, GUI quality, and migration tooling.

*Bron: blogpost 'Citrix Synergy 2015, here's what to expect. Or not.' (2015-05-06), basvankaam.com.*
