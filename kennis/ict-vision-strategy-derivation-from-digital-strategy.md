# ICT Vision: IT and Cloud Strategy Derive from the Digital Strategy

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

Framework vision statement: "Cloud as an enabler for becoming a Modern Enterprise."

An organization's IT strategy should be highly aligned with its strategic objectives and ICT vision. Since no organization (unless you are AWS or Google) has "provide cloud services to the business" as a strategic objective, the IT strategy is usually an element of the Digital Strategy. The Digital Strategy sets goals for transforming business activities so the organization can act on emerging trends or new business models, and radically redesign processes to gain competitive advantage, typically using components like Machine Learning, Data Analytics, IoT and Blockchain.

This is where cloud enters: such technology is best consumed as-a-service from the cloud. Hence the IT strategy and the underlying cloud strategy are usually derived from the Digital Strategy.
