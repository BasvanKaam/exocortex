# SCAF Chapter 8: The Insight (Inzicht) Phase Foundation

In Bas's **Step-Cloud Adoptie Framework (SCAF)**, Chapter 8 covers the Insight (Inzicht) phase: creating insight into the current situation before deciding what's possible with Cloud services. It builds on the strategy phase and goes beyond the technical side, looking at data, applications, networks, systems, processes, governance (bestuur en beleid), teams and more.

The goal is a complete picture, including the team's knowledge level and experience, possible skills/knowledge gaps, and the tools/software that can support. As in the strategy phase, information is gathered through interviews and workshops with various people. It helps answer questions such as:
- Which applications, infrastructure/network components and databases/datasets can (fully, partly, or not) be replaced or moved to the Cloud, and how? ("Wave" 1/2)
- Is it worth investing time to modernize certain applications, infrastructure components or datasets?
- How do you handle Cloud governance; does it fit your current IT policy/governance model?
- What information/data security measures are in place?
- What does the IT organization look like (existing processes, roles, responsibilities)? A **RACI model** helps here.
- Where are your biggest bottlenecks/challenges; what isn't going well?
- Are there direct, visible risks?
- Where are the 'gaps' in knowledge or manpower (technical, organizational and personal)?
- Who are the key suppliers and what do they do?
- Which teams are needed for an upcoming migration (Cloud adoption team, ambassadors, migration team)?
- What are the costs? A compact business case must be assembled at the end of this phase or start of the next (execution).

New roles may be introduced (e.g. Chief Innovation Officer), and a Cloud Center of Excellence (CCoE) can add value by bringing different forms of expertise together.
