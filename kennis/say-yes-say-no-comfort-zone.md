# Say yes to the unknown, learn to say no, leave the comfort zone

Three paired career rules from Bas's 20-years essay. Say YES to things you've never done and deep-dive ("Just Do IT"), but only if it fits your schedule and doesn't cost nap/family time. Learn to say NO; it's a skill acquired over time and counters fear-of-missing-out, since "once in a lifetime" opportunities rarely are. Step out of your comfort zone because "it's where the magic happens" and "if you do what you always did, you will get what you always got." His own example: he dreaded public speaking, practiced into it, and eventually enjoyed it. Try, Fail, Learn... Repeat.
