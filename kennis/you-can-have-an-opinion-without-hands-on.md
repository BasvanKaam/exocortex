# You can judge a product without hands-on, if you do the research

Bas used the Moonshot piece (June 2015) to stake out a meta-position on tech blogging: hands-on experience is the best approach and he would prefer it any day, but you can still hold a defensible opinion on a product you have never built with, provided you base it on facts rather than assumptions. His test for "reading the book": going through manuals, support docs, forums, seeing the product in action, and reading multiple blog posts from people who used it. His one red line: do not be "overly negative and burn a product or solution down to the ground without having done proper research." He admitted outright he had zero hands-on with Moonshot. This shows his early stance on intellectual honesty and earning the right to an opinion.
