# Bas's blog house-style and platform-hygiene rules (ComeGetIT review)

From Bas's internal review of the ComeGetIT blog platform, a compact set of his editorial standards:

- **Shorter paragraphs.** Large blocks of text are a recurring complaint; break them up.
- **Alternate formats.** Mix the 'interview-style' blogs with the 'written from yourself' (first-person) blogs so the feed has variety.
- **Terminology is non-negotiable: use 'on-premises' or 'on-prem', never 'on-premise'.** He is emphatic that 'on-premise' is '100% not correct' and notes countless discussions have been had about it.
- **Avoid repeating the same connective.** He flags the word 'echter' (however) appearing several times in a row.
- **Add a concrete practice example.** Where a post explains a product (e.g. Teams), suggest grounding it in real use, e.g. 'we use it inside Salomon for almost all projects.'
- **Platform security hygiene:** log in and update items at least once a week to keep the platform as secure and closed as possible; add two-factor authentication (e.g. Google) to the publishing platform.

Source: Bas van Kaam, internal feedback document on ComeGetIT, Salomon IT era.
