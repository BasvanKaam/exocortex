# SCAF: Salomon Cloud Adoptie Framework (Origin)

The Salomon Cloud Adoptie Framework (SCAF) is Bas van Kaam's early, pre-2025-book cloud adoption framework, developed at Salomon IT and first teased publicly in 2019 (with broader rollout planned through 2020). It is the seed of the later 'Step-Cloud Adoptie Framework'.

Defining characteristics as positioned:
- Part of a broader Roadmap proposition, but also usable standalone.
- Applicable to both small(er) and large organizations.
- Fully vendor- and brand-independent: explicitly not a clone of Microsoft, Amazon, Google or any other provider.
- A pragmatic and tested model where the technology connects flawlessly to the stated (business) objectives, wishes and requirements coming from 'the business'.
- A Cloud exit strategy is one of the topics addressed within the SCAF.

This note records the framework's origin and lineage; for its method and pillars see related SCAF notes.

Source: Bas van Kaam, Salomon IT blog, 2019-08-28.

*Bron: blogpost 'Wat een Cloud exit strategie inhoudt? Het SCAF?' (2019-08-28), salomon-it.nl.*
