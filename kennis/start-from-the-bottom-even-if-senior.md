# Re-doing the full cert path from the bottom isn't a bad idea, even for seniors

A small but characteristic learning-and-development opinion (June 2014). Explaining that once the upgrade exams retired you'd have to start at the bottom and work up, Bas added that this 'isn't a bad idea to do anyway, even if you're a CCIA already.' He treats certification not as a box-tick but as worthwhile re-grounding in fundamentals regardless of seniority. This early pro-learning stance prefigures his later education/L&D direction.
