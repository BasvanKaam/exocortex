# Bas's COVID-era asides and skepticism on the stats

Bas lived the COVID cloud rush firsthand and colours the statistics with hard-won, characteristic asides:

- He notes 2020-2021 saw many rushed, ill-considered steps ("veel ondoordachte stappen") because organisations normally take time to prepare big moves but suddenly had none.
- On Microsoft offering Teams "free" for a longer period, he warns it was a smart play: "als iets gratis is, ben jij vaak het product, zo bleek ook toen."
- On capacity: there were regular shortages of cloud resources, with everyone "vechten" for their piece of cloud. He dryly pushes back on the idea that on-prem could have absorbed demand: even with budget, how fast did they think they'd have built and operationalised all that hardware?
- He is skeptical of the multi-cloud hype: Flexera's 92% multi-cloud figure is "een beetje vertekend natuurlijk, zonder enige context," and although reports keep showing multi-cloud as popular, in practice he honestly doesn't see it often.
- On Gartner's prediction that ~85% of business applications would run in the cloud by 2025, he flatly disagrees: "dit lijkt me niet juist, hierover later meer."
- He reminds readers that physical laptops managed from the cloud (e.g. via Intune) also count as a form of hybrid.

He also quips that remote/hybrid work is now embedded in company policy "behalve als je voor Tesla werkt, blijkbaar."
