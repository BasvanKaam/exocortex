# Citrix Lifecycle Management blueprints

How it worked circa 2015-2016. A CWC service to accelerate and automate the design, deployment, and ongoing management of Citrix workloads (including enterprise apps). It rolls out complete Citrix environments fully automated onto your hypervisor or cloud platform of choice (the Resource Location), using Citrix-certified, predefined blueprints.

- Out-of-the-box blueprints for XenDesktop, XenApp, XenMobile, NetScaler, and the Workspace Suite, plus a library of Citrix- and partner-made blueprints.
- Subscription tiers (Deploy / Design and Deploy / Design, Deploy and Manage) gate whether you can author and edit your own blueprints.
- Adds alerting, monitoring, disaster recovery (recovery of specific service components or entire services, with multiple recovery destinations), and a fully automated upgrade path from XenApp 6.5 carrying over application and policy configuration.
- The same Cloud Connector is used to reach the Resource Location and AD and to provision/configure blueprint-based machines.
