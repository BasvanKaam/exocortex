# Everyone sweeps the floor (the Nerdio culture stance)

What keeps Bas at Nerdio, in his own framing: the people, team, vision, ambition, technology, partners, success. He singles out that 'lines are extremely short and doors are always open to anyone' and that 'we all sweep the floor, no exceptions', which he calls a unique mindset. He frames the most rewarding part of the job as solving real-world IT/cloud problems and pointing companies in the right direction even when they don't end up buying. Contributing to others' and the company's success is what drives him: 'There's no better feeling.'
