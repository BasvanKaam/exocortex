# Milestones in the development of Cloud computing

Key milestones in Cloud computing history as listed by Bas:

- 1969 ARPANET launches, further developed by the US military.
- 1970 First form of virtualisation software introduced.
- 1977 The term Cloud computing is defined by prof. Ramnath Chellappa.
- 1998 Google is founded as part of a PhD study by Larry Page and Sergey Brin.
- 1999 Salesforce.com is founded - very controversial at the time, much resistance, SaaS.
- 2005 YouTube launches; shortly after Apple offers the first films via iTunes.
- 2006 Google buys YouTube, (The) Facebook is founded, and AWS launches EC2 and S3.
- 2008 First iPhones go on sale - a digital revolution.
- 2009 One in 8 marrying couples in the US met online.
- 2010 Cloud computing goes mainstream; Windows Azure (now Microsoft Azure) becomes publicly available.
- 2011 Wikipedia passes 25 million pages; Facebook reaches 800 million active users (2.1 billion internet users); Microsoft introduces Office 365, ushering in the SaaS era.
- 2013-2018 Cloud computing grows to a market value of around 175 billion per year.
- 2020 Edge computing is integrated for faster data analysis.
- 2021 Companies massively embrace hybrid Cloud solutions (Azure Stack HCI).
- 2022 AI and ML become more accessible via the Cloud; rise of NVIDIA.
- 2024 Serverless computing becomes the standard.
