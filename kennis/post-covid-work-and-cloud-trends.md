# Post-COVID work models and continued cloud growth

By 2025 many COVID-driven changes have proven permanent. Cited figures:
- Gallup (2024): ~58% of workers worldwide prefer hybrid models, 28% work fully remote, only 14% full-time in-office.
- IDC: global cloud spending in 2024 expected to keep ~19.6% annual growth, with hybrid and multi-cloud staying popular for flexibility and risk reduction.
- CBS (Netherlands): 64% of organisations offer employees flexibility to partly work from home, reducing demand for fixed office space in favour of smaller flexible spaces.
- Google and Amazon continued hybrid policies, encouraging only a few office days per week.
- Gartner predicts ~85% of business applications will run in the cloud by 2025 (a figure Bas disputes).
- Investments in Security-as-a-Service solutions grow nearly 21% per year, as cyberattacks increasingly target cloud environments.

Unchanging benefits organisations experience: scalability, cost efficiency, flexibility, and the ability to respond quickly to market change, provided it is well managed and configured. Future growth is driven by AI, machine learning and advanced data analytics.
