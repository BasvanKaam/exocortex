# CGIT internal go-to-market: promo evening + 'On Tour' (idea)

Bas's proposal to grow CGIT (Cloud Generation IT) adoption inside the parent organisation. Two moves:

**1. Promo evening (September).** An evening session with food and drink, roughly a one-hour presentation ('we don't want to needlessly drag it out') with plenty of room for questions, remarks, suggestions and discussion. Possibly streamed for people who live too far away. Multiple people may present, or each does their own part. Announced via a blog (written by Bas) that explains not just CGIT but the broader goals: getting to know your colleagues better and sharing knowledge among each other.

**2. CGIT On Tour.** Visit the business units where CGIT has no foothold yet (Client ICT, Audit Connect, ICT Vision), dependent on their cooperation and interest. The premise: each BU already runs internal knowledge sessions from time to time; CGIT joins as a guest and takes ~15 minutes to explain what CGIT is, its plans, and how they can work together. Larger BUs (e.g. ICT Vision) may be bundled.

Bas commits to being present at every stop, or at least doing his best to be.
