# Vendor partner congresses are marketing-first roadmap events

Every vendor of any size runs an annual flagship event. Microsoft Inspire (held in Las Vegas, Nevada) is the partner-focused one: it shares the roadmap for the coming year, where Microsoft's fiscal year runs from July 1. Bas characterizes Inspire as less technical and more marketing: the core message to partners is what added value the products bring to their customers and how to sell the vendor's products and services so both sides profit. Bluntly: how to make money together.

Alongside sales tracks and introductory (level 100) technical sessions, the real value is the floor: talking to vendor staff, networking, meeting new people, and forming or reviving partnerships.

Useful framing when reading vendor announcements: separate the marketing layer from the technical substance, and remember these events exist to drive the vendor's commercial agenda.
