# Citrix's constant rebranding confuses customers

In July 2013, with the CloudGateway brand killed off and its components scattered into the new XenMobile App and Enterprise editions, Bas voiced a gentle but real critique of Citrix's naming habit: 'Since Citrix is keen on name change every once and a while it does tend to confuse people.' He saw the edition shuffle partly as marketing exercise ('and of course the marketing team did their job as well'). His pragmatic verdict on the product itself was balanced: powerful but not easy. 'XenMobile can be complex to set up and configure, it's great, but not easy.' This is an early, recurring Bas pattern: fan of the technology, but willing to name the friction Citrix creates for the people who have to learn and run it.
