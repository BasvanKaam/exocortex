# Industrial IoT will be the biggest sub-vertical (2017 call)

Writing in July 2017, Bas predicted that the Industrial IoT, though generally seen as a sub-vertical of IoT, would become 'one of the most important and biggest one going forward, in let's say the next 3 to 5 years' from a business perspective. He framed IoT/(I)IoT as on their way to becoming the new IT buzzwords while most companies still struggled to find their place: 'Everyone wants a piece of the action but it can be hard to figure out where to start.' He positioned himself as an early voice riding into the space ('we're here for the journey'), candidly admitting he was still relatively new to it.

*Bron: blogpost 'All 'things' connected, the 'I' in the IoT - a closer look. Part one!' (2017-07-10), basvankaam.com.*
