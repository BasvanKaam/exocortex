# Bas on cloud, hybrid and the future of on-premises VDI

Bas's stance on cloud for EUC: there is no one-size-fits-all — there are multiple cloud services to choose from. Even moving most of your on-premises infrastructure may pay off long-term, but it takes careful planning and execution. His advice is to start small and go hybrid (e.g. CWC); many companies benefit just by using the cloud for Burst Capacity and backup. Don't forget printing and scanning bandwidth limitations when hosting RDSH/VDI in the cloud.

He reiterates that 'true' VDI/DaaS from the cloud (one-to-one desktop-OS VMs) remains hard, mainly due to Microsoft licensing restrictions, so most DaaS is RDSH/XenApp-based shared desktops. His prediction: the cloud will have a major impact on future infrastructure, but on-premises RDSH and VDI are here to stay for at least another five to ten years — 'my guess is longer'.
