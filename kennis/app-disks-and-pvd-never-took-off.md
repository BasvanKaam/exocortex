# App Disks and PvD never took off; Unidesk was the right call

Bas is blunt (Jan 2017) that two of Citrix's own image/layering technologies underdelivered: App Disks 'never really took off' and the same can be said for PvD (Personal vDisk), which had been around longer. He frames the Unidesk acquisition as Citrix correctly buying its way to a mature layering technology rather than persisting with its own, calling it 'a great move' and predicting Unidesk would replace both App Disks and PvD over roughly a year. His underlying belief: layering matters strategically because it makes moving applications to the cloud easier (cloud portability), which is why Citrix wanted a real layering company. In a reply he adds candidly that he did one project with Citrix Streaming and 'didn't like it very much to be honest.'
