# Cloud adoption goes far beyond technology alone

Bas frames his Cloud Bootcamp around the stance that adopting 'the cloud' goes far beyond technology alone. He deliberately foregrounds the new business reality and the often-forgotten cultural shifts, the changing role of the CIO/IT management, and how business and IT can or should grow closer together. The course is positioned as a healthy mix of the business side and technology, fully vendor-independent.

The pedagogical corollary: he runs an independent, pragmatic adoption framework as the red thread, claimed to be applicable to both very small and very large organizations, and insists on interaction, discussion and peer learning ('learning from each other is central') rather than one-way delivery. He also positions exit strategy as a 'necessary evil' that must nonetheless be addressed.
