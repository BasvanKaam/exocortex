# Convenience Does Not Excuse Ignoring Privacy

Bas's stance during the early COVID scramble: the speed and convenience of adopting collaboration tools is no excuse for ignoring what those tools do with your data. He singles out the gap between celebrating a capability ('Zoom supports 49 people') and never asking what Zoom does with your data in the background. His position is that even under crisis pressure, organizations can and should still go back and address security, privacy and governance, doing this is 'een must wat ons betreft', and not blindly following internet articles or a competitor's advice.

Source: Bas van Kaam, Salomon IT blog, 2020-04-01.
