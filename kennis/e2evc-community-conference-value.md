# E2EVC as a flat, high-value community conference

How it worked then (2014): E2EVC (Expert-to-Expert Virtualization Conference), organized by Alex Cooper, ran multiple editions a year across major cities (in 2015: Berlin, Lisbon, Hong Kong, Florida). Bas frames its value as deeply technical content at a fraction of the price of vendor events (he cites it being 300-400 euros cheaper, with early-bird discounts on top), run largely on volunteer effort. Its signature trait: a flat social structure where junior admins and 25-year senior consultants mix as equals, and the big names you normally only meet on Twitter or LinkedIn will buy you a beer. A durable lesson on what makes a community event work, even though the specific 2015 lineup is dated.

*Bron: blogpost 'Who needs Citrix Synergy Europe when we have events like E2EVC?!' (2014-10-29), basvankaam.com.*
