# SCAF management-summary 'palet' templates per phase

In the SCAF, each phase closes with a final report and/or summary that feeds the overarching end report. Bas provides management-summary 'palet' templates per phase:

- **Adoptie management samenvatting palet**: summarises the adoption phase, describing delivery of the Cloud platform, handover to management (with documentation and processes), the communication model, cost clarity and cost-awareness, skill-readiness progress, largely operational governance, continuously optimised agile working, and the role of ambassadors/training.
- **Inzicht management samenvatting palet**: management summary for the insight phase, covering findings and decision points such as first migration waves, security/network initiatives, license inventory, a **Go-NoGo moment** with a concise business case, knowledge levels, teams and gaps, and the governance model.
- **Eindrapportage management samenvatting palet**: guideline for the overall final report, more varied in topics, covering drivers for Cloud migration, strategic goals, risks and identified gaps, financial and operational impact, implementation/transition progress, involved teams and a summary of modernisation activities.

The per-phase **handleiding templates** (Strategie, Inzicht, Uitvoering, Adoptie) that explain how to walk a phase are NOT included in the toolkit.
