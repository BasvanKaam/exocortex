# Citrix/EUC community podcasts

Community podcasts Bas recommends:
- Ask Eric (xenappblog.com/askeric): Eric lets people verbally record a question, answered by Eric or another field expert. Eric also offers free and paid CBT training videos at xenappblog.com.
- Login TechCast (soundcloud.com/logintechcast): hosted by Sander Noordijk and Eric van Klaveren; a big hit in the Netherlands, mostly in Dutch but some English shows depending on guests. Bas was a guest twice and praises their humour and industry knowledge.
- Frontline Chatter (frontlinechatter.com): a fortnightly community podcast on the End User Computing (EUC) market and community, with the latest EUC news and interviews.
- EUC Podcast (eucpodcast.com): a community-driven podcast for IT professionals, geared toward application, desktop, and server virtualisation, encouraging community interaction to keep authors honest and non-biased.
