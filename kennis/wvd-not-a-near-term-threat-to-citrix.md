# WVD is not a near-term threat to Citrix; enterprise features still favor CTX

In this March 2019 fact list, Bas offers his read on the Citrix-vs-WVD question. His call: 'Will the WVD be a threat to Citrix? It might be in the distant future but not on short notice.' His reasoning is that Citrix has built (or is building) its own DaaS solution on top of WVD, and that most enterprise features are still lacking in WVD, 'this is where CTX still shines'. He frames WVD's roadmap as long and impressive, and is candid that the picture could 'turn into old news quickly'. He also surfaces the dissenting community voice (Claudio Rodrigues, @crod, and his 'WVD BS' talks) rather than presenting only hype. A measured prediction from the pivot moment when Microsoft entered Citrix's core territory.
