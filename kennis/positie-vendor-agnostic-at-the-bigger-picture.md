# At the Bigger Picture, Stay Vendor-Agnostic

Bas draws a deliberate line between event formats: at "bigger-picture" events you talk about challenges, common situations and general solutions "without sticking a label/brand/specific product on it." Specific technologies and products belong only at the internal, technically-focused knowledge events. Even on the workspace-aggregation topic he frames the value as solving real frustration with a relatively simple solution, and cautions the audience to come prepared with critical questions about a vendor's bold claims (Awingu positioning itself as a cheaper, simpler replacement for Citrix): "prepare your questions, I already have a few myself."

This is the early Salomon-era expression of the vendor-agnostic stance that runs through his later work.
