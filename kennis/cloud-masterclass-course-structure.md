# Cloud Masterclass: two-day course structure

The Salomon-IT Cloud Masterclass is a two-day instructor-led class (delivered as "Class of 2020") built around the SCAF / Step-Cloud Adoptie Framework, with each agenda block tagged to a SCAF pillar (Strategy, Insight, Execution, Adoption).

The content arc runs: Once upon a time (history of cloud), Cloud principles and models, The new business reality, The cloud anno 2020 (providers/stats/trends), Cloud economy, Architectures and services, Security, Data and governance, Exit strategy, Community and practical tips, Future and trends, and Cloud adoption. Day 1 focuses on roadmap planning and the SCAF Strategy and Insight pillars; Day 2 on the Execution and Adoption pillars plus Data/governance, security, exit strategy and trends. Both days run roughly 08:30 to 16:00/16:30 with fixed breaks and lunch.

This is the training wrapper around the book; the SCAF model itself is distilled separately.
