# Bas's navigation metaphor: a cloud journey needs a destination

Bas frames cloud adoption with a road-trip metaphor that recurs as a core framing of the book. You get in the car with a full tank, good music and a sense of adventure, but there is one problem: you never set the navigation, or you type something in without looking. You drive somewhere with no clear destination. You may pass beautiful landscapes, but you are likely to end up in places you did not want to be, hours later, fuel wasted, stuck "in the middle of nowhere."

The cloud works exactly the same way. Without a clear destination, without goals, a plan and insight into what you want to achieve, you waste time, money and energy without really moving forward. A good plan is like a navigation system that brings you step by step closer to your goal, via the fastest route, without detours or waste. So before you begin your cloud "reis" (journey), know where you want to go, set your navigation, and head deliberately toward your ideal destination.

This ties into Bas's recurring point that, as in life, asking the right questions is essential; the book is positioned as a guide and a source of answers to questions you meet along the way.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> Een goed plan is als een navigatiesysteem dat je helpt om stap voor stap dichter bij je doel te komen, zonder omwegen, de snelste route en zonder verspilling.
>
> Uiteindelijk ben je uren verder, heb je brandstof verspild en sta je ergens in the middle of nowhere.
