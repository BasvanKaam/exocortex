# If your main reason to migrate is cost savings, rethink your strategy

Bas's stance (2018 update in Inside Citrix): hosting your infrastructure/datacentre in the cloud is not cheap, at least not by default, and the transition itself takes time, testing and careful planning. "If you think 'the cloud' will be cheaper, and this is your primary reason to consider migrating, you need to rethink your strategy."

*Bron: Inside Citrix (2016), 'Inside Citrix Ch3 - Is the FMA still relevant (cloud vs on-prem)'.*
