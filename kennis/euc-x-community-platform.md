# EUC-X: an umbrella for community side-projects (2019)

EUC-X was conceived as a Dutch non-profit foundation (registered at the chamber of commerce) acting as an umbrella platform rather than a single thing. Planned pillars: a bi-weekly podcast, an EUC content aggregation platform with future "background intelligence" to tailor content to members, and talk of a vendor-neutral event. Board: Eric van Klaveren, Jits Langedijk, Bas van Kaam. Advisory board / podcast crew: Kees Baggerman, Johan van Amersfoort, Ingmar Verheij. The name was later forced to change to EUCdigest (see 2019-10-02). How it worked then: community initiatives were structured as formal foundations with sponsor packages.

*Bron: blogpost 'EUC-X... Heads up – A new and exciting community initiative is on the rise' (2019-07-24), basvankaam.com.*
