# Hard costs vs soft costs (both matter)

A framing Bas uses when people reduce the value of better tooling to "saving money." When asked which matters more, his answer is both.

- **Hard costs** = storage and compute resource costs (the line items you can point at).
- **Soft costs** = management effort, time, making life easier, reducing stress and risk.

The efficiency argument isn't only about the cloud bill; the soft-cost savings (enabling the biggest part of your IT workforce to work with WVD daily) are at least as important.
