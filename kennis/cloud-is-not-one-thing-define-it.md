# 'The Cloud' Is Not One Thing: Define It First

'Cloud' is a collective term for dozens, if not hundreds, of different services. One cloud service is not the other. Before you can say anything about whether a given application, dataset, or work environment is better placed in the cloud, you need a clear definition of what you mean by 'cloud' in that specific case.

The practical consequence: blanket statements like 'move to the cloud' are meaningless without specifying which service model and which workload. He treats the cloud as a toolbox: a tool you reach for when you need it, picking what fits the situation rather than adopting wholesale.
