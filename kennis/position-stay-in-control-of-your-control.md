# Blind faith in automation will not ensure happy outcomes

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

The framework holds that automation is necessary to stay in control in the cloud, but that automation itself must be governed: 'Blind faith in the power of automation will not ensure happy outcomes.' You have to stay in control of your control. That means continuously asking whether the automation works as expected, where it can improve, whether logging trails are correct, whether you still comply to your own standards, and whether you genuinely feel in control of risk. Automation is a tool, not a guarantee, and treating it as a guarantee is itself a risk.
