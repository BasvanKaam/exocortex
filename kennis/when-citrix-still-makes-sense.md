# When Citrix still makes sense (and when an alternative is better)

Bas's decision guidance from the 2019 alternatives deck. Stick with Citrix when:

- You are (very) satisfied with it, or already use a large part of the surrounding product stack.
- You deal with a bad network or limited WiFi.
- Bandwidth is limited / relatively low.
- You have thousands of users and/or workplaces.
- You run remote graphics-intensive applications.
- You want an extensive workspace portal.

Implicit corollary: for smaller scale, good networks, cloud-first automation and simpler needs, a lighter alternative (CloudJumper, Workspot, Parallels) is often the better, more cost-effective choice. The recurring deployment selling point he highlights for alternatives is automation at scale: 'hundreds instead of thousands' of manual steps.
