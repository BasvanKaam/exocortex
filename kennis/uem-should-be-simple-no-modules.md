# UEM should be simple, single-console, and not sold in tiers

In this Nov 2017 pitch Bas argues that User Environment and Workspace Management is fundamentally about simplicity and ease of use, 'or at least that's what they should be': one console to manage everything that matters to users and their digital workspace, whether on-prem, cloud, virtual or physical, and a backend that is straightforward to install. He repeatedly underscores Liquidware's all-in-one stance as a differentiator and a value position: 'We don't do modules, no bronze, silver or gold etc.' He backs the 'demand is surging' claim with his own anecdote of handing out a record number of trial licenses and getting ProfileUnity running on his MacBook Pro in 45 minutes without extra infrastructure. This is his pre-sales-era framing: products should be easy to evaluate and adopt, and tiered/modular licensing is implicitly a negative.
