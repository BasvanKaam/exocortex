# Salomon's Two Event Formats: Internal Technical vs Bigger-Picture

Bas's idea (set out in a Dec 2018 internal memo planning the 28 March 2019 event) for a recurring Salomon knowledge-sharing event series, with a deliberate split into two distinct formats:

- **Internal knowledge (sharing) events with a technical angle.** Here it is acceptable to talk about specific technologies and/or products. Primary audience is colleagues, with possibly customers, partners and prospective colleagues mixed in. Target attendance was set ambitiously at 80-100 people.
- **Bigger-picture events.** These focus on challenges, common situations and general solutions "without sticking a label/brand/specific product on it" — vendor-agnostic by design.

The series was planned as roughly quarterly (dates floated: 28 March, 27 June, 24 September, 19 November 2019), with locations chosen per event (Kasteel Woerden floated for the first).

A further idea he floats: Salomon should fully own at least one event end-to-end — all sessions prepared and presented in-house (including duo presentations) — as something he feels is "a little obligatory to our standing." He suggests pencilling in 19 November for that without committing a theme yet.
