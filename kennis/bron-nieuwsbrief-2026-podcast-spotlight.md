# Newsletter: Podcast spotlight, same nugget two ways

Format edition, June 2026 (five episodes live at the time). Gives the EUC News Nuggets Podcast the spotlight after several editions on the written side.

## Core points
- Most content assumes you sit behind a screen; often you are in the car, on a bike, on a plane, walking the dog or doing the dishes.
- Same nugget, two ways: sometimes the cheat sheet came first and the episode is the spoken walk-through, sometimes the other way round.
- Episodes stay under ten minutes, part of the microlearning principle he applies to almost everything he makes.
- Finn Morgan is AI, openly: research, thinking and words are Bas's, Finn narrates. "The slop is the care a human refused to put in, it is not the tools' fault. So I front-load the craft into it."
- Alongside Nerdio: Intune Policy Studio and Nerdio Compass are relevant to anyone running EUC at scale; as Principal Learning Architect he builds the courses around them (Nerdio University). This channel is "a more private (and proud) way" of sharing, and not only about Nerdio.
- Content also comes from Cloud Mastery (SCAF, IT-business gap, cloud economy).
- Why the nugget format exists: his old cloud cheat sheets (more than a dozen) were popular but almost impossible to keep current; nuggets are small, current and easy to replace.
- AI as a topic: not hype or fear, but where it helps, where it does not, and how he uses it without handing over the thinking. "If the idea is human, AI can assist!"
- Show, do not promise: no big reveals, things appear when they are ready.
- Asks for five-star ratings ("tip your Uber driver") and topic suggestions in the comments, "that is often where the next episode starts".
- Links: Spotify https://shorturl.at/DvYT2, Apple https://shorturl.at/5Qsiw, web https://shorturl.at/95Bfp.

## Notable lines (verbatim)
> Same nugget, two ways to consume, your choice.

> Finn is AI, and I am open about that, because here showing the AI is the point.

> But I am going to show you when they are ready, rather than promise a big reveal.

> It might be with the help of AI, but if anything it brings its own challenges (read: additional work).
