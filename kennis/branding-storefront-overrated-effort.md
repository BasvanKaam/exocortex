# Companies overspend on StoreFront branding

While demonstrating the new Logon and Header branding options in StoreFront 3.0 (July 2015), Bas slipped in a pointed aside: "Although it's a cool addition, feature wise, I do think that a lot of companies spend way too much time on this." His stance: the cosmetic customization is nice to have, but not where teams should be sinking their hours. A small but characteristic instance of him cutting through feature excitement to flag where effort is actually warranted.
