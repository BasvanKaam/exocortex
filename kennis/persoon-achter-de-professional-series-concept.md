# 'De persoon achter de professional': humanising colleagues as a content format

A recurring content format Bas introduced at Salomon: a short, light interview series called 'de persoon achter de professional' that introduces colleagues as people, not just job titles. The intent is to show the team behind the company, warts and all ('een tikkeltje eigenwijs zo nu en dan maar ook dat heeft zo zijn charmes'), as a humanising, trust-building employer-branding device.

The fixed question set is reusable: who are you and what do you do; how did you end up here; describe the company in max 3 sentences; describe yourself in max 3 sentences; the nicest thing in the last 3-6 months (work or private); where do you see yourself in 5 years; are you (very) good at anything / a hobby; what can we wake you up for; which traits are you allergic to; which question did I forget to ask (and its answer); anything else to add.

The sample subject, Bob Molenaar (Business Consultant, found via Bas through the EUC community), signs off with a stance that aligns with the house view: 'Cloud is niet altijd de oplossing en vermijd lift & shift naar de Cloud' - already captured in the brain under Bas's own lift-and-shift notes.
