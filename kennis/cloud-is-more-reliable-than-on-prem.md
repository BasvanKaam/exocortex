# Cloud is more reliable than on-prem, be realistic

Bas's stance, written after a stretch of (seemingly) global Azure outages.

- You can design around some failures with regions and availability sets/zones, but **some core-service outages you simply cannot architect against.** Not everything is designable-around.
- "Stay on-prem then" isn't the safe answer it sounds like: if you use Office 365 for, say, mail, you're hampered anyway, and more than 50% of organisations do. The same caveat applies to multi-cloud.
- Cloud is still relatively new, a learning process, with different SLAs to match. Azure is growing strongly and saw heavy demand and challenges during Covid; with so much being added and changed, things do occasionally go wrong.
- Even so, **the cloud is online more often, with fewer outages than on-prem**, whatever people claim. Be realistic and honest about it. Bas admits he thought differently a few years ago, at least partly.

The new way of working is here to stay, and WVD plays an important role in it.
