# Innovation Boards Fail Without Recognized Time

A recurring, candid stance in Bas's strategy memo: the Detron Technology Board is full of good initiatives but follow-through keeps falling short, and he names the root cause plainly. Everyone is too busy. The honest mechanism: writing hours for the Technology Board means NOT being 100% billable at a customer that week, and people (and the business) won't accept that trade-off. "Uiteindelijk snijden we onszelf hiermee in de vingers."

He pushes for explicit agreements on what the board membership actually delivers to its members ("wat levert het de TB en haar leden op?"), and openly doubts whether a solution will be found. The transferable position: innovation and internal initiatives die not from lack of ideas but from lack of protected, recognized, non-billable time, and pretending otherwise is self-defeating.
