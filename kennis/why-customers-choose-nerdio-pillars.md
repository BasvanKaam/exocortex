# Why customers choose Nerdio: the pillars

The set of reasons I present when explaining why customers pick Nerdio, framed around the idea that End User Computing is going through its "Cloud Moment":

- Sustainability
- Security & Compliance
- Market leadership
- Return on investment
- Simplicity

Product positioning I use alongside it: Nerdio is a modern, unified management platform for Cloud VDI, Cloud PCs, on-prem VDI, and physical endpoints - i.e. one console across all four estates, not just AVD. And the recurring frame: Nerdio Manager extends the native AVD admin experience rather than replacing it.

The "EUC is going through its Cloud Moment" line is the macro thesis I open with - it positions the whole category shift, then the pillars explain why Nerdio is the vehicle for it.
