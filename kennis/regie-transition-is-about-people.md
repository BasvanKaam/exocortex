# The Beheer-to-Regie Transition Is About People

Bas stresses that moving from a beheerorganisatie to a regieorganisatie does not happen overnight. Above all, it is about the people: employees must acquire new skills, which takes time. Traditional roles often remain, but how they are filled changes.

A key question every organisation must ask is: **who fills the regie role?** This can sit with the organisation itself, the customer, or the supplier, depending on context. Bas shares from experience: he worked for a company that often took on these tasks temporarily, usually until most of the work was done and the customer was ready to take the reins. His principle here is to involve the customer as much as possible throughout the journey — "al doende leert men."

His hard rule: before moving to the cloud, the regie function must be properly set up; IT processes and roles must be in order. If not, it may be wiser to postpone the move to the cloud until the regieorganisatie is fully arranged.

He also warns that orchestration includes regular (re)selection of suppliers — for example when you are less satisfied — so you don't end up in a 'spaghettiwestern' after onboarding yet another vendor. This is where the cloud exit strategy (chapter 12) comes into play again.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> je wilt ook niet gaandeweg in een spaghettiwestern terechtkomen na het onboarden van de zoveelste leverancier
>
> Al doende leert men.
