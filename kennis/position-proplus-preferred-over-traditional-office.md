# ProPlus is usually the better choice over traditional Office

Bas van Kaam's stance in the 2019 whitepaper: when choosing between Office 365 ProPlus and traditional Office 2019 (notably for RDS scenarios where the Server 2019 support gap forces a decision), his preference in most cases goes to ProPlus. His reasons: the subscription licensing model, the flexibility the software brings, and the continuous stream of new functionality, which many companies see as a major plus. Both scenarios have pros and cons, but ProPlus wins by default for him.
