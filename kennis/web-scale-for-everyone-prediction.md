# OEM deals will democratize web-scale infrastructure

Bas's framing (Nov 2014): he reads the Dell-Nutanix OEM deal as a win for both parties and, more interestingly, as a step toward making web-scale (software-defined) hyper-converged infrastructure available to all companies 'regardless of size and budget.' He calls it 'a good thing if you ask me' and predicts it is just the first line of appliances with more to follow. The thing he 'really likes' is that Nutanix kept its software identical across hardware partners rather than forking a watered-down OEM edition. An early-era optimistic take on hyper-convergence going mainstream.

*Bron: blogpost 'Powered by Nutanix! Check out the new Dell hyper-converged XC series!' (2014-11-05), basvankaam.com.*
