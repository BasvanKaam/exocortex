# Salomon 2020 marketing: knowledge-first, strategic over technical, no hard CTAs

2020 marketing direction for Salomon, centred on three pillars: the Roadmap, training (Masterclass), and the (Cloud) Adoption Framework (SCAF).

Focus shift:
- More focus on Roadmap and Cloud adoption framework; less technical, more strategic (a bit of technical is still fine).
- Use components of the Roadmap, training, and SCAF as input for blogs, without it becoming advertising.
- Underline common challenges and show how 'we' help; translate practical examples into Roadmap/training/framework components.
- No direct calls to action (no 'let's grab a coffee'). It should simply be clear that we know our stuff; the audience is intelligent enough to find us.

Execution shift ('hoe anno 2020'):
- Stop MailMonkey/MMM (adds nothing); self-track traffic and possible leads, send the newsletter in-house.
- New logo, house style, and website.
- Build an ambassadors group (WhatsApp); a 'like' should always be allowed.
- Quarterly quotes as a new format.
- A more personal approach via LinkedIn; not 100% website-focused; supply ready-made text snippets.
- Repurpose content: re-share best-read articles ~6 months later (ICYMI, hot topic).
- More visualisations (in-house illustrator, Snappa); a professional template presentation for Salomon, Roadmap, and SCAF.
- LinkedIn campaigns around the Roadmap, trainings, and adoption framework; news posts when the framework and trainings/Masterclass are (nearly) ready.

Dated: tied to specific 2020 tooling and channel choices (MMM/MailMonkey, then-current website rebuild).
