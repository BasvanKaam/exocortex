# Position: a cost-aware organization is the ultimate goal of cloud governance

In the SCAF Adoption template Bas frames cost awareness not as a side benefit but as the explicit end goal of cloud governance: 'a cost-aware organization is the ultimate goal' ('een kostenbewustzijn organisatie is het ultieme doel'). The position translates into concrete, non-negotiable conditions: all cloud resources and their costs must be visible, ownership must be assigned to a person or team, cost tooling must be in use and mapped, and reporting must exist. The continuous-improvement logic backs it up - eliminate unused or under-utilised resources and automate repeating tasks precisely because they are unnecessary cost. Governance, agile working and FinOps-style cost control are treated as one fabric, not separate initiatives.
