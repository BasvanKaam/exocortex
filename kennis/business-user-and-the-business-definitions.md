# Bas's terminology: "gebruiker", "business gebruiker" and "de business"

Bas defines the vocabulary he uses throughout the book. The "gebruiker" (user) is the end-consumer of cloud services and IT solutions, the people who work with these technologies daily and experience the benefits (if all goes well). The "business gebruiker" (business user) often refers to the same group but in a slightly broader context, looking at the role those users play within their organisation.

When he says "de business" he means the people, processes and products that matter for setting and realising strategic business goals. The business is not an abstraction but consists of concrete roles: CEO (algemeen directeur), COO (operationeel directeur), CFO (financieel directeur), CTO (technisch directeur), plus sales, HR, marketing and department managers, together usually forming the management team (MT) or board.

He flags that a major theme of the book is how these groups collaborate, or fail to, the "gat" (gap) between the business and IT.
