# Take notes by hand: the pen beats the keyboard

Bas's durable habit and advice: write things down by hand. He cites studies that handwriting activates brain activity, improves recall, stimulates creativity and information processing, and develops visual-motor skill in ways a keyboard does not. His signature line: "the pen (still) is (and will always be) mightier than the keyboard." Paired with making daily/weekly/monthly lists ("To Do" or "Success" lists) for peace of mind and organization.
