# Lead enablement with hands-on labs, not slideware

Across every event format Bas designs, the hands-on lab is the point and the presentation is the setup. Openings and slide segments are deliberately short (10-45 minutes); the bulk of the time is people doing the work in a real environment. The more community-focused the audience, the more this holds: the Tech Insider format carries an explicit NO marketing rule and a 10-15 minute intro cap, because the moment a community session feels like a pitch, you lose the room. Marketing intensity should scale inversely with how community-driven the audience is.
