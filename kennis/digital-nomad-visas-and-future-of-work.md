# Digital-nomad visas and the future of work

More countries are catering to location-independent workers. Estonia, Portugal and Barbados, for example, offer special digital-nomad visas so nomads can legally stay longer. Cities are adapting too, with more coworking spaces, flexible housing and amenities to attract this group.

Bas's view of the future: it probably lies somewhere between fully remote and traditional office culture, with the emphasis shifting toward results rather than physical presence — less control, more trust. "As it should be, if you ask me."
