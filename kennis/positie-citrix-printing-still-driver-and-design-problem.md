# Most Citrix printing pain is still bad drivers and bad architecture

Stance (October 2015): after walking through years of print-driver evolution, Bas's verdict is that despite all the Microsoft improvements, 'not that much has changed.' Admins still face delayed logons and printing, crashing services, blue screens, CPU spikes and auto-creation failures. When those issues trace back to Citrix printing, in most cases the root cause is still faulty drivers and/or badly designed or judged print architecture - not the platform itself. His implicit position: printing problems are overwhelmingly self-inflicted through driver hygiene and design choices, which is why he keeps steering readers toward None/Shared isolation and sound architecture rather than blaming the stack.
