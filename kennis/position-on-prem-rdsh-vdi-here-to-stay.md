# On-prem RDSH/VDI is here to stay; hybrid is the way forward

Bas's stance (Inside Citrix, 2016, with 2018 updates): most companies will not move their entire on-prem datacentre into the cloud within 5-10 years, and his guess is the timeline is longer. Hybrid solutions and architectures are the way forward, especially for the virtual desktop/workplace. Start small and take it from there. The cloud will leak into how we build everything, but on-prem RDSH and VDI (FMA) infrastructures remain to manage and maintain. His 2018 verdict on his own 2016 prediction: still true.

Reasons companies stay on-prem or go partial: company-sensitive data location and access, apps that need to be near their data, reluctance to authenticate users "in the cloud", uptime/SLA/responsibility/performance concerns, fear of no exit strategy, unwillingness to hand infrastructure to a third party, internet connection becoming a single point of failure.

Good cloud fits even for the cautious: burst capacity and backup.

Why the knowledge still matters: even with Citrix Cloud, IaaS and PaaS leave you installing, configuring, managing and troubleshooting XenApp/XenDesktop, the OS, data and apps. Knowing how the components and services interact is exactly what makes hybrid and troubleshooting work. "The FMA is still very much alive."

*Bron: Inside Citrix (2016), 'Inside Citrix Ch3 - Is the FMA still relevant (cloud vs on-prem)'.*
