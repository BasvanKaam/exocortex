# Deliberately omitting Zero Trust product names

In the ch.10 security glossary of 'Cloud Mastery', Bas explicitly states he deliberately left out the many product and service names tied to the layered Zero Trust model, judging that listing them would be overkill. His recommendation: if a reader wants those specifics, look them up via Google, ChatGPT, or a similar platform by searching the product or service name. Signals his editorial preference for vendor-neutral, durable framing over a product catalog that dates quickly, and comfort pointing readers to AI tools for lookups.
