# Come Get IT Proposition and Contributor Pitch

The 'CGIT on tour' deck is the internal recruiting pitch for Come Get IT: a community initiative (blogs, events, podcasts) that Bas co-runs with Martijn Verheij and Sander Bruijs. Its purpose is to pull colleagues in as contributors.

## The pitch structure
Who we are, what we do, when we do it, how we organize it, what our goals are, what's in it for you, we need your help, roadmap and swag.

## The contributor value proposition (what's in it for you)
- Fun (side) projects alongside your 'normal' work.
- Your work gets published, which is genuinely cool.
- You get to know your colleagues in a different way.
- It contributes toward a possible community status (e.g. MVP-style recognition).
- You help build something good.
- It adds something to your CV.
- You learn something new.

## Ways to contribute
Topics, speakers, guests, writing, other suggestions.

## The idea, distilled
Come Get IT runs on volunteer contribution, so the deck sells the upside to the contributor (publication, profile, learning, community recognition) rather than to the organization. Recruit by making the personal payoff explicit.
