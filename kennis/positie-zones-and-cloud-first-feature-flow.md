# New FMA features land in Citrix Workspace Cloud first, then on-prem

In Jan 2016, Bas points out that Zones were already available in Citrix Workspace Cloud (CWC) before the on-prem 7.7 release, and generalizes: new XenApp/XenDesktop (FMA) features will first find their way into CWC before being built into the on-premises products. This is an early read of Citrix's cloud-first delivery model. He also predicts Connection Leasing will grow from 'nice to have' to important ('something tells me this will soon change'), and wishes for worker-group-style failover load balancing to come back applied to machine catalogs. His verdict on Zones: not the full XenApp 6.5 zones, but a real win because XenDesktop now has zones it never had. He is candid that he hadn't seen Zones 'in action' yet and corrects himself in the comments (e.g. agreeing with Carl Webster that per-zone admin restriction wasn't actually possible).
