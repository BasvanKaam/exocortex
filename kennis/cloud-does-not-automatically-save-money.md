# Moving to the cloud does not automatically save money

Bas opens his 30-tip AWS cost guide (Jan 2019) with a deliberately anti-hype stance: 'I will be the last one to tell or promise you that moving to the Cloud will save you money. It definitely could, but it's not a certainty by any means.' His position: if saving money is your only objective and short-term strategy, reconsider the move. The real value of cloud is the near-unlimited menu of services that let you get work done more efficiently, and the hard part is making the right decisions on what to use, where, and how to configure it. Cost savings come from active optimization (his 30 tips), not from migration alone.
