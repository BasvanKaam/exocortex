# LinkedIn newsletter series: snooker and my career

Series plan for a recurring theme inside the EUC News Nuggets newsletter on LinkedIn that maps snooker to work and career. Every edition does two things at once: it teaches the reader a piece of the game (a rule, a technique, how the pros think), and it maps that piece one-to-one to something in a career, with one concrete thing to try that week. Full BvK voice, English, see `standaarden/voice/voice-profile.md`.

Origin: first brainstormed in a separate chat session (not connected to this brain, so that output is lost unless Bas pastes it in). Rebuilt here on 2026-10-03.

## Format per edition
- Plain, direct opener in first person, no staged drama (see the 2026-07-14 lessons in `voice-corrections.md`).
- **The table:** one snooker element, explained so a non-player gets it (rule, shot, routine).
- **The mapping:** the same element in a career, grounded in Bas's own path where it is true and verified.
- **What would I do with this?** one thing to try this week.
- Sign-off "Thank you for reading. BvK." plus "Part of www.eucnewsnuggets.com", and a question back.
- Neutral for readers: no names of people (family, coaches, opponents, colleagues). Built on experience and results, both at the table and in the career.
- Results are welcome but modest: no award lists or title-flexing, no internal company numbers.
- Length: around 800 to 1100 words, a few minutes of reading.

## Editions (proposal, order open)
Each line: working title, what the reader learns about snooker, the career mapping, Bas's own material.

1. **The pot is not the point** (series opener). Snooker in two minutes, plus position play: every shot is a pot and a leave. Career: the task in front of you versus where it leaves you. Own material: start at 13 in IJmuiden, stopped around 20, back around 2021; helpdesk 1999 to Principal Learning Architect. Draft: [edition 01](01-the-pot-is-not-the-point.md).
2. **Colours round the table.** The boring routine every serious player does. Career: deliberate practice on fundamentals, the stuff nobody claps for. Own material: the training log (`../training/README.md`), 13+ years of self-study from helpdesk to engineer, 60+ certifications.
3. **The opponent is in the chair.** You cannot defend while the other player is at the table. Career: play your own frame, stop comparing. Own material: Engine 7 in the voice profile ("the opponent is always yourself").
4. **Safety first.** When not to go for the pot, and how a good safety wins frames. Career: patience, validate before you invest, "ship the six" as a probe. Own material: Q2 in the voice profile.
5. **Getting out of a snooker.** What being snookered means, cushions and swerves to escape. Career: constraints you did not choose, problem solving when the direct line is blocked.
6. **The foul and the missed ball.** Fouls give your opponent at least four points. Career: feedback is not negative, the label you put on it is. Own material: Q7 in the voice profile (sore loser, the reaction is the damage). Keep it about the lesson, not a confession.
7. **Concentration across a session.** Focus comes in blocks and drops when someone walks in for a chat. Career: deep work, protecting focus, accepting the dip. Own material: concentration notes in the training log (2026-10-02).
8. **Thinking three balls ahead.** Break-building and planning the route through the reds. Career: the 3 to 5 year horizon, start with the end in mind. Own material: Engine 2, the Citrix side-by-side ROI story (Q3).
9. **The first fifty, the first century.** Fast early progress, then the long plateau. Career: why the second year of anything feels slower than the first. Own material: first 50 after about six weeks, first century after eight or nine months (KNBB source in `../../bas-van-kaam-sport.md`).
10. **Coming back after twenty years.** What stays (the eye, the cue action) and what has to be rebuilt. Career: returning to a skill, re-skilling, it does not leave you.
11. **The team competition.** Snooker is individual, but the team competition is where you grow. Career: community as career engine. Own material: Dutch team titles (Super League x3), `../../positie-community-as-career-engine.md`. No club or player names.
12. **The coach and the first snooker school.** Learning from someone who has been where you are. Career: mentors, and later being one. Own material: having a coach early on (no name), "Don't imagine a beginner, remember being one."
13. **The 147.** The perfect break is rare, even for pros. Career: done is better than perfect. Own material: 147 twice in a line-up (practice routine), highest match break 123. Keep the difference between practice and match clear.
