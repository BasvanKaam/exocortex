# WVD with Nerdio on top is the new 'RDS with Citrix on top'

Bas's positioning of where Nerdio sits relative to native WVD and to Citrix. Native WVD on its own cannot offer what Citrix offers; native WVD combined with Nerdio can, in roughly 70% of cases (give or take) based on what he sees daily. He explicitly does not consider Nerdio a direct competitor to Citrix, but acknowledges customers often assume it is — before any conversation — and he understands why, from both a feature and a cost perspective.

His recurring framing: "Throughout the last 20 years we have had RDS with Citrix on top, today it's WVD with Nerdio on top." That doesn't mean WVD always wins — of course it doesn't. He also notes that Microsoft depends on partners like Nerdio to fill the gaps native WVD leaves (single sign-on, full Azure AD support, easier management than the ever-changing Azure portal), which he calls a win-win for both.
