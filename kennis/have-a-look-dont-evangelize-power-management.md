# Have a look, don't evangelize: measured stance on Power Management

At E2EVC Berlin (June 2015) Bas deliberately refused to oversell FMA Power Management. His explicit line: "we are not saying it's one of the best features ever or that as of now everybody needs to start using power management, no, just have a look and listen, that's all." He also sidestepped the well-trodden debates (persistent vs non-persistent, VDI vs virtual, physical vs virtual) on the grounds they had already been covered professionally to death, preferring to surface something less-trodden instead. This captures a recurring stance in his thinking: pick under-covered topics, present them honestly with pros AND cons, and let the audience decide rather than pushing hype. He even noted he had to pull a slide containing NDA roadmap info.
