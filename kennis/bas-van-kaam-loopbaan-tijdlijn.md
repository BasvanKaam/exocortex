# Bas van Kaam: career timeline (1999 to 2026)

The full professional arc, from helpdesk to Principal Learning Architect. Source of truth for dates, employers and role scope. Note the role evolution at Nerdio: he moved from Field CTO into Learning & Development.

## Current and Nerdio era
- **Principal Learning Architect, Nerdio (2026 to present).** Defines and leads end-to-end learning architecture for partner and customer success: scalable training ecosystems, AI-assisted content pipelines, instructional experience design (simulations, microlearning, labs), and Level 300 to 400 technical content. Aligns learning strategy with product innovation. Cloud Mastery book published (EN). eucnewsnuggets.com active.
- **Principal Technical Trainer, Nerdio (2025 to 2026).** Technical training delivery across EMEA, NME-200 certification curriculum, partner and customer enablement. Launched eucnewsnuggets.com.
- **Field CTO EMEA, Nerdio (2020 to 2025), transitioning to L&D in 2023 to 2024.** Joined June 2020 as the first employee outside the US and first foot on the ground in EMEA. Responsible for 100+ enterprise and MSP customers across EMEA, covering the region solo for the first three years; also Middle East, parts of Australia, and contributing to US operations. Helped drive European revenue from almost zero to $1M+ monthly recurring revenue (MRR). Owned sales, technical pre-sales, technical support, evangelism, partner management and business development. Signed 60+ MSP and enterprise partners and for multiple years managed the largest MSP partner in the entire Nerdio portfolio.

## Pre-Nerdio
- **Sr. Business Consultant / Sr. Cloud Technologist, Salomon IT Solutions (May 2018 to June 2020).** Developed the SCAF framework. Co-organized Come Get IT (Jan 2019). Co-founded EUCdigest (June 2019). Co-authored Byte-Sized Cloud Design Principles with Christiaan Brinkhoff.
- **Pre-Sales Director Northern EMEA, Liquidware (Oct 2017 to Apr 2018).** EMEA expansion across NL, Benelux and wider Europe. Founded and managed the Liquidware Tech Insider NL (LTI) programme.
- **Sr. Management Consultant, Technology Officer & Technology Board Member, Detron ICT Group (2015 to Oct 2017).** Computable expert columns, Elsevier interview, initiated the Detron Bloggers Borrel. Started as Citrix engineer/architect and left as Technology Officer, having written a book and become Citrix CTP.
- **Sr. Technical Consultant & Citrix Product Lead, Qwise (2012 to 2015).** Launched basvankaam.com (Jan 2013). First cheat sheet v1.0 (Nov 2014). FMA Internals Co-op with Mick Glover (Citrix Dublin).
- **Helpdesk Support Technician to Sr. Citrix System Engineer (1999 to 2012).** At Qmagic, KPN, Zorgbalans, Caase.com. Self-study driven progression over 13+ years.

## Headline framing (his own page)
- Principal Learning Architect @ Nerdio, Velserbroek, The Netherlands, born 03-02-1979.
- 26+ years in IT, 25+ in this segment. 51 LinkedIn recommendations received independently across two decades and a dozen companies.
