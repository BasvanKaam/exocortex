# EUC News Nuggets: platform pivot from basvankaam.com

After almost 16 years on basvankaam.com, Bas moved his activities to a new platform, www.eucnewsnuggets.com, focused on microlearning ("nugget") style content: PDFs, cheat sheets, and a mini-podcast (EUC News Nuggets Podcast). Episodes are kept to 5-10 minutes max.

The content scope deliberately goes beyond the usual AVD, Nerdio, Windows 365, Intune, FSLogix setups to also cover real-world challenges: migrations, architecture, the new business reality, adoption frameworks, business cases, the gap between IT and "the business", Cloud Center of Excellence, the cloud economy, delivery models, and governance.

He created an AI buddy, "Finn Morgan", to narrate most podcast episodes using AI voices; Bas still does all research, writing and content, and joins Finn occasionally. See also his Step-Cloud Adoption Framework (SCAF) work.

*Bron: Inside Citrix (2016), 'EUC News Nuggets platform launch'.*
