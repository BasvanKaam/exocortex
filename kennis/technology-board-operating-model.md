# Technology Board: An Advisory-Body Operating Model

A durable governance model Bas designed and chaired at Detron (as Technology Officer). Useful as a reusable pattern for running a technical advisory body.

**Structure**
- Meets every two weeks, alternating virtual (Skype) and physical (Veenendaal HQ); physical days alternate Tuesday/Wednesday so all members can attend enough sessions across the year.
- ~8 ICT architects and consultants drawn from different business units and expertises, so every unit is represented and the customer question can be approached integrally.
- Chaired by Bas van Kaam, Technology Officer.
- Split into 4 work domains: **werkplek (workspace), connectivity, security, on-premises/cloud infrastructure**. Each domain has a lead plus a second architect/consultant.
- Deliberately kept small to protect progress. Originally 12 members; the "extra" members are now pulled in on-demand for specific projects.
- Members think and debate across domains, not just their own.
- "Sponsors" from Detron directorates are assigned to the domains (themselves part of the Solutions Board); their job is to support and facilitate board members.

**Purpose and flow**
- Each domain produces roadmap plans, derived from Detron's overall vision (the "visie 2020" presentation).
- The board closely tracks technological developments in the market and at vendors, and tracks customers' technical questions, then brings the two together and decides *if* and *how* to fold new developments into the portfolio.
- It acts as an **advisory body to the Solutions Board**, which gives the eventual go / no-go.
- Members also feed content into blogs, white papers and reference cases, speak at conferences, and are positioned as authorities in their field, demonstrating Detron's trusted-advisor status in IT and Telecom.

The reusable idea: a small cross-BU board, organized by work domain, that systematically reconciles market/vendor trends with customer demand and feeds both portfolio decisions and thought-leadership output.
