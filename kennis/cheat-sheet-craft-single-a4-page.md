# Stance: keep the comparison cheat sheet to a single A4 page

In this Feb 2017 release of v3.0, Bas revealed a deliberate design constraint that shaped his recurring cheat sheets: keep it to a single A4 page for as long as possible. To make v3.0 fit he deleted some older data for the first time and shrank the font from 7.5 to 7 to free up space. He was also explicit about the limits of the format: 'this is by no means meant as an exclusive list' and he could 'never be able to include all changes, features, and so on of every product.' It shows his maker mindset, ongoing maintenance commitment, and honesty about a reference's boundaries.
