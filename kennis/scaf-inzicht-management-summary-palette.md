# SCAF Insight-phase management summary palette

A supporting-documentation template for the Insight (Inzicht) phase of the SCAF (Step-Cloud Adoptie Framework). A one-page management summary "palette" capturing the discovery/assessment outcomes against fixed checkpoints:

- Results of Wave 1 and 2, including a first estimate of whether to move to cloud and/or modernize.
- Security and network components / initiatives.
- License inventory (yes/no) and notable findings.
- A Go / No-Go moment, with an optional compact business case.
- Knowledge overview: teams, members, and GAPS.
- Governance model plus relevant processes.
- Supporting tools and other information.
- Conclusion / summary.

This artifact turns the SCAF Insight phase into a reportable management one-pager, including the wave-based assessment, the Go/No-Go gate and the GAP-based knowledge inventory; the framework itself is covered in the book.
