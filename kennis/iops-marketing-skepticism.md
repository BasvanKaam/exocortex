# Be skeptical of any vendor's headline IOPS number

Bas's recurring stance, distilled in this 2014 IOPS guide: a big IOPS figure on its own is marketing noise. 'When a random vendor tells you that their storage appliance is capable of doing an crazy high number of IOPS you probably have a few questions to ask them, right?!' He insists on the three golden parameters (latency, read/write ratio, block size) and on knowing how they tested. His core conviction: 'Latency is king, the less you have the faster your infrastructure will be.' Early evidence of his consultant instinct to push back on vendor claims and force real test methodology into the open. Notable that he frames himself modestly as 'no expert' even while writing a definitive primer.
