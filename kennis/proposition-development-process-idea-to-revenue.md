# Proposition Development Process (Idea to Revenue)

A lightweight product/service development process Bas designed and owned at Detron ("technisch beleid"), to take an idea from first sketch to a fully worked-out, revenue-generating proposition ("van idee tot omzet"). Reusable framing for any tech venture deciding what to build.

Two purposes:
1. Support the people forming and developing new ideas/propositions (or adapting existing services).
2. Make explicit which phases/steps are run, what the points of attention are, and what deliverables are expected. Important caveat baked in: not every step always applies. The process is a guide, not a gate. "Lang niet altijd iedere stap van toepassing zal zijn."

Structure (five artifacts, in reading/use order):
1. Process: Proposition Development - high-level overview of the steps from idea to finished proposition. A technology board advises a solutions board; the advice report is one of the most important deliverables.
2. Process: Product/Partner Inventory and Selection - sub-steps to arrive at a shortlist of products/partners to fill in the service.
3. Form: Product/Partner Advice Report - the draft proposition submitted to the decision board for judgment; filled in as completely as possible.
4. Form: Proposition Idea Form - short and crisp first capture of an idea. Explicitly designed to PREVENT a sprawl of ideas ("wildgroei aan ideeen") by forcing a quick first impression before the group decides whether to pursue.
5. Form: Partner/Product Feedback Form - quick capture of first impressions after meeting a product/partner.

Organizational model: a Technology Board split across work domains (Workspace, Connectivity, Security, Cloud), each with members and a team lead, gathers and shapes information per domain. Output is an advice report to the Solutions Board, which decides whether the service may be developed further.

Keepable principle: govern idea flow with tiny standardized forms up front so the team can triage cheaply, and keep the heavy process optional.
