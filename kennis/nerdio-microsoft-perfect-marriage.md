# Nerdio and Microsoft: a perfect marriage, not a competitor

Bas's foundational positioning of the Nerdio-Microsoft relationship: Nerdio does not compete with WVD, it innovates on top of it.

"Microsoft is doing an excellent job with WVD. They are constantly improving the core technology... With Nerdio Manager for WVD we continue to do the same, taking it a few steps further even, innovating on top of WVD, a perfect marriage, both companies work closely together."

The complementary framing: Microsoft owns and improves the core WVD service; Nerdio adds the automation, management, and optimization layer on top. He stresses customer feedback drives the roadmap. This 'on top of Microsoft' stance recurs throughout his Nerdio-era content and is one of his signature talking points.
