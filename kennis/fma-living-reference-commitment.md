# Keeping a living, NDA-bounded FMA reference

December 2016. This post shows his approach to authority-building content: rather than a one-off, he maintains a single canonical FMA services overview (graphical diagram included) that he updates with every release 'as soon as NDA has been lifted of course.' Much of the content overlaps with his book, which he references openly.

His framing reveals how he positions himself: a careful insider who respects NDA boundaries ('Unfortunately I can't share any additional information as all this is still under NDA. Just know that the services are already there, just not activated'), who builds in public, and who credits collaborators by name (Martin Zugec, and Mick Glover / XDtipster, whose lines like 'the Configuration Service is the glue holding the FMA together' he quotes and re-uses). The signature 'To be continued...' sign-off frames his blog as an ongoing, evolving body of work rather than discrete articles.
