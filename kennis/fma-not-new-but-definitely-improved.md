# FMA is not new technology, but it is definitely improved (May 2013)

Bas deflates the marketing hype while still endorsing the change: "Is it new technology? No. Is it improved? Definitely!" FMA already existed in earlier XenDesktop releases; XD7 reuses and extends it (FMA 2.0) primarily on the XenApp integration and server-agent side. He's also relaxed about IMA's death: he expects IMA "will probably be around for many more years to come" in deployed XenApp, while "it's all FMA from now on when it comes to future developments", and welcomes XenApp 6.5 FP2 extending XenApp's life as "a good thing." His instinct to puncture overblown launch claims ('One of the biggest releases in years! Of-course I'm just kidding') is a consistent part of his voice.
