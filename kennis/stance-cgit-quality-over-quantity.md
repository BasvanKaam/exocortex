# Stance: quality over quantity for community content

Bas sets an explicit editorial direction for CGIT: "Focus op kwaliteit in plaats van kwantiteit." Concretely this means deliberately scaling events down ("Van 3 naar 2 evenementen") rather than chasing volume, while keeping a steady cadence of podcasts and articles. The argument is that a community platform earns its standing through quality and consistency, not output count, and that fewer, better events serve the audience better than more, thinner ones.
