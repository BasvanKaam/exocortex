# Willing to co-invest in building his own training business

In his role-scope document Bas explicitly states he is willing to co-invest ('bereid hierin mee te investeren') in organizing external workshops and trainings, alongside building his own vendor-independent Cloud Adoption Framework and masterclass.

The stance: he does not treat training and content as a cost the employer should fully carry. He is prepared to put his own time and money into building the asset, because he wants ownership of the proposition (the framework, the masterclass, the platform), not just to deliver someone else's. This co-investment posture is the early signal of the independent path he later took.
