# Citrix App Layering's reputation problem (2018)

Noted plainly at E2EVC Amsterdam (June 2018) during Jack Cobben's session: 'Citrix App Layering does not have the best reputation when it comes to speed, flexibility and making things easy for its end-users, the administrators in this case.' The audience pushed back with tough questions during the demo. Bas reports this as accepted community sentiment, not just his own view, and it dovetails with his repeated preference for Liquidware FlexApp on usability grounds. He's still fair to the presenter (praises Jack for standing his ground on his first E2EVC talk), separating the product critique from the person.
