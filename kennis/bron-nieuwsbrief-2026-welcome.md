# Newsletter: Welcome and introduction

Separate welcome post, the day after the newsletter went live (around 9 June 2026). Thanks the 600+ subscribers from the first 12 hours, explains what the newsletter is, how often it comes, and how he uses AI.

## Core points
- Worst case you unsubscribe after a few short reads; best case you learn something new without it costing much time.
- AI use: "if the idea, the creative part is human, we are good". This welcome itself was written without AI.
- Cadence: sometimes 2 to 3 per week, sometimes none; next to daily LinkedIn posts since March 2026. "Quality for me, not quantity."
- Format per nugget: a cheat sheet works best horizontal on a screen, other topics land better in your ears on the move.
- Assumes practitioner knowledge to respect the reader's time.
- Audio around ten minutes, on the site, Spotify and Apple Podcasts; more platforms in progress.
