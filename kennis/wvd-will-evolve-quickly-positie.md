# WVD will evolve quickly because it's cloud-native

In April 2019, while WVD was still in preview, Bas predicted it would evolve fast precisely because it is cloud-native: 'Since it's Cloud native, I'm expecting it to evolve rather quickly.' He framed the feature gaps not as a flaw but as the natural starting point of a new product with a roadmap, and as the opening for partners to add value. He also stressed Microsoft's openness to feedback as a real lever: 'if we keep quiet nothing will happen, that's for sure.'
