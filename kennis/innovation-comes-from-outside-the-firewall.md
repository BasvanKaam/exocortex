# Innovation Comes From Outside the Firewall

A Bas reframe, used as a chapter motto: 'Innovatie komt van buiten de Firewall.'

His reasoning, from the provider's perspective: in a subscription/service model the customer becomes even more important, because you don't keep paying for a service you're unhappy with. See it as a way to get and stay closer to your customer — a continued, long-term relationship rather than a one-off project. To pull this off, the provider must overcome a number of obstacles.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> Innovatie komt van buiten de Firewall.
