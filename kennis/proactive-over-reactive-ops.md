# Reboots are a necessary evil; handle them proactively

Bas's operations stance (Aug 2014): reboots are unavoidable. App-V and PVS cache data that should be cleared periodically, and a physically installed Windows OS needs a refresh "every once in awhile, which we all know it does." He frames the responsible admin as one who works "proactive as apposed to reactive because in most cases reactive will mean that the damage is already done."

He also models the community-first humility that recurges in his writing: when Citrix Blogs published a similar reboot post the same week, he linked it rather than competing, and credited community scripters Shaun Ritchie and Carl Webster.

*Bron: blogpost 'How to: Rebooting your XenDesktop 7.x application servers.' (2014-08-19), basvankaam.com.*
