# Eventually we all migrate; it's only a question of when

April 2015. Bas argued it might be time to move XenApp 6.5 IMA setups onto FMA-based 7.6, choosing a deliberately optimistic 'glass is half full' framing and refusing to dwell on features still missing from FMA: "As the title implies I'm trying to take a positive approach here, so I won't focus on any of the features (still) left out." His core conviction: "Eventually we will all migrate, it's only a question of when... FMA will be the way forward!"

He is intellectually honest about the rhetorical stance, repeatedly winking at it ('Note that I'm trying to be the glass is half full guy here') and inviting pushback ('I'm sure you'll find some of my arguments a bit too far-fetched or perhaps plain stupid even, and that's fine'). His closing line captures the balance: "Xenapp 6.5 and IMA were great, but don't hold off on change." A clear positive-but-honest migration call rather than vendor cheerleading, anchored by approaching 2016 end-of-life dates.
