# Cost control is a short-sighted reason to go to cloud

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

the framework takes an explicit stance on motivation: getting a grip on costs and optimizing expenditure is an important motive for moving to the cloud, but he qualifies it as 'somewhat short-sighted'. The financial analysis exists to support the real goal, selecting the optimum landing site per application, not to chase savings for their own sake.

His position is that a cloud strategy can carry objectives that cannot be quantified immediately yet are the major drivers for the transition; you represent these with weights in the financial model rather than letting the spreadsheet's hard numbers be the only voice in the decision.
