# Just start writing, the topic and quality come by doing

Bas's stance on the biggest blocker for new writers: you don't need an original topic, and you don't need to know exactly what you'll write before you begin. Just start. Every covered topic is fair game to redo in your own voice, because readers come for your perspective, style and explanation, not for novelty. He admits he struggled with this early on, always hunting for an original subject; it worked, but it was unnecessary. Writing is a practice you improve by repetition: he writes something almost every day, and even writing to throw away is never wasted because the more you write the more you learn.

This is the operational, slightly more demanding flip side of his 'I'm no guru' philosophy: the antidote to the blank page is action, not inspiration.
