# Troubleshooting tip #1: document what you've done

Bas's number-one troubleshooting rule, framed from his own experience: always document what you've tried. Sessions spiral fast across CTX articles, KB pages, forums, Twitter, LinkedIn and interfering colleagues until you lose track of what's been done.

Practical heuristic: if an issue isn't fixed within two to three hours, or you haven't found a likely solution, stop and write it down: steps taken, articles/KBs applied (with dates), and which coworkers helped. This lets you resume where you left off, onboard colleagues, update your boss, and open a support case with full context. Durable operations discipline, independent of product version.
