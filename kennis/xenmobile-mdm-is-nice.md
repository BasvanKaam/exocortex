# Positive first take on XenMobile MDM (March 2013)

Writing in March 2013, just after Citrix's Zenprise acquisition produced XenMobile, Bas is clearly enthusiastic: the title itself says "and It's nice!" He calls the granular policy control "powerful tooling" and notes "there is very little you can't control using policies." He flags the Samsung restrictions as a good example of taking "total control" of a device, but adds his own judgement that locking machines down that hard "doesn't really say come and bring your own device if you ask me" - an early, balanced read on the BYOD-vs-control tension. He treats Citrix demo environments as the best way to explore new products and recommends partners use them.
