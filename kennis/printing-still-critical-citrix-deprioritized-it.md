# Printing is still critical; Citrix deprioritized it for cloud

Bas's take (Jan 2019): 'Printing needs to be taken more seriously', not just by Citrix. It remains a critical service for many users 'today, and for many more years to come' ('We all know how that paperless office idea worked out, right?'). He reads Citrix's 2018 printing stagnation not as printing being solved or unimportant, but as priorities shifting to cloud, pushing printing down the list. He notes third parties (UniPrint, Tricerat) kept innovating and wishes some of that were built into Citrix natively, 'but I guess that ship has sailed already'. On WVD: Microsoft has a poor printing reputation ('Citrix is way ahead'), so he expects MS to buy or partner with a third party; ThinPrint is a named WVD partner, but he argues people want options.
