# Top-Line vs Bottom-Line Cloud Drivers

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

Every cloud investment is driven by one of two motives, and naming which one matters for strategy:

- Top line: investments aimed at driving revenue through innovation and increasing the organization's innovative capabilities.
- Bottom line: investments aimed at driving operational costs down, typically by leveraging cloud automation capabilities.

Strategic drivers should be derived from the organization's ICT Vision and linked to the strategic elements in the Strategy Map. Generic top-line/bottom-line examples give a first impression, but the actual drivers must be identified for the specific organizational context.

Maturity signal: in traditional organizations the majority of cloud migrations address the bottom line and only a few address the top line. As cloud maturity rises, you should expect to see more top-line-focused initiatives.
