# Stance: lead with the pitfalls instead of routing around them

For the Bloggers Borrel / roundtable topic 'De cloud, het is niet alles goud wat er blinkt', the explicit instruction is that pitfalls are usually avoided, so make them the subject. Go into conversation with the audience, prepare questions, learn from each other, and discuss both pros and cons.

The stance generalises: honesty about trade-offs is the differentiator. Most marketing hides the downsides; he wants to lead with them and turn the candor into trust.
