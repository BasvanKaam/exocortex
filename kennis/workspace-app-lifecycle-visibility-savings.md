# Workspace/application lifecycle visibility uncovers waste and unmanaged machines

Durable point (April 2018, hospital context): most organizations, even in 2018, lack a clear picture of their application landscape and workspace estate, which machines are used heavily, lightly or not at all, and physically where they sit. Remote management increased efficiency but caused admins to overlook unmanaged, unattended, unmonitored machines tucked away in corners, a security risk as well as a cost one.

By inventorying and monitoring resources (Bas's example uses Liquidware Stratusphere UX), you surface rarely-used machines, OSes and applications and can phase out PCs and uninstall apps. In large US hospitals this saved clients tens of thousands of dollars per month/year across hardware depreciation, OS and application licenses, IT tooling (monitoring, distribution, security, patching), staff hours, power and cooling, while freeing admin time. Lifecycle management is often a neglected discipline that pays off directly.
