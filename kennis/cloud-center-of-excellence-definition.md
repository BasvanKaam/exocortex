# Cloud Center of Excellence (CCoE): Definition and Why Early

A Cloud Center of Excellence (CCoE) is a multidisciplinary team responsible for developing, implementing and managing cloud strategies, best practices and governance. It is a central point bringing together cloud knowledge and expertise, leading the migration and ensuring cloud initiatives are executed in a secure, cost-efficient and strategically sound way. Bas introduces it during the SCAF strategy/transition discussion because of its strategic place in the organisation, and revisits it in chapter 9 (Data and governance).

Bas argues for composing a CCoE early, for several reasons:
- **Strategic direction and coordination:** a consistent, centralised approach keeping all cloud initiatives aligned with business goals.
- **Governance and best practices:** clear guidelines for security, compliance and cost efficiency, preventing teams from developing their own (possibly conflicting) initiatives.
- **Risk control:** mapping risks early so security, compliance and risk management are built into the strategy, minimising later problems.
- **Guiding the organisation:** facilitating training and knowledge sharing to prevent a fragmented migration.
- **Stimulating innovation:** introducing new cloud technologies for accelerated innovation.
