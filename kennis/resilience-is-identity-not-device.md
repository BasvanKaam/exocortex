# Resilience is about identity, not the device

Bas's stance, crystallized around Windows 365 Reserve: "resilience is no longer about the device. It's about the identity." Once a user can reach a fully preconfigured Cloud PC from any borrowed device with just credentials and an internet connection, the endpoint stops being the thing you protect. The continuity guarantee moves to the identity layer. This underpins his broader argument that cloud workspace strategy should be designed around the person and their access, not the hardware in their hands.
