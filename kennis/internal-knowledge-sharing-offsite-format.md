# Internal Knowledge-Sharing Offsite Format

Bas's format idea for structured internal knowledge sharing, primarily aimed at Salomon (Avensus possibly later). Structure:

- Two full on-location days, taking time off at the client; investment carried by Abram.
- A Friday-afternoon-to-Saturday-afternoon session including an overnight stay.

First-session content ideas:
- "What do you run into most during your assignments? Give three examples" as an opener to surface real, recurring field problems.
- A session by Eric.
- A technical update session by Bas: what's hot, what's not, trends, the why and how. Not a deep-dive unless people want to go deeper.

The idea captures a lightweight, recurring team-learning ritual built around real assignment experience plus a curated trends briefing.
