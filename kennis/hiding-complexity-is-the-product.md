# Framing: the product's job is hiding complexity

June 2020. Bas's pitch crystallizes into a headline: 'It’s all about hiding complexity.' His promise: 'Nerdio makes deploying, managing and maintaining Windows Virtual Desktop a Breeze - That’s a promise.' He positions NMW as innovating 'on top of the great work done by Microsoft' and explicitly 'a few steps further.' This is the same innovate-on-top-of-Microsoft, hide-the-complexity thesis from his Nerdio joining post, now sharpened into a repeatable demo narrative (start with the basics, show how easy it is). It becomes his durable Nerdio messaging pattern.
