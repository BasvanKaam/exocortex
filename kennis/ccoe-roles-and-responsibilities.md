# CCoE Roles and Responsibilities

Possible roles in a Cloud Center of Excellence:

1. **Cloud Architect** — designs the cloud environment/architecture; leads the technical side of migration and translates current infrastructure into cloud architecture.
2. **Cloud Operations Manager** — manages daily cloud activities; ensures smooth operations, scalability, monitoring, backups and performance optimisation.
3. **Security Architect (CISO or Security Engineer)** — secures the cloud environment; develops and implements a cloud security framework, access control and encryption, ensuring compliance.
4. **DevOps Engineer** — automates cloud processes; integrates DevOps principles, CI/CD and collaboration between development and operations.
5. **IT Infrastructure Manager** — manages existing on-premises infrastructure; helps evaluate which parts move to cloud and ensures integration.
6. **Business Analyst** — translates business needs into cloud solutions; defines KPIs and success criteria; bridges the IT-business gap.
7. **Compliance Officer** — ensures legal/regulatory compliance (e.g. GDPR), privacy and data security.
8. **Finance Manager (CFO or Financial Analyst)** — manages cloud costs and budgets; runs TCO analyses and identifies savings, ensuring cost control.
9. **Project Manager** — manages planning and execution, deadlines, resources and progress; coordinates teams and risk resolution.
10. **Executive Sponsor (CTO, CIO or CEO)** — provides strategic leadership and high-level decision-making; approves investments and budgets, aligning initiatives with the broader business strategy.
11. **Change Manager** — guides the organisation through change, ensuring acceptance of new technologies and a change-management plan.

**Optional roles** (depending on size/complexity): Data Architect/Data Engineer (data and data-model migration), Application Architect (migrating and optimising cloud-native applications), Agile Coach/Scrum Master (applying agile/DevOps methods during migration).
