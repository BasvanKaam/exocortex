# Performance & Health Assessment (Salomon method)

A Salomon IT Solutions service offering / propositie for organizations whose end users hit unexpected problems and slowdowns that are hard to pin down quickly, and who want insight into where the bottlenecks sit.

It is framed as a nulmeting (baseline measurement). Primary goal: make infrastructure performance visible and positively influence the user experience where possible, separating low-hanging fruit from longer-term improvements.

Starting questions: how loaded are the various infrastructure components? Which bottlenecks exist? What is the 'health' of the infrastructure? And, expressed in objective numbers, how do end users experience system performance?

Method: a short workshop sets focus, pain points, and questions to answer. Results are delivered in a report that translates the binary/measurement data into graphical overviews, plus an interactive closing presentation (slotpresentatie). The report covers: answers to the posed questions; component load; bottlenecks; infrastructure health; user-experienced performance in objective numbers; a high-level overview of the IT landscape; a performance analysis; the Top 10 critical systems/volumes/connections; and recommendations.

Positioning: no surprises, no unexpected costs. Tagline: 'wij geven het inzicht, u maakt de juiste keuzes!'

The distinctive idea across Salomon's assessment line is quantifying end-user experience in objective numbers rather than relying on anecdote.

Note: corporate Salomon marketing material (contact Eric Kuiper), not BvK personal voice. Captured as a method/propositie for reference.
