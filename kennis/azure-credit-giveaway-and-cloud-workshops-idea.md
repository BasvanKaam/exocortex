# Idea: give away Azure credit and run experience-based cloud workshops

A low-friction lead and goodwill play: give away Azure credit, share knowledge for free, and offer no-strings advisory time. The pitch pairs this with a discount for working-from-home and with cloud workshops grounded in real-world experience rather than slideware.

The credibility behind the workshop offer: founder of and active in several IT communities, member of technical-content groups, twenty years across ICT from helpdesk engineer to CTO and senior business consultant, 5+ years as enterprise/workplace architect, hundreds of technical articles and blogs, two books plus e-books and cheat sheets, national and international speaker, and the leading certifications across Citrix, Microsoft, Amazon, TOGAF, ITIL, PRINCE2, and Scrum.

The underlying mechanic is reusable: lead with generosity (free credit, free knowledge, free time) to open the door, then convert through experience-led workshops.
