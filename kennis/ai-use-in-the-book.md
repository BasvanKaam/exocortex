# How AI was used in writing the book

Bas is transparent about his (limited) use of AI in producing the book, and marks the relevant passages with a dedicated icon for text that is 80%+ AI-written, found mainly in the per-chapter Key Takeaways.

Specifics:
- ChatGPT was used to compile the key takeaways of each chapter, helping him formulate the main points clearly and concisely.
- The references and glossary (begrippenlijsten) were assembled with ChatGPT's support.
- Two images were generated with DALL-E via ChatGPT.
- The "Artificial Intelligence" explanatory section itself was written by ChatGPT.

He also tried Microsoft Copilot in Word, but judged its results not close to ChatGPT's quality and usability. He frames the result as a nice balance between human input and the power of AI as a tool, and pre-emptively apologises for any AI-introduced errors despite his checking.
