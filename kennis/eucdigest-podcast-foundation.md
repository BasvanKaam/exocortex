# EUCdigest podcast and foundation

EUCdigest is a Dutch non-profit foundation (stichting), registered with the Kamer van Koophandel, that Bas van Kaam co-founded in June 2019 with Eric van Klaveren and Jits Langedijk; the three are board members. The advisory board and core podcast crew also included Kees Baggerman, Johan van Amersfoort, and Ingmar Verheij.

The podcast launched its first episode later in 2019 and ran a regular series (episodes 1-21 by the 2020 recap) plus extra episodes (e.g. Citrix Summit 2020, Login VSI VDI Performance Summit 2020), covering EUC topics: remoting-protocol wars, Windows Virtual Desktop, IGEL and community, working from home, AI, AR/XR, the pandemic's impact on EUC, GPU acceleration, and avoiding common VDI/DaaS mistakes.

Biographical/community context, not technical reference.
