# EUCdigest podcast and foundation

EUCdigest is a Dutch non-profit foundation (stichting), registered with the Kamer van Koophandel, that Bas van Kaam co-founded in June 2019 with Eric van Klaveren and Jits Langedijk; the three are board members. The advisory board and core podcast crew also included Kees Baggerman, Johan van Amersfoort, and Ingmar Verheij.

The podcast launched its first episode in January 2020 and ran a full Season 1 of 21 episodes through the December 2020 recap, plus two extra episodes (Citrix Summit 2020, Login VSI VDI Performance Summit 2020), covering EUC topics: remoting-protocol wars, Windows Virtual Desktop, IGEL and community, working from home, AI, AR/XR, the pandemic's impact on EUC, GPU acceleration, and avoiding common VDI/DaaS mistakes. The series continued into a **Season 2 in 2021** (with a recurring "ThrowDown" format, e.g. S02E03 on 2 Mar 2021); a 2026 web sweep confirmed the run extends beyond the original 21 episodes. Per the eucnewsnuggets dashboard the total reached 87 episodes across 3 seasons (S01 21, S02 13, S03 53, 2020 to Jan 2023).

Biographical/community context, not technical reference.
