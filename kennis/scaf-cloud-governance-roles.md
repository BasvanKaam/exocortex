# SCAF Cloud Governance Roles

In the Execution phase the governance model analysed during Insight is applied, or a new one is implemented if none exists. It clarifies who is responsible for which processes, decisions and security measures in the Cloud environment. For each role Bas records what it entails, which process belongs to it, and who is responsible. Common roles:

- Cost control (Kostenbeheersing): monitoring and managing Cloud resource costs, spotting deviations, optimising. Owner: finance department or a designated Cloud-cost manager.
- Security policy (Beveiligingsbeleid): maintaining and updating security policy for Cloud services, apps and data. Owner: Security Officer or IT security team.
- Platform and automation: managing and automating Cloud platforms for consistency and efficiency (auto scale up/down, applying updates). Owner: platform team or DevOps team.
- Continuity (Continuïteit): ensuring availability and drawing up contingency plans; disaster recovery and back-ups. Owner: IT operations or business continuity manager.
- Ambassadors (Ambassadeurs): promoting Cloud use, training and supporting users, being the point of contact. Owner: IT advisors or a dedicated Cloud-adoption team / internal sponsors.
- Incident, problem and change management: handling incidents/problems and implementing changes without disruption. Owner: service desk or IT management team.

A visual governance overview helps show how roles, processes and interdependencies hang together. Supporting processes are documented with a process-flowchart template (Excel) and a process-description template (Word). Governance policy and risk analysis tie in via impact/risk analysis (likelihood and impact from very high to low) and policies are periodically revised under Plan-Do-Check-Act.
