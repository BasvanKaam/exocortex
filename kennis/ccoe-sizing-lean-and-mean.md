# CCoE sizing: keep it lean and mean

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

The sizing of the CCoE depends on the actual workload. Because the CCoE is a support team and considered overhead by some, it should be kept lean and mean. A properly functioning team can be as lean as 4 FTE-equivalents or as large as 20 people. Some complex organizations in fact need multiple CCoE teams in different parts of the organization.

**Way of working.** If the organization pursues an agile way of working, the CCoE should work in an agile manner and align with the sprint frequency of the teams it serves; in other scenarios it can determine its own cadence. For organizations using the Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe), the framework provides additional guidance on aligning the CCoE with SAFe.
