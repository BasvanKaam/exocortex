# Two types of CCoE: Advisory vs Prescriptive

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

The framework distinguishes two types of Cloud Center of Expertise based on the organization's maturity:

- **Advisory.** Suited to organizations with a somewhat higher level of maturity. For this type, interactions are defined between roles, with other processes, and with the use of technology.
- **Prescriptive.** The implied counterpart for lower-maturity organizations that need more directive guidance.

The choice between them is a function of where the organization sits on its cloud-maturity curve, consistent with the TOM's principle of maturing incrementally rather than demanding a perfect end-state from day one.
