# Converged infrastructure will displace SAN-centric design

In February 2014 Bas made an early, confident bet that converged/software-defined infrastructure was the coming shift, not a fad. His argument: the hyperscalers (Amazon, Google, Facebook, Microsoft) had embraced and effectively invented these unified architectures, so "the best practices we once knew will soon become obsolete and known as 'just' practices, the way we once did stuff." He was careful not to overclaim: SANs would not vanish overnight, they are too expensive to throw away and still usable in most cases ("I do like em, really"). But as a Citrix engineer chasing massive IOPS, low latency, flexibility, scalability and ease of management, he saw companies like PernixData, Atlantis and Nutanix as the way to overcome those challenges, and "change is coming and it's approaching fast!" Note the meta-honesty: he flagged he was still in the research phase on Nutanix and would not bluff the technical detail.
