# Salomon's Assessment-to-Roadmap Model

The service proposition behind Salomon IT, as drafted for the website.

The pipeline: Assessment -> Roadmap Design -> Roadmap Management (Roadmap as a Service) -> Ontzorgen (long-term partnership). Each stage feeds the next.

Assessment: "meten is weten." Salomon delivers assessments in and around IT. "In IT" = the technical components that let the end user work efficiently (infrastructure, the workplace whether physical or virtual, cloud components, XaaS). "Around IT" = the factors that shape technical choices: GDPR/AVG legislation, IT policy and strategy. Bringing both worlds together produces a realistic picture so the customer can make grounded choices, define projects on solid footing, execute quick wins fast, and plan short and long term.

Roadmap Design: long-term vision + concrete short-term projects + quick wins. But who guards progress afterward?

Roadmap Management / Roadmap as a Service: Salomon takes over orchestration of the roadmap and guarantees the agreed goals are reached within a pre-set budget, without sitting in the chair of the IT vendors. It works on behalf of the client alongside the internal IT team and existing IT partners, checking at multiple moments whether goals are being met and course-correcting with small sprints when needed.

The differentiator: Salomon analyzes the IT organization from the end user's perspective — measuring how satisfied, productive and efficient employees actually are and whether their IT resources suffice. This EUC/user-experience lens is the wedge that separates it from generic IT consulting.

The adagium: "Wij geven het inzicht, jij maakt de juiste keuze." (We provide the insight, you make the right choice.) Choices based on facts from the client's own organization; actions based on Salomon's market knowledge and on understanding the organization's real driver.
