# Firm believer in choice: spread options, but cap the platform count

A personal philosophy Bas states plainly: 'I am a firm believer in choice, I don't believe in a one size fits all solution. I'd rather spread my options.' Applied to IoT platforms he advises a cloud-first approach for scalability (handy for analytics and machine learning) and recommends evaluating multiple platforms, but with discipline: adopt no more than two, maybe three at most, because IT (and IoT especially) is complex enough already. As a Citrix CTP he name-checks Citrix Octoblu (SaaS in Citrix Cloud, on-prem private cloud, or edge/mesh, runnable on Intel/Dell/HPE gateways) alongside Azure and AWS, signalling vendor openness even while flagging his own affiliation with a wink. The position generalises beyond IoT: deliberate optionality over lock-in, balanced against complexity cost.
