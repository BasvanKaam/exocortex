# CGIT 'Come Get IT On Tour' as a Community Flywheel

Bas ran a repeatable roadshow format under the Come Get IT (CGIT) banner: visit a partner or business unit (SBC, Client ICT in Weert, Avensus in Almere), run a session, and let it seed connections. The mechanics he describes:

- Each stop starts slow but ends strong once interaction kicks in; colleagues who did not know each other start exchanging ideas, plans, and contact info.
- The dialogue continues after the meeting by phone and mail, turning into concrete agreements around knowledge sharing.
- A CGIT social-share WhatsApp group captures and amplifies the results of each trip, feeding the next one. The group grew by handfuls after each stop and each blog (one blog on 09-01 added ~30 people to the event list).
- Output is tangible: the first guest blog from Client ICT came in for the CGIT website directly off an on-tour visit.

The pattern: small, in-person, partner-hosted sessions that compound through a shared channel and recurring content. He explicitly wanted to repeat sessions at multiple locations and centralise the recorded knowledge sessions (e.g. broadcast via Teams).
