# Bas on the Cloud roadmap as a strategic tool

Once a solid start with Cloud is made, new use-cases inevitably appear; sometimes you know in advance you start with use-case A and B, C, D follow quickly if A succeeds. Bas notes that a journey of one or more years is not unusual. Just as a software vendor builds a roadmap of new features, you can build one for your internal (future) Cloud expansion, an additional strategic tool that remains a dynamic affair.

Ways a Cloud roadmap contributes:
- **Structure and overview**: visualises the journey, especially when adoption/execution phases take a long time.
- **Prioritisation**: prioritise new use-cases by business goals, resources and expected impact, giving the most valuable/urgent ones precedence.
- **Transparency and communication**: a shared roadmap keeps stakeholders aligned for the long term.
- **Measuring and adjusting progress**: acts as a reference point; adapt it as needs change. Bas adds a realistic caveat: this is easier said than done and often depends on the company culture and organisation size.
- **Adoption and culture change**: continuous use of a roadmap supports a Cloud-first culture and gives direction to the actions needed.
