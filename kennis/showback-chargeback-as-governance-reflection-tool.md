# Showback/Chargeback as a Governance Reflection Tool

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

In the transition phase, showback (or chargeback) reports are used as a moment of interdisciplinary reflection, not just billing. The recurring question is: are expectations being met, and if not, what are we going to do about it?

As a governance instrument the report makes alignment (or misalignment) between business goals and IT spend visible, and becomes a lever to optimize cost. It turns finance reporting into a feedback loop between business and IT rather than a back-office accounting artifact.
