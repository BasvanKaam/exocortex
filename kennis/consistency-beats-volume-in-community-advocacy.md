# Consistency Beats Volume in Community Advocacy

When deciding how to reward and rank community advocates, consistency is the more important metric than raw numbers. Bas's stance, expressed while designing the NVP program: perks like a conference invite should go to the people who show up consistently, not the people who post the most in a single burst. He even suggests making selection a contest framed around consistency. The principle is that durable advocacy is built on sustained, reliable engagement rather than spikes of activity.
