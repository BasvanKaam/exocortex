# Don't hide costs from customers: full-disclosure pricing builds trust

Bas's stance, written in response to public scrutiny (Claudio Rodrigues raised the underlying PaaS 'hidden costs' on Twitter): a vendor should put all of it in one place, including the PaaS services its own product runs on, the licensing math, and the design choices. "We don't hide anything from our (potential) customers or partners, why would we?" He treats community/competitor feedback as input to improve, names and thanks the critic, and publishes the awkward parts (e.g. the PaaS overhead his own talk had skipped). The position: transparency is a competitive advantage, not a risk.
