# Idea: Cross-BU Collaboration via a Light Forum and Innovation Board

From meeting more people across business units, Bas noticed a real appetite to do more together between BUs: exchange ideas, share knowledge, map who is good at what, and learn how each BU works (their propositions, products, services) so they can strengthen and optimise each other.

His proposed first steps, in order of effort:

1. An internal forum or similar as a simple, near-zero-cost first step. He names Yammer specifically: it costs nothing and is already there.
2. A Technology or Innovation board staffed by a few colleagues. Start small, build out slowly, the way most of their initiatives grew. Just start somewhere, with some preparation.

The through-line is his standard operating style: pick the cheapest existing tool, start small, and expand incrementally rather than designing the whole thing up front.
