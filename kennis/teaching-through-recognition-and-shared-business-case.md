# Teach the masterclass through recognition and a co-built business case

Bas's stance on how cloud training should be delivered: not as a one-way transfer of theory, but by anchoring every topic in the participants' own reality and producing something concrete together.

Two convictions stand out from his teaching notes:
- Create recognition. He deliberately uses examples from his own life and practice and asks participants for analogous situations, so the abstract framework lands against lived experience.
- Build a business case together, live, during the session rather than only presenting one. The shared exercise is the vehicle for learning.
- He explicitly normalizes failure as part of learning ("the more mistakes you make, the more you learn"), signaling a safe, participatory room over a polished lecture.

This positions his masterclass as facilitation-led and practice-anchored, not slide-driven.
