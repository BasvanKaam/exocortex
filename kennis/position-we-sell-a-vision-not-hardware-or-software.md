# We sell a vision, not hardware or software

Bas's positioning line for the kind of IT partner he wants to be: 'Wij verkopen een visie, geen hard of software.' Together with the client, and where useful the client's or Salomon's partners, the goal is to get to a more cost-efficient way of working and to be ready for what comes next.

This frames the value as advice, architecture and direction rather than a product transaction, and it pairs with Salomon's self-description as a 'fundamenteel andere aanpak': standing on the client's side in steering its suppliers, with the combination of project management, IT architecture and deep technical expertise as the differentiator - technology and IT service management always going hand in hand. It is the consultancy-first, vendor-neutral stance that recurs throughout his work (and later resurfaces in the SCAF's vendor-neutral framing).
