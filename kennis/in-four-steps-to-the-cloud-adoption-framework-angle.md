# In four steps to the cloud: an adoption-framework session angle

A content idea for a Salomon update session: present "how to move to the cloud" as an adoption framework, packaged as "in four steps to the cloud" ("In vier stappen naar de Cloud"). Bas pairs this with a SCAF walkthrough and a technical update on the latest market developments. The recurring structural device he likes is a stance-prompt for the room: "Wat vinden we van... <stelling>" (what do we think of this proposition), turning the session into a debate rather than a one-way update.

A related sub-idea from a colleague (EJP): contrast working as a seconded engineer (gedetacheerd) versus working as a member of a project team, and the different behaviour each working mode demands of an engineer or consultant.

*Bron: Salomon-IT 'Ideen inhoud' (Update sessie Salomon 2 x per jaar).*
