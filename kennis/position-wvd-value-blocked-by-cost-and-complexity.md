# WVD's value is blocked by setup time and unpredictable cost

His stance: WVD is the go-to modern workspace solution, flexible, scalable, optimized for Office 365/Teams on the Azure foundation, and especially relevant now that working from home is the norm. But cloud resources are costly if not managed properly, and that applies to WVD. The decisive problem for buyers is not whether WVD solves their challenges (it does) but that the setup time plus the unpredictable ongoing Azure cost make it a near-impossible sell to management. His argument: the right tooling (autoscale, automation, ease of management) removes the time and cost barriers, which is what unlocks WVD's value. Cost optimization, not just deployment, is the point.
