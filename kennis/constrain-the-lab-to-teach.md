# Constrain the lab environment so the learning point can't get lost

Bas deliberately narrows the choices available to lab attendees before the moment that matters. In his NME MicroHack he has them build a resource rule that leaves only four VM sizes and a single allowed image, then runs the image-import and host-pool exercises inside that cage. The point isn't to restrict for its own sake; it's that an over-open hands-on environment lets people wander into dead ends that have nothing to do with the lesson. By constraining first, every subsequent step funnels toward the concept being taught, and the success criteria stay unambiguous. Guided self-paced learning works best when the rails are built one exercise before they're needed.
