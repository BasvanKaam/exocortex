# Simplicity is a feature, not a drawback (VIAB)

Bas's stance (Nov 2013): the things people see as VIAB's limitations (single delivery model, no PVS/MCS, no image versioning) 'are not' drawbacks. 'VIAB is designed with simplicity in mind, keeping it simple but effective and it delivers just that! A top notch product, in my book anyway.' His rule: if you need PVS or another delivery model, buy XenDesktop or another vendor; pick the product whose business case fits, don't fault a simple tool for being simple. He frames it as 'you get what you pay for, which is still a lot by any standard.' This is an early statement of a theme he keeps: match the tool to the use case rather than always reaching for the most capable product. (VIAB was later end-of-lifed by Citrix, so the product is dated even as the philosophy holds.)
