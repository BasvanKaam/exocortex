# When the Cloud is a good solution - and when it is not

Bas stresses the Cloud is not always the best solution. His example of organisation 'abc': they ran compute grids in-house for large calculations but their own hardware was not flexible enough to expand quickly when demand rose. The Cloud solved this - they could simply rent the compute they needed, and crucially when they were done after a few weeks they could 'give it all back', paying only for what they used. Bas captures this as "IT uit de kraan" (IT from the tap), as 'they' sometimes say.

Conversely, the Cloud is sometimes the wrong choice: companies handling sensitive data (healthcare, finance) may keep data on-premises due to strict regulation and security norms. The choice between Cloud and on-premises depends heavily on the organisation's specific needs and constraints. Today much time and money goes into hybrid solutions, where part of the infrastructure and data sits in the Cloud and part on-premises - often multiple solutions or a mix are possible.
