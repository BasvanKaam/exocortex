# A cloud exit strategy is insurance you hope not to need

Bas flagged cloud exit strategies as a less-obvious 2020 trend (and noted workload repatriation back on-prem as a related trend). Once companies had their first taste of cloud, some wanted workloads back on-prem and hit challenges doing so. A proper exit strategy means those challenges don't come as a surprise and can be handled timely without unforeseen investment.

Triggers it covers: a provider changing SLAs (which they can do without notice), poor support or performance, services no longer available, the vendor going out of business, or moving data to another / multi-cloud provider. His framing: 'It’s like an insurance you hope you won’t need, but when you do you are glad you have it.'
