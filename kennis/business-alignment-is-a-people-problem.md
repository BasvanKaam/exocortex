# Business-IT Alignment Is A People Problem, Not A Tooling Problem

Bas's stance: the gap between 'the business' and IT is caused by behavior, not technology. The root causes he names are all human and procedural: no joint work, no structural meeting cadence, jargon, and knowledge that isn't shared. His prescribed fixes are equally human: listen, ask questions, record expectations, define success up front, and physically put business stakeholders in IT's Agile rituals (standups, sprint planning). He treats the old-fashioned 'business steers IT strategy' model as still correct, dismissing those who call it outdated. The remedy is collaboration discipline, not a new platform.
