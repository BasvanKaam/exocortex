# Come Get IT Event Format: Dinner, Networking, Two Curated Sessions

The recurring Come Get IT event template Bas used (this edition at Landgoed Salentein, Nijkerk):

- Reception with a drink, then a shared dinner, then the well-known networking drinks to let people get to know each other.
- A small, curated set of talks. This edition pivoted from three to two sessions: one technically substantive, one technique-adjacent.
- Deliberate balance of soft and hard content: a trainer/inspirator running a practical mini-workshop (Rob Tummers, 2enable) alongside a senior solution architect doing a technical deep-dive (Jits Langedijk, NVIDIA, on deep learning, VR, vGPU and GRID), with the explicit promise to skip the marketing talk.
- A premium, atmospheric venue is treated as part of the draw ('die is wederom uitstekend (understatement)').
- Attendees are encouraged to bring a colleague, contact, customer or other interested person, feeding the community flywheel.

The through-line: content must connect one-to-one to the attendees' daily work as IT specialists, and marketing is consciously kept out of the technical session.
