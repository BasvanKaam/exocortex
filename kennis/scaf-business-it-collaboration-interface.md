# SCAF draft: the Business-IT collaboration interface ('where the magic happens')

A set-aside SCAF support draft (filed under 'Opzij gezet') depicts the Business-IT relationship as two sides meeting at an interface labeled "where the magic happens". Business "stuurt aan" (steers/directs); IT "maakt mogelijk" (enables). Spanning the interface are the shared concerns: wishes/requirements/needs, priorities, opportunities and initiatives, goals and IT standards, innovation and automation, governance and processes, and skills and training. The connective tissue is the behavior: "betrekken, luisteren, vragen, leren, samen" (involve, listen, ask, learn, together).

Value here is the slide-level framing that business-IT value is created at the collaboration interface, with collaboration behaviors as the mechanism. This is a draft visual, not finalized framework content.
