# Cloud is not a different way of working, it demands a different way of thinking

Bas's stance: the title says it. "Als je doet wat je altijd al deed, krijg je wat je altijd al kreeg." If you just copy your on-prem environment into the cloud you reproduce all its problems, plus a pile of maintenance-heavy VMs. Cloud is not so much a different way of working but it asks for a different way of thinking, a completely different approach to (re)building what you already have. He is openly aware this is easier said than done.

His frustration with the discourse: people still think too much in the traditional way of "doing IT" while cloud (especially on the infrastructure side) has so much more to offer, and that traditional thinking slows adoption of cloud-native solutions. His advice is to look at what is possible, experiment, and pre-sort for what may be coming.

The position is balanced, not zealous: sometimes a more traditional approach is fine, sometimes it cannot or need not be otherwise. The constant refrain, "gaan voor de beste oplossing, altijd", go for the best solution, always, even when that is traditional.
