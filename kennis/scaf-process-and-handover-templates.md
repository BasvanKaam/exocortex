# SCAF process, governance, communication and handover templates

Further SCAF toolkit templates described by Bas:

- **Cloud governance overzicht**: a visual overview of the primary governance components: lifecycle management/optimisation, communication and ambassadors, innovation and management, compliance and best practices, monitoring and reporting, security measures, cost control, and policy/project management. (Not in toolkit.)
- **Communicatieplan template**: a structured communication plan with objective, situation analysis, audience identification, message content, communication medium and a schedule; flexible and adaptable per (customer) circumstances.
- **Lessons learned template**: a structured table describing issues across applications, processes and systems, with columns for problem description, solution/workaround, responsible person and impact.
- **Overdracht-checklisttemplate**: ensures a structured handover to the management phase: checks for functional and technical design, user manuals and a known-error list; verifies process handover (incident/problem management) and that all configuration items are in the CMDB; verifies SLA agreements, service-catalogue inclusion and maintenance windows; checks skill matrices and end-user training plans; confirms a communication plan, promotion campaigns and appointment of ambassadors/key users; and checks for an implementation/test plan, risk insight and an aftercare (nazorg) plan.
- **Proces flowchart template**: to visually map workflows, process steps, decision points and activity order.
- **Procesvastlegging template**: to document business processes in detail (name, purpose, owner, roles/responsibilities, steps, control mechanisms, input/output, process boundaries, and references to flowcharts).
- **Cloudadoptie-migratie-implementatie planning template**: planning template describing steps, roles, responsibilities, resources, communication requirements, an execution schedule and expected impact/risks. (Not in toolkit.)
