# The risk of writing books on fast-moving products

A durable insight from reviewing a XenMobile book: products under constant development are dangerous to write books about. Even a 112-page book takes roughly 4 to 6 months to write, review, print and market; in that window XenMobile had been updated two or three times, so the book covered 8.5 while 8.6.1 and 8.7 were already out. The lesson: "if you write about a product which is still so fresh and under a constant development, like XenMobile, chances are that your book is already outdated as soon as it hits the shelves, it's risky. At least focus on the basics", which change far less. This is a general principle for technical documentation, not just this book.

*Bron: blogpost 'Book review! Citrix XenMobile Mobile Device Management, published by Packt.' (2014-03-16), basvankaam.com.*
