# Bet: Windows Virtual Desktop Will Be a Major Force in 2019

In late 2018, planning a March 2019 event, Bas calls out Windows Virtual Desktop (WVD) as "expected to be of great influence during 2019" and notes Microsoft is investing heavily in it. He ties this to the Microsoft/Citrix collaboration expected to surface around Citrix Summit, and was in contact with Christiaan Brinkhoff to present on it (with himself as fallback presenter).

The planned event theme was "the modern, largely cloud-based workplace, this time with Microsoft in a leading role," with three interconnected sub-topics that together form a total picture: WVD, workspace aggregation, and Microsoft Autopilot / Out Of The Box Experience. This is an early signal of his pivot from a Citrix-centric to a Microsoft-cloud-centric workplace view.
