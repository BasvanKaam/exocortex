# Don't leave desktop power management to users alone

Bas's stance: putting users in full control of powering their own machines on and off, without intelligent automation behind it, is a questionable approach. People forget to log off, leave machines running for hours while doing other things, and never shut down. He calls this normal, human behavior that applies to everyone, but it is simply not economical. The answer is automated, aggressiveness-tuned autoscaling that respects UX while defaulting to cost-saving, with the end-user role as a convenience layer on top rather than the primary control.
