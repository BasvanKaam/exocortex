# Function before technology: filter requirements against wishes

Bas lays out a small but durable working method for approaching any cloud (or other) decision.

1. Start with the people, the users, the actual strength of your organization, not the technology.
2. Give staff the chance to say how they want to work and what they need to do their job.
3. Filter the requirements (eisen) against the wishes (wensen), forget the underlying technology (cloud or otherwise), and focus on functionality.
4. At board level, run IT strategy and security and provide a framework against which you can mirror the organization's wishes.
5. Only then ask whether "the cloud", in whatever form, fills a large part of your functional requirements and wishes. If yes, great. But never the other way around, because then you risk forcing yourself and your organization to change just to accommodate a solution.

The core test: technology serves function, function never bends to technology.
