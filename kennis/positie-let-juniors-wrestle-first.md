# Let juniors wrestle first, then bring in AI

Give a junior their first real incident or design document and the tempting route is to let AI hand them the answer, fast and clean. Bas argues for the other route: let them wrestle with it, form their own picture, get it half wrong, and only then bring in AI to pressure-test what they came up with. "The first route produces output, the second one produces a senior, eventually."

## Why
The effort you remove with AI is often the exact effort that builds the skill. Skipping it every time means the skill never forms. It is the human-idea-first rule applied to how people grow.
