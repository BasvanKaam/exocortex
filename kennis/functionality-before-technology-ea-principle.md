# Functionality before technology; business objectives drive cloud adoption

Bas's architectural stance, expressed through the EA principles he teaches: focus on functionality and added value rather than the technology, and let business objectives sit at the base of cloud adoption, not the other way around. His principle set reads: Cloud (SaaS) first... unless; automate as much as possible; information management is everyone's responsibility; design for the failure of one, several or all components; user experience is central, think from the customer need; secure data as close to the source as possible; and functionality goes before technology. He positions Enterprise Architecture as a discipline that brings structure and clarity, with EA architects combining technical and business knowledge to steer the organization, while explicitly allowing the possibility to work outside the architecture principles when a business objective demands it. He also pushes back on the EA-as-bureaucracy stereotype, contrasting an IT-mode EA that says no and causes delay with a business-technology EA that says yes, facilitates and brings new ideas.
