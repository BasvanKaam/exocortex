# The -1000 series: shorter posts to ship more often

Framing (March 2015): Bas introduces the '-1000' blog series, where -1000 is the target word count - posts ranging from ~300 to ~1000 words, versus his usual 2500+ (sometimes 3-4000) word deep dives. The motivation is pure throughput: long posts are time-consuming (writing, research, image hunting, lab time, screenshots, home-made Visio), and he wants to produce more content more often without dropping his deep dives.

The editorial goal: 'create useful, yet easy to read or skim, blog posts while (hopefully) still providing an added value to the community. Short enough to read within a few minutes, but long enough to share certain technical details, insights or whatever thoughts.' An early, explicit content-strategy decision - mixing long-form authority pieces with skimmable short-form, a format split that maps neatly onto modern content thinking.
