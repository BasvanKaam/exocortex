# Citrix acquired Unidesk to own application layering

On 9 Jan 2017 Citrix announced it acquired Unidesk, the application packaging/layering and lifecycle-management vendor (and arguably the first to bring application layering to market). Citrix bought the company, not just the tech, so engineers and staff transferred too.

What it meant then: Unidesk layering would be made available to all editions of XenApp and XenDesktop at no extra cost, with cloud-portability and elastic layering (assigning layers at login based on AD membership) reserved for Platinum customers on an active maintenance ('Select'/Customer Success Services) contract. Unidesk layering worked across all major hypervisors and clouds (KVM, XenServer, vSphere/ESXi, Hyper-V, Azure, AWS, Nutanix AHV) and that cross-platform support, including VMware Horizon, would continue, with standalone licenses available. App Disks and PvD (Personal vDisk) were slated to be replaced/deprecated slowly by Unidesk; PVS and MCS were not going anywhere. Dated history, but useful for understanding the lineage of Citrix App Layering.
