# Bas's friend's right-to-be-forgotten test on Facebook

Bas calls the United States 'the (pink) elephant in the room' when discussing data protection. He recounts a war story: a few years ago he spoke with an IT friend he had worked with for years who was deeply interested in this topic. As a test, the friend contacted Facebook, deleted one of his accounts, and then demanded that Facebook prove with evidence that everything had genuinely been 100 percent deleted (invoking the right to be forgotten / recht van vergetelheid). Bas's verdict: that was no easy process.
