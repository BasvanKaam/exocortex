# Liquidware is a US success but underrated in Europe

In his Sept 2017 move announcement, Bas joins Liquidware as Pre-Sales Director Northern Europe/EMEA after three years at Detron (where he was Pre-Sales Consultant, Management Consultant and CTO). His stated view: he finds it surprising that the Liquidware portfolio does so well in the US while remaining 'fairly unknown' in Europe compared to competitors, despite Liquidware being EUC pioneers with 'tons of industry firsts'. His framing of why he took the role: he gets to do what he loves while continuing to learn and share, and he commits to keeping the community involved and to staying honest about product development. This marks the start of his explicit vendor-aligned era (he notes basvankaam.com content stays the same, but the Liquidware portfolio 'will get a bit more attention').

*Bron: blogpost 'A new chapter! Excited to announce that I will be joining Liquidware' (2017-09-25), basvankaam.com.*
