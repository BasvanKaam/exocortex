# Nerdio's four lab-driven event formats

Bas's portfolio of lab-driven enablement events, each tuned to a different audience and level of formality. The shared backbone across all of them: hands-on labs are the center of gravity, not slideware. Marketing is dialed down (or off) the more community-focused the format gets.

**Innovation Days** - Run with and at Microsoft (HQ). Highly interactive and flexible, 20-50 attendees. Structure: joint Nerdio + Microsoft opening (30-45 min), Microsoft presentations (30-45 min), a Nerdio added-value presentation, then hands-on labs.

**MicroHack event** - Also with and at Microsoft (HQ). Smaller and tighter, 15-20 attendees. Short joint opening (10-15 min), then self-paced hands-on labs delivered via GitHub, with plenty of time to showcase Nerdio Manager for Enterprise.

**Partner Training** - On-site or remote via Teams. Tailored to a specific partner's needs, ~10 attendees. More extensive and more formal: includes install, docs, exam, and deeper hands-on labs than usual.

**Nerdio Tech Insider** - The community format. Delivered via Teams (for now), 20 attendees max, tied to an existing Meetup. 100% community focused with a short Nerdio intro (10-15 min) and explicitly NO marketing. Main focus is hands-on labs, informal in tone.

The design principle Bas applies: as you move from Innovation Days toward Tech Insider, attendee count shrinks, formality drops, marketing recedes, and the proportion of hands-on lab time rises. Pick the format by audience (prospects/customers vs partners vs community) and the message you can credibly carry there.
