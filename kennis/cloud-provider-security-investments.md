# Hyperscaler security investments and certifications

The big cloud providers — Microsoft Azure, Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Google Cloud — invest billions of euros annually to safeguard the security of their services, protecting customer data, meeting ever stricter regulation and fending off cybercriminals. Microsoft announced a total of 20 billion USD in cybersecurity investment over a five-year period (roughly to 2030). Amazon and Google don't release specific figures but clearly spend substantial amounts too.

Common security standards and certifications these providers meet:
- **ISO/IEC 27001** – requirements for an Information Security Management System (ISMS); globally recognized.
- **ISO/IEC 27017 and 27018** – extensions of ISO 27001; 27017 for cloud-specific security controls, 27018 for protecting personal data in the cloud.
- **SOC 1, SOC 2, SOC 3** – internal controls over financial reporting (SOC 1) and operational processes and security (SOC 2 and 3).
- **GDPR (AVG)** – measures for European customer data.
- **HIPAA** – relevant for US healthcare customers (medical data).
- **FedRAMP** – US standard required for cloud use by the federal government.
- **PCI-DSS** – for businesses processing card payments (payment data security).

Providers also keep strict internal standards and publish transparency reports and compliance documentation.
