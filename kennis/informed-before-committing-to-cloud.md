# Get informed before you commit, not after

Bas's stance: digitalisation is rarely a threat and mostly makes work more effective and efficient, but cloud and SaaS are sold as trivially easy and that appearance deceives ('schijn bedriegt'). The recurring failure mode is that problems only surface once enough people are hindered: you invest first, then discover the downside.

His argument is for proactive client education over reactive firefighting. Anyone can take a next step; the value is in taking the right one with eyes open. He offers '60 minutes and a good cup of coffee' to walk clients through the trade-offs so they come prepared ('beslagen ten ijs').
