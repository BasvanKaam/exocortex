# Non-persistent plus layering = the best of both worlds

Bas's recurring conviction, stated here (Jan 2017) in the Instant Clones context: the historic objection to non-persistent VDI (it feels impersonal / users lose their stuff) dissolves once you layer applications and an environment-management solution on top. Combine fast non-persistent provisioning (Instant Clones / JIT) with App Disks/Unidesk, VMware App Volumes, a UEM or Citrix's WEM (former Norskale), and 'non-persistent will feel persistent -- the best of both worlds.' Notable that he treats Citrix and VMware tooling interchangeably here and cheekily corrects himself mid-sentence ('App Disks, Uhm... I mean Unidesk'), reflecting the just-announced Citrix/Unidesk move. The position: stop choosing between persistent and non-persistent; engineer non-persistent to feel persistent.
