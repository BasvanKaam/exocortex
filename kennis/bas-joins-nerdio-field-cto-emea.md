# Bas joined Nerdio as Field CTO EMEA on 1 June 2020

Key biographical anchor. As of 01-06-2020 Bas van Kaam joined Nerdio in the role of Field CTO for the EMEA region, leaving Salomon IT. At the time he was an active Microsoft MVP in the Enterprise Mobility category (which then included RDS and WVD). His mandate as described: talk to customers/partners about building a Cloud practice on Azure, feed field feedback back to the Nerdio team, build out the community brand, on-board channel partners, and run webinars/articles/presentations/events. This is the start of the Nerdio chapter of his career.
