# Skip XenApp 6.0, go straight to 6.5 (April 2013)

Bas's clear recommendation in April 2013: "if you are upgrading / migrating from an earlier XenApp version skip 6.0 and go with 6.5. In practice this is most likely what will happen anyway." His reasoning: 6.0 isn't meaningfully different from 6.5, a newer version (Excalibur) is already on the horizon, and both 6.0 and 6.5 require Server 2008 R2 64-bit anyway so the painful OS jump is the same either way. He's similarly dismissive of buying extended support: it costs money and "unless you have plenty you might want to reconsider spending it on your future upgrade that will eventually sneak up on you."

*Bron: blogpost 'End of Life... to Migrate or not to Migrate?' (2013-04-28), basvankaam.com.*
