# FMA evolution key dates

Bas's recap of the main FMA evolution milestones:

- 16 Nov 2009: XenDesktop 4.0 released, still IMA-based.
- 17 Dec 2010: XenDesktop 5.0 released. FMA 1.0 (first FMA release; 6 services).
- 26 Jun 2013: XenDesktop 7.0 released, including XenApp. FMA 2.0.
- 26 Mar 2014: XenDesktop and XenApp 7.5 re-released as separate products. FMA 2.0.
- 30 Sep 2015: XenDesktop and XenApp 7.6. FMA 3.0.
- 28 Dec 2015: XenDesktop and XenApp 7.7. FMA 3.0.
- 25 Feb 2016: XenDesktop and XenApp 7.8. FMA 3.0.
- May 2018: XenDesktop and XenApp 7.18.

Pre-history: the true birth of XenDesktop was 'Citrix Desktop Server' (basic VDI, not considered enterprise-worthy), quickly replaced around 2008 by XenDesktop 2.0 (IMA-based), which introduced Citrix's PortICA technology, a huge leap forward. Over time the FMA grew from 6 services (2010) to 13 services (2018).
