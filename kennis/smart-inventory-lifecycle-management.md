# Smart inventory for workplace and application lifecycle

Method for surfacing waste in a workplace/application estate. By inventorying and monitoring apps and workplaces you make visible which apps and machines are used a lot, a little, or not at all, and how heavily systems are loaded.

The cost picture each unused system carries: hardware (power, cooling, depreciation), the operating system, monthly/annual application licences, plus the ongoing maintenance, monitoring, patching and updating of all of it.

Execution: a specialist tooling Proof of Concept can be stood up in hours and yields first reportable results the next day. Acting on it (phasing out PCs, uninstalling apps) saves clients up to tens of thousands of euros per month/year in the best case and frees admin teams for other work. Works across Microsoft-supported Windows desktop and server, macOS, Linux/Unix and several thin-client types, physical and virtual, on-premises and cloud.
