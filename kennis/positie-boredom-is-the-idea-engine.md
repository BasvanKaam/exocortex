# Boredom is the idea engine, do not let AI fill every gap

AI cannot lie awake. It is never bored, restless or staring at the ceiling at 2am, and that is exactly where ideas come from. Boredom, friction, a broken night, the shower, a walk, the long drive home: "those moments are the production engine for every original thought you have ever had."

## Why
If you let AI fill those moments (summarize every article, draft every first version), you are not just offloading work, you are draining the pool your ideas come from. "Boredom is a good thing. It might be the most underrated tool you own."

## Personal grounding
He is a bad sleeper and uses the broken nights for ideas, research and building. Several newsletters started there.
