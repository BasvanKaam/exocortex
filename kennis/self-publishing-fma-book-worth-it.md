# Writing the FMA book was brutal but never regretted

Looking back on 2016 (writing Jan 2017), Bas reflects that writing his book on the Citrix FlexCast Management Architecture was 'no small matter' and that he thought about quitting more than once, but pulling through and self-publishing it just before Synergy 2016 was 'something I'll never regret doing.' He treats the book, the Synergy talks, and the CTP nomination as one connected leap out of his comfort zone, made while he was technically only ~3 years into the field. The stance: betting big on visible community work (book + speaking) before you feel ready pays off, even when the process makes you want to quit.

*Bron: blogpost '2016 -- A personal look in the rearview mirror' (2017-01-03), basvankaam.com.*
