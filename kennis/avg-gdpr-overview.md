# AVG / GDPR: purpose and benefits

The Algemene Verordening Gegevensbescherming (AVG) is a law with rules for handling personal data, applying to companies and government organizations across the entire EU. Its goal is to safeguard the privacy and protection of EU citizens' personal data while allowing data to be freely exchanged between EU countries. It applies not only to European companies but also to organizations outside the EU that collect or process data of EU citizens. Abroad it is called GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation), which amounts to exactly the same thing.

Stated benefits of the AVG:
- One privacy law instead of roughly 28 separate ones.
- No more notification obligation to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
- Focused on digitalization.
- Lower administrative costs.
- A single supervisory authority.
- Greater legal certainty.
- A level playing field.

If your organization determines the purpose for which personal data is processed and how, the AVG sees you as the 'verwerkingsverantwoordelijke' (data controller).
