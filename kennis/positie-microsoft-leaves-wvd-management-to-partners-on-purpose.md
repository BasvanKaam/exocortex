# Microsoft deliberately leaves WVD management gaps to partners

Bas argues that WVD's awkward, roundabout way of configuring and maintaining environments is, in 2019, strongly dependent on partners to simplify, and that this is a conscious choice by Microsoft. His reasoning: he cannot imagine that building a solid management portal around WVD is a real challenge for Microsoft. He notes Microsoft is working on it (they showed early work at Ignite) but is unimpressed so far, 'a step in the right direction, that's it', and that they are taking their time for what are presumably valid reasons. The implied stance: there is room and reason for partners like CloudJumper to add management value while Microsoft holds back.
