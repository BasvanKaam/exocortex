# Bas's reframe: chase trends and innovation only in moderation

Bas pushes back on trend-chasing: too much emphasis on following new developments distracts from the daily focus on a well-functioning cloud environment, supporting your users, and practical improvements. It is unrealistic to expect every new technology to be a decisive success within months or even years. Customers want help with their *current* situation and immediate needs — they don't jump onto every new trend. His hard-won line: "Ik heb dit te vaak fout zien gaan, helaas" (I've seen this go wrong too often, sadly). IT innovation needs serious investment, and customers want to see the value of existing solutions before taking on new risk. Stay current — but don't overdo it; focus first on what you're good at and what customers expect.

A characteristic national aside: in his view the Netherlands is far ahead of other European countries, with above-average knowledge levels even compared with the United States — "al verwacht je dat misschien niet. Mooi voor ons natuurlijk."
