# Method: Build Colleagues Toward MVP by Plugging Them Into Communities Early

Bas drew up a two-year (give or take) plan with Niels for how Niels could also become an MVP, and Martijn asked to join. His reasoning for involving colleagues in the community work he gets asked to do: by participating they get to know the communities and the people, learn what it takes, and start building credit early toward being admitted to such a programme.

The principle: MVP status is earned through accumulated community contribution and relationships, so the fastest path is to embed aspiring people in real community activity well ahead of time rather than coaching them in isolation.
