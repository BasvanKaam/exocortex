# An Office rollout is part of a wider workplace transformation

Bas's stance closing the whitepaper: something as seemingly simple as deploying Office (ProPlus) is never just an install. Successfully implementing, or slowly transforming toward, a new digital workplace brings a new way of working with it, and there is far more to it than the technical rollout, both technically and organizationally. He deliberately scopes the whitepaper as an awareness piece (what to account for) rather than a complete implementation guide, signalling that the organizational change around the tool matters as much as the tool. He underlines it with Marc Benioff's line that cloud computing is, simply put, a better way to run your business.
