# Salomon IT Solutions financials 2018

Concept annual accounts (jaarrekening, CONCEPT) for Salomon IT Solutions B.V., reporting period 1 Jan - 31 Dec 2018.

Key figures (EUR):
- Revenue (omzet): 610,318, of which 433,743 external and 176,575 intercompany (groepsmaatschappij) - so a group structure with intercompany billing is now in place.
- Cost of sales: 52,840; gross margin 557,478 (91.3%).
- Total costs: 572,526, of which personnel costs 461,184 (75.6% of revenue) and car costs 72,596 (11.9%).
- Operating result: -15,048; net result: -15,468 (loss), an improvement over 2017's -24,244.

Reading: continued strong revenue growth and narrowing losses, but persistently high personnel and lease-car cost base. Private company-history financial data, calibration only.
