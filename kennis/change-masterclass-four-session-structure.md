# Four-session, case-driven structure of the change masterclass

The Masterclass Organisatieverandering is delivered in four sessions, each mapped to one of the 10S base steps:

- Session 1 - Theoretical frame, observation and diagnosis. Covers the psychological aspects of change; ends on diagnosis. Homework: groups produce a diagnosis for one of the chosen cases.
- Session 2 - Meaning of the diagnosis and design. The change strategy is the most decisive step: based on diagnosis and change task, choose the most effective lever. Homework: write a vision for your own case (what already goes well, external reason for change, change goals, implementation strategies).
- Session 3 - Implementing. Theory on steering a change; participants act as advisors to each other's cases. Between session 3 and 4: each participant individually writes an overall programme plan, which is the assessed deliverable that qualifies for certification.
- Session 4 - The approach and certification. A second facilitator joins; together the two play a fictional board to whom the programme plans are presented and given direct feedback. Closes with handing out personal certificates.

Delivery mechanic: every session starts with practitioner input (three selected participant cases), then theory reflecting on that practice, then theoretical input for the next session's topic. Non-case-holders attach to whichever of the three cases they find most relevant. The three cases recur throughout the programme.

Audience: IT directors/managers and (internal or external) consultants wanting to deepen their change craft; participants must bring their own practice case. Each participant also gets a roughly 2-hour personal advisory session afterwards to apply the learning to their own situation. Listed price: EUR 1.495 p.p. excl. VAT.
