# Kwetsbaarheden Assessment (Salomon vulnerability method)

A Salomon IT Solutions service offering / propositie, inside-out, for organizations worried about the risks a vulnerability carries, for whom data availability is essential, or who fear the day their security is put to the test.

Goal: automatically map which risks are present in the infrastructure and how to fix them, distinguishing low-hanging fruit from longer-term improvements, to make the infrastructure more stable and bring it to a higher level.

Starting questions: which vulnerabilities are present? How can they be fixed? What categorization applies? Which procedural recommendations follow?

Method: a short workshop with the IT decision-maker and the engineer sets the focus, known pain points, and the questions to answer. Salomon then identifies, advises, guides, and supports ('identificeert, adviseert, begeleidt en ondersteunt').

Deliverables are explicitly two reports: a management report that translates the binary data into a readable overview, and a technical report engineers can act on directly (optionally supported by Salomon). Findings are categorized by severity and analyzed component, specifically: Top 10 critical systems/volumes/connections; patch backlog (patchachterstand); SSL-certificate issues; misconfigurations; recommendations.

Positioning: no surprises, no unexpected costs. Tagline: 'wij geven het inzicht, u maakt de juiste keuzes!'

Note: corporate Salomon marketing material (contact Eric Kuiper), not BvK personal voice. Captured as a method/propositie for reference.
