# Roadmap as a Service (RaaS)

A consulting method packaging the IT roadmap as a recurring service rather than a one-off report. The sequence: jointly review the current IT landscape, run a detailed assessment, inventory challenges, wishes and requirements, then deliver a solid IT roadmap plus the follow-up on it.

The stated principle is "no assumptions, but facts" covering both the technological and the policy/governance side. The goal is to avoid choosing a product, solution or approach only to discover two years later that you have to start over. Decision-quality, not just decision-speed, is the point: "Een volgende stap zetten kan iedereen, de juiste stap zetten is waar de winst ligt."

*Bron: blogpost 'Applicatie lifecycle en delivery, tijd voor een heroverweging' (2018-06-04), salomon-it.nl.*
