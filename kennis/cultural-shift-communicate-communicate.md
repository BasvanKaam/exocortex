# Cultural Shift: Communicate, Communicate, Communicate

Chapter 4 ('De Nieuwe Zakelijke Realiteit') argues that moving to the cloud brings not just technical change but major cultural and organisational shifts, affecting both ordinary staff and IT personnel.

Bas's most important advice: involve the whole organisation in time. His refrain is 'communiceer, communiceer, communiceer.' Be clear about: what we're doing, why, what improves, what changes, how staff will be guided, what trainings come, and the planning. Concretely:
- Organise a monthly progress session (depending on project type/duration).
- Create a drop-in session for questions, comments, suggestions.
- Make sure the board clearly conveys how happy it is about this.
- Be clear about the new/upcoming policy and continuously stress the benefits.

Key takeaway quote: 'Mochten er grote zorgen zijn of ontstaan, ga dit niet uit de weg, maar praat erover.'
