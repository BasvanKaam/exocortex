# Fast, Good, Cheap: You Can Now Pick All Three

Bas opens the WVD whitepaper by flipping the classic project-management triangle. The old saying — "fast, good, cheap: pick two" — assumes the three are mutually exclusive. His claim: with WVD that no longer holds. You can be fast (scale in hours), good (best-ever Office 365 / Windows 10 experience), and cheap (pay only for what you use, reserve for 40-70% off) at the same time.

This is his signature framing device for the whole proposition: take a maxim the audience already believes, then assert the cloud has made it obsolete. Related: it-as-a-utility, wvd-whitepaper-propositions.
