# Byte-Sized: Bas's crowdsourced community Cloud-tips book

A few years back Bas had the idea, inspired by Frank Denneman (Chief Technologist for AI at VMware at time of writing), to compile a book of short 'Byte-Sized' Cloud tips and best practices: quotes, one-liners, anything Cloud-related people wanted to contribute. He asked Christiaan Brinkhoff (Principal Product Manager and Community Director for Windows Cloud at time of writing) to co-create it.

Using their built-up network, they invited anyone interested to submit text; Bas and Christiaan edited the entries and placed them nicely in the book. The format was inspired by the concept of Twitter (as it was then still called). They received hundreds of submissions and presented the book at one of the E2EVC events. Part of the proceeds went to the charity One Laptop per Child.

Bas frames this as another perfect example of the power of community. The book is no longer for sale, but going to basvankaam.com and searching 'Byte' leads to a page where you can download a digital copy for free.
