# Detron's 2017 new/in-development proposition portfolio (Bas as Technology Officer)

The slate of new and in-development propositions Bas was driving at Detron in 2017. A snapshot of his bets at the time:

- **(I)IoT** — partnership with Advantech; the OT/IT integration angle (network segmentation, data classification & encryption, security & notifications, secure remote access, restricting internet access). Also orienting on big-data analytics and its infrastructure.
- **Detron Secure Datashare** — productising their existing Citrix ShareFile use (secure store/edit/share, customer picks the location: Detron DC, own DC, or Azure). Sold but never marketed; Bas saw GDPR and the data-security wave as the chance to push it.
- **Communications** — Detron selected by Microsoft for the Practice Development Programme for Office 365 Cloud Voice; jointly rolling out and quality-assuring Cloud Voice toward a broader unified-communications proposition.
- **Detron Security in a Box** (powered by CyberAnt) — all-in-one vulnerability-management scan, 24/7, to prevent/reduce/identify/resolve technical vulnerabilities and incidents. Goal: make cybersecurity understandable, accessible and affordable to safeguard business continuity. Launching September.
- **GDPR compliancy checker** (with Eset) — an enabler for further security services.
- **Other security services** — talks on DLP, security scan, social-engineering scan, SoC-as-a-Service; the In Control proposition's security component was due for replacement.
- **Hyperconverged** (talks with Nutanix) — three use cases: (1) replace on-prem/legacy three-tier without becoming dependent on a cloud provider, keeping data and the user close; (2) private/hybrid cloud, partly on-prem partly in the Detron cloud, to ease a later move; (3) full hybrid cloud, build out step by step, keeping what must stay on-prem.
- **Azure Stack** — Microsoft's answer to the rise of hybrid/private on-prem; Bas's view that this hybrid model is 'the model for the coming 5 to 10 years'.
- **WaaS (Wireless as a Service)** — flexibility and security, predictable cost, one monthly invoice, no upfront investment, clear SLAs, single point of contact; three tiers (basis, plus, premium); access points scale with the organisation; 3-step buy/implement (site survey, onsite implementation/patching/testing, go-live).
- **Detron cloud platform redesign** — multiple workspace types from the cloud, a customer portal, heavy background automation, a multi-cloud strategy, and aggregating apps/workspaces from a single central console.
- **SaaS suite** — Backup-, Archiving-, Storage-, and Disaster-Recovery-as-a-Service.
- **Feasibility studies** — big data, blockchain, artificial intelligence and others.

This is a primary-source record of which technologies Bas was backing in 2017 and the reasoning behind each.
