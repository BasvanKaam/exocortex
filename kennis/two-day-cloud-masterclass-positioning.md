# Positioning of the 2-day Salomon Cloud Masterclass

How Bas positions and frames his own 2-day Cloud Masterclass (distinct from the book):

- Fully vendor-independent ('Cloud onafhankelijk'). It surveys several of the largest cloud providers, their architectures and services, but is not tied to any one of them.
- Deliberately not purely technical: a healthy mix of the business side and technology. The technique is treated almost as secondary because too little attention normally goes to the impact cloud has on the organization.
- The red thread of the training is his own independent cloud adoption framework, taught step by step, presented as pragmatic and 'kneedbaar' (mouldable) so it applies to both very small and very large organizations - 'a proven step plan based on existing methodologies, but done a little differently.'
- Level runs from beginner to advanced; no prerequisites required. Audience: ambitious cloud/business consultants, administrators and IT managers.
- Heavy emphasis on learning from each other (whiteboard, prepared questions, active note-taking, business cases, discussion). Explicitly covers exit strategy, security, data and GDPR/AVG, the gap between IT and 'the business', and the often-forgotten cultural shifts.
- Origin story used as a credibility claim: built on hundreds of hours of research, hands-on experience, and input from colleagues, specialists, partners, community members, websites, books and other trainings. Learners are introduced to the cloud and EUC community as a resource.
- Delivery hooks: each module sends learners home with concrete handvatten (handles), templates and tools to start immediately, plus continued contact after the training.
