# Microsoft builds the core, Nerdio closes the management feature gap

Bas's stance on the Microsoft/Nerdio relationship, stated generously toward Microsoft: Microsoft is doing excellent, fast, inspiring work on the core WVD services and the Azure portal (central management plane across regions, the ARM-based model, MSIX AppAttach, Windows 10 Enterprise multi-session). Without that, Nerdio couldn't do what it does; the partnership is valuable to both sides.

But the native portal is still far from what sysadmins were used to with traditional VDI management, and probably won't come close anytime soon because it's a fundamentally different approach and model. Nerdio's role is to add value on top of WVD and close the feature gap that holds many companies back from adopting WVD at all, including day-one support: if Microsoft ships a PowerShell script, Nerdio builds it in and automates it from GA. Dated: reflects the WVD-era (2020) product and naming.
