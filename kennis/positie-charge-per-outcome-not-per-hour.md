# MSPs should charge per outcome, not per hour

Bas argues MSPs should move away from hourly billing on low-margin operational work and instead productize it into outcome-based service tiers (e.g. per secured device per month). The platform's policy baselines, scripted actions, and auto-scaling are the backbone that makes standardized, repeatable, packageable services possible. The shift is mindset before tooling: turn recurring work into repeatable revenue.
