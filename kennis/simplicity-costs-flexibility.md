# Simplicity comes at the cost of flexibility

Bas's stance on Cloud PC vs AVD: "Simplicity also comes with less flexibility, unfortunately." Cloud PC eases management by reusing the tools you already run for physical and other virtual desktops, but that simplification removes control (no autoscaling on a fixed monthly price, no network control in self-service, no image-based management on persistent VMs). His position is not anti-Cloud-PC; it is that you must read the prerequisites and limitations before getting excited, and choose per use-case with manageability and cost in mind. It is rarely either/or: it can be both AVD and Cloud PC side by side, with Nerdio bridging them.
