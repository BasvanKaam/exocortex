# Come Get IT Roadmap: Quality Over Quantity

The Come Get IT roadmap pivots from output volume to output quality. Concrete moves:
- Focus on quality instead of quantity.
- One or more podcasts per month.
- Joint recordings with EUCdigest (cross-pollination between initiatives).
- Multiple articles per month.
- Reduce events from 3 to 2 per year.
- Cross-pollination between the different initiatives.
- Rebuild the website.

The strategic stance is deliberate concentration: fewer events, more consistent serial content (podcasts, articles), and partnering with adjacent communities (EUCdigest) rather than competing for the same audience.
