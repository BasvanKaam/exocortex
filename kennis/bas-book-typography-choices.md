# Bas's deliberate typography choices for the book

Bas explains a design decision as a reader-comfort choice, not padding. He likes larger letters and reasonable line spacing — for him it can even be a dealbreaker that stops him buying a book (if the typography isn't comfortable he finds another way to absorb the information). Hence this book uses Garamond 14pt with 1.15 line spacing, which "leest lekker relaxed." He insists this has nothing to do with making the book look thicker; shrinking the font might have saved 25-30 pages, but he finds this format fitting, especially for his images and tables — and cloud is, after all, a 'big' subject. Plus, it lies nicely in the hand.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> Dat leest lekker relaxed en heeft niets te maken met de dikte van het boek (het dikker laten lijken).
