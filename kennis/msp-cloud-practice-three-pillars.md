# Building an MSP cloud practice rests on three pillars

Bas's framing for MSPs moving to the cloud: building a cloud practice comes down to three pillars: standardization, simplification, and cost control.

Why MSPs hesitate (his diagnosis): many think the cloud is risky because they are unfamiliar with the hourly/monthly consumption model and struggle to package and price Azure services, having always billed the traditional way. They also do not know where to start: Azure offers 1000+ services and, in his view, 95%+ of them are irrelevant for an MSP starting out. Only a handful matter at first.

His position: WVD delivers standardization (Microsoft runs the backend, so customer deployments are consistent), simplification (no separate broker/database/web-interface VMs to maintain), and the consumption flexibility you need for cost control, while Nerdio Manager for MSP adds the pricing, deployment, management and optimization layer (including a Cost Estimator for per-user-per-month pricing) that turns native WVD into something an MSP can run profitably and at scale (multi-tenant, single pane of glass).
