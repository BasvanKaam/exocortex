# The idea has to be human, AI can assist, in that order

Bas's core rule for working with AI: the idea, the angle and the thinking come from a human; AI comes in afterwards to clean up, pressure-test and deliver. "Human idea, AI assist, in that order." The other way round, AI first and human second, "not so much."

## Why
- AI only knows what we gave it. The undocumented fix, the 3am thought, the thing in your environment that contradicts the docs: none of that is in any model.
- The order changes the outcome. He leans on the MIT study "Your Brain on ChatGPT": the group that thought first and got the tool later showed higher recall and engagement, while the group that leaned on AI from the start could barely quote their own essay (cognitive debt). Same tool, opposite outcome.
- The effort AI removes is often the effort that was making you better. Skip it once, fine; skip it for years and the skill never forms.

## How he applies it
His own newsletters: idea from a broken night, words typed on his phone with typos, AI fixes the spelling afterwards. On the podcast Finn reads, but the research, angle and words are Bas's.
