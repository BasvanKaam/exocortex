# Take: big-data analytics is the real advantage of the cloud

Writing in April 2017 in his Detron Technology Officer role, Bas used the Connected Cow as evidence for a broader conviction: the genuinely valuable part of cloud computing is not just hosting, but the intelligence and big-data analytical capabilities layered on top. His words: this is 'one of the real/true added advantages of cloud computing if you ask me.' He signalled appetite to keep collecting such examples: 'I'll continue to post more of these use-cases as I come across them, and I'm expecting to see plenty going forward.' This marks his early-2017 pivot in interest from pure EUC toward cloud and IoT.
