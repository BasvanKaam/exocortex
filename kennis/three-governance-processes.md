# Three Governance Processes

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

The framework derives its governance from The Open Group's three questions: "Are we doing the right things?", "Are we doing them the right way?", and "How do we know?". The Open Group pairs these questions with three governance processes, and the big idea is to pair those processes to the processes of the Target Operating Model:

- Communication: provides information about methods, IT policies, process RACI, standards, the Cloud Reference Architecture, and more. In practice it can take the form of a wiki, a guild meeting, a pizza session, or someone from the CCoE joining a Sprint planning. Any updated methods, repositories, designs, and standards are synchronized and shared back.
- Compliance: carries out a specific compliance checkpoint review. Note this is compliance to the process and methods (compliance to Cloud Governance itself), best done by reusing existing process reviews or measuring moments.
- Dispensation: has the authority to grant exceptions when a current policy runs counter to core principles and goals because of requirements not anticipated when the policy was set. Example: when a cloud reference architecture lacks certain functionality, a team can request an addition or alteration.

These processes exist to answer the governance questions and keep the proper checks and balances in place.
