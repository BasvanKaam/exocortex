# How an Innovation Board operates, end to end

Bas details the full lifecycle of an Innovation Board — a team/committee that stimulates, evaluates and guides innovation initiatives so ideas align with business goals and add value:

1. **Set objective and innovation vision** — decide where to focus (customer satisfaction, efficiency, new products/services); the strategy guides which ideas get attention.
2. **Collect ideas** — encourage staff via brainstorms, hackathons or a simple online intake form; assess on feasibility, cost, potential return and strategic value. Many product organisations also gather input via customer/partner advisory boards; you can cross-reference internal and external output to set priorities.
3. **Evaluate** — cost/ROI, risk and its manageability, strategic relevance (fit with mission/vision), and timing (now vs later); then prioritise — pick some ideas to develop, shelve or reject others.
4. **Allocate budget and resources** — funding, people, tools/tech for approved ideas.
5. **Guide teams** — coaching, best-practice advice, arranging external partnerships; act as a sounding board.
6. **Track progress** — against milestones; keep on budget and schedule; stop projects that fail to meet expectations or hit major obstacles.
7. **Implement and scale** successful innovations into daily operations.
8. **Document Lessons Learned** — capture knowledge so future projects learn, fostering a culture of continuous improvement.
