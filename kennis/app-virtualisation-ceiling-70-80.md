# Application virtualisation tops out around 70-80% in practice

How it worked then (2015): the VRC data showed it is near impossible to virtualise all applications (e.g. with App-V); achieving 70 to 80% was in most cases considered above average. Reasons some apps stay non-virtualised: many apps weren't meant to be virtualised or can't be, sometimes the wrong technology is used, and sometimes it's lack of knowledge - 'virtualising applications is a specialism on its own and is not to be taken lightly.' Company size and the number of applications involved heavily affect the outcome.

*Bron: blogpost 'The (final) results are in - Project Virtual Reality Check 2015' (2015-03-04), basvankaam.com.*
