# AVD Cost Modelling: Modeler + User Cost Attribution

Two complementary jobs in the AVD financial lifecycle, both part of Nerdio's Advisor suite.

**AVD Modeler (pre-deployment):** build detailed cost models for new host pools before you deploy. You input every component (it prompts so you don't forget anything) and it calculates anticipated cost: compute, disk, storage (FSLogix and image), data egress, and Nerdio licenses. It then shows the savings from autoscale/automation (stated up to 75-80%) and breaks the cost down **per user**. Purpose: lower the barrier to start with AVD by making the business case concrete, with text and graphics output, and let you tweak settings to hit requirements.

**User Cost Attribution (ongoing):** once in production, tracks all AVD costs and breaks them down per user, per component. Monthly reports cover total sessions, average sessions per user, machine active hours, session duration and more, also useful for spotting anomalies and further optimization.

Together they give full control over the financial side of AVD across its lifecycle, from business-case to running operations.
