# The origin of Citrix and the ICA protocol

Edward Iacobucci founded Citrix in 1989; the company actually started out as 'Citrus'. They licensed the OS/2 source code from Microsoft and developed Multiuser, their first major release. ICA was introduced when Citrix Multiuser launched around 1990/1991 — making the ICA protocol over 25 years old.

Shortly after Multiuser, Microsoft announced it would drop OS/2 and move to Windows. With help from other companies (Microsoft included) Citrix stayed in business, patented ICA, and worked on an improved version. A later agreement gave Microsoft access to the ICA source code — and that is how Microsoft's RDP protocol came to exist.
