# Early bullish take on Excalibur: impressive, simplified, the right direction

Writing in February 2013 from the Excalibur Tech Preview, Bas is clearly excited and bullish. His repeated value judgments frame the merger of XenApp and XenDesktop as a genuine 'step forward': mixed-OS Catalogs under one Delivery Group is 'Simplified management!', Intelligent Configuration Validation makes him say 'No more missed steps, how cool is that?!', and MCS creating XenApp Catalogs happens 'with a snap of your finger.' His overall verdict on first contact: 'I must say, it looks impressive so far.'

The stance worth recording is his read on direction: he treats the death of IMA, the single console, and the unified FMA architecture not as risky upheaval but as obvious improvement, and he frames the whole thing as needing a 'XenDesktop state of mind', i.e. that XenApp admins should mentally reorient toward the XenDesktop model because that is where Citrix is taking everything. History proved him right: Excalibur shipped as XenDesktop 7 and FMA became the foundation Citrix built on for the next decade.

*Bron: blogpost 'Project Avalon... Excalibur! Part one' (2013-02-13), basvankaam.com.*
