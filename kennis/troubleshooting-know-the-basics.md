# You can't troubleshoot what you don't understand

Bas's core method, stated plainly in this post: to troubleshoot a XenDesktop environment you must know which components and services are involved, how they interact, and what is supposed to happen during normal operations. He generalizes it as a life rule: if you don't understand the basics of what you are dealing with, you're bound to get lost fast. The value of knowing the 'happy path' is that most day-to-day processes are invisible until something breaks. A durable, product-agnostic principle that runs through all his internals writing.
