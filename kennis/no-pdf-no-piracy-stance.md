# Stance: no public PDF of the book, full stop

In this Feb 2017 FAQ Bas drew a hard line on distribution: the book ships only through Amazon as paperback and Kindle, and there is no public PDF. His phrasing was blunt and a little exasperated: 'No, there is no public .PDF version available. Please stop asking, I won't send you one.' It signals both a deliberate channel choice (Amazon-only) and a low tolerance for the recurring ask, delivered with his usual directness rather than corporate softening.
