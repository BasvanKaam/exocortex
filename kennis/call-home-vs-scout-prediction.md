# Call Home will phase out Scout, but not soon

As of October 2016 his read on the Call Home vs Scout question: Call Home is meant to slowly phase out Scout over time, but he expects that to take a while. Despite Call Home, Scout was still the number one tool used by Citrix support, and his guess was it would stay that way for at least another year or two.

He also flagged a usability gap and a recommendation:
- Custom upload schedules required PowerShell. His take: this functionality should be built into Studio or Director one of these days, 'or at least that is what they should do.'
- He actively encouraged readers to enable both Call Home and CEIP (the Citrix Experience Improvement Program), treating telemetry participation as a net good for product quality.
