# DPIA, FG appointment and controller obligations under the AVG

The Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (AP) maintains a list of processing operations for which a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) is always mandatory before processing begins.

**When a Functionaris Gegevensbescherming (FG, data protection officer) must be appointed:**
- Government bodies and public organizations always must appoint an FG, regardless of the data they process.
- Organizations that, as a core activity, track or profile individuals on a large scale: e.g. profiling for risk assessment, camera surveillance, personnel monitoring systems, and health monitoring via wearables. (Source: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.)

**Rights of data subjects (betrokkenen):** access (inzage), erasure/right to be forgotten (vergetelheid), rectification and supplementation, data portability, restriction of processing, automated decision-making and profiling protections, objection, and clear information.

**Obligations of the processor (verwerker):**
- A processor agreement (verwerkersovereenkomst) is mandatory.
- Appropriate technical and operational security.
- Accountability (verantwoordingsplicht): FG accountability, maintaining a processing register, performing DPIAs, keeping a register of data breaches, evidence of consent, and optionally a code of conduct, certificate, etc.
