# Microsoft sustainability goal: carbon negative by 2030

Microsoft puts significant focus on sustainability and energy efficiency when designing its datacenters. By building its own hardware it can optimise energy efficiency, contributing to its broader goal of reducing the carbon footprint of its operations. Microsoft has set itself the goal of being carbon negative by 2030, meaning removing more carbon from the atmosphere than it emits. Designing its own energy-efficient servers is a key part of this strategy, alongside continued innovation in technologies like AI and quantum computing with the same 'green' goal.
