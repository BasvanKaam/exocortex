# How Microsoft RDP descended from Citrix's ICA

Durable history: Microsoft funded Citrix when it was on the verge of closing, specifically to develop ICA. Once ICA existed and was patented by Citrix, Microsoft licensed Citrix's ICA technology for use with Windows NT 4.0, 5.0 and onward. As part of that renewed agreement (for which Microsoft supposedly paid well), Microsoft also regained the right to build a competing protocol of its own, and this is where RDP originated, based on and built from the technology and ideas of Citrix. So Microsoft's RDP traces its lineage directly back to Citrix's ICA. Bas's framing: the two companies oscillated between rivalry and partnership but 'eventually they became friends again.'
