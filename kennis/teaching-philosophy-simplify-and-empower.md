# Teaching Philosophy: Simplify, Educate, Empower

Bas's core professional driver, stated when he moved from Field CTO to Principal Technical Trainer / Content Creation at Nerdio (early 2025, after nearly five years there):

- The most rewarding part of the work is simplifying complex technical topics into easy-to-understand nuggets of information, something he does inside and outside Nerdio.
- Educating and empowering others, whether partners, customers, tech communities, or internal teams, has always been his passion.
- As Field CTO his focus included bridging the gap between technical teams and business stakeholders, making sure technology strategy aligns with business goals and outcomes. He carries that 'technical + business' perspective into the training role.
- Meeting partners and customers face-to-face (London to Dubai) and hearing their success stories is what keeps him going.

His tagline for the new chapter: Train, Inspire, Grow. 'Strategic training inspires minds, impacting results and empowering growth.'

This anchors the L&D/content work that follows in the Nerdio era.
