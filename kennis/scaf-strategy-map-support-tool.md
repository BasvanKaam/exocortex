# SCAF support tool: the strategy map (read up, build down)

A SCAF supporting-documentation draft adapts a strategy map (Kaplan-Norton / balanced-scorecard lineage) as an aid for the strategy phase. Its defining instruction: read from bottom to top, but build from top to bottom.

Layers (top to bottom in build order):
- **Vision / Mission** at the top, expressed as what the organization receives.
- **Financial**: revenue growth and productivity.
- **Customer**: product leadership, customer focus, operational excellence.
- **Internal processes**: growth and innovation, customer relationships, operational excellence, stakeholder interaction.
- **Organization (learning & growth)**: develop and grow skills and knowledge.

Each lower layer carries an "improve something" (verbeter iets) prompt, making it a worksheet learners fill in to connect activities upward to vision. This is the angle a reader would not get from the book's SCAF chapter: a strategy-map worksheet linking SCAF's strategy phase to the four scorecard perspectives.
