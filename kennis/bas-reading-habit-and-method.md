# Bas's reading habit: the 10-pages-a-morning method

Bas describes a personal transformation: he barely read books before, though he already read 30-40 pages a day of blogs, KB articles and forums. Now he reads a lot — mindfulness, effectiveness, efficiency (which he notes are two very different things), productivity, business and vitality. Everything he reads is non-fiction; a novel feels to him like "Netflixen", time-wasting, because you learn nothing from it — "zo ben ik nu eenmaal geprogrammeerd."

His method and tip: from each book pull the lessons most important to *you* (sometimes one, sometimes several) and apply them daily. "Iedere dag één procent beter is ook beter" — every day 1% better adds up. The concrete habit: at a fixed time of day (for him the early morning) read at least ten pages of a book of your choice; you'll soon read fifteen, twenty. Average books are ~300-350 pages, so that's at least twelve books a year, give or take — and after starting this he quickly read a book a week and still does. He prefers physical books for the feeling of pride/satisfaction of holding them and seeing them on the shelf.

His money reframe: books cost roughly €15-25, sometimes more, but you're not paying €30 for paper and ink — you pay for knowledge that often took years to gather and assemble. His financial tip: when your salary comes in, the first thing you do is set aside a small amount for personal development (open a separate savings account for it) and buy books and materials from it.
