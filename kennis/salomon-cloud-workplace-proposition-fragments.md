# Proposition fragments: cloud-workplace advisory at Salomon

A grab-bag of proposition angles around the cloud workplace, several tied to a specific moment in time:

- 'Citrix x 5' / 'Citrix op Azure': lay out the available Citrix-on-Azure options and what to watch for, licensing in particular.
- 'Waarom niet AWS': AWS as IaaS or Workspaces as a real alternative to consider, not just Citrix/VMware.
- RDmi (Remote Desktop modern infrastructure) flagged as a good alternative to Citrix Cloud services (XenApp/XenDesktop).
- XenApp 6.5 is end of life, a trigger to review application-delivery and lifecycle options.
- 'Doe meer met minder resources': spend less time firefighting daily incidents, free up time for improvement and innovation; surface unused PCs, applications, and therefore license, monitoring, patching, and power savings, and how to inventory that efficiently.

Underlying stance: 'Wij verkopen een visie, geen hard of software', we sell a vision, not hardware or software.
