# Key Takeaways - the execution phase (Chapter 11)

Bas's summary of the execution phase: it is where plans become reality - the phase of action, but not without structure and preparation. Everything analysed and documented in the insight phase now serves as the foundation, leaving no room for impulsive choices; every step builds on earlier decisions.

Key points he flags:
- Primary business applications may need specific adjustments to function optimally in the cloud, requiring deeper evaluation and modernisation, with workshops and application owners central. He frames this as an iterative approach focused on modernisation and rationalisation.
- The definitive choice of a Cloud Service Provider (Azure, AWS) often is already made, but for smaller providers consider vendor lock-in - switching to another provider or back on-premises can become difficult and costly. He recommends thorough research, weighing pros and cons, and planning a cloud exit strategy ahead of time (not only with smaller providers). This book deliberately focuses on a single platform rather than multi-cloud to minimise complexity and operational challenges.
- Earlier documentation now comes into action: RACI models, GAP analyses and system inventories are finalised as the implementation guide.
- The governance model from the previous phase is refined and implemented (cost management, security, automation, continuity).
- Agile with iterative sprints/MVPs and PDCA fits the flexibility required.
- Skill readiness and internal ambassadors prepare and enthuse staff for the change.
