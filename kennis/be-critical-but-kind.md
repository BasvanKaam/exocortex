# Be critical but kind; not OK to share everything you think

Bas's stance on discourse, quoting his father: "It's OK to think whatever you like, it's not OK to share everything you think" (he notes it sounds better in Dutch). Speak your mind constructively, think first, apply tact and empathy. He explicitly calls out vendors going at each other and people being overly critical online as the wrong move. A clear early statement of the measured, kind public posture he carries into his later brand.

*Bron: blogpost '20 years in IT, here’s what I’ve picked up along the way. Big read, skimming optional' (2019-10-08), basvankaam.com.*
