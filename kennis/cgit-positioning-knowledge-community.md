# CGIT positioning: by IT pros, for IT pros

The Come Get IT (CGIT) venture is consistently framed across its tagline brainstorm as a Dutch IT knowledge platform and community, not a product or consultancy. Recurring positioning pillars distilled from the tagline options:

- It is a knowledge portal / platform: "Het IT kennisportaal van Nederland", "Het IT kennisplatform waar je naar op zoek was".
- It is community-driven and peer-to-peer: "Door IT professionals, voor IT professionals", "Verbindt IT professionals".
- Sharing is the core value: "Delen staat centraal", "De grootste waarde ligt in delen", "Geeft terug aan de IT Community".
- It spans three channels: "Podcast. Website. Evenementen."
- It carries a slightly contrarian tone: "Wij doen IT anders", "IT kennis delen, maar dan anders".

The three-channel model (podcast, website, events) and the give-back-to-the-community ethos are the durable propositions behind the brand.
