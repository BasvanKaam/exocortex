# Come Get IT event of 2 April 2020 cancelled due to COVID-19

The Come Get IT community event scheduled for 2 April 2020 was cancelled because of the early Covid-19 outbreak; the team judged it irresponsible to proceed. The edition had sold out within a very short time. Confirmed speakers who had agreed to present were Maarten Eekels, Erwin Derksen and Michel Roth. The organisers said they were exploring whether and how to reschedule, and were also weighing an online/virtual alternative, but cited many dependencies and uncertainties. This is the second Come Get IT edition referenced (the first ran in 2019).
