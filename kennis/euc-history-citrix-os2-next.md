# EUC history: OS/2 apps on NeXT via Citrix

From EUCdigest episode 15 with industry veteran Steve Greenberg: an early-EUC anecdote where he made OS/2 applications available on NeXT computers using Citrix technology, after starting his career as an audio engineer. The episode's throughline is that EUC has evolved a great deal yet so much has stayed the same, and that the community played an important role in the early days, eventually leading to the EUC Masters Retreat.
