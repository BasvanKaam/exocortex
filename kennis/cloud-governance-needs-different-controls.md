# Cloud and Agile Need a Different Kind of Governance

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

His stance: cloud and agile require a different implementation of governance. Self-steering teams and highly elastic resources enable speed and innovation, but they do not benefit from restrictive controls. Bolting old governance onto cloud kills the very advantages you went to cloud for.

That is why he argues for a cloud-specific Target Operating Model paired with three governance processes, rather than relying on the operating model you already have. He is explicit that most organizations already have an operating model, but if it actually got things done you would not be looking for a new one. The TOM should be an enabler for self-managing and DevOps teams and double as a communication tool for the digital transformation, not a control mechanism that slows them down.
