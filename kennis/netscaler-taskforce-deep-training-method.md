# NetScaler Taskforce: a deeper training method than a 3-day course

Demand for Citrix NetScaler expertise (ADC, Gateway, SD-WAN, Web-Gateway, Web App Firewall) is high and scarce; basic networking knowledge is no longer enough. To close that gap, Salomon IT, Tien (X) and SBC-Solutions joined forces and set up a NetScaler Taskforce.

Bas's contrast with the standard path: anyone can take a 3-day NetScaler course and maybe pass a cert, but that only gets you basic knowledge, some familiarity with the GUI and the wizards. The Taskforce chose a more extensive approach, starting from a very solid foundation and building up from there.

The method (late 2018 to mid-2019):
- An 8-person group, first met in Q4 2018, led by veteran NetScaler trainer Jeroen Bogers who owned the through-line and supplied varied teaching material.
- Recurring homework assignments between sessions ('figure this out', 'what do you actually know about this', 'what would you want to learn').
- Each participant designed more or less their own learning path; in-person sessions shared experiences and hands-on lab work so they learned from each other, not just from the trainer.
- Scope went beyond NetScaler itself: network protocols, topologies, traffic flow, methodologies, authentication mechanisms, DNS, tooling and troubleshooting.

Result after ~7 months: 8 certified specialists with real hands-on practice, not just theory. The durable idea is the training model: blend a strong fundamentals backbone, self-directed learning paths, peer knowledge-sharing and hands-on practice to produce specialists, rather than relying on a short certification course alone.
