# We Give the Insight, You Make the Choice

Salomon's adagium and its stance: "Wij geven het inzicht, jij maakt de juiste keuze." In an exploded IT market of products and services, the only sound basis for choosing is facts — facts surfaced from the client's own organization, seen from the end user's perspective.

The deliberate boundary: Salomon commits to the success of the IT plans "zonder op de stoel van de IT-leveranciers te gaan zitten" — without taking the vendors' seat. It acts on behalf of the client, guarding progress and guaranteeing delivery, but does not become the implementer or displace existing partners. Independence and the user-experience vantage point are the position, not just the method.

He also commits to long-term relationships over transactional work: "Salomon gelooft in lange termijn relaties" — growing with the client to keep improving together. Related: salomon-assessment-to-roadmap-model.
