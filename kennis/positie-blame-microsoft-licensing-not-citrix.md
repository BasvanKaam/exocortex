# Positie: the cloud blocker is Microsoft licensing, not the product

After a conference call with Citrix's Kurt Moody (and Martijn Bosschaart), Bas reconsidered his earlier post but concluded he still felt the same way (Sept 2013). His refined position: the technology is ready, the design guide still has holes, but the real obstacle to cloud adoption is consistently Microsoft licensing.

In his words: 'it's Microsoft and their licensing program keeping us from taking full advantage of all that the cloud era has to offer.' He conceded a fair counter-point: 'I do agree that Citrix shouldn't be the one explaining Microsoft's licensing structure, a reference would have been nice.' This post shows his pattern of holding a position under pushback while publicly thanking those who engaged and quoting Kurt's line 'any progress is a good thing'. A recurring theme across his early cloud writing: licensing, not technology, is the gatekeeper.
