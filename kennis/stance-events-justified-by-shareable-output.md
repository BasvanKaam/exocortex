# Stance: conference attendance must be justified by shareable output

In the Ignite proposal Bas does not treat a flagship conference as a perk; he justifies the spend by committing to concrete, shareable returns: podcast episodes, targeted knowledge harvested against pre-surveyed BU needs, internal knowledge sessions, and social media material. The implicit argument is that attending an event is only worth funding if the knowledge is captured and redistributed to the wider organization and community, turning a personal trip into measurable ROI for multiple business units.
