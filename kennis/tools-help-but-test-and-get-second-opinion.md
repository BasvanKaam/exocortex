# Use sizing tools, but test and get a second opinion

Reviewing Project Accelerator (February 2014), Bas's stance was pragmatic. Sizing a XenDesktop deployment (storage, IOPS, RAM, CPU, network, delivery models, and how it all blends architecturally) is hard for most people, so any tool that assists is welcome, if done properly. But he refused to oversell it: it will not automate everything or remove your worries completely. His repeated rule: "in the end, it all comes down to testing, testing and test a little more." He endorsed Citrix's own advice to consult a Citrix representative or partner on exact numbers until the tool is finished, adding that a second opinion never hurt anybody "unless you know exactly what you are doing, and even then." He was also forgiving of Citrix being perpetually one step behind its own changing portfolio.
