# Common WVD/Azure adoption challenges (from the field)

Bas's field-distilled list of why companies already running WVD/AVD struggle (and end up looking for better tooling). Roughly 75% of companies he talks to weekly already have an existing WVD deployment.

- Lack of in-house Azure knowledge; Azure is overwhelming and WVD has a steep learning curve.
- Lots of manual PowerShell tasks.
- Automation is hard to get in place at all, takes a lot of time, and is not for everybody.
- Once automation exists, it is hard to maintain and update.
- Worry about knowledge leaving the company.
- Ongoing user management is a challenge.
- Application/image management is cumbersome and slow.
- Monitoring is hard to set up; alerting is limited or impossible.
- Azure/WVD can get very expensive without the right tooling and knowledge of what to watch.
- Perceived feature gap: many think WVD isn't production-ready or lacks enterprise-grade features.

Key insight Bas repeats: **getting set up is just step one; it's the other 80-90% (day-2 management, updates, optimisation) that companies actually worry about.**
