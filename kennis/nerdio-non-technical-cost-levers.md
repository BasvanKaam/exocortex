# Nerdio non-technical cost levers

Beyond the technical Azure-resource strategies, Bas frames two 'softer' cost levers that Nerdio addresses:

1. **Reduced learning curve / cost of deployment** — automating AVD with raw PowerShell or Azure DevOps has a steep learning curve, not everyone can or will learn it, and it creates key-person risk (what happens when your most experienced engineer leaves?). Nerdio lets engineers with only basic Azure knowledge build, fine-tune and cost-optimize at scale; the claim is that 80%+ of an IT workforce can use it productively after a few hours, with most tasks reachable in three clicks or fewer via a GUI and wizards.
2. **Customer Success Packages** — structured, paid training packages delivered over multiple days spread across weeks so learning can be applied between sessions. Sessions are recorded, attendee count is unlimited, advanced packages include semi-annual follow-ups, all for a one-time fee. The point is to remove the steep learning curve and get teams productive fast.

The broader argument: Nerdio's cost story isn't only Azure-resource savings; the time saved on repetitive day-to-day tasks and the reduced key-person risk are comparable wins.
