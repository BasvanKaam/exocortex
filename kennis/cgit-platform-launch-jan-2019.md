# CGIT (comegetit.nl) launched 16 January 2019

The knowledge portal 'for IT professionals, by IT professionals' comegetit.nl (abbreviated CGIT) went live on Wednesday 16 January 2019, marked with a bloggers borrel. Bas credits the core team behind the concept: Sander Bruijs, Xander Hartog, Martijn Verheij, Tijn Benedek and Milou Heusdans.

At launch the platform already had its first blogs published and its first podcast online, with Abram Schermer as the main guest. The first CGIT knowledge-sharing event of 2019 was scheduled for 28 March, the first of four planned for the year; from then on these events ran under the CGIT flag.
