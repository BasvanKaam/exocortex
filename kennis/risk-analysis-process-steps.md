# Risk analysis process steps

A risk analysis is a structured process to identify, assess and prioritize risks in a project, organization or system. It is repeated periodically, but its initiation starts during the insight phase (inzichtfase). Input can come from your and colleagues' findings, audits, results of earlier risk assessments, third-party software, etc.

Typical steps:
1. **Preparation and scope definition:** define exactly what is analyzed, the goals and reach; identify stakeholders and assemble a responsible team.
2. **Risk identification:** gather information on possible risks via brainstorming, interviews, historical data and audits; list all relevant potential risks (security, operational processes, legal requirements, financial aspects, etc.).
3. **Risk assessment (probability and impact):** determine the probability each risk occurs (e.g. low/medium/high or a 1-5 scale) and its impact (damage, reputational loss, financial loss). A risk matrix is often used to assess and prioritize by probability and impact.
4. **Risk evaluation and prioritization:** combine probability and impact to rank each risk; prioritize by severity (which are most urgent), often categorized as high, medium and low risk.
