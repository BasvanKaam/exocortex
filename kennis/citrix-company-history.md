# Citrix company history: from Citrus to WinFrame

Citrix is an American software company founded in 1989 by Edward Iacobucci, a former IBM developer. Its first office was in Richardson, Texas, relocated later that year to Coral Springs, Florida, with 18 FTE. It started as 'Citrus Systems' but, after another company claimed the trademark, changed its name to Citrix.

Early on it focused on remote access products for Microsoft's OS/2. After two years it released its first product, Citrix Multiuser, an extension to OS/2 where the ICA protocol was born. Citrix acquired/licensed the OS/2 source code from Microsoft.

Milestones: Multiuser 2.0 (Multi-Win, 1992) ran on Microsoft DOS, up to 5 users; WinView (1993) ran DOS and Windows apps; the company grew past 65 employees in 1994, launched its first authorised reseller programme, signed Tech Data as first national distributor, and reached $10M net revenue. In 1995 it launched **Citrix WinFrame**, a true multi-user OS based on Microsoft NT, allowing up to 15 simultaneous users.

In 1997 it opened a new HQ in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, and **Mark Templeton** (formerly VP of Marketing) became CEO, a role he held until 2015. Citrix today provides server, application and desktop virtualisation, networking, SaaS and cloud computing.
