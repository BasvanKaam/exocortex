# Taking the stage: Bas pushes past his fear of public speaking

Personal framing (March 2015): after two years of 'hiding behind my MacBook', Bas takes the DuCUG stage in Amsterdam as his first real public-speaking gig in front of ~150 IT pros. He's candid that it scares him and that he'd previously made excuses to avoid stages. He motivates himself with the well-worn stat that public speaking ranks as a more common fear than dying, and reframes the talk as 'an opportunity to finally get over myself and take that next step.'

This marks the start of his speaker career (DuCUG, then E2EVC Berlin with Mick Glover / @XDTipster on XenDesktop Power Management). A documented moment of deliberately stepping outside his comfort zone - useful context for how his public/community presence evolved.

*Bron: blogpost 'I'm presenting at the Dutch Citrix User Group - and to me that's huge' (2015-03-06), basvankaam.com.*
