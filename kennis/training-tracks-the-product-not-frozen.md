# Training is a living document that tracks the product, not a frozen deliverable

I build version numbers and a changelog into every course from day one, and I rename ADDIE's last phase to "Examine" with three standing questions (what works, what doesn't, what changes). The to-do for the next version is part of the template itself. A module isn't shipped and forgotten; it is reviewed monthly, updated quarterly to align with product enhancements, and rebuilt when the look-and-feel or feature set changes. Content that doesn't track the product goes stale and quietly misinforms learners.
