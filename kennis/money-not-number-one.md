# Don't make money your number one priority

Bas's stance on compensation: getting rewarded for expertise makes sense, but there's danger in chasing the paycheck. Before a job change, do research, talk to current AND former employees, use Glassdoor, weigh pros and cons, and get agreed points in writing and signed. Always leave a current employer in style and with respect. "don't make money your number one priority, it's not 'worth' it. Have fun, and while doing so reaching your full potential is much more likely to happen – the money will follow." Notably this is pre-FIRE thinking and stands as an early datapoint on his money philosophy.
