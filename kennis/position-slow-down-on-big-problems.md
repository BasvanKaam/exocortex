# On Handling Big Problems: The Bigger the Problem, the Slower You Respond

A principle Bas adopted from astronaut Mike Massimino and now repeats as his own: when something goes wrong, the bigger the problem, the slower you respond. Take ~30 seconds to regret it, call yourself every name in the book, then move on slowly. Rushing can make a bad situation worse (you can always make things worse) and lowers your chances of success. Get an overview of the issue, form a plan, then take action.

Related mindset notes he endorses: in any team it's never about one person; even a 0.00001% chance isn't zero, so persistence pays; and if you find someone difficult to work with, it's probably because you don't know them well enough — find a positive thought about them before responding.
