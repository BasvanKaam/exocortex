# SCAF strategy interview question battery

The concrete discovery questions Bas uses in the SCAF Strategy phase to extract a client's cloud strategy (the instrument layer, not in the book). Themes:

- Primary drivers for choosing cloud - made as specific as possible.
- Desired outcome, business goals, expectations; when is the implementation successful; which KPIs.
- Specific wishes/requirements to account for; running projects whose success or failure would materially impact the organization.
- Mission, vision, core values.
- Key stakeholders for cloud adoption and why; who must we absolutely speak to and why.
- Role of innovation; appetite for a more Agile way of working.
- Existing IT and/or security strategy/policy in force.
- Current IT partners/suppliers and what they do.
- Strengths, weaknesses, where improvement is needed; opportunities and biggest threats (feeds the SWOT).
- Existing cloud experience.
- Primary IT-related processes currently in place and whether they work.
- Where they are (dis)satisfied, specifically.
- Whether business goals drive/contribute to the strategic IT plan.
- How well business and IT actually cooperate.
