# Slop is not what AI makes, it is the thinking someone skipped

Bas does not buy the easy take that AI produces soulless slop. "The tool did not skip the thinking, the person using it did." Slop is what is left when someone hands over the one part they were supposed to keep: reading, rereading, and asking whether this actually helps the person on the other end.

## Why
- Fluent is cheap now. Content can be grammatically spotless and still read like a half-asleep conversation: smooth on the surface, empty underneath, forgotten by morning.
- Readers feel whether someone was really there. Adding typos on purpose to look human misses the point.
- So the fix is not avoiding AI, it is front-loading the craft: prep, reread, and build your own voice into it.

## His test
Before anything goes out: "Did a human actually think here, or not?" If not, it goes back in the drawer and may never be published.

This is the same answer he gave in the voice interview (Q5: "only if you give it no care"), now in published form.
