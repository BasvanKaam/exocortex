# Bas's Closing Reflection: Good Questions Always Move You Forward

In the book's closing ("Ter Afsluiting"), Bas frames the end of the chapter as closing an important part of a *shared* cloud journey — "de rest is aan jullie" (the rest is up to you). His stated goal was not merely to transfer knowledge, but to inspire the reader, prompt them to (extra) action, and support them at every step of their path to the cloud.

His core parting conviction: asking good questions always moves you forward, and new insights usually emerge from continuing to learn and from sharing your knowledge with others. He insists this is not only valuable but genuinely fun ("onwijs leuk om te doen"). He invites readers to use the book's lessons as a foundation but to put their own spin on it — share insights, help each other, and keep growing together.

He reminds the reader, as he wrote at the start, that the book is grounded in his own experience, including the era when cloud "nog vrijwel niets voorstelde" — when the cloud as we know it today still seemed very far off.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> Onthoud dat stellen van goede vragen je altijd verder brengt
>
> Geloof me, dat is niet alleen waardevol, maar ook onwijs leuk om te doen.
>
> Gebruik de lessen en ideeën in dit boek als een fundament, maar voel je vrij om er je eigen draai aan te geven.
