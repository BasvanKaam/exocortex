# How Bas runs his Field CTO day: agenda over to-do lists, dynamic over rigid

Bas's own operating method as Field CTO EMEA, reusable as a personal-system reference:

- He wears many hats: product marketing, sales, events, demos, product/business development, light support, community initiatives, partner management. He values the diversity and the autonomy to control his own agenda across time zones (US colleagues).
- He has dropped to-do lists entirely. He organizes his Outlook agenda and inbox as the single system to stay on top of everything, adds reminders where useful, and uses the CRM to track team and individual work. When he does write things down (daily), it is pen and paper.
- He plans the day in his head, not on paper, and treats embracing change as a core skill: "being able to embrace and except change is something you will have deal with."
- Health/sustainability habit: 12-hour days broken up with short 10-15 minute walks for fresh air, phone always nearby.
- Continuous-learning habit: he watches Nerdio's internal "demo of the day" videos at lunch to keep up with fast product change, specifically so he can translate new features into explanations for partners and customers.
- Feedback loop: he collects feedback/ideas/requests from the field over several days and sends them to product development, where they often reach the roadmap.
