# The 7 Cloud Baby Steps (behavior-driven FinOps)

Bas's own framework, explicitly modeled on Dave Ramsey's "7 Baby Steps" for personal finance and reframed for cloud operations. The framing argument: managing cloud (track costs, optimize resources, build buffers, prepare for failure, invest in skills) is logical on paper but most teams still fail to do it consistently. The reason is the same as with money: simple doesn't mean easy. Outcomes are clear; the path is personal, messy, underestimated and often overlooked.

The approach is deliberately behavior-driven, not tooling-driven: discipline, intentionality and clarity are the universal carry-over from Ramsey, even though Ramsey's specific mechanics are US-centric. Concrete moves he names: sometimes you sell off unused assets (a "cloud garage-sale"), sometimes you start small, sometimes you do the boring thing consistently. The punchline he keeps returning to: "the steps may look obvious. But execution is what really matters."

Planned as a visualized "7 Cloud Baby Steps" graphic plus a follow-up series unpacking each step with examples and practical tips.
