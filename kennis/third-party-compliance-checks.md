# What to demand from cloud suppliers for compliance

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

Because cloud means consuming services provided by third parties, it is your responsibility to check their compliance to your standards. Things to check on and demand from suppliers:

- Audit / assessment reports
- Cloud provider certifications
- Security scans

Setting up logging and auditing on IaaS and PaaS is absolutely necessary and requires attention and configuration. The basic principles of auditing still apply, but if you engage through an MSP you must make sure everything is on the up-and-up.
