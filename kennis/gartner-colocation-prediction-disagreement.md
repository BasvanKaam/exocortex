# Bas Disagrees With Gartner on Co-location's Demise

A pointed Bas opinion: Gartner has said for several years now that the co-location model will disappear from the market fairly quickly. Bas's reaction — "ik zie dat niet snel gebeuren" (I don't see that happening any time soon). A small example of his willingness to push back on analyst consensus from his own field experience.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> Overigens zegt Gartner al een aantal jaar dat dit model vrij snel van de markt zal verdwijnen, ik zie dat niet snel gebeuren.
