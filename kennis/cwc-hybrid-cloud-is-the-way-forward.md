# CWC hybrid model is the way forward for Citrix

Bas's stance (2016): Citrix Workspace Cloud is "the ultimate hybrid cloud model and the way forward according to most, me included." The appeal: cloudify the infrastructure and management layer (Controllers, DB, Studio, Director, StoreFront move up to Citrix) while you keep the VDAs and NetScaler in your own Resource Location, on-prem or in any cloud you like.

He also flags it as the closest thing to a true desktop-OS-based DaaS at acceptable cost: because of Microsoft's licensing restrictions on desktop operating systems, a genuine desktop-OS DaaS is hard to deliver economically. CWC lets you host the infra in the cloud and run your own on-prem, 100% desktop-OS VDI VDAs, which comes close to a private-cloud desktop-OS DaaS. His recommendation throughout: "take it for a test-drive."

Note (dated): this was the early precursor to Citrix Cloud; Bas predicted in 2016 the name, subscription model, and services would likely change before year-end, which they did.

*Bron: Inside Citrix (2016), 'Inside Citrix ch25 - Citrix Workspace Cloud'.*
