# Position: Workspot's aggressive anti-Citrix positioning is a mistake

Bas argues that Workspot's habit of not just competing with Citrix but actively positioning itself against it, going as far as the occasional public/digital confrontation, is 'niet een hele handige aanpak' (not a very smart approach). He attributes the edge to lingering 'pain' among ex-Citrix executives now at Workspot, which he says shows and which he finds a shame ('zonde'). Crucially, he separates the marketing critique from the product: this says nothing about the quality of the solutions Workspot offers. This is consistent with his standing view that aggressive vendor marketing is off-putting even when the product is fine (he made the same point about Workspot in 2017 and about #CitrixCan).
