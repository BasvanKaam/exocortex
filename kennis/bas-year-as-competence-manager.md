# Bas's year as a competence manager

About fifteen years ago Bas spent a full year as a competence manager. The role involves composing learning goals and competence plans for a company's employees, plus organising knowledge sessions (or "pizza sessions") where you enthuse colleagues to share knowledge with the group, or invite an external speaker on a product or technology the organisation works with or is interested in.

The role taught him two things. First, that he is not a manager. Second, and more valuable, how valuable knowledge is (or can be) for a company in general and for the individual, and the added value of someone who knows what they're talking about and can help with solid planning and guidance.

His advice: ask about this at your current employer (e.g. via HR). If you are the leader yourself, be proactive and start doing this for your team. In Bas's framing, personal training plans are worth their weight in gold ("Persoonlijke opleidingsplannen zijn goud waard").
