# Prediction: an explosion of XD7 blogs at GA

Writing just before XenDesktop 7's general availability, Bas predicted the community wave: 'as soon as XD7 is officially out on the market we'll see an explosion of Blogs all describing the above features in more detail, screenshots included.' His framing positions himself as an early mover getting ahead of the crowd, and he's openly enthusiastic about the community energy a launch brings rather than territorial about it: 'it's always fun to see how enthusiastic people get when new products are released! Me being one of them.'
