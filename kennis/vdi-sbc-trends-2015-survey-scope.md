# What the Project VRC 2015 survey measured about VDI/SBC

How it worked then (2015): The Project VRC 'State of the VDI and SBC union 2015' survey surfaced industry-wide answers on the hardware in use, the real impact of (hyper)converged infrastructures, how VDI scales versus Terminal Server, most-popular antivirus, the appeal of DaaS, top EUM (End User Management) solutions, GPU usage, hypervisor choice, and reasons customers pick VDI plus which vendor came out on top. Per Ruben Spruijt, the participant base was roughly 55% EU and 40% North America. A useful record of which topics defined the VDI/SBC conversation in 2015. Specific results are dated.
