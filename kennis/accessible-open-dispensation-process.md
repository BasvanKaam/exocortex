# Make Dispensation Accessible, Not a Monthly Board

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

His stance: design the dispensation process to be accessible and open, and explicitly avoid a rigid mechanism like a monthly Architecture Board. A heavyweight, infrequent approval gate is exactly what agile cloud teams should not have to wait on.

Instead, embed governance where the work happens: have cloud architects join Sprint plannings and product demos. He points to the SAFe framework's Architectural Runway process as anticipating these kinds of requests in flow. The underlying conviction is that checks and balances should keep pace with the teams, not throttle them.
