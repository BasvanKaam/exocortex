# Gartner Magic Quadrants and the cloud-relevant ones

Gartner Magic Quadrants are charts helping organisations evaluate vendors across technology areas. Each plots vendors on two axes: **Completeness of Vision** (x) and **Ability to Execute** (y), divided into four quadrants:

- **Leaders** — strong vision and high execution; dominant, stable, broad solutions.
- **Challengers** — strong execution but less innovative/forward-looking vision; reliable, not always market-leading on innovation.
- **Visionaries** — strong vision and innovative solutions but limited execution capacity or market share.
- **Niche Players** — focused on a specific segment; effective in their niche but less influence on the broader market.

Cloud-relevant Magic Quadrants Bas names: **Cloud Infrastructure and Platform Services** (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud); **Enterprise IT Service Management (ITSM) Tools** (ServiceNow, Atlassian); **Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)** (Splunk, IBM Security); **Data Center and Cloud Networking** (for hybrid environments); and **Desktop as a Service (DaaS)** (Microsoft, Amazon, Citrix and others).
