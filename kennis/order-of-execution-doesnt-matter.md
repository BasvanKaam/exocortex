# Bas: the order of execution barely matters - just have fun

Closing his execution-phase chapter, Bas makes a deliberately relaxed point: the order in which you carry out the described tasks does not matter much. In practice many of them happen in parallel. What matters most is that it gets done, that there is good collaboration and communication - and, he adds, try above all to have a bit of fun.

This section sits under the chapter quote: 'The role of IT is to enable the business, not to be the business.'

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> Het voornaamste is dat het gebeurt, er goed e samenwerking en communicatie zijn en probeer vooral een beetje lol te hebben.
>
> The role of IT is to enable the business, not to be the business.
