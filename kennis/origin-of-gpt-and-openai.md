# Origin of GPT and OpenAI

ChatGPT is a product of OpenAI (acquired by/partnered with Microsoft per Bas's text), a research organisation founded in 2015 by technology pioneers including Elon Musk, Sam Altman, Greg Brockman and Ilya Sutskever. OpenAI's stated goal is to develop AI that benefits all of humanity.

GPT stands for **Generative Pre-trained Transformer**. The concept rests on transformer architectures, a machine-learning breakthrough introduced in 2017 in the paper "Attention is All You Need," which let AI models understand and generate language more contextually and coherently. GPT models result from years of iteration and are trained on enormous amounts of text from sources like books, articles and public websites, intended as a tool usable across education, healthcare, business and personal assistance.
