# On outsourcing creative work cheaply: you get what you pay for

Writing in June 2016, after his book launch, Bas tried three different Fiverr formatters/designers for the Kindle version and the cover, and called every result horrible. His conclusion: "I guess you truly get what you pay for." The deeper point he keeps returning to is his own perfectionism ("I am not easily satisfied") and the fact that he had actually read the Amazon Kindle Publishing Guideline while the so-called professionals clearly had not. His stance: cheap outsourcing of work that carries your name is a false economy; if you care about the result, expect to either pay properly or do it yourself.
