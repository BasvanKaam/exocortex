# Bas's Candid Critique of the Masterclass Material

In the "Reflectie" of his NCOI Masterclass Cloud 2019/2020 end-essay, Bas is bluntly honest about the training itself: he found the course material disappointing. His core objection is currency. He calls it unacceptable for a 2019/2020 training to lean on books from 2011 whose examples reach no further than 2013-2014, arguing that Azure, AWS and GCP today bear no comparison to what they were around 2011/2012, so a large part of the training was no longer relevant and missed important technological developments and insights. He notes he raised a question about this in one of his assignments and got no feedback.

He is fair about it: he still found it fun and interesting, and did pick things up, and he rates the assignments themselves (built on the course material) as fine. His biggest personal gains were on the business side rather than the technical: cultural change and how to anticipate it, uniting IT and "the business", IT as internal broker, and the impact on day-to-day operations and processes.

The stance worth keeping: training content has a shelf life, and cloud material more than most; dated examples undermine an otherwise good course. It also explains why his own later teaching (the Salomon Cloud Masterclass, the book) leans on current, field-tested material and the business/governance angle.
