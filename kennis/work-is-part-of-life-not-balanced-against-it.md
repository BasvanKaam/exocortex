# Work-life isn't a balance; work is one part of life's whole package

Bas's reframing of "work-life balance" (credited to a perspective shared by Joseph, Nerdio's CRO/co-founder, in Bas's own words): it isn't about balancing work *against* life. Work is simply part of life, alongside family, friends, hobbies, the parties, events and birthdays you choose to attend, time with your partner and kids and parents. You balance the whole package, and work is one part of the equation.

He adds his own stance on what makes that work: doing what you love daily helps, but so does how a company treats people and the freedom it gives them to do their job the best they can — recognising that no two people are alike or perform best under the same circumstances. He puts that kind of freedom (set your own agenda, decline meetings that don't fit, work from anywhere, arrange your days to balance things out) at the top of what he'd look for in a job. He also keeps running/walking through the workday to clear his mind and reach new insights while staying fit.
