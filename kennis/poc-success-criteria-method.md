# Run a PoC Against Agreed Success Criteria

Bas's reusable pre-sales method for a 30-day free trial / proof of concept:

1. At the start of the PoC, discuss and agree on success criteria with the (potential) customer.
2. Act on those criteria in the weeks that follow: schedule additional deep-dive sessions, demos, one-to-many questionnaires, and one-to-one meetings as needed.
3. Do a quick health check in between to make sure you are on track to get the most out of the trial window.

The goal is that together you extract maximum value from the trial period, rather than leaving the customer to evaluate alone. This works even where there is no formal accelerator program attached.
