# Printing is business critical, and underrated

Bas openly likes printing, an unfashionable EUC opinion he owns (June 2016): "First off, I like printing." Not because he's built many complex print architectures (a couple, far from many) but because he likes the technology and the processes behind it. His core argument: printing is considered business critical at almost every customer he's visited, even if people underrate it. He illustrates with everyday behaviour, users print the important email or whitepaper, annotate it, take it home. He admits he's biased: "Although I prefer pen and paper over a keyboard as well, so perhaps I'm not the best reference." The stance matters because it frames why he treats printing improvements (like 7.9 UPS HA) as significant rather than minor, and shows his willingness to champion the boring-but-essential parts of the stack.
