# Application layering narrows to the big three (2017)

How it stood then (Sept 2017). Bas's v4.0 cheat sheet narrows the application layering field to three vendors: Citrix App Layering (the former Unidesk, acquired by Citrix), VMware App Volumes, and Liquidware FlexApp. He dropped FSLogix from the comparison on the grounds that it is not an application layering company (despite doing good work). He also dropped Citrix App Disks, predicting it would be deprecated following the Unidesk acquisition, as would Personal vDisks (PvDs). Concrete signal: App Disks are no longer part of the 7.15 LTSR release, though they still appear in the Current Releases (e.g. the upcoming 7.16 CR) per the Citrix E-docs at the time.
