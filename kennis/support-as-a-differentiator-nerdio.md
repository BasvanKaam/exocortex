# Support is a Differentiator, Not an Afterthought

Bas's stance: when you sell to medium-sized and enterprise companies, support has to be top notch. You cannot afford single points of failure, and customers want access to a team of experts who do the heavy lifting when things go wrong.

Key arguments he makes repeatedly:
- Support being included (no separate contract to purchase or renew yearly) is itself a selling point.
- Support should already be available during the PoC / free-trial phase, not only in production. Helping during the trial lowers the barrier, saves time and money, and gets everyone on the same page.
- The point of the trial is for the customer to have experienced every facet of an enterprise relationship before they ever go to production.

He personally tries to help customers who hit a misconfiguration or error during a PoC, but is happy to hand them to the support team because he trusts them to resolve it the same day, often the same hour.
