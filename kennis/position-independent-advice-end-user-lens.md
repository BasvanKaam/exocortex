# Independent advice, seen through the end-user experience

Bas/Salomon stake out a clear consulting stance: 'wij doen het anders' (we do it differently). The differentiators he commits to:

- Advice is independent and therefore always in the client's best interest — Salomon stands on the client's side in steering the client's own suppliers, explicitly not sitting in the IT suppliers' chair.
- Start with facts: a solid factual analysis before any plan ('meten is weten').
- Clearly described services that can be delivered fixed-price.
- Combine project & program management, IT architecture and deep technical expertise.
- The client's business objectives, values and current culture lead the whole.

A second deliberate lens: analyze the IT infrastructure from the end-user-experience perspective — how satisfied, productive and efficient employees actually are with the IT they have — rather than purely from a technical or cost view.

He also bets on long-term relationships over transactional work: 'samen groeien' with clients to keep strengthening each other and keep improving. Core values stated: expertise, commitment, accountability for results, customer focus, transparency and openness.
