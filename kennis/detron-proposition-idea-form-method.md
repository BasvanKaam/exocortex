# Bas's proposition idea form method (Detron, 2017)

As Detron Technology Officer, Bas designed a one-page proposition idea intake form (Solutions Board approved) so a new service/proposition idea could be made discussable in the Technology Board, and later the Solutions Board, with minimal friction. It is deliberately short and partly based on the later, fuller advice report, so the idea form can feed straight into that report.

The form collects, in short, concise answers:

**General**
- Submitted by: name, department, email, phone.
- Which work domain it falls in: Workspace, Connectivity, Security or Cloud (Detron's four core domains).
- Concise description of the proposition/service/product (its goal).
- The trigger: which problem(s) does it solve.
- For whom it is interesting: target market/audience.

**More specific**
- Does it use a particular technology or product type (which, briefly)?
- Which suppliers/vendors qualify (do not forget current partners); give alternatives.
- Which has the preference and why.
- Is specific product knowledge needed (think also of sales colleagues)?
- Impact on Detron's IT infrastructure: storage, VMs (type), compute, etc.
- Sales/revenue model: how does Detron make money from it; give a clear example.
- Risks.
- Short-term plan: what the next steps could (or must) be within two weeks.

**Other remarks** as applicable.

The design intent: a lightweight intake that still forces the submitter to name the problem, the target audience, the revenue model with a concrete example, the risks, and a two-week next step before anything reaches a board.
