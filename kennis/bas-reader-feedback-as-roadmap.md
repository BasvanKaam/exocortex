# Bas's Roadmap Is Driven by Reader Feedback

Bas anticipates that readers will have wondered why he skipped certain items or treated some topics only briefly. Rather than defend those choices, he explicitly invites feedback and frames it as the input for his own roadmap — where the audience's input is the single most important factor ("het allerbelangrijkste is, daar luister ik naar").

He welcomes all ideas: specific additions, an online extension of the book, things he forgot, or points the reader disagrees with — "allemaal prima." He closes by thanking readers for their interest, time, and willingness to make the journey with him, and gives his contact address (basvankaam@vandebasistotmeesterindeCloud.nl) for questions, comments, or constructive criticism, signing off wishing them success and fun on their cloud adventure.
