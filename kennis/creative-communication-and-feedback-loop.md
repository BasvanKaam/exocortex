# Creative Communication and Putting Responsibility on Staff

Bas's advice on making cloud transformation feel less abstract for employees. A single announcement isn't enough — communication must be regular and consistent, and not only formal via email.

Use physical spaces creatively: the hall, cafeteria, corridors, elevators, even the stairwell, to carry the message and keep constant awareness. Use visuals like infographics or even cartoons (keep it light) at strategic spots to convey the importance of the changes humorously and accessibly.

Develop a clear home-work policy: expectations around remote work, how performance is measured (Bas flags this as a hard topic), which tools to use, and how to secure data. Involve the user organisation as much as possible.

Most important: listen attentively and act on it. By involving staff and putting part of the responsibility on them, they feel engaged — with all the benefits that brings.
