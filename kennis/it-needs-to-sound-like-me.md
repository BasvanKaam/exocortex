# It's my book, it needs to sound like me

At launch (May 2016) Bas reveals he was offered the help of a professional technical writer on multiple occasions and deliberately declined: "It is my book, and as such it needs to look, feel and sound (or read) like me, at least that's the way I feel. Hopefully that is a good thing." He's open and slightly anxious about being a non-native English speaker and can't guarantee an error-free book, but won't trade his own voice for polish. This is a foundational statement of his authorship principle: authenticity and personal voice over outsourced perfection. It connects directly to the later voice-profile work, the insistence that output sounds like him is not new, it's been his stance since at least his first book. He also reflects on writing as a love (this informs his ongoing identity as a writer-practitioner, not just an engineer).
