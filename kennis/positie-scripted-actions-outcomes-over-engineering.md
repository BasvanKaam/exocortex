# Scripted actions: solve for outcomes, not engineering

Bas's stance on automation in Nerdio: scripted actions exist to solve for consistency and compliance, with the focus on outcomes rather than engineering. The value is repeatability and single-pane visibility into deployment, not clever code. The related stance on testing: every environment has different rules, regulation and compliance, so staging and scheduled review aren't optional bureaucracy - they reduce the likelihood of catastrophic OS or application outages. 'It happens, but we can solve for the possibility.'
