# Transformation: From Product to Service, Ownership to Usage

The core business-model transformations Bas says cloud drives:
- **Product to service** — hardware/software once sold as one-off products are now subscriptions (SaaS). Customers pay for access plus updates, enabling continuous value and a closer customer relationship.
- **Ownership to usage** — instead of owning and maintaining infrastructure, companies pay for what they use, driven by IaaS (compute, storage, network) and the Pay-as-you-Go principle, eliminating upfront hardware investment.
- **Shop to direct access** — buying shifts from physical stores to digital platforms; e.g. Netflix and Spotify give direct access to entertainment without physical purchase, enabling personalised, realtime customer experiences.
- **Capex to Opex** — capital expenditure to operational expenditure; variable costs aligned to actual usage (Bas notes long depreciation of upfront investment can also have advantages).
- **Ownership-focus to access-focus** — companies need access, not ownership, of datacenters/software, freeing them to focus on core activities.
- **Goal-directed to flexible** — instead of planning IT years ahead (an exercise that traditionally repeats every 3-5 years, though planning starts much earlier to time licence/contract cut-offs), the Opex model makes companies more agile without long-term lock-in.
