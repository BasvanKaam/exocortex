# User Environment Management belongs on every workspace, not only virtual

A durable EUC principle Bas argues in 2018: User Environment Management should not be scoped to virtual desktops alone. Across physical, virtual, on-prem and cloud, users expect the same look, feel and personal settings to follow them regardless of device or location, and admins want that managed centrally. With OSes by then updating 3-4 times a year (vs every 2-3 years previously) and apps installed locally, virtualized, layered or consumed as a service in any combination, the value of a single UEM layer that decouples the user's settings and data from the underlying OS is higher than ever. The operating maxim he coins: "Configure once, migrate many." Tooling and product names date quickly here, but the principle (separate the user environment from the OS so it roams across any workspace) is durable.
