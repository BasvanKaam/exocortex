# Older SAN/NAS isn't dead; flash caching keeps it alive

Bas's take (Sept 2014): SAN and NAS solutions older than three to four years are "rapidly being driven into the legacy corner," but he predicts they'll be around for many more years because SSD/flash caching solutions (he names Atlantis and PernixData in the replies) can speed up older infrastructure, and converged/software-defined platforms can absorb old SAN/NAS for archive and data tiering.

He also nods to the rise of converged and software-defined storage (Nutanix repeatedly, "there they are again"), signalling where he saw the market heading without writing off the installed base. A balanced both/and prediction rather than a hype call.
