# Cloud only? No. Cloud first or hybrid? Yes

Bas's stance, the thesis of his graded final persuasive essay for the NCOI Cloud Masterclass (title: "Cloud only? Nee. Cloud first of hybride? Ja. De enige weg voorwaarts.").

His position: cloud-only is the wrong destination for most organizations. Cloud-first or hybrid is the only sensible way forward. He argues the case deliberately around the things that make pure cloud unrealistic: legacy applications and the human factor, rather than treating cloud as an unconditional good.

Telling detail on his conviction: the assessor's feedback said he could have emphasized the advantages of cloud more in his conclusion. Bas explicitly disagrees ("vindt hij. Ik niet"). He is not a cloud maximalist; he refuses to oversell cloud benefits even when a grader nudges him to. That restraint, weighing legacy and people over hype, is the through-line of his cloud philosophy.
