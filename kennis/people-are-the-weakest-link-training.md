# Role-Specific Security Training (Don't Call Staff the Weakest Link)

Bas on the 'Wat Je Kunt Doen' security angle. Mobility and access from various locations bring data-security challenges, especially with remote work and personal devices. Measures like two-step verification, encryption, and secure connections (VPNs) are essential.

His characteristic aside: 'Je personeel is immers de zwakste schakel, maar dit zou ik niet zo letterlijk met ze communiceren.' (Your staff are the weakest link — but I wouldn't communicate that to them so literally.)

Key method: tailor trainings to the specific roles of staff within the organisation — don't keep it too generic. Regular cybersecurity and correct-cloud-use sessions minimise potential security weaknesses.
