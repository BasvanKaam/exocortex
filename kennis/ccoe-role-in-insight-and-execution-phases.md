# CCoE Role in the Inzicht and Uitvoering Phases

**Inzicht (Insight) phase** — focused on gathering data and analysing the current IT infrastructure, processes and applications to decide what can migrate and the impact. The CCoE:
- Performs a detailed assessment of the current IT environment (workloads, applications, data, infrastructure) to determine what is most suitable for migration, how and in what order.
- Runs a Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) analysis and risk analyses to surface pitfalls and compliance issues.
- Provides training and knowledge sharing to migration teams and the wider organisation.
- Selects cloud models (public/private/hybrid) and technologies fitting business needs, working with IT and business — the definitive choice belongs to the Uitvoering phase.
- Ensures stakeholder communication so IT, business and security agree on the steps.

**Uitvoering (Execution) phase** — the actual migration is planned and executed. The CCoE:
- Leads project management and coordination (planning steps, allocating resources, keeping teams on schedule).
- Provides technical expertise (configuring cloud services, optimising infrastructure, safeguarding security).
- Ensures governance and compliance alignment.
- Monitors progress, intervenes on bottlenecks and continuously optimises the cloud infrastructure for efficiency and stability.
