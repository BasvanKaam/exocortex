# Writing the book was sparked by talking to peers who had done it

In this August 2016 reflection Bas credits conversations with other self-publishing authors in the community as the trigger for his own book. Talks with Frank Denneman and Ruben Spruijt about 2.5 years earlier, both of whom used Create Space successfully, inspired him to write in the first place, and a Nutanix Sr. TME, Brian Suhr, later helped with publishing and Kindle questions. His implicit stance: peer example and community generosity are what get people over the line on big personal projects, and he pays that forward by documenting his own process in detail.

*Bron: blogpost 'Inside Citrix - The making of & lessons learned!' (2016-08-15), basvankaam.com.*
