# EUC News Nuggets on LinkedIn: the newsletter and its editions

Bas launched the EUC News Nuggets newsletter on LinkedIn in June 2026, as the notification layer on top of eucnewsnuggets.com. The launch went live together with the Press post on Microsoft's seven new AI models (8 June 2026); a separate welcome post followed the next day. More than 600 people subscribed in the first 12 hours.

## What it is
- **Purpose:** take one topic, work out the part that matters, and hand it over in a form you can finish between two other things. Read it, listen to it, or skip it; at least you know something new is out.
- **Formats:** written (web, PDF, cheat sheet) and audio (the EUC News Nuggets Podcast, around ten minutes, on Spotify, Apple Podcasts and the website). The format is picked per nugget, sometimes both.
- **Audience assumption:** practitioners. He assumes you know what a tenant is, "because I would rather respect your time than over-explain."
- **Cadence:** deliberately no fixed schedule. Some weeks two or three, some weeks nothing. Quality over quantity. Runs next to his regular LinkedIn posts, which he has published daily since March 2026.
- **Edition types:** (1) essays on AI and how he uses it, usually opening with a personal "true story"; (2) analysis of a single news item with a "what would I do with this" section; (3) practical lists: the monthly known-issues edition (recipe in the newsletter workflow) and the monthly roundup.
- **Subscribe link:** https://www.linkedin.com/build-relation/newsletter-follow?entityUrn=7467831309860593665
