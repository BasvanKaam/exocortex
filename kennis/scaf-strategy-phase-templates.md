# SCAF strategy-phase templates: Summary Palet, Strategy Map, SWOT

The strategy phase of Bas's Step-Cloud Adoptie Framework (SCAF) is supported by a set of templates that capture the strategic foundation of a cloud project:

- **Strategy Management Summary Palet**: an at-a-glance template recording the drivers, goals and strategic choices for cloud adoption — primary reasons for cloud use, specific requirements, mission/vision/core values, challenges, risks, and a list of ongoing high-impact projects that may influence the strategy. It also holds desired outcomes, satisfaction criteria, KPIs and IT/security policy.
- **Strategy Map**: a visual of strategic goals and activities. It *reads bottom-to-top and builds top-to-bottom*: the base is learning & growth (skills/knowledge in the organisation), then internal-process improvement (innovation, customer relations, operational excellence), supporting customer focus and product leadership, ultimately feeding financial goals like revenue growth and productivity. This layered structure links operational actions to strategic outcomes.
- **Strategy template** (not included in the toolkit): structures the whole strategy phase with mission/vision/core values, a strategy map and a SWOT analysis, filled via interviews and workshops.
- **SWOT template**: internal strengths/weaknesses (distinguishing qualities, valuable assets, past complaints, vulnerabilities) and external opportunities/threats (untapped opportunities, market trends, economic shifts), closing with a summary that helps prioritise action points.
