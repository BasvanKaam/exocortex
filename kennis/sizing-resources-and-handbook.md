# Sizing resources: XenDesktop Design Handbook and community calculators

Resources Bas recommends for compute/storage sizing:
- Citrix XenDesktop Design Handbook (based on 7.6, somewhat outdated but full of best practices for building/configuring a Site, including compute and storage sizing for Delivery Controllers, StoreFront, database, VDAs, etc.).
- The XenDesktop 7.5 sizing calculator by Andrzej Gołębiowski.
- Thomas Gamull's post on real-world XenApp 7.x sizing (Citrix blog).
- Citrix Project Accelerator (project.citrix.com).
- mycugc.org (search for server/machine sizing threads).

Bas's sizing Key Takeaways also stress: when load testing, always incorporate any exotic applications you have - those are the ones to be most curious about.
