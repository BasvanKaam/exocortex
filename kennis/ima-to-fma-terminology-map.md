# IMA-to-FMA terminology: Farm to Site, Data Collector to Delivery Controller

How it worked then (2015). With XenDesktop 7.x, XenApp moved into the Flexcast Management Architecture (FMA), bringing terminology and conceptual changes from the old IMA world. Functionally the equivalents are close even where the technical implementation differs. Bas published a cheat sheet mapping former 6.5 (IMA) components to their FMA equivalents (e.g. Farm to Site, Data Store to central Site database, Data Collector to Delivery Controller, Web Interface to StoreFront, plus Studio, Director, VDA, Delivery Groups, Connection Leasing, and the removal of LHC/Zones). He intentionally excluded features that existed in IMA but were still missing in FMA. He held the article back until after Synergy 2015 in case announcements changed the architecture; afterwards the FMA mapping was essentially unchanged. An offline PDF reference was provided. Part of his -1000 blog series.

*Bron: blogpost 'Citrix IMA vs. FMA... XenDesktop components and terminology cheat sheet' (2015-05-26), basvankaam.com.*
