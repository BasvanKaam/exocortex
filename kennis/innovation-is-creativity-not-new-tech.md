# Innovation Is Mostly Creativity, Not New Tech

Bas argues that innovation is too often equated with a new product or technology, when in most cases it is creativity: differently marketing, packaging or deploying an existing product or service. By paying more attention to what you already have, you can relaunch or even "re-innovate" services cheaply, with a catchy description, a press release, video, and by explicitly tying into current market developments.

His worked example: securely storing and sharing data, a service Detron already offered but advertised in no way, while data leaks and the looming GDPR were in the news almost daily. The transferable stance: a company can shake up the market on a relatively simple basis without inventing anything, just by repackaging existing capability against a timely problem. "Innovatie wordt vaak geassocieerd met een nieuw product of technologie, echter, in veel gevallen heeft het meer te maken met creativiteit."
