# The Innovation Day / MicroHack hands-on event format

Bas's reusable enablement-event format from his Nerdio field-CTO era, run repeatedly across Europe (often co-hosted at Microsoft offices). The model:

- Format: a packed room (30+) of AVD and Windows 365 enthusiasts; a mix of overview lectures and hands-on labs, with Microsoft and Nerdio specialists on stage.
- The MicroHack: a timed, gamified hands-on assignment where attendees who have never seen the product build a complete AVD environment (e.g. a 5-machine host pool with autoscale and FSLogix) from scratch. Bas tracks completion times as a leaderboard / "record" (sub-six-minute and ~nine-minute completions), which doubles as proof of the product's ease of use rather than of his teaching.
- Why it works (his repeated observation): every session turns out differently even with a fixed agenda — the value is in being flexible and focusing on the partner's/audience's needs. He measures success with end-of-day feedback forms and scores.

The principle he reuses: lead with hands-on, let people do it themselves, and the experience sells the simplicity. "The proof is in the pudding."
