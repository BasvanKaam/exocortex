# XenMobile a leading EMM platform on broad device support (2014)

Writing in February 2014, Bas positioned XenMobile (built on the acquired Zenprise) as one of the leading EMM/BYOD platforms, and expected it to stay that way "for years to come." His standout differentiators: NetScaler SSL offloading to keep MDM off the DMZ, and unusually broad device support beyond just iOS and Android, including Windows, Kindle, Symbian, Samsung KNOX and SAFE, and BlackBerry. He acknowledged support was limited for some device types but valued that they were all at least present. He also noted XenMobile can be tricky to set up, making the Citrix-hosted cloud editions (with SLA, backups, HA) a potentially smart move. Sales context: at his employer Qwise demand for EMM and XenMobile was rising sharply.
