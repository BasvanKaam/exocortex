# Layering and virtualization are partners, and the cons are soon forgotten

As of December 2015, Bas pushes back on the framing that layering replaces app virtualization. He argues they are complementary: virtualization handles what it can (50-60%), layering handles the legacy tail so it doesn't pollute the base image. He acknowledges the cons of adding a layering product (extra technology, interface, cost, no isolation) but his stance is that these 'cons, if you can call them that, are soon forgotten' once you see the management benefits. The framing: avoiding the 'big bad image' is worth the added stack complexity.
