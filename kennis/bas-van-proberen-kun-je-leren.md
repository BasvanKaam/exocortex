# Bas's 'van proberen kun je leren' philosophy

Bas opens his feedback section with a personal story: 'van proberen kun je leren' (you can learn from trying) is something his son is taught at school, and the family applies it at home too. The idea: try it, and if it doesn't work three, four, five times (the number of attempts depending somewhat on his son's mood) then you can ask for help. Bas notes his son sometimes uses this line back on his parents.

He maps this onto business: a company can learn a lot from its users and employees, especially when this process is introduced from the insight (inzicht) and execution phases. Users often have valuable insights, suggestions and (constructive) criticism, while employees are responsible for collecting, analysing and converting feedback into concrete actions. Involving them actively from the start, giving responsibility, and offering room to speak openly and honestly yields better collaboration and a product/service that truly fits the target group's needs.
