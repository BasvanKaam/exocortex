# Risk management focus areas and attention points

**Focus areas of a risk/security program:**
- Risk identification.
- Analysis and assessment.
- Analysis of current measures.
- Compiling and executing an action plan.
- Measuring, monitoring and reporting.
- Incorporating results into decision-making.

**Points of attention:**
- There is no single standard inventory approach.
- ISO is too large and complicated for many organizations.
- Not all risks deserve the same attention.
- Options per risk: avoid, reduce, transfer, or accept (vermijden, verminderen, overdragen of accepteren).
- Risk management is a continuous, ongoing process.
