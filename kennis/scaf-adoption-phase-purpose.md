# SCAF Adoption phase: finishing what you started

Chapter 14 of Bas's Step-Cloud Adoptie Framework (SCAF) covers the Adoption phase. By this point most of the groundwork is done and several results achieved; this phase is about repetition and finishing what you began. Where the execution (uitvoering) phase focused on introducing and adjusting processes, the adoption phase focuses mainly on fine-tuning. See it as an extension of the execution phase, aimed at reaching a fully operational and robust Cloud environment ready for daily use.

A key recurring point Bas stresses: using Cloud services generally brings a different way of working and thinking. There must be continuous attention to cultural shifts, such as a more iterative (agile) way of working. You can keep going the old way and it will work, but you won't extract maximum return from Cloud-based services, products and technologies. This SCAF model is applied continuously across the lifecycle of services, products and technologies: always asking whether what we do is right, how it could be done differently or better, and then acting on it ("eigenlijk zoals we dat ook in het gewone leven zouden moeten doen").
