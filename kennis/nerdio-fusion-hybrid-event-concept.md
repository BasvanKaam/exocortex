# Nerdio Fusion: a hybrid vision + skill event concept

Bas's designed event concept that fuses the two formats into one multi-day program. Theme: "Connect the vision. Master the tools. Prove your expertise." Format: 2.5 days (evening pre-event + 2 full days).

Structure:
- Evening 'Kickstart': welcome drinks, lightning talks (vendor + top partner), a 20-minute live demo, optional certification pre-flight check, swag + breakout schedule builder. Purpose: energy and alignment before Day 1.
- Day 1 (Innovation Day): joint vendor keynote, customer/partner success panel, roadmap deep-dive, choose-2 breakout tracks (Modern EUC Strategy, Cloud Cost Optimization, Windows 365 in the Enterprise, Partner Playbook), optional CFO-ready business value workshop.
- Day 2 (Training Camp, 100% enablement, no sales): role-based hands-on lab tracks (AVD Pro: autoscaling/FSLogix/image management; Automation Architect: scripted actions/JSON editing/policy backup-restore; Windows 365 Essentials), a gamified live-lab challenge, and a mini express certification track with live exam slots and printed certificate + digital badge.

Reusable design devices he layers in: an 'Ask a Nerd' expert bar; a live build wall showing real-time attendee progress on a shared screen; gamification (points/tokens for sessions, labs and certification feeding a leaderboard and prizes, with a physical-token collectible mechanic); and a closing 'Master MicroHack' team challenge under time pressure (interchangeable with a mini certification). The throughline: pair strategic inspiration with hands-on mastery and visible, competitive proof of skill.
