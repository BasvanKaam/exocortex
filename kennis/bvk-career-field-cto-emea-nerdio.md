# Career milestone: Field CTO EMEA at Nerdio

Public press-release facts (announced publicly, not confidential).

- Bas van Kaam was appointed **Field CTO EMEA** at Nerdio (Chicago-based startup enabling MSPs and organisations to build a Cloud Practice on Microsoft Azure, with Windows Virtual Desktop in a prominent role).
- Prior background described: former CTO and Cloud Technologist; strategically, technically and organisationally involved in large transition and implementation projects; co-responsible for building out and professionalising the Salomon IT portfolio and bringing several propositions to market.
- Recognised by Microsoft as **Microsoft MVP in the Enterprise Mobility category** (covering WVD, Remote Desktop Services, etc.).
- Stated focus of the role: the European partner channel; talking to parties about current and future challenges; introducing Nerdio's cloud-native software; guiding, supporting and training organisations before, during and after their move to Azure, with Nerdio Manager for WVD and Nerdio for Azure.
