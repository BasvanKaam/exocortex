# Citrix Service Provider (CSP) program

The **Citrix Service Provider (CSP) Program** is a Citrix partner program for service providers who provide or resell hosted software services to end customers. It targets offsite, multi-tenant hosting (augmentable with on-premises equipment for SLAs). The end customer is not the licensee and does not manage or access the provider's management infrastructure.

CSP grants providers the 'right to use' Citrix products as the underpinning of their delivery infrastructure, with a monthly **'active subscriber'** pricing/licensing model — providers always get the most current product versions and only pay for actual end-user usage or active accounts in the previous calendar month.

Benefits include monthly service-provider licensing for complete mobile workspaces, fast entry into the DaaS market, active-subscriber pricing, volume discounts, no membership fee, no upfront commitments or minimum purchase, the right to offer free 30-day end-user evaluations, lower TCO with higher density, and access to reference architectures, the Center of Excellence, sales/marketing resources and Citrix Partner Central. Microsoft has supported the program (the two companies have worked together for more than 26 years), and CSP reporting is kept in sync with the Microsoft SPLA program.
