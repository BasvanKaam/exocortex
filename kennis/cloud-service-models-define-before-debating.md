# Define the cloud service model before debating its merits

Cloud is not one-size-fits-all. Before discussing pros, cons, advantages or disadvantages, first establish which model you mean: SaaS, PaaS, IaaS, or DaaS. The trade-offs (what the provider manages vs what your own IT still manages) differ per model, so an undefined "the cloud" debate goes nowhere.

With IaaS you still set up, configure and manage your own servers and OS; with PaaS the platform is delivered but apps, data and ongoing config/management remain yours. SaaS and DaaS remove most management but trade away control. The deeper you go toward SaaS/DaaS, the less you manage and the less you control.
