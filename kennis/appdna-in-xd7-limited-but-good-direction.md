# Limited AppDNA in XD7 is still a smart inclusion

Writing in June 2013, Bas liked Citrix bundling a cut-down AppDNA ('Automated App Migration Analysis') into XD7 and XA 6.5 Platinum to flag which apps are ready to virtualize. He's clear-eyed that it's far from the full AppDNA product ('it's limited in functionality'), but his stance is positive on the direction: 'I like what they did here.' He also flagged Seamless Local Apps as a pragmatic rescue for stalled VDI projects, asking rhetorically 'How often do VDI projects and alike get paused or cancelled because of one or two applications that won't perform on a VDI environment?!' For Bas, the new feature value was about removing real-world adoption blockers, not just spec-sheet additions.
