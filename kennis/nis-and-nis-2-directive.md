# NIS and NIS 2 directive

NIS 2 (Netwerk- en Informatiebeveiliging 2) is an EU directive aimed at strengthening cybersecurity and digital resilience of critical and essential sectors in the EU. It updates the original NIS directive from 2016.

The original **NIS 1 (2016)** was the first European cybersecurity legislation, requiring member states to draft national strategies and focusing mainly on essential service providers (energy, transport, telecom). In practice, implementation and enforcement varied strongly per country, leading to fragmented and often insufficiently effective protection.

The update to **NIS 2** was needed because cyber threats have grown significantly with more complex attacks and bigger impact on critical infrastructure. NIS 2 imposes stricter requirements on organizations important to the economy and society, expands the scope to more sectors and companies, and promotes uniform compliance rules across the EU. It also has stricter enforcement and sanctions: fines can be imposed, and although exact amounts vary per member state, they should be comparable to GDPR fines.

**Focus areas:** cyber resilience (robuster security measures), harmonized regulation (uniform level across member states), broader scope (more sectors covered), fast and mandatory incident reporting (cooperate with authorities), and stronger enforcement (higher fines, stricter compliance mechanisms).
