# Governance Definition and Strategic Hierarchy

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

Working definition used in the framework: for an enterprise or government organization, governance is "ensuring a strategic approach in context of longer-term organizational goals and sustainability, while executing day-to-day activities in a coordinated way."

That definition reveals a hierarchy: a strategic approach presupposes a strategy, from which organizational goals are derived, which in turn steer day-to-day decision-making and activities (strategic to tactical to operational).

A formal governance framework establishes chains of responsibility, authority and communication. It describes the roles of the people involved, their responsibilities, how they interact, and the general rules and policies governing the lifecycle of IT services.
