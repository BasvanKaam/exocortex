# NMW is built for enterprise scale; small shops should look elsewhere in the portfolio

Bas is candid about fit rather than selling to everyone: NMW for WVD is designed for larger enterprises (aim ~250 users and up), which is where the monthly minimum and the PaaS overhead stop mattering per-user. Below that, the fixed PaaS cost weighs too heavily per user. He also argues NMW doesn't require deep technical expertise, but you still need WVD fluency, which is where smaller shops and MSPs can fall short. To avoid Nerdio becoming a services company answering underlying-WVD questions, he'd steer those customers to a different product in the portfolio, claiming it works out better for both parties in 95%+ of cases. The stance: honest product-fit steering beats forcing a poor fit.
