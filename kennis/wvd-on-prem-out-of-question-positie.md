# On-prem WVD looks off the table, GA by summer 2019

May 2019. Two of Bas's clearest predictive reads from the workshop:

1. On on-prem WVD: Microsoft was investigating Azure Stack for it, but general on-premises use 'seems to be out of the question, at least that's my take on it', dependent on customer demand. He explicitly marks this as his own interpretation, not Microsoft fact.

2. On timing: he reported the workshop signal that 'WVD will go GA within 4 months from now, the summer of 2019.' (WVD did reach general availability on 30 September 2019, close to that window.)

These show his habit of separating what was stated from what he infers, and of committing to a datable prediction.
