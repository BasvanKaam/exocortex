# CGIT contributor pitch and roadmap (quality over quantity)

CGIT (Come Get IT) is run by Bas van Kaam, Martijn Verheij (Avensus) and Sander Bruijs (SBC Solutions). The internal pitch to recruit colleagues as contributors and the operating roadmap:

Why a colleague should contribute ("wat je er aan hebt"):
- Fun (side) projects alongside regular work.
- Your work gets published, which is rewarding.
- You get to know colleagues in a different way.
- It contributes to a possible community status.
- It adds something to your CV and you learn something new.

Roadmap / operating principles:
- Focus on quality instead of quantity.
- One or more podcasts per month, including joint recordings with EUCdigest.
- Multiple articles per month.
- Reduce from three to two events.
- Cross-pollination between initiatives.
- Website overhaul.

The model centers on cross-organization collaboration (multiple business units / companies feeding one shared platform) and personal/career upside as the recruitment lever for volunteers.
