# More Is Not By Definition Better

A personal principle Bas applies to content and, by extension, to how he works. Reflecting on the blog's first half-year (19 posts), he resists the reflex that more output is automatically better. Of course you can always do more, and he intends to, but in this case more is not by definition better, and judged by what the blog actually returned he could not be more satisfied.

He describes his preferred approach as simple, accessible, clear, and to the point: no long-winded stories, short but powerful with a clear message every time. This quality-over-quantity stance mirrors the way he frames consulting advice elsewhere.

He is openly a numbers person ('ik ben een statistieken man, ik hou van cijfers en overzichten'), which is why the Salomon 'meten = weten' (measuring is knowing) concept suits him so well. He values measurable results but also acknowledges the honest difficulty of quantifying what a blog actually delivers.
