# Test quality updates, not just feature updates

November 2016. Two recurring positions in this post:

1. Don't trust Microsoft's compatibility promises blindly. Microsoft claimed Windows 10 runs everything Windows 7 ran and needs no new hardware. His stance: 'no matter what they tell you, we all know that thoroughly testing your applications on new OS builds... is a must for all companies out there.'

2. Microsoft only talks about testing feature updates, but he repeatedly reminds readers that testing security/quality updates can be just as important. Quality updates are mandatory, but knowing in advance whether one breaks an app lets you deal with it beforehand or at least warn users.

Underlying pragmatism: you won't get rid of Windows any time soon, so 'adaption is key.' He deliberately leaves the actual strategy choice to the reader.
