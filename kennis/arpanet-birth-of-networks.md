# ARPANET (1969): the birth of networking

The history of Cloud computing does not begin with the Cloud as we know it, but with the first networks. ARPANET, created in 1969 and financed by the US government, was originally built to connect researchers at different universities so they could share information more easily. The biggest challenge at the time was that each university used different hardware and software, making communication between systems difficult, yet ARPANET laid the foundation for what would later become the internet.

On 29 October 1969 the first successful communication took place between UCLA (University of California, Los Angeles) and the Stanford Research Institute. The first attempt to send the text 'LOGIN' failed and only the letters 'LO' were received, but it was still a huge breakthrough in networking.
