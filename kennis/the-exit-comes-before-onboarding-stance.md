# Think about the cloud exit before you onboard

Bas teaches a cloud exit strategy as a first-class concern, captured in his slogan "the exit comes before the onboarding." He concedes in the practitioner notes that taken literally this is usually not realistic, it is a bonus if you genuinely manage it that early, but the underlying stance holds: it is only realistic to assume a moment will come when you want at least part of your applications, data or systems elsewhere (vendor lock-in, SLA breaches, price changes, a provider stopping a service or going under, data leaving the country, a strategy shift). Address it early and you will be glad you did. He insists an exit plan be reviewed by CTO/CIO/CFO and approved by the CEO, and that you focus on data and applications first, systems later.
