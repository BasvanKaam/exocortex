# SCAF Strategy Pillar: Workshop Delivery Sequence

The step-by-step sequence Bas uses to deliver the SCAF strategy pillar as a guided engagement (the choreography a consultant or trainer runs, distinct from the strategy content itself):

1. **Kick-off** - explain the approach, identify the people involved, the stakeholders and the teams.
2. **Workshop 1** - surface drivers and objectives (drijfveren, doelstellingen); allow space for discussion/debate.
3. **Data processing** - work the input through the intake form (invulformulier), a SWOT and a strategy map.
4. **Interviews** - gather additional input one-on-one.
5. **Workshop 2** - discuss the first outcomes with the group.
6. **Workshop 3** - determine and record the CCoE (Cloud Center of Excellence).
7. **Final report** - deliver and discuss the strategy-pillar end report (eindrapportage).

This ordering shows how the strategy phase is run as a facilitated cycle: workshop to open, then process/interview, then converge over two more workshops, ending on the CCoE and a report. It complements (and precedes) the Insight-phase workshop guidance.
