# Come Get IT platform and the role Bas wanted inside the group

Idea/proposition note. In his role-scope document for the Salomon/Avensus group, Bas sketched the role he wanted to carve out - an early blueprint for what later became his independent positioning.

The role he proposed:
- Coordinate the PL group and professionalize the 'Come Get IT' platform (website, podcast, event).
- Generate content and technical marketing: blogs, whitepapers, social media, and pull the business units along in it.
- Organize internal workshops and enthuse colleagues.
- Own the Salomon website and house style: design, content, copy, promotion. Plus a marketing plan.
- From his Cloud-plus-security specialization, organize external workshops/trainings (willing to co-invest in this himself).
- Event organization end to end: script ('draaiboek'), host, present, content, speakers.
- Act as a sounding board for proposition development across the business units.
- Be the evangelist for the group's product/service portfolio - at the client, at events.
- A 'technical pre-sales' role for himself ('hier hebben we het wel eens eerder over gehad').

The through-line: content engine + events + evangelism + his own training business, with him willing to co-invest. The same combination (content, podcast, event, evangelism, vendor-independent training) became the core of his later independent identity.
