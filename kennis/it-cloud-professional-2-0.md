# The IT (Cloud) Professional 2.0: from geek to strategic generalist

Bas argues the IT professional's role has changed drastically — from working behind the scenes to collaboration, strategic thinking and customer focus. The new "IT-professional 2.0" is a versatile generalist combining technical knowledge with strategic insight, who must constantly adapt and learn. He lays out a then-vs-now contrast with an "extra" skill for each axis:

- **Person**: withdrawn geek -> social, communicative, customer-focused. Extra: empathetic leadership.
- **Thinking**: technical -> functional, eye on business goals. Extra: data-driven decision-making.
- **Role**: specialist -> generalist across domains. Extra: problem-solving ability.
- **Character**: focused on one task -> creative, inventive, stubborn (eigenwijs). Extra: innovation orientation.
- **On the floor**: directed -> sees work and gets going. Extra: adaptive capacity.
- **Engagement**: supporting -> strategic. Extra: stakeholder management.
- **When**: pulling all-nighters/weekends -> goes for efficiency. Extra: continuous learning.
- **Workplace**: fixed office PC -> mobile, multiple devices. Extra: sense of responsibility.
- Plus: insight into cybersecurity & privacy, and strategic understanding of how tech serves the long-term vision.

Why it matters: technology now touches every layer of a business, so communication, collaboration, data-driven problem-solving, innovation, security awareness and continuous learning all become central. Reassuringly, Bas insists these can be learned — "als je denkt, dit ben ik niet, je kunt het leren!"
