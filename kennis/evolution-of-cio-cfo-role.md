# The Evolution of the CIO and CFO Role

Bas contrasts the IT-leadership role before and after ~2010/2024.

**Before 2010** — CIO/CFO focus was on cost control and operational efficiency. Organisation structured in silos; internal users were the main stakeholders. Decision-making was reactive, strategies aimed at short-term profit maximisation, with emphasis on servers, networks, and datacenters. Waterfall was the norm; innovation was (virtually) not on the agenda.

**2024/25 and beyond** — focus shifted to adding value and developing strategic initiatives that help the organisation respond to customer needs. Technology is no longer just a supporting function but a driving force behind innovation and strategic growth. C-level involvement has become proactive; executives act as strategic advisors. Talent is skilled and driven; tech focus shifts to mobile apps, SaaS, microservices, containers, and AI. Project management evolved from waterfall to agile and DevOps, and user experience became a continuously monitored success factor for customer satisfaction and loyalty.

Bas frames this with a quote: 'Cloud in al haar vormen is een manier van IT-bedrijven, geen strategie op zich.' He also concedes readers may not agree with every claim in his accompanying figure — the point is only that a big change has demonstrably happened and will keep manifesting.
