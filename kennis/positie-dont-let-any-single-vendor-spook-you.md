# Don't let any single vendor spook you

Bas's stance, set up by the title 'Laat je niet gek maken door Microsoft. Of Citrix of VMWare etc...': don't let any one vendor rattle or stampede you. Vendor congresses (Microsoft Inspire and equivalents from Citrix, VMware and every other vendor of size) are roadmap-and-marketing exercises designed to get partners selling. That is legitimate, but it means the messaging is commercially motivated, not neutral advice.

The implied posture is vendor-independence: take vendor roadmaps as input, not gospel, and keep your own judgment. This sits alongside his broader 'don't stress about the cloud' line and his vendor-independent advice positioning.
