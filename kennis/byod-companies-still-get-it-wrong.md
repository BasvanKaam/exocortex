# Most companies still get BYOD wrong - policy and management

In March 2020, at the start of mass work-from-home, Bas argued that BYOD remains a big unsolved challenge for most companies despite years of discussion. From where he stands, only a few have got it right from a company perspective. The failure is almost always two things at once: a decent company policy is nowhere to be found, and there is no solid, modern management solution in place.

His framing: browser-only access (HTML5) helps BYOD adoption because the endpoint requirement shrinks to a browser, but the technology does not fix the organizational gaps - policy and modern device management still have to be there.
