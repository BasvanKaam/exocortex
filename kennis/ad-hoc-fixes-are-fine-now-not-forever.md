# Ad-Hoc Fixes Are Fine for Now, Not Forever

Bas's position: the ad-hoc IT decisions made under crisis pressure were fine for the moment ('Prima voor nu'), but that does not mean it should be left there. Mistakes were inevitably made or things overlooked, which is not bad in such situations, but solutions that look good now can create new challenges and investments a few weeks or months later. The responsible move is to circle back, assess the impact, align the stop-gaps with strategy and policy, and turn lessons learned into being future proof so the next crisis can be met with more control. This 'don't let temporary become permanent by accident' stance is the throughline of his 2020 crisis writing.

Source: Bas van Kaam, Salomon IT blog, 2020-05-20.
