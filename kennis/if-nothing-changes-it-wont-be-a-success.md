# If Nothing Changes, It Won't Be a Success

Bas's blunt diagnosis of Salomon's execution problem. Even if everyone currently has an assignment, continuing as-is will not make it a success, let alone a big one. The proposition never quite comes together: they keep making agreements but keep failing to keep them. Extra effort has to go in.

He also questions the value of intermediaries — he experienced Tijn mainly as a slowing factor over the past two years and argues they should get things done in-house ("vanuit eigenbeheer"), needing little more than some software paid monthly. The deeper point: progress stays slow because everyone is too busy with assignments, and without dedicated technical staff the odds of doing more after delivering the Roadmap stay low.
