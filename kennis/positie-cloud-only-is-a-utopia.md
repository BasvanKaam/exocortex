# Cloud-only is a utopia; cloud-first or hybrid is the only honest answer

Bas's position, held consistently from 2019 into 2025:

- The cloud is not the answer to everything, and the major providers' marketing departments want us to believe otherwise. It is not sacred, and it is not the only valid way to do IT.
- A cloud-only world is a utopia for at least the next fifteen to twenty years, possibly longer.
- This is not an argument against cloud. The potential is enormous and the outlook is bright; the success stories are real but often exaggerated. Cloud is not a cure-all.
- Consultants and advisory firms should focus on what best fits the situation, and sometimes that is not the cloud. The seasoned consultant, architect or technologist should see through the marketing hype.
- Headline stats like "94 percent of respondents use the cloud" (Flexera, 2019) are marketing. Using a SaaS app or webmail is not the same as being cloud-only. Most companies use cloud where it makes sense, which is different from having transitioned.
- The verdict: cloud-first or hybrid, applied step by step, only where it adds value.
