# Positie: Citrix was inexplicably late adding delegated admin to XenDesktop

In Oct 2013 Bas welcomed full delegated administration in XenDesktop 7 but openly questioned why it took so long, given XenApp admins had custom roles all along: 'I wonder why Citrix waited this long to include it as far as XenDesktop is concerned. I can't imagine this being extremely complicated to implement.'

He also kept the post honest about its own depth: the feature is so straightforward that 'you don't need a manual to figure this one out', and he frames the whole piece as something he wrote because he was playing around with it, not because it was hard. The title 'I'm just saying' captures the lightly critical, shrug-tone stance: a reasonable expectation that should have been met earlier. A small example of him gently calling out a vendor gap while staying friendly.
