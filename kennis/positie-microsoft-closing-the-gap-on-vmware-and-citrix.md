# Positie: Microsoft is closing the gap on VMware and Citrix

In Oct 2013 Bas's recurring take across this Server 2012 R2 piece is that Microsoft is steadily catching up: on the hypervisor (Hyper-V vs VMware) and on mobility/storage (Work Folders, StorSimple vs ShareFile). On Hyper-V: 'In the end, Hyper-V will always be cheaper, and that's probably one of the biggest advantages they have over VMware.' On the broader picture: 'they're doing an excellent job in pursuing their rivals; the cap is getting closer by the day.'

He is deliberately even-handed and declares no hypervisor allegiance ('I don't have a personal preference when it comes to hypervisors, VMware, Hyper-V, or XenServer'). He rates Work Folders as 'a must-do technology' and StorSimple as 'pretty close to ShareFile'. As a Citrix specialist he is notably generous to Microsoft here, an early sign of the Microsoft-centric direction his career later took. Self-aware that the hypervisor purists will disagree ('some of the hypervisor gurus out there will probably not agree with me on that one').
