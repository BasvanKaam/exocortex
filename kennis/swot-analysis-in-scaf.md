# SWOT Analysis in SCAF

A SWOT analysis is a strategic-planning method that maps strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats — holding up a mirror to the organisation. Both individuals and companies use it to understand their current situation, future possibilities and shortcomings; Bas notes a good example is gaining insight into the current knowledge level (revisited in chapter 8). You usually begin a SWOT with a desired end goal or specific objectives in mind, which fits SCAF's approach.

Bas's take: although a SWOT looks simple, it often yields deeper insights than expected, such as hidden interests and priorities. It is mainly a practical way to quickly get an overview of the key factors that can influence the strategy. True to his 'keep it simple' philosophy, he created his own Word-document SWOT template.
