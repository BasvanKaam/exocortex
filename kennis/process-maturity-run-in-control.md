# Assess process maturity to reach 'Run in Control'

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

Rather than redesigning incident management wholesale, evaluate process maturity and determine which changes are needed to 'Run in Control'.

Key governance activities:

- Communicate about RACI models for future and transitional modes of operation.
- Report on the maturity gap and the activities to close it up to 'Run in Control' level.
- Allow specific changes in the process when the situation calls for it, through a dispensation route.

If you are contracting an MSP or introducing a new one, creating a detailed overview of responsibilities during the transitional phase is a very important to-do.
