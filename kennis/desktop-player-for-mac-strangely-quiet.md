# Why was Desktop Player for Mac so quiet?

Writing in March 2014 (and as a fresh, enthusiastic MacBook Air convert after 14 years on Windows), Bas was puzzled that Citrix Desktop Player for Mac generated so little buzz two months after release, despite Macs being popular. He turned it into an open question to readers, listing candidate explanations: maybe there are fewer Mac users than he thought, maybe people do not use private Macs for business or are not allowed to, maybe Desktop Player lacks marketing, maybe it lacks features, or maybe Macs are simply managed some other way. He suspected manageability was a big factor in why Macs had not been adopted as fast as tablets and smartphones. His own stance, echoed in the comments: Apples are a dream to work with but harder to manage, and BYOD will always be a mix-and-match process. He liked that vendors were trying to solve the administration problem.
