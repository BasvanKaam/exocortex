# IGEL UMS can manage Windows endpoints, not just IGEL thin clients

A point Bas flags as little-known in 2018: the IGEL Universal Management Suite (UMS) offers add-ons that let you centrally manage more than just IGEL thin clients, including Windows 7 and Windows 10 devices. There is also an IGEL Cloud Gateway add-on enabling remote management of devices outside the corporate network. The takeaway he pushes: IGEL by 2018 is best understood as a software/endpoint-management company, not merely a thin-client hardware vendor.
