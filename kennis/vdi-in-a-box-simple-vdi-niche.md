# VDI-in-a-Box served a real niche: simple, cheap, small-scale VDI

How it worked then (early 2015): VDI-in-a-Box (VIAB) was Citrix's appliance-style product for spinning up and managing a basic VDI without much hassle. Its value was not technical sophistication but simplicity: easy to set up, relatively cheap, and straightforward to manage if all you needed was a basic VDI infrastructure.

When Citrix axed it as part of a reorg, the gap mattered: many smaller companies were still on the lookout for a simple, cheap way to spin up and manage a VDI, and Citrix had nothing close to replacing it in their portfolio at the time. The lesson that outlasts the product: a 'flawed but simple' SMB tool can still be exactly what a large slice of the market wants.
