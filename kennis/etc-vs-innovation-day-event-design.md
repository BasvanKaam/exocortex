# Two event types: Training Camp vs Innovation Day

Bas's reusable distinction between two fundamentally different event formats, useful any time you're designing technical-community or partner events:

Enterprise Training Camp (ETC): technical deep-dive for existing customers and partners. Goal is enablement, not sales. Audience: current customers, partners, IT admins, engineers, cloud architects. Content: advanced topics (auto-scaling, FSLogix, image management, MSIX, scripting), full hands-on labs and real-world deployment walkthroughs. Structure: full-day, classroom-style, interactive. Commercial focus minimal; sales typically not present. Run independently. Outcome: practical skills, configuration know-how, operational confidence.

Innovation Day: strategic, forward-looking, co-hosted with the platform vendor (Microsoft). Goal is to inspire, inform and connect with new prospects through thought leadership. Audience: new business contacts, IT leaders, decision-makers, potential partners. Content: vision keynotes, roadmap overviews, customer stories, live demos, light hands-on. Structure: half- to full-day conference-style with networking. Commercial focus strong; sales and business development involved. Outcome: awareness, excitement, a pathway to new opportunities.

The design principle: keep enablement and commercial intent in separate tracks/days, because the audiences, content depth and the presence (or absence) of sales differ sharply. Mixing them dilutes both.
