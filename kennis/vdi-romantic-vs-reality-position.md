# VDI is more romantic on paper than in reality

Bas's recurring 2013 stance: VDI started as "a very promising technology" but "didn't flourish the way they'd hoped." He blames image-management complexity (especially dedicated desktops), storage requirements and the IOPS that come with them. On dedicated desktops he warns the solution "might seem a bit more 'romantic' on forehand than it turns out to be once you implement it."

His verdict on the fix vendors: PernixData FVP (server-side flash caching) he has "seen in action, it really works"; Atlantis ILIO is "innovative and ahead of its competition by miles" but the licenses "don't come cheap." The honest-broker tone is the position itself: enthusiastic about the tech, blunt about cost and complexity.

*Bron: blogpost 'VDI, storage and the IOPS that come with it. Part 1 & 2.' (2013-11-12), basvankaam.com.*
