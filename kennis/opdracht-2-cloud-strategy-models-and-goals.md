# Opdracht 2 - Tailored Cloud strategy: the puzzle of models and goals

Bas's second assignment, again for XYZ-Care Health Group with its mix of legacy systems, compliance challenges and need for scalability and flexibility. The standing instruction applies: do extra research where needed to drive action beyond reading.

The task: compare IaaS, PaaS, SaaS and DaaS (DaaS detailed in chapter 6) and determine which model or combination best fits XYZ-Care's needs and why; advise on hybrid vs public Cloud with emphasis on AVG (GDPR) compliance; and develop a mapping of fundamental Cloud principles and delivery models to XYZ-Care's key operational objectives.

The worked answer (Uitwerking): individual reflection helps team members analyse which principles and models fit the business goals and IT strategy. Linking fundamental principles (elasticity, scalability, self-service) to business goals - e.g. better care through more efficient access to the EPD (electronic patient record), or cost reduction via scalable solutions - creates clear strategic direction.

Bas's key method point: make these goals as specific and measurable as possible by attaching numbers - e.g. this action previously took X minutes, now Y; monthly costs for this service were previously ABC, last month XYZ. The team compares the models on flexibility, control, implementation time and technical complexity, considering XYZ-Care's specific dependencies (between the EPD, planning tool and reporting tool). It evaluates hybrid vs public Cloud considering AVG-compliance, patient-data sensitivity and data sovereignty, with concrete scenarios (e.g. sensitive data stays on-premises, other workloads in public Cloud). Finally it identifies migration readiness by exposing gaps in knowledge, tools or processes, testing chosen models against scenarios like peak-period scalability or hybrid compliance solutions.
