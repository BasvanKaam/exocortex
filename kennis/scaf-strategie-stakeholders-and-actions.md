# SCAF Strategie: Stakeholders, Actions and Results

**Stakeholders** crucial to the SCAF Strategie phase (varies per organisation): the board and directors (CEO, CFO, CTO, CIO), IT management / end-responsible person, senior (systems) architects, and a CISO and/or security analyst/engineer.

**Actions** planned over the first days/weeks (not exhaustive): one-on-one or group interviews; group-only workshops; collecting relevant documentation (digital preferred, e.g. email, or printed); references to websites/digital sources with vision/mission statements; and digitally recording all relevant information. Bas's guiding principle: "begin with the end in mind" — the desired end result is clear beforehand.

**Results** after completing the phase:
- Substantiated demonstration of the key drivers for choosing cloud, with discussion held and priorities assigned where needed.
- A clear picture of the desired result, expectations and their alignment with business objectives, written clearly, possibly visualised, and agreed by both parties.
- Strategic/other partners mapped.
- The IT-business relationship made clear, with action taken as needed.
- Current IT (security) policy, mission, vision and strategy discussed and recorded.
- Challenges, risks and concerns inventoried — a SWOT analysis can help.
- Everyone aligned ("alle neuzen dezelfde kant op"), with agreements made and next phase scheduled.
- Innovation and running projects recorded separately (see Inzicht); a strategy map produced; the next evaluation moment planned.
