# Citrix Diagnostic Toolkit and Supportability Pack collections

Two large collections of free Citrix(-related) tool sets, with overlap:
- Citrix Supportability Pack (CTX203082): updated April 2016 with tools including the AppDisks diagnostics tool, Audio Volume Persistence Tool, Database Sizing Tool, Foreground Lock Timeout Tool, SmartHub, VDA Cleanup Utility, and PreSCAN.
- Citrix Diagnostic Toolkit (CTX135075).
