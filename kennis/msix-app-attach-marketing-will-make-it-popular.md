# App attach is rebranded layering, but Microsoft's marketing will make it win

Bas's stance, May 2019. He repeatedly punctures the novelty: app attach 'brings us all the perks that Application Layering and App-V have been doing for years' and 'the concept is the same' as application layering, point by point (streamed from a file server, no deployment servers, VHD mounted via filter drivers). He's skeptical of the claimed advantages: reusing MSIX packages only helps 'in theory' because 'MSIX packages don't just fall from the sky', and on the no-performance-impact claim he says 'we'll just have to wait and see/find out'.

But he predicts it wins anyway on distribution, not invention. He draws the parallel to WVD ('the whole world suddenly has an interest in Virtual Desktops, it's like they've never heard of VDI, DaaS') and concludes app attach 'might become overly popular as well. Microsoft certainly has the marketing tools to get the job done.' His framing: eventually it becomes the native default for WVD apps, '(abstraction) layers all the way down. From the user profile/data to applications.'
