# App virtualization is still the best way to deliver traditional apps in a hybrid world

Bas argues that for most companies a full move to the cloud is a long-term effort, so they will choose a hybrid solution; too many applications cannot move to the cloud for various reasons. He cites data residency and legal constraints ('wet en regelgeving') and technical reasons like latency to the backend or peripherals. Some technical limits are solvable, but not for all apps any time soon. His conclusion: in his view, certainly in a hybrid situation, application virtualization is still the best way to deliver 'traditional' applications. On desktop virtualization's role: there are valid use cases, but when delivering traditional apps to users who already have a workplace, the user is better served by being able to launch applications 'locally'.
