# Nerdio self-service portal: delegated machine control for power users

Bas's explainer for the use case where AVD power users or developers need more control over their own machines. Through the Nerdio Manager self-service portal, admins keep full control over which image and machine types users can choose from, while users can:

- Stop, start, restart their machine(s) and log off their session(s).
- Resize their machine(s), including storage.
- Re-image and restore their machine(s).

Machines can also be set to auto-restore when necessary or desired. The point he makes: it gives flexibility to demanding users without the admin losing governance over the estate.
