# What a Proposition Must Contain

Bas's checklist for what a productized service proposition needs before it goes to market. A proposition is not ready until it has:

- A standard approach with template documents, partially pre-filled
- A clear problem statement plus the offered solution
- A point of differentiation (onderscheidend vermogen)
- A slogan
- A standard slide deck: roughly 2 slides on who Salomon/the team is and what they stand for (slogan + portfolio in brief), and max 3 slides on the actual product or service
- Market-demand research and a defined target group
- A digital flyer, a news item, multiple blog posts, and data sheet(s)
- A LinkedIn campaign

The underlying idea: standardize and package the service so it can be sold repeatedly, rather than reinventing it per deal. He explicitly wants the existing Roadmap service standardized and the portfolio broadened with multiple propositions, ideally adjacent to the Roadmap service.
