# Why high availability and DR matter: ransomware and data hostage

How you get data into the cloud and use it matters little if you don't ensure it is stored safely and consistently. The key questions: what happens during an outage - is your data still available or does the business grind to a halt? How fast must you be operational again (what requirements do you set)? And how do you protect data against unauthorised access, loss, or - worst case - being held hostage by ransomware? Once data is taken hostage you lose control and are often forced to pay ransom to get it back.

To prevent this, high availability is essential: data and systems set up to stay available even if an entire Azure datacenter fails. A good disaster recovery plan provides a 'plan B' for major disruptions, e.g. mirroring data to multiple locations (Azure datacenters or even regions) or automated, quickly restorable back-ups - keeping data safe and the business running in emergencies.
