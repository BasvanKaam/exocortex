# Receiver Clean-Up utility

The Receiver Clean-Up utility helps when: errors occur during upgrade from an earlier Receiver or Online Plug-in; unexpected behaviour/performance occurs after such an upgrade; or a Receiver upgrade is impossible due to feature incompatibility and a clean uninstall is required. It removes components, files, and registry values of Online Plug-in 11.x/12.x and Receiver for Windows 3.x/4.x (Online Plug-in 13.x/14.x), including the Offline Plug-in component if installed. Reference: CTX137494.
