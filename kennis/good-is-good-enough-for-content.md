# Good Is Good Enough: Ship Content, Don't Polish to Perfection

Bas's stance on content production efficiency. Getting production-ready faster matters: good is good enough, it doesn't have to be perfect down to the last detail. Perfectionism costs too much time, and there is nobody who trips over it or even notices. In other words: increase efficiency by accepting "good enough" and shipping.

He pairs this with concrete anti-patterns to avoid: episodes that linger too long on one topic (inefficient, wastes time) and a single-person editing bottleneck that is a single point of failure.
