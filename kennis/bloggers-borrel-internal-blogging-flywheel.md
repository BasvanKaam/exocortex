# Method: the Bloggers Borrel as an internal blogging flywheel

A recurring informal session to grow blogging culture inside a company: get people together, have each briefly present why they blog, how they started, what their goal is, and what they expected. Then open the floor, review each other's work (set up a light process for it), and discuss what topics will get attention in the coming period.

Ground rules he applies to the format: plan two months ahead, pick a location that is well reachable and not your own office, keep the core presentation under 10 minutes, and prepare or pre-circulate discussion questions so people can come ready. The explicit purpose is 'vooral om van elkaar te kunnen leren', above all to learn from each other.

This is the same playbook he later credits with growing Detron from 2-3 to 12 active bloggers.
