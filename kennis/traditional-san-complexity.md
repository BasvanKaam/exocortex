# Why traditional SANs are complex for virtual workloads

Traditional storage networks (SANs) are complex: switches, disk arrays, fibre-optic HBAs. They are not very flexible to scale, often need a dedicated specialist team for configuration, expansion and troubleshooting, and carry expensive support contracts. They were not designed for today's virtualized workloads and the IOPS demand that comes with them. Performance-boosting products exist (e.g. PernixData, Atlantis) but remained, in 2014, far from ideal. This is the problem space that converged/hyperconverged platforms set out to solve.
