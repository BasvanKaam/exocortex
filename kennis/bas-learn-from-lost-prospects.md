# Bas's contrarian tip: get feedback from prospects you lost, not just customers

While describing how an Innovation Board gathers external feedback, Bas offers a sharp opinion: instead of only asking existing customers, approach the companies that (almost) became customers but did *not*. In his view you learn more from them. Customers are already customers — they wouldn't be if they weren't reasonably satisfied, and they're unlikely to leave over a single late fix or a missing feature, so their feedback is softer. Organisations that chose *not* to buy come with concrete reasons you can actually act on, or angles you may never have considered before.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> Waarom niet de bedrijven die (net) niet klant zijn geworden benaderen en vragen om feedback, daar leer je naar mijns inziens meer van.
