# Stance: the best technical speakers bridge IT and the business and cut to the essence

In praising Erwin Derksen, Bas lays out what he values in a speaker: someone who acts as the bridge between IT and "de business", who can reduce complex matters back to their essence, and who presents with a strong dose of humour, a practical mindset and preferably bulletpoint-free slides ("bij voorkeur bulletpoint-free presentaties"). This is Bas's implicit rubric for good technical communication, not just an event blurb.
