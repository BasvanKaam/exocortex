# Scripting every video first measurably raises quality

Bas's stance, learned through 2025 hands-on production: every video should start from a *finalized* script containing dialogue, pacing, and visual direction before recording. He states this practice 'really improves the overall quality as I have come to learn throughout 2025.' Likewise, separating scripts into their own version-controlled store (so edits don't break video/audio sync) is a deliberate discipline, not bureaucracy.

Adjacent stance: strict editorial and formatting standards feel annoying in the moment but are what set the team up to deliver at a high standard. Consistency is engineered, not accidental.
