# Byte-Sized Cloud: A Community-Written Book Project

Bas van Kaam (then at Salomon IT) and Christiaan Brinkhoff (Microsoft) co-curated a book focused 100% on 'Cloud' in the broadest sense: 'Byte-Sized: Cloud design principles and architectural recommendations'.

The book was written largely by IT community members from around the world, from helpdesk staff up to CTO/CEO level: 140 people from 20 countries contributed. It is available through all online Amazon channels and was described as a major success. The book was used as a giveaway for attendees of the Come Get IT event on 4 July 2019.

This is a marker of his community-organizer role: co-curating a large international community publication alongside a Microsoft figure.
