# Certs are worth it, and the best study material isn't always Citrix's

In 2013 Bas was openly invested in certification, both personally and for his employer, and treated it as a route worth mapping out for readers. His stance went beyond Citrix's own catalogue: he steered people to community and third-party resources he rated highly, notably Trond Eirik Haavarstein's xenappblog.com ('some of the best Citrix and App-V training material you'll ever come across, a small investment with a big payoff for sure') and the free Citrix Support Forums ('If you can't get your questions answered here, it's pretty much hopeless'). This is an early sign of Bas as a community-connected practitioner who points to other people's good work rather than gatekeeping, and who valued hands-on/free learning alongside official paid training.
