# Plan the cloud exit before you onboard

Bas takes the stance that a cloud exit strategy is not an afterthought but a prerequisite: 'the exit comes before the onboarding.' You decide how you'll get out before you commit to getting in.

He argues the exit is cheap insurance against expensive failure — like a costly car insurance you'd rather not pay for but are grateful for when something goes wrong. The risks he weighs are concrete and largely commercial/legal rather than purely technical: SLA erosion, unilateral price changes, loss of Dutch data residency, and outright provider bankruptcy. The implication is that organizations rushing into cloud without an exit plan are accepting lock-in risk they have not priced.
