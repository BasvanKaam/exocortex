# XenDesktop 7 editions and licensing models

How it worked at launch. XenDesktop 7 came in VDI, App, Enterprise and Platinum editions. Three licensing models:
- **Per-user**: access from an unlimited number of devices.
- **Per-device**: unlimited users from a single device.
- **Concurrent**: tied to a session, not a user or device. A license is checked out when a session launches and returned to the pool on logoff.

The App edition was a near-replacement for XenApp: publishes Hosted Shared Desktops, apps on VMs or RDS, and supports App-V for offline apps. It was free for XenApp 6.5 Enterprise/Platinum customers with valid Subscription Advantage (could run alongside an existing XenApp 6.5 farm), but XenApp 6.5 *Advanced* license owners had to pay.
