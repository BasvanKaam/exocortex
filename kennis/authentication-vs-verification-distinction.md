# Bas's distinction: authentication versus verification

Bas calls out a precise distinction he uses throughout the book (an FMA fact): authentication and verification are not the same thing.

- Authentication is making sure somebody is who they claim to be.
- Verification (he also uses 'validation') is finding out which resources are assigned to the user, i.e. their permissions.

During login the Delivery Controller plays a role in both, a process also called connection brokering.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> Note how I mention user authentication and user validation. There is a difference. Authentication is to make sure that somebody is who he or she claims to be. Verification is done to find out which resources are assigned (permissions) to the user.
