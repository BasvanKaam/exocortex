# Salomon's digital-experience measurement proposition

A Salomon IT Solutions service offering built on a simple argument: a poor digital workplace demotivates an employee, a demotivated employee works worse, and the company therefore loses efficiency. So the end-user experience (EUX) matters, and the way to know whether it is good is to make it visible by measuring it - 'meten is weten'.

The method, as described by Eric Kuiper: use specialist software to put a number on the user's digital experience. It does not just look at how each component of the central infrastructure performs in isolation, but at how the components work together, and assigns a health score. A dashboard then shows per user whether an incident exists or is about to arise, and where it sits. This shortens time-to-resolution: when a user reports a slow workplace, you can see immediately where the problem is and fix it in a more targeted, faster way, so the employee does not get demotivated and the company gains efficiency.

Beyond live monitoring, all data is analysed into a findings report that makes both the end-user experience and the risks visible. Each piece of advice is discussed in terms of its purpose/necessity and the measures that can be taken, and the output feeds a roadmap so progress becomes measurable.
