# Criteria for Selecting the First Workload to Migrate

When teaching SCAF, Bas gives concrete criteria for choosing the very first workload to migrate, so the first move is a controlled, low-risk win rather than a big-bang. You do not need to finish inventory and rationalization before you start; pick a first candidate in parallel with the second, more detailed analysis round.

The ideal first workload has:
- **Minimal dependencies** on other assets or platforms.
- **No access to or interaction with secure/sensitive data.**
- The ability to **start small and be broken into pieces.**
- **Extensive test possibilities**, so you can validate the approach safely.

While this first workload runs, keep rationalizing, prioritizing and scheduling the next workloads (roughly the first ten) for migration. Rationalization does not have to happen all at once; you can deliberately limit the choices, then come back for a deeper second-round analysis.
