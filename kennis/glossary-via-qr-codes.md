# Per-chapter references and QR-code glossaries

A characteristic design choice: after each chapter Bas includes a reference list and a glossary (begrippenlijst) of jargon used in that chapter, including search tips on where to find more information. Via a QR code you can navigate directly on your phone to the relevant glossary.

He chose this for two reasons: first, it works very well and is a cool concept; second, it let him make the physical book almost 40 pages thinner without losing the information (it just disappears from the physical book). Because chapters need not be read in order, some terms deliberately recur per chapter.
