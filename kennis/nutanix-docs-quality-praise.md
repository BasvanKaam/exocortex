# Nutanix's documentation is so good it's hard to find something to add

Writing as a Nutanix Technology Champion in Jan 2017, Bas opens by saying the hardest part of blogging about Nutanix is finding a topic, because there is too much to choose from AND because 'their own documentation is just outstanding.' He singles out their fact-sheets, website and Steven Poitras's The Nutanix Bible (which he quotes directly for the HA section). His stance: Nutanix has set a bar on documentation and community content quality that few vendors match, and a blogger almost competes with the vendor's own material. Reflects his admiration for the company at this point and explains why he leans on the Bible rather than reinventing it.

*Bron: blogpost 'New in AOS 5.0: Nutanix Acropolis File Services' (2017-01-05), basvankaam.com.*
