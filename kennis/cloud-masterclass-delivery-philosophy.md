# Cloud Masterclass: delivery philosophy

How Bas runs the room: slides with images, text and tables, but deliberately heavy on questions, comments, feedback, discussion, examples and whiteboarding so the group learns from each other. Plain language, no fluff, a no-nonsense approach, with generous breaks and catering. He frames the material's provenance as hundreds of hours of research and hands-on experience, tested against practice, drawing on colleagues, specialists, partners, community members, books and other trainings. Interactive checkpoints recur throughout the deck ("Wie zijn jullie?", "Hoe ver zijn jullie?", "What else?", "Discussie!") to keep it a dialogue rather than a lecture.
