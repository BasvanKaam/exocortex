# Opdracht 6: The Mirror of Enterprise Architecture (EA Self-Assessment)

Bas's exercise "De Spiegel Van Enterprise Architectuur: Een Reflectie Op Invloed En Samenwerking" is a team self-assessment of EA's role and influence. Method: each team member scores six statements **individually** on a 1-5 scale (1 = not at all applicable, 5 = fully applicable), then the team discusses results together to surface differences in perception and concrete improvement points. ("Niet vooruitkijken!" - don't peek ahead at the answer.)

The six statements (sum gives a total score):
1. Our EA team has great influence on IT investments.
2. Our EA team is described via principles, standards and patterns rather than piles of documentation.
3. Our EA team is recognized and put forward by the business as the party to support driving changes.
4. Our EA team has been responsible for (significantly) reducing complexity in the organization.
5. The way we integrate 'everything as a service' is driven by EA.
6. All our projects are compliant with our EA principles.

**Score interpretation:**
- 6-14: short-term success, but risk of long-term integration problems.
- 15-24: successes achieved with Cloud and EA, but long-term strategy and integration still need attention.
- 25-30: give the EA team a leading role in researching and implementing Cloud solutions.

Next steps: collect results, identify trends, map perception differences, run a workshop with broader stakeholders (IT, management, business), establish EA principles if none exist yet, and set up a monitoring/evaluation plan with assigned responsibilities.
