# Plan the Exit Before You Commit

Bas argues it is only realistic to assume a moment will come when you want (at least part of) your applications, data and systems somewhere else, so you should prepare for the exit even while entering a long-term relationship with a provider. You do not expect to leave, but you must account for it, and if it ever comes to that you will be glad you addressed it early. It is mostly a matter of weighing the risks against the benefits. This reframes 'exit planning' from pessimism into prudent strategy.

Source: Bas van Kaam, Salomon IT blog, 2019-08-28.
