# Mobile management acronyms: MDM, MAM, MIM, EMM

Vendor-neutral terms that get confused:
- **MDM** (Mobile Device Management): monitor, secure and manage the physical device via configuration/data policies.
- **MAM** (Mobile Application Management): manage and isolate the corporate *apps* (containerization), separating business from personal apps and data. Bas saw this becoming the preferred standard.
- **MIM** (Mobile Information Management): secure access to and editing of corporate *data* across devices (Citrix's example: ShareFile).
- **EMM** (Enterprise Mobility Management): the umbrella combining all of the above.

This taxonomy is durable even though the products around it have changed.
