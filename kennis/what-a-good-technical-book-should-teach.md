# A technical book should teach the why, not just the clicks

Bas's standard for technical educational content, articulated through this review: an admin buying an IT book wants to learn the technology, how it works and why, not just click-next-click-finish steps. For XenMobile that means the basics he expected and found missing: application wrapping and signing, the MDX technology, the MDX Vault, micro VPNs, InterApp, the Worx app suite, licensing, overall data flow and architectural overviews. His complaint was that the book showed how to deploy an app via AppController but not why it is secure or what technology is involved. He also warned against over-using screenshots: they ate space that could have explained concepts, and the troubleshooting section was under a page. Useful as a checklist of what good instructional content must include.

*Bron: blogpost 'Book review! Citrix XenMobile Mobile Device Management, published by Packt.' (2014-03-16), basvankaam.com.*
