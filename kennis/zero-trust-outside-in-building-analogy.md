# Zero Trust Works From the Outside In (Building Analogy)

Bas explains that Zero Trust works 'van buiten naar binnen' (from the outside in): you don't just protect the network's perimeter, you apply security at every level down to the core of your data and applications.

His analogy: imagine your network is a building. Traditionally you secure the front door well (and maybe the back) but once inside, anyone can move around freely. With Zero Trust you secure not just the front door but check every room and corridor, over and over. Everyone must re-identify to go further, even if they are already inside. This makes it far harder for intruders to reach the most important data, even after passing the first 'door'.
