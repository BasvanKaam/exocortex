# Risk Register (Risicoregister)

A risk register is a document or database in which all identified risks of a project, organisation or system are recorded and managed. It helps organisations deal with risk in a structured, clear way by describing each risk and monitoring its status. It holds information on the nature of the risk, the likelihood it occurs, the possible impact, and the planned measures to control or reduce it. Colour codes are often used to create a clear overview; the rest of the information is written out as text.

Typical elements:
- Risk description (short)
- Risk owner (person/department responsible)
- Likelihood and impact assessment
- Control measures (beheersmaatregelen) to limit or avoid the risk
- Status (e.g. in behandeling, gecontroleerd, geëscaleerd)
- Date of last review/update

Purpose: make risks visible to stakeholders, provide accountability over how risks are managed, support decision-making, and ensure control measures are executed and monitored. Bas calls it "een eenvoudig maar doeltreffend hulpmiddel" (a simple but effective tool).
