# Position: Don't Let MSP Tooling Choices Create Lock-In

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

the framework warns that when working with an MSP, tooling choices must not create an unwanted lock-in. The stance is to design the platform and its integrations deliberately rather than inheriting the supplier's stack by default, keeping the demand organization free to switch partners.

He pairs this with the insistence that the importance of the design and integrations be fully understood and supported by the business, so IT does not go at it alone. The recurring imperative: think ahead, define the areas of importance, and design and build for them rather than reacting after lock-in has set in.
