# The 'Internet Minute' and global data growth

The 'Internet minute' captures how much data is uploaded to the internet every minute: nearly 10 million gigabytes per minute. Recent analyses (source: exploidingtopics.com) predict global annual data production will reach 181 Zettabytes by 2025, driven by general growth, datacenters, AI and Big Data. One Zettabyte equals one billion Terabytes. These are projections based on current trends; actual volumes can vary due to technological breakthroughs, policy changes or shifts in user behaviour. At the time of writing, an estimated 67 percent of the world's population has internet access (source: ITU), leaving room for further growth.
