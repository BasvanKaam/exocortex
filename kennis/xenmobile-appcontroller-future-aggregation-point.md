# App Controller will eclipse StoreFront as the aggregation point

Writing in April 2014, ahead of Synergy, Bas read the tea leaves and predicted that Citrix's App Controller would grow to play a bigger, possibly dominant role in application delivery, potentially superseding StoreFront/Web Interface as the aggregation point for Windows, Mobile and Web apps. He quoted a community blog saying exactly that and noted, pointedly, that Worx Home wasn't mentioned once in it: 'Interesting right? Worx Home isn't mentioned once throughout the whole article :-) so which one is it going to be?'

He was careful not to over-claim: 'This post isn't meant to cause any confusion and I'm also not recommending one solution over the other.' His honest framing about the messiness is part of the take: 'This is where it potentially gets complicated and a lot of people lose track of what to configure under which conditions, me included.' Context: this prediction reflects his sense that Citrix was mid-pivot on its mobility client strategy, and that change was clearly coming.
