# The Triple-Whammy of Service Delivery Tooling Integration

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

Cloud changes service delivery technology, and often an MSP becomes the supply partner while internal IT is (or pivots to) the demand organization. This creates what the framework calls a "triple whammy": integrating service delivery tooling across three parties, the CSP and/or MSP and internal IT.

Concrete integration challenges between the organization and its CSP(s)/MSP(s) include financial systems, ticketing systems, CMDB data, deployment scripts, and code repositories. These cross-party seams are where platform design has to do its hardest work.
