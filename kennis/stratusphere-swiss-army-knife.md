# Stratusphere UX is the Swiss Army knife of the portfolio

Bas's lasting framing of Liquidware Stratusphere UX: he 'often referred to it as being the swiss army knife of the Liquidware portfolio.' His favourite thing about it is that you use only what you need when you need it, you don't have to collect all data or monitor every component all the time, so you're never overwhelmed with detail unless you choose to be. He loved presenting it precisely because it does so much that he almost always had to choose what to leave out of a deep-dive session.

Written just after leaving Liquidware (June 2018), the post is openly enthusiastic about a former employer's tech, and he's transparent about the conflict of interest ('Yeah, I know, I worked for Liquidware before'). Notable also: Stratusphere was positioned as a tool to proactively manage Spectre and Meltdown remediation performance impact, a topical concern at the time.
