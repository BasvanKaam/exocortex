# Price is not always the most important thing in a deal

Bas takes the stance that in a sales process price is not always the deciding factor, and that discounting is a symptom of mispricing rather than a tool: "Als je korting moet geven ben je eigenlijk altijd al te duur." The consequence he draws is that the work is in answering or rebutting the customer's arguments cleverly, and in becoming more successful with existing customers, not in racing to the lowest number.
