# Office vs Office 365 vs Office 365 ProPlus: what is what

Bas untangles three things organizations routinely confuse.

**Office 365** is a subscription service giving you the most recent Microsoft productivity apps (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote, Outlook, Publisher, Access). Plans exist for home/personal, SMB, enterprise, education and non-profit, with more or less functionality depending on the plan.

**Office 365 ProPlus** is the full Microsoft Office, installable on a physical or virtual workplace, available standalone (subscription) or as part of an Office 365 plan. No internet connection required to use it, but the extra services and built-in functionality run well ahead of the "plain" Office.

**Traditional Office (2010, 2013, 2016, 2019)** is a one-off purchase (Capex), no new functionality after release (only security updates), and you must repurchase to upgrade.

Key differences ProPlus/365 vs Office 2019: 365/ProPlus gets latest features and security updates automatically (weekly/monthly via Azure cloud) and bundles extras like Skype minutes and OneDrive storage; 365/ProPlus is subscription (Opex, 365 monthly / ProPlus yearly) vs Office 2019 one-off (Capex); 365/ProPlus runs in lockstep with the Windows 10 Semi Annual Channel while Office 2019 aligns with the Long Term Servicing Channel (hence the big functionality gap); 365/ProPlus activates online per user, Office 2019 uses KMS or MAK.

Dated: tied to the 2019-era product lineup and naming (ProPlus was later renamed Apps for enterprise).
