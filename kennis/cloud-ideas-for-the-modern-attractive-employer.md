# Cloud ideas for the modern, attractive employer

Bas offers a catalogue of creative ways employers can use cloud technology to attract new staff and support existing staff in professional and personal development. In an increasingly competitive labour market, younger generations don't just want a job but a place where freedom, autonomy and personal growth are central.

- **Work from Anywhere** policy — let staff choose where they work (Bali coffee shop, Lisbon coworking space, or just home near the kids).
- **Virtual onboarding** — via Zoom/Teams plus a personalised onboarding dashboard with tasks, trainings, contacts, and intro videos sharing the company's mission and values; a warm welcome even remotely.
- **Connection tools** — Slack, Teams, Zoom for virtual coffee breaks, informal meetups, Friday drinks, and virtual team-building (online escape rooms, cooking workshops, game nights).
- **Development** — cloud LMS platforms like LinkedIn Learning, Udemy for Business ("goede ervaringen mee") and Coursera for Teams, with personalised learning paths.
- **Creative tooling** — Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma, plus Photoshop, Canva Pro or Google Analytics for designers and marketers.
- **Hybrid working** with the office as a "collaborative hub" for brainstorms and team meetings.
- **Virtual rotation programmes** and **experience weeks** (shadowing other departments via Zoom/Teams).
- **Hackathons / innovation contests**, **mentorship programmes** (platforms like Chronus, MentorcliQ), **VR team-building**, personalisable virtual workspaces, **wellness programmes** (Headspace, Calm, FitBit), **Lunch & Learn / pizza sessions**, **gamification** (badges, points, leaderboards), and **virtual volunteering programmes**.

Bas's framing: by using (not all of, but) these options companies show they value flexibility, development, wellbeing and collaboration, helping attract talent and build an engaged, loyal, motivated workforce.
