# CGIT x LoginTechcast Collaboration Idea

Eric and Sander (LoginTechcast), plus other community members (Jits Langedijk, Ingmar Verheij), planned to continue after leaving Login Consultants. Both Martijn and Bas spoke with them; both sides are positive about working together, and it isn't limited to the podcast. Abram is very interested in a vendor-independent event.

No concrete plans worked out yet, but candidate forms of collaboration: co-hosting, sharing content, blogs, a vendor-independent event, sharing editing of material, and backing each other up. The idea: mutual reinforcement that could be a good boost for CGIT.
