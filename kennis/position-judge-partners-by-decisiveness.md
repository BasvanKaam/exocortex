# Position: Judge a Partner by Decisiveness, Not Just Interest

On a stalled plan with Marcel Kornegoor (newly promoted to director of NT Computing), Bas held back rather than chasing: he found Marcel not very decisive so far and the relationship one-directional, so he chose to wait and see. His read is that stated willingness to proceed is worth little without follow-through, and that one-way effort is a signal to slow down rather than invest more.
