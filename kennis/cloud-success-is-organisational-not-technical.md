# Cloud success is an organisational and adoption problem, not a technical one

His position, visible across the 2020 blog backlog: the hard part of cloud is not the technology but the organisation around it. The gap between IT and the business, governance, strategy, the move from reactive to proactive IT, the business case, adoption, communication, and ambassadors are where the real work and the real value sit. He frames an explicit blog topic as 'using cloud: everything except the technology, and its impact on your organisation', and pairs strategy with discipline: 'only ask questions if you know what you will do with the answers.'

This also includes a measured AI take: AI does not deliver a 12-hour work week (a pushback on hype attributed to James Ma), but smarter use of technology can still win a lot.
