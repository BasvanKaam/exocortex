# Make the Best of the Crisis: Prepare for Permanent Remote

Bas predicts that while part of the home-working population will return to the office once allowed, a large group of permanent (or several-days-a-week) home workers will be added. His position: rather than treating the crisis arrangements as temporary, organizations should make the best of this period and prepare now, 'Waarom niet het beste van deze periode maken en je hier alvast op voorbereiden?!' This is the same future-proofing argument that runs through his crisis-era posts: turn ad-hoc into deliberate.

Source: Bas van Kaam, Salomon IT blog, 2020-04-01.
