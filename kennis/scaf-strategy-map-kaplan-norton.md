# Strategy Map (Kaplan-Norton) within SCAF Strategy phase

The SCAF Strategy template embeds a Strategy Map - a visual representation of the client's strategic business goals and their interconnections - created during the Strategy phase and used as a reference frame for the rest of the process. It can be filled in jointly with the client or built from earlier-collected information and discussed later. It organizes business goals and priorities across the planning and execution phases.

Origin: Robert S. Kaplan and David P. Norton. The four perspectives the model focuses on (especially for profit organizations):
- Financial (revenue growth, productivity).
- Customer (product leadership, customer intimacy, operational excellence).
- Internal processes (growth and innovation, customer relations, operational excellence, stakeholder relations).
- Organization / learning and growth (skills and knowledge).

Alongside it, a SWOT analysis (strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats) is used as a fast strategic-planning instrument that starts from a desired end-state, fitting SCAF's goal-oriented approach. Available as a Word document.
