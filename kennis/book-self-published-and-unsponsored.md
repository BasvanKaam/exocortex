# The book is self-published, unsponsored, built on 25 years of experience

Bas states in his disclaimer that the book is fully independent and self-produced ("in eigen beheer"), built on 25 years of IT experience across a wide range of roles. He carefully did his research over the years but does not claim to be error-free and accepts no liability; "Ik heb mijn best gedaan, zoveel is zeker."

Crucially, the book is free of any form of sponsoring. Every company, brand, product and technology he names or references stands apart from the book; all topics were chosen and worked out by him, and represent his own experiences, successes, failures and tips. All tables and images he made himself, citing sources where applicable. ISBN: 9789090393988.
