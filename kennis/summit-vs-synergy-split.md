# Citrix Summit vs Synergy, and the 2014 split

How it worked then (2014): Citrix ran two distinct conferences with different purposes.

- **Summit** is the partner/channel event: Solution Advisor programs, partner status (silver/gold/platinum) requirements, certification, sales-level requirements and revenue. From 2014 Citrix split Summit and Synergy into separate conventions and made Summit more technology-oriented under a "Better Together" slogan that folded in (pre)sales. Even so, sessions were prepared with pre-sales in mind, so the balance was roughly 60/40 partner-vs-technical, not the 50/50 some technicians expected.
- **Synergy** is the deep technical event with genuine deep dives.

In the same move Citrix retired the European Summit/Synergy events, replacing them with multiple one-day, partner-oriented European events, a loss many regretted. Bas notes Summit was under NDA, so attendees could not share what was presented.
