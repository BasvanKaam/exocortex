# Good, fast AND cheap is achievable, not an impossible triangle (with IGEL)

Bas builds this Dutch article (Nov 2018) around the classic "good, fast, cheap, pick two" tension and argues that with IGEL plus Citrix you can actually have all three ("het kan dus wel degelijk"). Good: Citrix Ready / HDX Ready certification guarantees graphics, real-time audio/video (e.g. Skype) and quality standards, customers are fed up being fobbed off with sub-optimal quality that hurts communication and productivity. Fast: Citrix environments (especially via Citrix Cloud) spin up quickly with much manual install/config automated and pay-for-what-you-use billing. Cheap: the IGEL UEM Suite is 100% free with IGEL hardware/software, and converting old hardware into thin clients extends device life by years, raising ROI again. This is overtly partner-advocacy content, but it captures a value framing he reuses (ROI through hardware life-extension, single-console management) and shows him writing in his full Dutch voice for the local market.
