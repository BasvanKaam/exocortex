# NIST CSF and ISO 27001 (PDCA) working with NIS 2

**NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF):** a risk-based framework built around five functional areas — Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover — helping organizations manage cyber threats, especially in critical-infrastructure sectors (energy, transport, financial institutions, healthcare). It aligns well with NIS 2's emphasis on risk management, continuous improvement, supply-chain risk, and timely detection/reporting/response. Companies already using the NIST framework have a clear head start on NIS 2 compliance.

**ISO 27001:** a globally recognized standard for setting up and managing an Information Security Management System (ISMS) — a framework of policies and processes following the **PDCA cycle (Plan, Do, Check, Act):** set goals and identify risks/measures (Plan), implement measures (Do), monitor and measure (Check), then improve based on results (Act). Like NIS 2, ISO 27001 emphasizes comprehensive risk management, strong governance and policy, a culture of continuous improvement, and demonstrable compliance (via internal/external audits for ISO; documentation and evidence for NIS 2).

**Combining them:** many European companies combine NIST's practical functional approach (Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover) with ISO 27001's systematic management-system approach to more easily meet NIS 2 requirements such as risk management, continuous improvement, incident management and supply-chain collaboration.
