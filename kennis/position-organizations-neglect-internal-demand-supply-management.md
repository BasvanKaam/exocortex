# Organizations under-invest in their own demand and supply management

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

The framework holds that the failure point in cloud adoption is usually internal, not technical. The internal management of organizations is often insufficiently developed and adjusted when they opt for cloud. Both the role of the business itself and well-thought-out demand management and supply management are crucial, and they are routinely neglected.

This is why he positions the CCoE between the Demand and Delivery ends of the IT value chain and makes raising cloud maturity, knowledge development across the whole organization, its primary purpose. The technology is not the hard part; getting demand and supply management right is.
