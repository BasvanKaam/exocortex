# Cloud as the backbone of the digital-nomad life

Bas calls cloud technology the literal backbone of the digital-nomad existence — without cloud solutions this way of working simply wouldn't be possible. Storing files, collaborating, communicating: all happens online.

- File and document access: Google Drive, Microsoft 365, Dropbox (and don't forget Spotify — working without music is no option for most).
- Communication: Slack, Zoom, Microsoft Teams keep you in contact with clients and colleagues regardless of location; as long as you have wifi you can work as if at the office.
- Freelancers benefit further by flexibly scaling software, storage and tools to need — no fixed contracts, no huge investments.
- Security: working in hotels, cafés or shared coworking spaces brings risk; cloud-based security protects against hackers and unsafe networks. A VPN and two-step verification are standard kit for any serious digital nomad.

Aside: Bas notes Google Mail adapts to your location when abroad (the language changes depending on where you are) — small things that fit the always-moving nomadic life. He admits personally he couldn't imagine being as efficient constantly working from a different place, but understands why the lifestyle appeals to many.
