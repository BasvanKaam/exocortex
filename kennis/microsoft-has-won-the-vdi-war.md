# Microsoft has won the VDI war; AVD is the DaaS leader

The market position Bas presents: Azure Virtual Desktop has become the leader in DaaS, and the broader contest is decided. The framing he uses (quoting a colleague) is blunt: "Microsoft have won the VDI war."

Supporting arguments as he presents them:
- The Azure platform accounts for the large majority of DaaS workloads, with Microsoft's own native technologies a big share of that.
- RDP protocol improvements have given organizations the confidence to deploy native Microsoft solutions.
- Organizations are converging on a **mixed model** of AVD, Windows 365 and Intune as their future workspace strategy, not picking one.

The gap he points to (and where he positions Nerdio): native AVD lacks enterprise-grade management tooling, and rolling your own DevOps pipelines is costly and complex. That gap is the reason a management layer like Nerdio exists.

Stance worth preserving: bet on native Microsoft EUC, but don't pretend the native admin experience is enterprise-ready on its own.
