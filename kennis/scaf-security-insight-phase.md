# SCAF Insight Phase: Security Topics to Surface

Although risk analysis (Chapter 10) and Cloud security/governance (Chapter 9) are covered later in the book, the SCAF Insight phase already pays attention to security. You want clarity on what processes and responsibilities exist and any specifics to account for, including all physical components that are part of it (Waves).

Topics Bas wants information on:
- Existing policy.
- Identity and access.
- Data protection, encryption and classification.
- Infrastructure security and AV (antivirus).
- Deployment of firewalls, scanners, etc.
- Routers, gateways, switches, load-balancers, etc.
- Line/internet, LAN, WAN connections.
- Risk analysis and prioritization.
