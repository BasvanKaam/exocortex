# Positie: the 2014 Summit/Synergy split forces a real choice

Writing in Sept 2013 about attending Citrix Summit 2014 (Orlando), Bas noted a meaningful change: Summit and Synergy, historically run back-to-back in the same week so attendees could do both, were being split for 2014. Synergy had been the more technical event; Summit was traditionally the partner/sales kickoff. His read: now people may have to choose, and Summit 2014 was being rebalanced to roughly 50/50 technical and (pre)sales, which suited him because he wanted both. For his first US trip he also preferred Orlando over LA. Useful as a marker of his early career stage: a partner/consultant (via Qwise.nl) building a public profile in the Citrix community and using events for visibility and certification.
