# Original Salomon Cloud Masterclass: pricing and certification basis

The earliest scoping of Bas's own Cloud Masterclass, captured in his Salomon/Avensus role-scope document. This predates the later polished masterclass material and records concrete commercial and content-sourcing details not found elsewhere.

Format and price:
- Two-day masterclass, also bookable per day.
- Masterclass price: 1550 EUR per person, or 900 EUR per person per day. Including drinks and lunch ('verder uitwerken').
- Plus an advanced follow-up training of 1 day, going deeper on Governance and Security.
- Plus a separate 1-day Cloud Adoption Framework / Programme training.

Day split:
- Day 1 primarily intro: the cloud, the promise, current state, the why/why-not, the different way of thinking and managing, first steps in Cloud Governance and Security, Cloud ROI, with specific attention for the MKB (SMB) - 'Ons framework!'.
- Day 2 the technical fill-in: concepts, the ecosystem, the different providers, cloud models and their primary differences, how/when to deploy, first steps toward cloud, migration, transition, again briefly stressing the framework plus practical examples.

Knowledge base he planned to build the content on (used as template/source material, not as the product):
- Three Arcitura modules: Certified Cloud Professional and Certified Cloud Technology Professional.
- NCOI Cloud Masterclass (used as a template and partial fill for his own training).
- Three Arcitura modules: Certified Cloud Governance Specialist.
- Three Arcitura modules: Certified Cloud Security Specialist.
- Governance and Security material feeding an advanced part-2 training.

Stated USP: completely vendor-independent, supplemented with hands-on knowledge and examples plus general views from cloud professionals worldwide; in two days more than just basics ('drinking from the firehose', information overload, 'zorg dat je goed bent uitgeslapen').
