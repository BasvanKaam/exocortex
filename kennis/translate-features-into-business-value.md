# The Field CTO job is translating features into business value and next steps

Across his day, Bas's repeated pattern with prospects and partners is the same: short intro, understand the company, walk through portfolio and added value, answer AVD-market questions, then always agree explicit next steps. He helps prospects build the internal case to convince management AVD/Nerdio is the way to go, including a financial business case and a comparison overview.

His stance: the technical features are the entry point, but the work is converting them into business value and a concrete path forward for both parties. He treats partner enablement (webinars educating MSP communities on AVD, Windows 365/Cloud PC, and how Nerdio helps) as core, not a side activity.
