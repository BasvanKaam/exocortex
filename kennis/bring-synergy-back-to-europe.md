# Citrix abandoning Synergy Europe was a mistake

Bas's stance (Oct 2014): Citrix erred when, ~2012, it stopped running Synergy in Europe (and even cancelled an upcoming European event months before it would have taken place), replacing it with one-day regional events. He argues those regional events are too focused on marketing and sales and lack the high-quality technical content people expect from Synergy. With both flagship events (Summit in January, Synergy in May) now held in the US, getting the 'approved' stamp from employers became a real challenge of money, time and jetlag, and independents feel it too. He says he is even considering skipping both himself. He carefully balances the critique: he likes Citrix, its products and what it represents, and concedes it is easy to be critical from the sidelines when you are not involved. He notes the community is not forgotten (Citrix ran a strong XenMobile/XenDesktop/NetScaler master class at E2EVC Barcelona) but insists that does not compensate for Synergy Europe. His framing: 'We should be able to do both.'
