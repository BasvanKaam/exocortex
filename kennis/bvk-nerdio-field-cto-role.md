# What the EMEA Field CTO role covers (Bas's own description)

Bas's self-description of the role, useful as the canonical phrasing of what he actually does day to day:

- EMEA Field CTO, responsible for day-to-day business in the region. Part of the Enterprise team but 'wears multiple hats' and focuses on the MSP side of the business as well.
- Sales and marketing activities; daily interaction with customers and partners on their Azure and AVD challenges and needs.
- Evangelizes/showcases Nerdio wherever possible; signs up new partners and onboards them to the Enterprise and MSP programs.
- Presents regularly; attends events in-person and virtual; runs local workshops.
- Heavy content generation: blog posts, KB library help, exam curriculums, social posts, tech comparisons, whitepapers.
- Runs the Nerdio NVP program. Generates ideas. Light support work. 'Always learning.'
