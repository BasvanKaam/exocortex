# Positie: Byte Squared is a nice add-on but not a big step

Bas welcomed the Byte Squared acquisition but downplayed its significance (Sept 2013). His view: it pushes Citrix's competition (Good) out of play and saves licensing cost/time/effort by cutting ShareFile out of the document-editing flow, but the capability already existed via ShareFile plus XenApp-published Office, so it is not a big leap.

In his words: 'A great add-on and again a step in right direction... I doubt if it's a big step though, since we are already able to use ShareFile.' He also voiced a practical-user skepticism: 'I wonder how many of you like to use their iPhone for document editing purposes. I guess viewing documents works fine in most cases.' He nets out positive though: 'yes... I like it!' Characteristic of his balanced product takes: acknowledge the upside, question the magnitude.
