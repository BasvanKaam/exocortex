# Bas's troubleshooting philosophy: master the basics before reaching for tools

Bas argues that while there are many free troubleshooting tools for XenDesktop/XenApp, a lot of IT administrators forget about the basics: you need to know and understand the products you are working with before anything else. These points apply to all technologies, not just Citrix. His pre-conditions for effective troubleshooting:

- Understand the architecture you are dealing with (the FMA in our case): its main components, services, and communication paths.
- Know expected behaviour and interaction: how does it all work under 'normal' circumstances?
- Understand traffic flow throughout the infrastructure and its components, to help identify potential bottlenecks.
- Assemble a personal tool kit, and do the research beforehand on what each tool does and how to operate it.
- Only after the first three steps can you know where and when to apply which tool.
- Know where to find information: forums, (ex-)colleagues, community folks, a personal top 10 of blog authors, and social media.

His recurring point: by preparing yourself in times of 'peace' you save valuable time when things go wrong.
