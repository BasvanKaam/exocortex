# The promises of the Cloud (and Bas's invitation to challenge them)

Bas lists the powerful promises that made the Cloud attractive, but explicitly invites the reader to challenge them - do they hold up, or do you have reservations? (see Opdracht 1). He notes elsewhere that today we know not all promises are equally easy to keep. The nine primary promises:

- Overal bereikbaar (accessible everywhere): access systems and data from any location.
- Altijd beschikbaar (always available): providers guarantee high uptime, ensuring business continuity.
- Open (source) standards: easier integration and avoidance of vendor lock-in.
- Beheer uit handen (management taken off your hands): providers handle hardware, software updates (but not in all cases) and security, freeing IT teams for strategic work.
- Innovative and cheaper: access to advanced tech like AI and ML at lower cost via pay-as-you-go, without large up-front investments.
- Veiliger (more secure): providers like AWS and Azure invest enormously in security - Bas notes virtually no organisation in the world invests so much, literally and figuratively, in Cloud security.
- Direct beschikbaar (instantly available): immediate access instead of waiting weeks for hardware installation.
- Eenvoudig in gebruik (easy to use): intuitive interfaces simplify complex infrastructure management.
- Oneindige schaalbaarheid (infinite scalability): scale capacity up or down with demand, ideal for variable workloads.
