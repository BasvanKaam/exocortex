# Prediction: more cloud outages as datacenters hit capacity

In his 2020-and-beyond list Bas predicted cloud outages would become a bigger trend. His reasoning: as more companies move to the cloud, datacenters get closer to full capacity, and resources are consumed much quicker than new ones can be built. Not something providers advertise, but a logical consequence. He pointed to recent reports of VMs failing to start in some US datacenters due to regional resource shortages and concluded 'We’re only just getting started :)'. A bearish, capacity-grounded take that cut against the prevailing cloud-optimism of the period.
