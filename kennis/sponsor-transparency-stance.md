# Owning your own platform means owning the sponsor disclosure

In this sponsored post (Aug 2014) Bas is upfront about the commercial nature: "That's the beauty of writing for, and hosting, my own website, in the end I get to decide what goes up there." He frames sponsors as partners who both fund the site and teach him about new technology, calling it "a very powerful and useful combination."

His stance: he'll run sponsored content, but he discloses it openly and reserves editorial judgement because he owns the platform. Early signal of the independence and personal-brand-first thinking that later defines BvK.
