# SCAF Toolkit: 20 downloadable templates and what's excluded

Bas's SCAF documentation toolkit is accessed by scanning a QR code in the book, which leads to his website where you can download all **20 templates**. He asks readers not to share them ('please don't, you paid for them'), and estimates that assembling such a set yourself would take many hours, or hundreds of euros if outsourced. The templates are made on and for Windows 10 and higher; he expects no problems on Office for Mac, though formatting is sometimes not perfectly carried over.

Importantly, **not all referenced documents are part of the toolkit**. Excluded are the Strategy, Insight (Inzicht), Execution (Uitvoering) and Adoption (Adoptie) phase **templates**, because these are the manuals explaining how to walk through a phase (what you do where, which questions to ask and why), which is already described extensively in the book. For answering the per-phase questions during workshops, you can adapt an existing document or just start with a blank A4. Nearly all other templates seen in the book are in the toolkit.
