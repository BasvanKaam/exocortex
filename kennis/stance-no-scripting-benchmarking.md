# Benchmarking should not require scripting or fake accounts

In this March 2021 write-up, Bas clearly favors the low-friction approach VDI Drones takes to load testing. He highlights with approval that it needs "No scripting knowledge required" and quotes the creator that you don't need to "worry about PowerShell or creating hundreds of user accounts, which is often time consuming or even impossible in some corporate environments." His framing values tooling that removes operational friction in real corporate environments, and he flags small developer-friendly touches enthusiastically (a portable single .exe: "nice!"). He also likes that it lets you check whether Microsoft's promised IOPS are truly delivered by Azure/WVD VMs, an early skeptical, verify-the-claim stance on cloud performance promises.
