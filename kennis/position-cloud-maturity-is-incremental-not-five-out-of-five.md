# Cloud maturity is built incrementally, not demanded at 5/5

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

the framework's governance philosophy is explicitly incremental. The Target Operating Model is a way to mature an IT organization step by step; not everything has to be a 5 out of 5 from the start. He frames the TOM's building blocks as 'to address' items, not a mandate.

A second, related stance: the CCoE is deliberately not a dumping ground for responsibility. It is not the intention for all tasks and processes in the Target Operating Model to be placed under the responsibility of the CCoE; some processes simply remain at a higher level (for example, Financial Management). He even reframes standing up a CCoE as 'a good kick in the backside' to start the training and recruiting that builds real capability, rather than a box-ticking exercise.
