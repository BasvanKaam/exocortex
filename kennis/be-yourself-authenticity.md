# Be yourself; impersonating someone else never lasts

In his Oct 2019 career essay Bas leads with authenticity: don't impersonate or act like someone else, online or in person. It's fine to have idols and they can help you grow, but "don't do like they do, eventually it doesn't work. People will like you (or not) because of you." Faking it may work for weeks or months but won't last. This is a core, recurring BvK position that underpins his whole personal-brand philosophy.

*Bron: blogpost '20 years in IT, here’s what I’ve picked up along the way. Big read, skimming optional' (2019-10-08), basvankaam.com.*
