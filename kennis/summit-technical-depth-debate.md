# Defending Summit against the "not technical enough" complaint

After Summit 2014, Bas pushes back on peers who complained the event lacked technical depth and wasn't educational enough. His position: that misunderstands what Summit is for. Summit is the partner/(pre)sales event; Synergy is where the deep dives belong. "We have Synergy for that."

He argues technicians over-set their expectations toward 50/50 technical and felt let down, when 60/40 partner-leaning was always the deal and, in his view, the balance was right. He ties dissatisfaction to mindset: if you already know XenDesktop 7.1 cold, a "how to migrate from 5.6" session won't teach you much except roadmap, and that's on your expectations, not the event.

He's self-aware about sounding like a fanboy and pre-empts it: "no, I'm not a Citrix employee and I don't get paid for writing this." He tries hard to find criticisms and finds only minor ones (rescheduled sessions, lunch variety, the bus queue), conceding only that more in-depth breakout sessions would be the one fair ask.

*Bron: blogpost 'Citrix Summit 2014... What a ride!' (2014-01-20), basvankaam.com.*
