# Requirements Before Solution: Build the Business Case First

Before deciding whether any application, dataset, or workspace should move to the cloud, start with requirements, needs, and constraints, not with the technology.

The opening questions:
- What do we want to achieve? What must the environment or infrastructure satisfy? Which requirements do we set?
- What is important for our organization?
- What does our current environment look like (applications, workspaces, data)? What could or should change?
- What is the budget? Map the shortcomings and risks.

Only after answering these do you look at which solution fits best. The output of this exercise is a business case. The decision flows from the business case, not from a vendor's marketing list. He contrasts this explicitly with the internet's 'top 5 / top 10 reasons to choose cloud' lists, which recommend cloud without accounting for the client's actual situation.
