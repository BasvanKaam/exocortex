# Resistance: cloud is more than technology

In his paper Bas stresses that introducing a new way of working provokes resistance. Organisations face various cultural shifts — not a goal in itself but an often-unnoticed consequence — and processes change too. Addressing this early prevents a lot of unrest and greatly increases the chance of success, though in practice it's easier said than done: people aren't ready for change and are afraid or anxious.

His keywords here are breaking work into manageable chunks and communication. Cloud is more than technology alone; in fact, if you give ground on these soft elements, the chance of a successful cloud implementation is minimal. Not a blockade, but a weak point of many an organisation — and one that can't be tackled or changed overnight.
