# Independent Delivery Audit: Five-Step Method

A structured method for an independent party to review an environment delivered by another IT provider, used when a client wants a neutral check on what was built and on the supplier's competence. The review deliberately goes beyond the technical layer.

Scope is not limited to technical infrastructure (servers, desktops, applications, network). It also covers operational responsibilities, documentation quality, safeguarding of procedures, and the maturity and competence of the delivering provider. Operational agreements are typically captured in a DAP (Dossier Afspraken Procedures) document, alongside GDPR compliance, security, ITIL processes, policy, and SLA/KPI safeguards.

The five high-level steps:
1. Collect relevant documentation and information.
2. Interview the involved staff of the delivering provider.
3. Investigate the delivered infrastructure.
4. Test the provider on competence/maturity.
5. Produce a report including the IT roadmap and present it.

The engagement opens with an intake that defines scope, preconditions, success criteria, lead time, and cost, all put on paper before a formal go-ahead. Depth is tailored per client: not every organization needs the same kind or amount of information, so a bespoke plan is made together.

Why his motive-neutral stance matters: the reasons a client wants such an audit can vary widely and are, to him, often irrelevant. His job is to give insight so the client can make the right choices.
