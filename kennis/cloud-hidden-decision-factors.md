# Hidden decision factors before going cloud

Cloud looks like "a few clicks and done" but the easy surface hides factors clients are often not (well enough) informed about. The list to clarify up front:

- Data placement and ownership
- Application compatibility
- Impact on users, the IT department, and processes
- Security
- Internet connectivity
- The consequences of making wrong choices on any of the above

The same applies in hybrid scenarios (e.g. Office 365 as SaaS), where teams hit delays, additional costs and frustration after the fact. The remedy is being properly informed before committing, not after.
