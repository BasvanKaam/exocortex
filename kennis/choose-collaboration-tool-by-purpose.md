# Choose Collaboration Tools by Purpose, Not Default

Communication with colleagues, managers and clients is essential when working remotely, and Microsoft Teams is common, but it is not the only option. Some tools focus specifically on online meetings/video conferencing while document sharing is more limited, yet they are extremely good at what they promise. Depending on the goal (what matters for your organization), pick the right tool for the purpose, not the other way around. Technology should never be leading.

Examples named: Zoom, GoToMeeting, WebEx, Slack, Google Hangouts, Google Classroom. The simplest example may be WhatsApp and simply calling each other: make a group, agree some rules (important), and go.

Source: Bas van Kaam, Salomon IT blog, 2020-03-18.
