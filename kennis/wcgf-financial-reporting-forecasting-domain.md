# WCGF Domain: Financial Reporting and Forecasting

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

A dedicated WCGF operation domain, split out from Financial Management and Cost Optimization because data is the enabler and trigger for the financial management processes and therefore warrants its own spotlight.

Core mechanics:
- Arrange a periodic showback that provides real-time insight into cloud expenses, even when the organization is not yet mature in its cloud adoption. Budgets and expectations around cloud spend should be managed from the start.
- Reports built on the showback feed into general financial management processes to manage and forecast cloud expenses.
- Proper tagging of cloud resources lets reports be broken down in many ways (by unit, service, environment, etc.).

From a governance perspective, financial reporting and forecasting acts as a trigger for other financial management and cost-alignment processes and gives business stakeholders insight into cloud expenses and forecasts.
