# SCAF strategy map: a balanced-scorecard layering for the strategy phase

In the Cloud Masterclass, the SCAF strategy phase is taught with a strategy-map slide built on balanced-scorecard logic. Four stacked perspectives, read bottom to top and built top to bottom:

1. Organization / learning and growth at the base: develop and grow skills and knowledge.
2. Internal processes: growth and innovation, customer relationships, operational excellence, stakeholder interaction.
3. Customer: leadership in products, customer-centricity, operational excellence.
4. Financial at the top: revenue growth and productivity (vision, mission, revenue growth).

The reading direction is the teaching point: you read the map upward (capabilities enable processes enable customer value enable financial outcomes) but you build it downward from vision and mission. This is a training-specific framing device for connecting cloud strategy to business outcomes, not a separate model from the book.
