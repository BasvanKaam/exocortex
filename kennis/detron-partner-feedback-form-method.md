# Bas's vendor/partner feedback form method (Detron, 2017)

When Bas was Technology Officer at Detron he authored a standard product/partner feedback form, owned by him and approved by the Solutions Board. It is one of a set of four linked artifacts (idea form, feedback form, inventory & selection process, proposition development process) that together formed his proposition-development governance.

Purpose of the form: everyone present at a first vendor/product meeting fills in a separate copy right after the meeting, while impressions are fresh. The collected forms produce a structured first impression (a first conclusion) and can be reused later during the selection process.

A feedback form captures at minimum:
1. Product name plus a general functional description: which supplier delivers it, primary product properties and intended use, and the general impression of the presentation/demo (did they come across professionally, was it well prepared, could they answer all questions and how did they handle that).
2. Pluses and minuses: minimum 2, maximum 4 each.
3. Applicability within the proposition in question: scored 1 to 10, higher is better; below 6 must be explained.
4. Whether it could be deployed elsewhere in Detron's portfolio (yes/no, briefly explained).
5. What the supplier would need to change to (still) qualify for Detron.
6. Any other remarks.

The key design principle: capture the first impression structurally and immediately, with explicit room for a numeric score plus the reasoning behind it.
