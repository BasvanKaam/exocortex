# SCAF visual timeline and Bas's preference for visual artifacts

Bas opens this SCAF template by stating a personal preference: "Ik houd ervan als zaken visueel zijn vastgelegd" (I like it when things are captured visually). The Step-Cloud Adoptie Framework timeline gives an overview of the sequence and duration of each phase, from project initiation to final reporting. It shows the framework's four main parts — strategie, inzicht, uitvoering, adoptie — with an indication of duration per phase, ranging from weeks to months, and is shown *slightly animated*. It marks decision moments such as the business case and the Go/No-Go decision, plus reporting moments to evaluate progress and outcomes, so teams and stakeholders share a clear plan.
