# Cloud's origin around 2006 (Bas's paper, written 2020)

In his end-of-2020 paper Bas dates the cloud as having "seen the light of day around 2006, almost 14 years ago," albeit then in limited form. His point: it feels like a long time but is in reality still short — if these kinds of problems (datacenter limits, power, outages) already occur now, what will the world look like in 5 or 10 years? He repeats the ~14-year figure in the paper's foreword, noting the cloud journey has only just begun and we can't imagine how it will look in 10, 15 or 20 years.
