# A pre-sales vendor role wasn't the right fit

As of 1 May 2018 Bas left Liquidware after only seven months and joined Salomon-IT-Solutions as Senior Business Consultant. Working for a vendor had long been high on his list, and combined with the Liquidware portfolio (Stratusphere UX, ProfileUnity, FlexApp) the choice had been easy. But he concluded that a pre-sales role inside a software-driven organisation did not suit him well enough.

His framing is candid and without regret: he is grateful for the chance, learned a lot, helped build Liquidware's name in the Benelux and Nordics, and would not have wanted to miss it, but it simply wasn't his fit. He stayed connected as a Liquidware Tech Evangelist (Tech Insider Programme, community bloggers initiative) through 2018, because he genuinely rated the software. This marks his return to technical/business consultancy and architecture, the work he came up in at Qwise. Notable also: the parting happened in full harmony, with Salomon being one of Liquidware's key NL partners.
