# Cloud Governance: Distinct Aims and Business Alignment

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

Cloud governance shares IT governance's goals (compliance, conformance to standards and policies) but raises the bar above merely adequate and relevant technology. Because the digital transformation reshapes business models, organizational structure and processes (not just technology), cloud governance aims for the closest possible alignment with the business: business objectives drive digital transformation and should therefore drive cloud investments.

To achieve synergy it requires working agreements within the internal cloud ecosystem of users and suppliers, and contracts between suppliers and the demand/delivery parties.

Cloud introduces specific challenges such as ensuring data integrity and preventing data loss; a cohesive operating model and a well-defined change management process address these. Because cloud services improve and innovate quickly, continuous improvement should be embedded in the DNA of the new organization and its processes.
