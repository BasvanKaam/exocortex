# Current cyber threats and 2025+ trends

**Current threats:**
- **Ransomware attacks** – one of the most prominent threats, with major impact across sectors worldwide.
- **Phishing and spear phishing** – common; targeted spear phishing is increasingly sophisticated.
- **Supply chain attacks** – especially in software (e.g. SolarWinds), occurring more often.
- **Zero-day exploits** – exploiting unknown vulnerabilities remains a big problem.

**Trends for 2025 and beyond:**
- **AI-driven cyberattacks** – used to automate attacks and increase complexity.
- **Attacks on IoT and smart devices** – rising with growing IoT use.
- **Deepfake-based social engineering** – visual deepfakes, alongside voice deepfakes, a growing concern.
- **Quantum computing threats** – still developing, but a potential threat to encryption.
- **Targeted ransomware** – more personalized, highly targeted ransomware attacks increasing.
