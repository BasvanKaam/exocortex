# SCAF strategy-pillar intake workshop questions

The structured intake question set Bas uses to open the SCAF strategy workshop with a client, a concrete consulting tool from the Masterclass. He probes: the primary drivers for choosing cloud (as specific as possible); desired result and business objectives/expectations; what would make the implementation a success and which KPIs apply; specific wishes/requirements; any running projects whose success or failure carries major impact; the key stakeholders and why; who absolutely must be spoken to; the role of innovation; attitude toward a more Agile way of working; any existing IT/security strategy or policy; current IT partners and suppliers; strengths and weaknesses; opportunities and threats; prior cloud experience; current IT-related processes and whether they work; areas of high satisfaction/dissatisfaction; whether business objectives drive the strategic IT plan; how well IT and business collaborate; and who would staff a Cloud Center of Excellence. Optionally accompanied by a SWOT and a strategy map used to hold a mirror up to the organization.
