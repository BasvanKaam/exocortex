# Bas on the helpdesk as first point of contact

Within his KPI discussion Bas singles out the helpdesk, which he says people too often forget. It is the user's first point of contact, and how quickly and adequately they respond can have a huge impact on the whole experience.

His reframe: even if an incident isn't immediately resolved, the experience can still be good if the helpdesk genuinely helps the colleague who reported it, for example by keeping the reporter informed of progress during the resolution period. Or proactively: an incident is resolved and two days later the helpdesk employee checks back to confirm everything is still working as desired. Service quality, not just resolution speed, drives the perception.
