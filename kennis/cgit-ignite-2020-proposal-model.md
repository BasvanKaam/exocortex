# CGIT proposal model: fund event attendance via content output

A proposal to have the Come Get IT team attend Microsoft Ignite (planned autumn 2020, Orlando, Florida) funded by the business units, justified by the knowledge-sharing return.

The deal structure ("wat willen we ervoor terug doen") is the reusable idea: attendance is paid for by committing to tangible output.
- Summarize the visit across several podcast episodes.
- Survey the business units' knowledge needs beforehand and gather targeted knowledge at the event.
- Share the harvested knowledge in internal sessions at Client ICT, SBC, Tien, Avensus NL.
- Produce photos and video footage for Avensus Groep social media.
- Strengthen the BUs with Microsoft's new announcements; raise CGIT's profile; lay groundwork toward MVP recognition.

Cost estimate (deliberately calculated on the high side): event access 2x EUR 2,150.85 = EUR 4,301.70; AirBNB stay EUR 700; flights 2x EUR 475 = EUR 950; per-diem EUR 500. Total ~EUR 6,451.70.

Dated: tied to Ignite 2020 specifics and pricing.
