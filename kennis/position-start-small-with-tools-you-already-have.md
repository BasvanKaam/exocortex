# Position: Start Small With Tools You Already Have

When proposing cross-BU collaboration, Bas's instinct is not to design a grand platform but to take the cheapest first step with what already exists. For an internal forum he points straight at Yammer because it costs nothing and is already there. For an innovation board he says: start small, build out slowly, the way most initiatives grew, just start somewhere with some preparation.

His stance is that momentum comes from starting, not from up-front design, and that the existing, free tool is good enough to begin.
