# EDT and HDX.next milestones announced at Summit 2017

Network-performance highlights from Citrix Summit 2017, as it stood then: the new EDT (Enlightened Data Transport) transport layer would go GA with XenApp & XenDesktop 7.13, doubling the interactivity of Thinwire display remoting at high latency, with claims of 10x faster file transfers at high latency. The headline 'HDX.next' claim was cutting bandwidth by up to 60%, which Bas called the best news of the show. Also announced: XenServer 7.1 as the first XenServer LTSR (10-year support commitment) and a PVS accelerator in XenServer 7.1 (claimed 99% less network traffic, 25% faster boot, 93% less PVS server CPU). Dated version specifics, but marks the EDT/HDX.next inflection point in the remoting protocol story.
