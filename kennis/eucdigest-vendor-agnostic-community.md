# EUCdigest: vendor-agnostic, 100% community-driven EUC

At the January 2020 launch of the EUCdigest podcast (co-founded with Jits Langedijk and Eric van Klaveren), Bas framed the show's identity in three words: fully focused on EUC, vendor-agnostic, and 100% community-driven. That positioning is consistent with his broader stance from this era, where he describes himself as 'Cloud agnostic' and repeatedly elevates community over vendor allegiance.
