# Idee: Nerdio Tech Insider Community Program

Bas launched (announced ~2020, around the WVD era) a vendor-owned technical community: the Nerdio Tech Insider program. The model is reusable as a template for a no-strings-attached community as a product-feedback and advocacy engine.

## Premise
- The community is about the members and what the vendor can learn from them, not about selling. Explicitly tech-only: 'No marketing, no sales, leads, or anything like that.'
- Ask close to nothing in return except honest feedback from time to time. No upfront commitments, no salesperson pressure.

## What the vendor offers members
- Community-focused early release of the software.
- A training series on getting started with the product (Nerdio Manager for WVD).
- Best-effort support plus access to engineering and sales teams.
- Open discussion in a private Slack channel, plus a dedicated channel for feedback and feature requests.
- Broader training on WVD, EUC, and Azure.
- Occasional NDA roadmap previews and an early 'Heads Up' newsletter so members hear news first.
- A rebate/kickback program for those interested, and swag; in-person dinners when feasible.

## Mechanics
- Two-step join: a Meetup page (for RSVPs to events/webinars) and a private Slack channel (for collaboration).
- Launch via a webinar event.
- Co-branding encouraged: members may freely use the community logos on socials, sites, and blog posts.

Reusable lesson: a community works as a low-friction product-shaping loop when you remove the sales motion entirely and let practitioners who talk to hundreds of customers tell you what to improve.
