# How the conferencing cheat sheet was researched

In March 2020, at the start of mass remote work, Bas built a comparison cheat sheet of five well-known video conferencing / online meeting tools (the field included Zoom, Teams, WebEx, GoToMeeting, Hangouts/Meet, plus others people named: Chime, Skype for Business, Facetime, Slack, Circuit).

Method note worth keeping: he based it on personal research and experience, openly flagged that not everything was straightforward to find, and explicitly asked readers to correct errors and to point him to a source for the correction. He cross-checked his tool selection against a Twitter poll of what people actually use, and found his picks matched the top-of-mind tools globally. The product specifics are dated, but the transparent, source-it-or-flag-it, crowd-validate method is durable.
