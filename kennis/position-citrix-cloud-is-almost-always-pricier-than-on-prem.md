# Cloud, and Citrix especially, is almost always more expensive than on-prem

Bas's stance in his ComeGetIT review, pushing back on cost-saving claims for cloud Citrix: a direct on-premises comparison does not produce the savings people assume. 'Cloud, and Citrix especially, is almost always more expensive than a comparable on-prem solution.' The cost-saving framing collapses further once you account for the licensing lock-in (minimum 25 Essentials licenses, 1- or 3-year commitments), which removes the scale-down benefit.

This is consistent with his broader position that cloud does not automatically save money and that cost-saving is the wrong primary driver, but here it is sharpened specifically to Citrix-in-the-cloud economics versus on-prem.

Source: Bas van Kaam, internal feedback document on ComeGetIT, Salomon IT era.
