# Frame security guidance as a living starting point, not a final word

When releasing his AVD security cheat sheet (April 2023), Bas deliberately positioned it as a starting point and a work in progress rather than an authoritative complete reference. He explicitly states it's by no means his intention to provide a list of all security features available, and he openly invites the community to tell him what to add, change, or remove so it can keep evolving. This reflects a recurring stance: ship a useful v1.0, credit others' work (here, Aaron Dinnage's M365 license overview), and let the community iterate rather than waiting for completeness.
