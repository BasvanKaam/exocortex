# Growth Assessment: baseline, future state, gap

The Salomon Ready 4 Growth Assessment exists to answer 'are you ready for what's coming?' Its logic: if you want to go somewhere, you must know where you are starting from, otherwise you cannot plot the right approach route ('aanvliegroute').

Steps and outputs:
- Create a 'photo'/baseline of the current situation, capturing technology reality plus policy and business processes in detail.
- Define what the IT landscape should look like in future.
- Expose the 'gap' between the two, along with possibilities, limitations and financial consequences of any necessary change.
- Surface bottlenecks almost immediately, enabling short-term wins (days, not weeks or months) — the 'low-hanging fruit'.

Deliverable: a detailed report stating what to expect within each layer of the IT infrastructure, a detailed roadmap, the follow-up steps, and the quick wins to capture. Triggers for change include headcount doubling, a need for flexible scaling up and down, security/availability priorities, and organisational or process shifts.
