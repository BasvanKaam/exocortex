# Windows in the cloud as a Living Persona

Bas's concept piece reframing the cloud desktop from a system to a persona. Premise: we've spent years optimizing for speed, cost and scale and forgot the person behind the screen. A digital workspace should feel less like a system and more like a persona, shaped around behavior, context and experience.

The themes he wants to explore: desktops that understand their role; behavior-based automation that feels personal; personal recovery and memory; understanding user journeys through context-aware policies; monitoring "emotional health" (sort of); the living workspace. Closing line: "It's time to stop managing desktops and start designing experiences."

This is the experience-design counterpart to his more cost/resilience-focused frameworks.
