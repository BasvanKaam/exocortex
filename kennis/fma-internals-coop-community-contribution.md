# Pairing a community blogger with a Citrix insider as a community contribution

In June 2015 Bas framed teaming up with seasoned Citrix engineer Mick Glover (an XenDesktop/UPM/App Orchestration SME and author of the XD Tipster series) as deliberately combining two perspectives: the established community blogger and the inside-Citrix veteran. He pitched this pairing as "sort of a unique combination as far as we know" and explicitly positioned it as fun, low-pressure, and a contribution to the community rather than a commercial play. The plan: roughly one FMA Internals article per month (10-12 a year), ranging from short announcements to detailed reviews. The framing shows his early-career stance that community value comes from sustained, collaborative knowledge-sharing under a shared banner ("look for our logo"), not one-off posts.

*Bron: blogpost 'Introducing the FMA Internals Co-op' (2015-06-13), basvankaam.com.*
