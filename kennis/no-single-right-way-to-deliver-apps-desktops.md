# There is no single right way to deliver apps and desktops

Bas argues, with a Formula 1 analogy, that there is no one correct way to deliver applications and desktops to users, even though it often looks that way in the market. Just as F1 has no single tire for all (weather) conditions, there is no single right delivery method.

Citrix and VMware are not wrong; they are frequently and successfully deployed. But defaulting to them ignores a set of lesser-known (cloud) alternatives that can deliver the same outcome or better, often with lower investment and human effort. His stance: pick the solution that fits best, not the one everyone reaches for by reflex.
