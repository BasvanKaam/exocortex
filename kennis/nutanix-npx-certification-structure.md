# Nutanix NPX: hypervisor-agnostic, design-review-based expert cert

How it worked then (launched March 2015): the Nutanix Platform Expert (NPX) was Nutanix's flagship certification, built around web-scale and hyper-converged architecture. Notable design points:

- First hypervisor-agnostic certification of its kind - supports Hyper-V, KVM and vSphere, with candidates expected to have expert-level knowledge of at least two hypervisor platforms.
- Recommended (not required) secondary platform certs like MCSE, Citrix CCIA or VMware VCDX-NV.
- Two-stage process: review of the NPX Program Application, then the NPX Design Review (NDR), modeled on an academic viva voce - a live oral defense of a web-scale solution design before NPX-Certified Examiners, plus hands-on troubleshooting exercises.
- The Nutanix Platform Professional cert was a prerequisite; the program was free of charge. Comparable in rigor to VMware's VCDX (only ~190 VCDX-certified people existed at the time).
