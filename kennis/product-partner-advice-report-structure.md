# Product/Partner Advice Report (Draft Proposition Structure)

The canonical structure Bas defined for a "draft proposition" (advice report) that goes to a decision board. Reusable checklist for evaluating any new product/service before committing to build it. Deliberately a high-over draft, not a final proposal; serves as input for the definitive design. Sections:

1. General description: what the proposition/service/product is and its goal; the trigger and which problem(s) it solves; who it is interesting for (market); biggest competitors now and near-future; stakeholders/business units involved; risks (including the risk of doing nothing); a brief SWOT.
2. Process - selection and inventory: description of the inventory/selection process run; outcome (which products/vendors, with justification).
3. Commercial and marketing: commercial feasibility, concept pricing (optional); sales/revenue model; purchasing model and supplier overview; marketing ideas (presentation, sales pitch, website, blog, whitepaper, infographic); impact on the sales organization; unique/differentiating properties.
4. Operational: impact on the internal organization (do we use it internally, support model, SLAs, certification of technical and sales staff); impact on IT infrastructure (storage, VMs, compute).
5. Roadmap/planning: timeline; costs/investments and other resources needed.
6. Assumptions and dependencies.

The "risk of doing nothing" line is a notable inclusion: he forces the inactive option to be costed too.
