# Event runbook method: Wanneer, Waar, Wat, Wie

Bas's reusable structure for running an in-person event (used for the Come Get IT events). The draaiboek is built in three parts:

1. **Contact details.**
2. **Preparation / responsibilities** (who owns what, with a week-numbered timeline).
3. **Event runbook itself** (chronological, minute-by-minute for the day/evening).

Every activity row answers four columns:

- **Wanneer** (when): time, date or week number, in chronological order.
- **Waar** (where): location / room.
- **Wat** (what): detailed description of the activity or responsibility.
- **Wie** (who): initials of the people responsible.

Responsibilities are split across the organising group: programme content, speakers and hosting (Bas / BvK); website, invitation, venue, food and drink; budget/costs; presents, badges, save-the-date. Preparation is scheduled by week number (e.g. save-the-date week 17/18, invitation week 20/21, blog/promotion week 20-26) so nothing slips.

The evening template runs roughly: arrival and setup, guest reception with drinks and badges, opening and agenda, presentation, dinner, two more presentations, closing with speaker gifts and a Kahoot prize, networking drinks, teardown.
