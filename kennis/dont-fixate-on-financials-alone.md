# Do Not Fixate on the Financials Alone

While the financial impact often plays the leading role in a business case, Bas's position is that you should not stare blindly at the financial outcome. The cloud may offer advantages that are hard or impossible to express in money, and the gain to be had may lie on other (indirect) fronts. A proper business case therefore also maps alternatives, their pros and cons, risks, potential organizational impact, timeline, objectives and whether it actually 'fits'. Money matters, but it is not the whole picture.

Source: Bas van Kaam, Salomon IT blog, 2019-10-23.
