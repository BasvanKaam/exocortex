# Bas's teaching philosophy: action and presenting beat passive reading

Bas builds his whole exercise design around the Learning Pyramid. His mantra is "Meer actie = een beter resultaat!" (more action = a better result). The assignments and the five important questions at the end of each chapter exist to push the reader from passive reading into active processing and application.

He goes a step further and leans on the pyramid's top method, teaching others (90% retention). Even where an assignment does not say so, he advises collecting your results in presentation form and presenting them to colleagues or your team: "een betere manier van leren is er niet." This is the explicit method behind his optional "present your outcome" instruction across exercises.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> een betere manier van leren is er niet
>
> Meer actie = een beter resultaat!
