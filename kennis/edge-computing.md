# Edge computing and Edge-nodes

Edge computing, also seen as part of Industrie 4.0, is a key technology trend that enables smart factories, advanced maintenance and fast data processing. The global edge computing market was valued at 16.45 billion USD in 2023 and is expected to grow at a CAGR of 36.9 percent from 2024 to 2030 (source: Grand View Research).

Sensors collect real-time data, often to enable proactive maintenance: they gather data about machine condition and detect anomalies, so action (like scheduling maintenance) can be taken before problems arise. A core principle is moving data processing closer to the source of the data, by placing small datacenters or processing units (Edge-nodes) near the sensors. These mini-datacenters do an initial filtering/analysis, so only relevant information is forwarded, reducing traffic to the cloud backbone and lowering network load and cost. After local filtering, relevant data is sent to the cloud for further processing and storage (e.g. in datalakes or via big data/AI), enabling deep analysis and long-term insights.
