# Privacy Shield, Schrems II and EU-US data transfers

When the AVG was introduced, the EU-US Privacy Shield agreement was meant to ensure an adequate protection level for data transfers. It was declared invalid in July 2020 by the Court of Justice of the European Union in the Schrems II case, which ruled the Privacy Shield did not offer sufficient protection because of US intelligence agencies' access to European citizens' data.

After the invalidation, organizations must use other safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (modelcontractbepalingen) or Binding Corporate Rules (bindende bedrijfsvoorschriften) to lawfully transfer data to the US. In March 2022 the EU announced a new framework, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, but it still needs full implementation. Until then, the US does not automatically provide an adequate protection level under the AVG, and transfers require additional measures and care.
