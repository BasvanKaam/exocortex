# Semantic technologies and data science in the cloud

Semantic technologies are a collection of software and methods that help machines better understand and interpret data. They let computers not only store data but also recognize the meaning and relationships between data, leading to more intelligent and useful applications.

Processing these huge volumes of (big) data, often from many sources, demands large amounts of compute and storage. The cloud is well-suited because it delivers scalable storage and compute without the limits of local systems (though the cloud also has its limits). This practice of analyzing, interpreting and deploying data to gain insight is called **data science**, which uses statistical analysis, machine learning, AI and other advanced techniques to discover valuable patterns and trends in large datasets.
