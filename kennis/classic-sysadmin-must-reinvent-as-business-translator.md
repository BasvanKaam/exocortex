# The classic sysadmin must reinvent as a business translator

Bas's stance: the classic system administrator is, in effect, an endangered role. BYOD at the front and cloud/outsourcing at the back are hollowing out hands-on technical IT work, and the artisanal craft of the traditional sysadmin will inevitably disappear.

But he frames this as opportunity, not doom. The role does not die; it shifts. The Systeembeheerder 2.0 stops chasing every technical detail and becomes the functional bridge between the business and its IT partners — owning the functional fit and guarding the maintenance/SLA contracts. Less technically challenging, but more strategically central: the key to deploying IT effectively for the business. The implicit advice to admins: get ahead of this shift rather than cling to the old craft.
