# Cloud-First, But On-Prem AVD Has Legitimate Drivers

Bas's stance: cloud has many advantages, but he refuses to dismiss on-prem as legacy thinking. Some companies genuinely prefer to keep desktops and apps close to their users, and the two drivers he names as legitimate are latency minimization (top of the list) and being in control, or the feeling of control, over the back-end infrastructure. He explicitly grants that 'feeling of control' counts as a real reason, not just a technical one.

The resolution he argues for is not cloud-vs-on-prem but hybrid: Azure Stack HCI plus a management/automation layer gives you cloud-style scalability and flexibility while data and apps stay on-premises, so you don't have to choose.
