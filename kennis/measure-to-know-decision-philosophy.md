# Meten is weten: fact-based IT decisions

Salomon's adagium: 'Meten is weten, gissen is missen en kennis is macht' (measuring is knowing, guessing is missing, knowledge is power). The principle: first make a solid factual analysis before you make plans.

Applied: every engagement starts by measuring the IT organization and infrastructure — from the end-user-experience perspective — before defining next steps. Results become clear reports translated into recommendations, quick wins and a Roadmap from which the stakeholder can distil the right actions.

This is the methodological core of how Bas frames IT consulting: decisions grounded in measured facts that came out of the client's own organization, not in vendor opinion.
