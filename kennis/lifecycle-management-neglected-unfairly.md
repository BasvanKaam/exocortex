# Workspace/app lifecycle management is an unfairly neglected discipline

April 2018: Bas calls workspace and application lifecycle management 'een ondergeschoven kindje, volkomen onterecht' (a neglected child, completely unjustly). His stance is that organizations chronically lack visibility into their own app and desktop estate, and that this is both wasteful and dangerous (unmanaged, unmonitored machines hidden 'ergens achteraf'). He argues the disconnect between IT and the business widened once management went remote and admins stopped physically walking the buildings. The remedy he champions is cheap, fast measurement (a Stratusphere UX PoC standing up in hours) to drive concrete decommissioning and licensing decisions. Reflects his consistent measure-first, kill-the-waste philosophy, here in a Dutch healthcare framing.

*Bron: blogpost 'Werkplek en applicatie lifecycle management binnen ziekenhuizen - een grote zorg!' (2018-04-16), basvankaam.com.*
