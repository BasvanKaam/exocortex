# FSLogix is not an application layering company; App Disks are doomed

In the Sept 2017 v4.0 cheat sheet Bas takes two clear positions. First, he removes FSLogix from his application layering comparison, insisting that although they 'are doing some great things', they are not an application layering company, a distinction he says he already flagged in v3.0. Second, he predicts the death of Citrix App Disks: with the Unidesk acquisition, App Disk 'is bound to be deprecated not too long from now, as are PvD's.' This is an example of his willingness to make forward calls and categorise vendors strictly by what they actually do rather than how they market themselves. Note for evolution tracking: FSLogix would later be acquired by Microsoft and become central to profile containers, so his 'not a layering company' framing reads as accurate-for-its-category at the time.
