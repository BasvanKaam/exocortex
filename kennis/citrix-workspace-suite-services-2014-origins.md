# Citrix Workspace Suite and Workspace Services (on Azure) introduced at Synergy 2014

How it was announced then (Synergy, May 2014):

- Citrix Workspace Suite: a bundle of XenApp, XenDesktop, XenMobile, the Worx app suite (incl. ShareFile), Receiver and NetScaler, marketed as the most comprehensive mobile workspace solution. Trade-up discounts (up to 70% for existing XA/XD/XM/ShareFile customers, plus a VMware Horizon/View 'rescue' trade-in of up to 50%).
- Citrix Workspace Services: a cloud-based control plane to design, deliver and manage mobile workspaces across clouds and on-prem, using expert-defined deployment blueprints with auto-scaling and auto-recovery. Notably, its control plane was built and hosted on Microsoft Azure.

These 2014 announcements are the naming/strategy origin of the later 'Citrix Workspace' brand and Citrix's DaaS/cloud-control-plane direction. Product specifics are dated; the lineage is the durable part.
