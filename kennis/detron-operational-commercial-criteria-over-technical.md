# Operational and commercial criteria can matter more than technical in vendor selection

In his Detron selection process, Bas argues that when selecting a partner/product you look at the functional fit and technical criteria, but the operational and commercial criteria are 'maybe even more important'. He also refuses to let a 1-5 score be the whole story: he reserves a 'logica' bucket of questions and statements that cannot be graded numerically, and treats PoC findings and a degree of gut feel as at least as important as the scores.

The stance: don't let the spreadsheet decide. Quantify what you can, but the operational/commercial reality and human judgement carry real, sometimes decisive, weight.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> ...maar ook, en misschien nog wel belangrijker de operationele en commerciele criteria.

> Deze 'logica' bestaat uit diverse vragen en stellingen welke lastig tot niet te beoordelen zijn met een cijfer van 1 tot en met 5. Overige bevindingen, bv vanuit een PoC plus een stuk 'gevoel' zijn hierbij minstens zo belangrijk.
