# E2EVC: the Experts-2-Experts Virtualization Conference

Bas writes warmly about E2EVC (Experts-2-Experts Virtualization Conference), a non-commercial, community virtualization event started in 2003 with just 4 people, grown to over 130 attendees after 23 events, held in cities such as Munich, London, Copenhagen, Amsterdam, Brussels, Berlin, Frankfurt, Dublin, Paris, Los Angeles, Nice, Lisbon, Rome, Hamburg and Vienna. It runs Friday to Sunday and is at least 300-400 euros cheaper than other IT conferences, with early-bird discounts.

Organiser Alex Juschin (@E2EVC) runs it, but Bas notes a great deal of work is done by the Cooper sisters, Clare (@Clarecoops9) and Orla (@Orlacoops) - without whom, per Alex, the event wouldn't be what it is. The format: day one starts in one big room (first 3-4 sessions after Alex's intro), then splits into two parallel tracks; days run ~9:30 to ~6:00 with 10-minute breaks, all sessions recorded. Bas stresses anyone can present - just tell Alex - and the audience is forgiving and supportive.

It's deeply community-driven, with free coffee, lunch sandwiches, sponsored beer and pizza during Geek Speak, a free sight-seeing bus tour on Saturday, and a free airport shuttle. Bas says it's hard to find a former attendee not taken by 'the E2E virus'.
