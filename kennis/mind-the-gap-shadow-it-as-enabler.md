# Mind The Gap: Shadow IT as Enabler and the Entry Point

Bas's 'Mind The Gap' tactic for when the business is hard to convince. Use an existing, un-consulted (Shadow IT) cloud solution to demonstrate the benefits — and simultaneously make clear the dangers of NOT investing in these developments. In other words: use Shadow IT as an enabler.

**Workshop** — ideal for strengthening the IT-business bond. Surface the business drivers, let IT present the various cloud possibilities. Use technology or examples the audience already knows somewhat (mobile telephony, the web). Showing and using what people already know as an example works best.

**Entry point** quote: 'Begin klein en simpel. Het gaat om het laten zien van de toegevoegde waarde. Denk aan audio, video, web-conferencing, SaaS, testomgeving, etc.'
