# Opdracht 11: the human side of cloud transformation (XYZ-Care)

Bas's Assignment 11 drives home that a successful cloud migration does not end at technical implementation — employee adoption matters just as much. Without buy-in, training and clear communication, new systems cannot reach their potential. The fictional case is XYZ-Care Health Group, where care providers, IT and the business collaborate closely.

**The assignment**: (1) develop an adoption strategy covering training, KPIs and feedback mechanisms; (2) create a communication plan that informs and enthuses staff about the migration's benefits; (3) run a workshop where IT and the business jointly validate and improve the strategy with cross-departmental input; (4) apply earlier examples to XYZ-Care's situation.

**Worked solution** highlights: a training plan tailored to different user groups (care providers, IT, managers have different digital skill levels) delivered via a mix of e-learning, short physical workshops and guided hands-on training. KPIs include adoption rate of new tools (e.g. the planning tool and AVD), employee satisfaction scores via surveys and drop-in sessions, and a *drop* in incident reports/support requests for new systems. Feedback runs through an online portal and monthly check-ins. The communication campaign uses infographics, lunch sessions with live demos, and newsletter/intranet updates. The workshop surfaces pain points (e.g. EPD/EHR accessibility, tool usability) that are folded straight into the plan, ending with a roadmap.
