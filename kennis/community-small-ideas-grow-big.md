# An idea born small can grow huge fast

In the July 2019 EUC-X pre-announcement Bas stated his belief, from personal community experience, that "an idea born small can grow into something huge within a relatively short time-frame" and was confident it would happen again here. His framing of why it would succeed leans entirely on the people: the team behind it is the main reason he predicts success. A recurring BvK thesis: community momentum is driven by the right people, not the concept alone.

*Bron: blogpost 'EUC-X... Heads up – A new and exciting community initiative is on the rise' (2019-07-24), basvankaam.com.*
