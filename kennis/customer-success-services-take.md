# Mandatory paid support is not a bad thing per se

November 2016. His verdict on Citrix making support mandatory and renaming the model again: 'I like it.' The list of additional Select services impressed him on paper. He did not think mandatory paid support was inherently bad, even though it costs a little more than SA alone.

Caveats he raised:
- Citrix needs to communicate this carefully to partners and customers; he had already seen misconceptions arise, and recommended webinars/online training to clear them up. He took a jab at Citrix's habit of renaming things ('which Citrix is well-known for, unfortunately').
- Customers now pay for all the extra features even if they never use them, 'so they better make it good.'
- He wanted real-world reviews of the live-chat, e-learning, guided support and Premium Knowledge Center before fully judging the value. Net framing: 'interesting times ahead.'

He also relayed a recurring customer ask: dedicated on-site Citrix support engineers employed by Citrix (not third-party Consultancy Services), speculating Priority/Priority Plus might cover this.
