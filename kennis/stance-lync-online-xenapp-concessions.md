# Don't make concessions on end user experience when delivering Lync

March 2015. Bas went into a Lync-online-on-XenApp project expecting it to be straightforward (install, configure, size, test) and found real limitations instead. His framing: "Preferably we don't want to make any concessions when it comes to sizing, performance and ultimately, the end user experience." He rejected generic server-side processing as the default and pushed toward optimized HDX where the heavy lifting moves to the endpoint. When the cleanest optimized path (the Lync VDI plug-in) turned out not to support Lync online, his posture was pragmatic rather than dogmatic: it may still work technically, but unsupported means risk, so move to the next option. An early example of his consultant instinct to protect end user experience first and treat vendor support as a real constraint, not a footnote.
