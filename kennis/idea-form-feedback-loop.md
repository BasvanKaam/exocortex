# Structured Idea / Feature-Request Form as a Product Feedback Loop

Bas points to a recurring practice he values: an official feature-request / idea form (for Nerdio Manager for Enterprise) that lets anyone submit suggestions, which then ‘make it into our solutions.’ His operating rules for it:

- Make submission frictionless and public so community members and NVPs both use it.
- Ask for **one idea at a time** to keep submissions actionable.
- Encourage sharing the form widely.
- Close the loop by visibly shipping community ideas into the product.

The reusable principle: capture field ideas through a low-friction structured channel and then prove they land, so the community keeps contributing.
