# Community involvement as the engine of his career

Bas's 2015 review reveals his operating model: relentless community participation drives everything. In one year, the blog hit 48 articles and ~290k views; he ran the FMA Co-op with Mick Glover, moderated a Citrix Facebook group, was active on myCUGC (member of the month, leader acknowledgement), attended his 5th E2EVC and 1st Citrix Synergy, presented at DuCUG, renewed Nutanix NTC, became a Citrix SME for the XA/XD 7.6 exams, and was promoted to Management Consultant plus appointed to Detron's Technology Board. His framing: he's 'privileged to have met so many great community folks' and feels almost weird reflecting on accomplishments. This is the year right before his Citrix CTP award (announced early 2016).

*Bron: blogpost '2015! My year in review, and what a year it has been!' (2015-12-31), basvankaam.com.*
