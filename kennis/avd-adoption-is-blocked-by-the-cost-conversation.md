# The Real Blocker to AVD Adoption Is the Cost Conversation

Bas's stance, framed through a recurring customer scenario (the board approved AVD, now they want a cost breakdown and ongoing tracking): the biggest challenge for companies adopting AVD is not the technology, it's convincing management that costs can be kept low, performance stays on par with what they know, and that there's a defensible, itemized overview of components and costs to get started, and a way to keep it under control afterwards.

His argument: AVD has a lot of moving parts and is hard to build and configure properly, and that difficulty is identical whether it's 200, 2000 or 20,000 users. So the way to lower the barrier to AVD is to attack the cost-justification problem directly with modelling up front and per-user attribution afterwards, because 'you know how our board likes numbers.'
