# Newsletter: June known issues edition

First monthly known-issues edition, around 22 June 2026, built with the monthly newsletter recipe. "Consider it a new formfactor to the Nuggets newsletter portfolio." Items grouped by platform with the short version of why each matters; the full PDF (the EUC Source Log) is linked in the first comment. Status `achterhaald`: a dated monthly snapshot. External news curation, not distilled into kennis.

## Format notes
- Trigger: Steve Downs posted about one of the items that week; Bas credits him by name for the Arc extension reboot issue.
- Each item: what, workaround if any, one link to the best source (Microsoft Learn, or a named expert such as Kempeneers for the AVD Hybrid walkthrough).
- Closes with a call for next month's items and better workarounds in the comments. "Until next month."

## Items (headlines)
- **AVD:** AVD Hybrid public preview limits (PowerShell-only, no multi-session on-prem); Arc extension reboot race on Windows Server (install RDSH role first); AVD Hybrid onboarding triage (CloudDeviceExtension logs); AVD classic retires 30 September 2026; Remote Desktop MSI client end of support 27 March 2026.
- **Windows 365:** snapshot-based reset for Flex shared (default shift in early July); Windows 365 Reserve user-initiated provisioning (preview); Teams screen-capture protection not enforced on Cloud PCs; Frontline renamed Flex; Windows 365 Business price cut.
- **Intune:** iOS MAM SDK older than 20.8.0 (re-wrap LOB apps); Windows 11 25H2 baseline missing two settings; slow MDM profile removal reporting on Apple; Ubuntu 22.04 support ends August 2026.
- **Windows:** OLE automation apps failing to launch Office after June updates; 0x80073712 / 0x800f0993 after 24H2/25H2 upgrade; Secure Boot 2011 certs expiring (KEK CA 24 June, UEFI CA 27 June); desktop.ini hardening; Windows 10 consumer ESU ends 13 October 2026.

## Reconcile
- This edition says Windows 10 consumer ESU ends 13 October 2026 with no year two. The later June roundup (edition no. 10) reports Microsoft quietly extended free consumer ESU to October 2027. The later edition supersedes this item.
