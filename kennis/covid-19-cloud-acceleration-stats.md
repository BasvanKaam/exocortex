# COVID-19 as a cloud accelerant: the key statistics

The COVID-19 pandemic forced organisations worldwide to rapidly adopt new technology, with the cloud central to business continuity and flexibility. Figures cited:
- Gartner: global public cloud spending grew more than 23% in 2020 vs 2019, driven by the shift to remote work and digitalisation.
- McKinsey: digital technology adoption advanced in a few months as much as would normally take three to four years.
- IDC: more than 90% of large enterprises had embraced a hybrid model by end of 2021, up from 70% pre-pandemic.
- Azure Virtual Desktop (then Windows Virtual Desktop) usage tripled in Q1 2020 (a 200% rise).
- Microsoft Teams: +70% daily users in the first pandemic months, peaking above 115 million daily users in 2020.
- Zoom and Google Meet reported user growth of 169% and 435% respectively.
- AWS revenue rose 29.5% in 2020; demand for AWS Lambda and EC2 surged on flexible pricing and scalable infrastructure.
- Flexera 2021 State of the Cloud Report: 92% of surveyed firms had a multi-cloud strategy and 82% used hybrid cloud.
- Deloitte: companies saved on average 15% on IT costs by migrating to cloud, some up to 30% via IaaS and SaaS.
- Flexera: 61% of organisations actively invest in cloud cost-optimisation tools.

Sources cited: Microsoft, Gartner, McKinsey, Flexera, Deloitte, Gallup, IDC, CBS.
