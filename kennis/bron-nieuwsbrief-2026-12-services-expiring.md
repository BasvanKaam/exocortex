# Newsletter: 12 (cloud) services about to expire

List edition, mid June 2026. Bas gets reminded daily of services being deprecated within days or weeks, so he put them in one row. Each item has a deadline, what it is, why it matters and a Microsoft link. Status `achterhaald`: the deadlines have passed as of October 2026; kept for the format and as a dated record. This is external news curation, so the items are not distilled into separate kennis notes.

## Intro (his framing)
He has alerts and daily news set up; AI is the biggest disrupter, but complex environments have countless moving parts that need updates, upgrades and eventual deprecation. "I wouldn't be surprised if almost everyone reading this weeks' newsletter has at least one thing on there of which they think, damn, I almost forgot about that one." Advice: take your time, research when in doubt, "use it to your advantage."

## The twelve (as listed)
1. Secure Boot certificates 2011 to 2023 (June to October 2026), incl. AVD session hosts and Cloud PCs with Trusted Launch.
2. Conditional Access "Require approved client app" read-only (30 June 2026, MC1220751).
3. Enterprise State Roaming management leaves the Entra portal (end of June 2026).
4. External Teams call control retirement (30 June 2026).
5. Intune Data Warehouse reset and beta Power BI connector retirement (mid June 2026).
6. Intune Suite premium features fold into M365 E3/E5 (from July 2026).
7. End of standalone SharePoint / OneDrive plan sales (July 2026).
8. EWS in Exchange Online blocked (starts 1 October 2026, allow-list before end of August).
9. Vulnerability Remediation Agent moves to an Entra agentic identity (90-day clock from mid June 2026).
10. Intune TeamViewer connector replaced (12-month migration clock).
11. MDOP out of support: App-V, MBAM, UE-V (now).
12. AVD classic retirement (ongoing phase-out).

## Reconcile
- Item 6 says the Intune Suite features fold into E3/E5; the later June roundup says EPM and EAM become part of M365 E5 on 1 July 2026. The later edition is the more specific one.
