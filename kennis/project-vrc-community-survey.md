# Project VRC: a community-sourced SBC/VDI survey

How it worked then (2015): Project Virtual Reality Check (VRC) ran an annual community survey whose value, per Bas, was that the data is '100% authentic' - all of it provided by working IT professionals in the field rather than by a vendor. The 2015 survey covered topics like interest in DaaS, the biggest SBC/VDI challenges, AV solutions used, managing SBC/VDI, thoughts on strategic IT vendors, EFSS, plus persistent vs non-persistent and application-virtualisation rates.
