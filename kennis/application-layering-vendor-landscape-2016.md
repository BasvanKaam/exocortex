# Application layering vendor landscape circa 2016

How it worked then: by mid-2016 the application layering market that Bas tracked in his cheat sheet spanned a recognisable set of vendors and products: Citrix App Disks / App Containers / AppDNA, VMware App Volumes (Horizon/View, vSphere, VMDK), UniDesk, FSLogix, RES, Liquidware Labs (FlexApps), all built on layered virtual disks (VHD/VHDX/VMDK) and filter-driver technology, targeting RDSH, XenApp and XenDesktop. Useful as a snapshot of who was in the layering space before the consolidation that followed (FSLogix to Microsoft, UniDesk to Citrix).

*Bron: blogpost 'The application layering technology cheat sheet, version 2.0!' (2016-07-19), basvankaam.com.*
