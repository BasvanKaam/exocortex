# Method: whitepaper downloads as a lead-generation engine

Generate leads via gated whitepaper downloads, drawing topics from a backlog of real client pain points. Candidate topics he lists: Office 365 offline use (a far bigger blind spot than people think), secure data share (e.g. on ShareFile, riding the AVG/GDPR concern that tops everyone's list and is simple to implement), the challenges of modern products, cloud data location and GDPR, desktop transformation including the often-forgotten training of staff, the reality that Windows 10 is not the 'one OS to rule them all' given six-monthly updates, and migration pitfalls.

The recurring theme across topics: 'moderne technologieen/oplossingen kennen moderne uitdagingen', modern technologies bring modern challenges that are routinely overlooked or simply unknown to the parties involved. Surfacing those challenges is the content hook.
