# Vendor-Independent, Customer-Driven Advisory Model

NOTE ON ATTRIBUTION: this post is signed by Eric ('Persoonlijk ben ik (Eric) ervan overtuigd...'), not Bas. It is captured here as Salomon company knowledge, not as Bas's personal voice or position. Treat with the same care as other non-Bas guest material.

The model: advice is always customer-driven and objective, given from the client's perspective rather than from a product or partnership interest. The firm frames this as avoiding the 'wij van WC eend adviseren...' problem (the Dutch idiom for self-serving recommendation) and the 'slager die zijn eigen vlees keurt' (butcher inspecting his own meat) conflict of interest.

Three audiences value the independence: colleagues (freedom to advise neutrally), clients (an extension of their own organization that looks outward from their use case), and even IT suppliers (who hire Salomon to test their own delivered solutions/architecture without a competitive threat, since Salomon's business is often not comparable to the supplier's). The claimed outcome: better solutions and stronger relationships. This independence theme is consistent with Bas's own stance elsewhere, but this specific articulation is Eric's.
