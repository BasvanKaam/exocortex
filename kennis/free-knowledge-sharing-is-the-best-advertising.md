# Stance: freely sharing knowledge is the best advertising a company can get

His central conviction about company growth: cultivate a knowledge organisation and let people share knowledge freely, and the brand follows. On growing bloggers from 2-3 to 12: 'Betere reclame kun je, je als bedrijf niet wensen', you couldn't wish for better advertising as a company.

The unselfish framing is load-bearing: 'Het belangeloos delen van hun kennis en kunde binnen de community en natuurlijk Detron blijft niet onopgemerkt.' Sharing knowledge selflessly, inside the community and around the company, does not go unnoticed and converts into recognition, community awards, and visibility. The act of giving is the marketing.

*Bron: Salomon-IT 'Detron als kennis organisatie' (Diversen).*
