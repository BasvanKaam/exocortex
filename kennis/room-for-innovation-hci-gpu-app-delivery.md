# Plenty of room left for innovation in HCI, GPU and app delivery (2015)

Bas's read of the early 2015 survey data: there is 'still plenty of room for innovation with regards to (hyper) converged infrastructures, GPU / video acceleration technology and application delivery in general.' This is where he saw the VDI/SBC market heading. Tracks consistently with his enthusiasm in the same period for Nutanix/Dell hyper-convergence (idx 79) and Framehawk graphics delivery (idx 83), showing a coherent thesis: the platform layer was maturing and the next wave of value would come from converged hardware, graphics/GPU and smarter application delivery.
