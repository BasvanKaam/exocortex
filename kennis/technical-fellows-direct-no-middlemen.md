# The collective model: direct expert access, no middlemen

Bas joined Technical Fellows (May 2018), a Dutch initiative of seasoned IT pros (~20 years experience on average) across the Citrix and Microsoft portfolios, some self-employed, some employed. The model he endorses: a customer communicates and contracts directly with the specialist in question, with no companies, brokers, or other parties in between, and invoices come straight from that pro or their employer. 'Direct, to the point and no messing around, that's us.'

The value proposition is bundled forces, a group of independent experts helping each other (including hard-to-find NetScaler/ADC specialists) so customers get the best of the best across complex troubleshooting, architecture and implementation. The title 'Why join the navy if you can be a pirate?' captures the ethos: independent operators over corporate structure. They even ran a shared WhatsApp group to help each other out.
