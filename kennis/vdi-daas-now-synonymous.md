# VDI and DaaS Are Now Practically Synonymous

Bas's nuanced take: although the origins of VDI and DaaS genuinely differ — and VDI once started as a single-user technology — today they stand virtually synonymous, depending on who you ask. Many people principally insist on holding to how it was originally conceived. Bas's playful point: when you think about it, it's not so strange — VDI stands for Virtual Desktop Infrastructure, which "sounds pretty cloud-like, don't you think?"

He also notes that nearly all well-known VDI developers/publishers (single- and multi-session), like Citrix and VMware (he keeps using the pre-acquisition names so everyone knows who he means), now also offer their products as a cloud solution. Where you previously built an entire environment in your datacenter machine by machine (often physical), installing 'roles' for web portal login, the broker (routing users to the right machine with the fewest users), etc. — all these roles are now offered as a cloud service, externally maintained. You state how many machines you need, assign users, done. In theory, at least (there's more to it). What was physically and locally managed in the datacenter is now offered as a cloud service with configs and roles integrated and automated, but still with trade-offs per solution.
