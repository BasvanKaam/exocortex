# Measure to know (meten = weten)

Bas's stance: organisations routinely run their IT without clear sight of what they actually have, and that is a waste because the wins here are easy to book ('hier valt op eenvoudige wijze veel winst te boeken'). He insists on quantified inventory before action, so you move on facts rather than guesses.

This is the early form of the 'meten = weten' (measure = know) concept that recurs as a Salomon signature: you cannot improve, prune or right-size an estate you have not measured. Low-hanging fruit ('laag hangende fruit') is the framing for the quick, cheap wins that measurement exposes first.
