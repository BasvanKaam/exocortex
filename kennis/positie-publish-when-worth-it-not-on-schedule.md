# Publish when it is worth your time, not because it is Tuesday

For the EUC News Nuggets newsletter Bas refuses a fixed schedule. "I would rather send you something when it is worth your time than send you something because it is Tuesday." Some weeks two or three editions, some weeks none. Quality over quantity.

## The rest of the contract with the reader
- Respect their time: short, one topic, and assume practitioner knowledge ("I am going to assume you already know what a tenant is").
- Pick the format per nugget: a cheat sheet works on a horizontal screen, other topics land better in your ears on the move. Same nugget, two ways, your choice.
- Show, do not promise: things appear when they are ready, no big reveals.
- Small and replaceable beats big and stale. His old cloud cheat sheets were popular but impossible to keep current; the nugget format is the answer.

## Tension worth knowing
His daily LinkedIn posting since March 2026 and his earlier "consistency beats volume" advice for community advocacy are about showing up regularly. The newsletter rule is about not forcing a deliverable onto a calendar slot. Both hold: be present consistently, publish substance only when it is ready.
