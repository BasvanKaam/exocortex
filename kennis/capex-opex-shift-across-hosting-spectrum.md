# Reading the Hosting Spectrum - Capex Falls, Opex Rises

Bas combines the IaaS/PaaS/SaaS models (from Chapter 2) with the hosting models. Reading the spectrum left to right: capital costs (CAPEX) decrease while operational costs (OPEX) increase. Costs for installing, configuring and maintaining physical systems drop (less CAPEX), while OPEX shifts from internal staffing costs to external service providers.

With co-location, costs depend on services taken (e.g. onsite support, monitoring). With managed services, costs vary by services offered and can be billed per user or per component.

Key questions that influence monthly cost and how much you do in-house: Is monitoring proactive? Are helpdesk services included? Are problems only detected or also (proactively) solved? Is 24/7 support available? In Bas's diagrams, the dark fields indicate what is taken off your hands.
