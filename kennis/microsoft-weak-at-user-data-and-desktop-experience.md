# Microsoft has never managed profiles and desktop UX well

A recurring BvK stance, stated bluntly in Jan 2019: 'Microsoft has never done a very good job when it comes to managing user data, profiles, monitoring, and the overall desktop experience in general.' Even after acquiring FSLogix, he argues there is more to consider, which is why third-party UEM partners matter. His broader EUC thesis: WVD will be good enough for many users but 'it will never be a 100% fit', and large-scale adoption will take years, not months. Companies will not just drop their existing physical/virtual/on-prem desktops, so an adaptive, multi-cloud-capable workspace management layer is the smart hedge.
