# From Beheer to Regie: The Regieorganisatie Model

Bas frames a key cloud-driven shift: the traditional management (beheer) role evolves into a directing/orchestration role (regie), shifting focus from the internal organisation to a balance between customer and supplier. This requires a new operating model, the regieorganisatie, which aligns customer demand with supplier supply. It has two core parts:

- **Demand management:** organises the demand side, focused on the customer / internal organisation. People in this role have strong affinity with business processes and how information provision supports them. Called the 'requirements perspective'.
- **Supply management:** organises the supply side, focused on the supplier. These roles handle procurement of IT services and issuing assignments. Called the 'solutions perspective', centred on expertise in market developments and IT solutions.

The IT regieorganisatie acts as a bridge between demand and supply, requiring knowledge of both business processes and the IT market. Where the traditional beheerorganisatie focused on internal processes and systems, in a regieorganisatie the employee becomes a link between the business (customer) and external suppliers, demanding new competencies: stakeholder management, communication skills, and insight into both business and IT.
