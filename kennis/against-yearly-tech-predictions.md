# Against the 'year of X' prediction ritual

Writing in January 2020, Bas opens by arguing that predicting tech developments on a yearly basis is not the way to go and that 'predicting' isn't even the right word. Technology slowly evolves into something bigger when successful, so declaring 'the year of X' is unfair without context. He uses VDI as his example (carefully noting he's neither pro nor anti VDI): if your rationale is that the product matured and marketing did its job, then fine, 2019 was the year of VDI, but the better approach is to provide context or discuss developments across multiple years.

This shows his skeptical, context-first stance toward industry hype cycles, and his irritation at marketing-driven term conflation (VDI being rebranded as DaaS).
