# Bas on 'Angst': the human side of cloud adoption

Bas argues that companies moving to cloud quickly see the upside (more efficient processes, time saved, fewer manual errors) but often overlook the human side of the change. Employees, especially those who have worked a certain way for years, can feel threatened: if automation takes over the tasks, what's left for them? He draws an explicit parallel to what is happening today around AI. This fear can turn into resistance, where people work against change or view new technology more negatively even when the benefits are obvious.

His war-story example: an IT department in a medium-sized company that, before cloud, spent its days firefighting daily recurring problems and repetitive work. When the company decided to migrate, resistance flared and people asked out loud, 'Wat gebeurt er straks met ons?' (What happens to us?).
