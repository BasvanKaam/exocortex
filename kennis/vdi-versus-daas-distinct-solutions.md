# VDI and DaaS are distinct, not interchangeable

In early 2020, driven by the marketing momentum around Microsoft Windows Virtual Desktop, the industry started interchanging the terms VDI and DaaS. Bas's correction: these are two distinct solutions, similar in some ways but different nonetheless, and conflating them is incorrect.

His meta-point on 'year of X' claims: a product, trend, or technology slowly evolves into something bigger over many years. Calling 2020 'the year of X' isn't fair without proper context; better to talk about developments across the span of multiple years. (WVD-era framing, but the discipline about terminology and multi-year framing is durable.)
