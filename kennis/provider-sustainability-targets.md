# Azure, AWS and Google sustainability targets

Stated sustainability goals of the three big providers (per their websites/reports):
- Microsoft Azure: aims to be carbon negative by 2030 and to fully remove (replace) all the CO2 it has ever emitted by 2050. Publishes annual sustainability performance. (microsoft.com/sustainability)
- AWS: aims to run fully on renewable energy by 2025. Amazon as a whole signed The Climate Pledge, committing to net-zero CO2 by 2040 - ten years ahead of the Paris Agreement target. (aws.amazon.com/sustainability)
- Google Cloud: was the first big cloud provider to be climate neutral and aims to run entirely on carbon-free energy by 2030, meaning datacenters operate green 24/7. Publishes regular data and an annual environmental report. (sustainability.google)
