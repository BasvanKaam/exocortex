# IT as a Utility

Bas frames the cloud/WVD endgame as IT becoming a utility ("IT als een Nutsvoorziening") — something you consume like electricity or water rather than build and own. WVD is his concrete proof point: you pay only for the VMs you use, scale up or down on demand, and let Microsoft run the plumbing.

He pairs this with "het perfecte plaatje" (the perfect picture): the modern desktop delivered from the cloud, where the underlying infrastructure disappears from the customer's concern. The argument is directional, not just technical: this is where the industry is heading, and the role of the IT pro / partner shifts from building boxes to delivering an outcome.
