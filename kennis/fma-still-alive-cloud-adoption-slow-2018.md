# The FMA is far from dead; cloud adoption is real but slow (2018)

Bas's stance in July 2018: despite Citrix's cloud-first development focus, the FlexCast Management Architecture 'is very much alive and will be for years to come.' Most customers were still on much earlier on-prem versions (he'd seen multiple XenApp 6.5 deployments that year), and adoption of XA/XD cloud services and Essentials was still limited. Deployments were either classic on-premises (he insists: 'no, not legacy') or IaaS-based, with IaaS itself still limited in numbers, and the FMA applies to IaaS too.

He's confident cloud adoption will pick up eventually but is clear it hadn't yet from what he was seeing and hearing in the field. The decision to give the book away free, chapter by chapter, flows from this: the on-prem FMA knowledge remains useful and worth preserving even as Citrix's own attention moves to the cloud.
