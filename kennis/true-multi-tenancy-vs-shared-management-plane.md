# Real multi-tenancy keeps the MSP in control of its own tenant, not a shared plane

Bas's architectural position: deploying the management platform as native Azure PaaS into the MSP's own tenant is materially better than a shared, hosted multi-tenant management plane. The MSP supports hundreds of customers yet still owns and controls its data, networking and identity, and decides where metadata lives and who can access it, with no infrastructure shared with other MSPs. He frames this as an 'excellent story to tell' from a security and privacy perspective, and as 'the best of both worlds' versus the trade-off other models force. He also leans on the claim that this lets ~80%+ of an IT workforce build and manage Azure/WVD with everything in three clicks or fewer (a product/marketing assertion, not an independent fact).
