# The CCoE is overhead, so keep it lean

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

the framework is candid that the CCoE is a support team and is considered overhead by some. His position follows directly: keep it lean and mean, and size it from actual demand rather than ambition. A functioning CCoE can be as small as 4 FTE.

The corollary is flexibility over fixed org charts: roles can be combined in one person, the team structure should flex with whether the organization is mid-migration or doing cloud-native development, and large complex organizations may run several CCoEs rather than one bloated central team.
