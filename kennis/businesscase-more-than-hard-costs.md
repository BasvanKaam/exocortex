# A Business Case Is More Than 'Hard' Costs Alone

Bas's core reframe on business cases: a business case is about more than just the financial picture or — as often happens — comparing direct costs and licenses. Financial costs and returns matter (greatly), but a business case must look more broadly at strategic and operational impact. It's about creating value for the organisation — optimising processes (saving time), controlling risk, improving operational efficiency and productivity. In short: doing more with less.

Financially, **ROI** (Return on Investment) and **TCO** (Total Cost of Ownership) play the lead roles. ROI measures the return of an investment relative to its cost, clarifying how fast and to what degree the organisation benefits. TCO gives a complete overview of all costs over the entire lifespan — initial purchase, operational costs, maintenance and any future expenses. Together they assess financial feasibility short- and long-term.

Beyond that: change management, long-term impact on organisational culture, customer satisfaction, technological flexibility and regulatory compliance are all determining factors. A successful business case makes an all-encompassing trade-off between these elements, helping the organisation make well-considered choices that go beyond 'hard' costs.
