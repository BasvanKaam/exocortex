# Everett Rogers' technology-adoption categories mapped to the hype cycle

Organisations and people are categorised by how fast they adopt technology, based on Everett Rogers' technology-adoption model, which Bas maps onto the hype-cycle phases (with clear differences per country, region and organisation type):

- **Innovators** — pioneers experimenting at the Technology Trigger; high risk tolerance, first to explore and implement.
- **Early Adopters** — follow quickly, often during the Peak of Inflated Expectations; see potential and trust the benefits even before full stability.
- **Early Majority** — adopt during the transition from Trough of Disillusionment to Slope of Enlightenment; wait until tech is more practical, tested and lower-risk.
- **Late Majority** — adopt around the Plateau of Productivity; conservative, wait for broad acceptance and proof.
- **Laggards** — wait longest, adopt only when tech is nearly standard and risk is minimal.
