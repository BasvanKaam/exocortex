# Policy is the starting point of every IT project

Principle surfaced in a Salomon colleague interview (Johan Nieuwhoff) and consistent with Bas's own requirements-before-solution stance: always ask 'why?' before reaching for an IT solution. The solution people think they want often turns out not to be what they actually need.

The reason: the requirements have usually not yet been distilled into a policy (beleid). A policy should be the start of every project. It captures the most important requirements and conditions: the company's core values, security, and integrity. Those have to be translated into a solution, because they determine what is actually needed, and they give direction to what the IT specialists end up requiring.

This mirrors the broader Salomon/roadmap method: derive solutions from stated strategy and requirements, not the other way around.
