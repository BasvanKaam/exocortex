# The shift to a regie organization is a people problem, not a technical exercise

Bas frames the move from beheer (management) to regie (orchestration) explicitly as not just a technical exercise. The biggest area of attention is the competency domains, and these carry personnel consequences. His stance: organizations underestimate the human and skills side of becoming an orchestration organization, and that is where the transition actually succeeds or fails - not in the tooling.

In the masterclass he reinforces this by opening the module with a question put to colleagues/peers (a discussion exercise) rather than a tool walkthrough, deliberately surfacing the people dimension first.
