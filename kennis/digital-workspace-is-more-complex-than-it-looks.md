# The Digital Workspace Is More Complex Than People Make It Look

Bas argues that the digital workspace, and notably it does not live only in the cloud, is more complex today than people think or make it appear. His stance: consultants and sales engineers exist precisely to hide that complexity from clients, but doing so credibly requires real conceptual knowledge of the matter. He is mildly pointed that some make it 'overkomen' as simpler than it is. The teaching motive is explicit: even non-technical sales people should grasp the core concepts so they don't get lost 'door de bomen het bos niet meer'.
