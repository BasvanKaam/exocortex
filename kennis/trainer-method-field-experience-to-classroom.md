# Training Method: Bringing Field Experience Into the Classroom

Bas's instructional approach as a technical trainer rests on translating real-world field experience into training that resonates with both technical and business audiences.

Three pillars he draws on:
- **Strategic alignment** — understand both the technical challenges and the business drivers behind cloud adoption and digital transformation, so the message can be tuned to executives, IT pros, or end users.
- **Technical depth** — engineering background lets him answer complex technical questions in the room.
- **Storytelling** — years of consulting/advisory work shape compelling stories that illustrate key lessons.

Content design rules he follows:
- Build around real-world scenarios and case studies rather than theory alone. A cost-optimization session includes concrete examples of how organizations reduced Azure costs, not abstract strategy.
- Provide follow-up materials (guides, cheat sheets, playbooks) so learning continues after the session and complex concepts stay actionable.
- Connect theory to practice: learners should understand not just *how* something works but *why* it matters in their business context.
- Tailor per audience: with partners, focus on building technical skill plus the ability to deliver value to their customers; with end customers, shift to adoption and business benefit.
- Aim for a ripple effect — encourage participants to share what they learned, build action plans, and spread knowledge so they become champions.
