# Citrix 2013 solutions-focused certification structure

How it worked in 2013. Alongside XenDesktop 7, Citrix relaunched its certification program as solutions-focused, with three levels: Associate (CCA), Professional (CCP), Expert (CCE), under the new 'App and Desktop' track. Built on the familiar pillars of Designing, Deploying and Managing. The XD7 exams: 1Y0-200 (Managing), 1Y0-300 (Deploying), 1Y0-400 (Designing), open at Pearson VUE. Each credential stacks 1-3 exams.

Upgrade paths: existing CCIAs and CCEEs could update by passing a single exam (needed an unexpired credential); existing CCAs and CCAAs could reach CCA-AD with one exam. Instructor-led courses: CDX-400/CDX-300/CDX-203 (5 days each), plus CNS-206 for NetScaler 10. Citrix signalled Networking certs for late 2013 and a XenMobile track for 2014. A passed exam credential was 'yours for life'.
