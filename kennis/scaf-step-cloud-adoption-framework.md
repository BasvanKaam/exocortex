# SCAF: Step-Cloud Adoption Framework and its toolkit

The Step-Cloud Adoption Framework (SCAF) is Bas's own cloud adoption and implementation framework, documented in a book. It ships with a Documentation Toolkit: 19 ready-to-use working documents and templates in Word, Excel and PowerPoint, delivered as a single .zip under 2 MB.

The toolkit covers nearly all templates described in the SCAF chapters and is organised around the framework's phases. A few guides (Strategy, Insight, Execution, and Adoption templates) are intentionally left out of the toolkit because they are already described in detail in the book itself.

Related themes he covers under this banner: adoption frameworks, business cases, Cloud Center of Excellence, the cloud economy, delivery models, and governance.

*Bron: Inside Citrix (2016), 'SCAF Documentation Toolkit'.*
