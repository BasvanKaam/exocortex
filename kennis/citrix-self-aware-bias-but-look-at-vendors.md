# A self-declared Citrix hugger who still tells you to shop around

In this Aug 2013 essay Bas openly names his bias ('Since I'm a Citrix hugger', 'I'll start with my personal favourite') and argues Citrix has the edge on mobility through the Worx/MDX ecosystem and the third-party Citrix Ready Worx program ('For me this is where Citrix has the edge over their competitors'). But, and this is the telling part, his actual *advice* is vendor-neutral and consultant-honest: 'Don't put all your eggs in one basket... Look at different vendors! Just because company X recommends product Y doesn't mean it's right for you.' He repeatedly concedes competitors offer similar functionality and that 'it all depends', leaning on the consultant's mantra. The position captures a core Bas trait: passionate advocate for his favourite tech, but intellectually honest enough to separate his enthusiasm from buying advice.
