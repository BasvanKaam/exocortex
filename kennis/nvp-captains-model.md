# NVP Captains: Seeding a Community Program With Founding Members

When Bas launched the NVP program he didn't just name members, he named the first cohort as **NVP Captains**: a small founding group given an explicit role in shaping the program itself. Their job is program development, identifying potential future NVPs, and providing the outside-perspective feedback that keeps the initiative honest.

Reusable design pattern for standing up an advocacy/MVP-style program:
- Anoint a tiny founding class (3 people) and give them real influence over the program's design, not just a badge.
- Make them co-owners of selection and direction so the program has community legitimacy from day one.
- Roll out additional members on a **quarterly** cadence, selected, named and announced publicly.
- Position the founding members "at the very center" of shaping the future of the program.

The idea: put community leaders inside the machine rather than treating them as an audience.
