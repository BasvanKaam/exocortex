# How Citrix's solution-based certification tracks were structured (2014)

How it worked then (mid-2014): Citrix moved to solution-based certification tracks aligned to three pillars: Virtualization (design/deploy/manage XenDesktop 7.x), Networking (NetScaler), and Mobility (XenMobile).

- The credential levels stayed CCA / CCP / CCE (Associate, Professional, Expert), but the '-AD' suffix was replaced with -V, -N or -M for the chosen solution. So CCE-AD/CCP-AD/CCA-AD (themselves the renamed former CCIA/CCEE/CCA) became e.g. CCP-V.
- Upgrade paths via exams 1Y0-300 and 1Y0-400: e.g. a current CCEE took only the CCP-V exam to renew. Once those exams were discontinued you'd have to meet full requirements from the bottom up.
- Networking/Mobility had no upgrade paths (too new); CCA-N was not a prerequisite for CCP-N. A CCE-N was in development.
- All three tracks valid for three years; CCEE and CCIA exams retired 28 November 2014.

Fully dated certification trivia, kept as a snapshot of the Citrix cert landscape at the time.
