# Four types of resistance to cloud adoption (Step-Cloud Framework)

From Bas's Step-Cloud Framework. Core stance: cloud adoption stalls not because the tech isn't ready but because people aren't, and "not all resistance is bad, but you need to know what kind you're dealing with." He treats resistance as data, not friction. Understanding why people push back tells you where to focus leadership energy. Four recurring archetypes, each with his fix:

1. The Silent Obstructor: nods in meetings but doesn't act; projects quietly die in their inbox. Fix: engage early and give them ownership in the process.
2. The Tech Skeptic: usually someone who mastered the old world, so change feels like loss. Fix: don't lecture, coach; let them win in the new environment.
3. The Overwhelmed Middle Layer: caught between strategy and execution, afraid of being exposed. Fix: offer air cover, simplify deliverables, create small wins.
4. The Legacy Power Broker: built the current system, so walking away is personal. Fix: acknowledge their role, invite them to shape the new model.

The key isn't to bulldoze resistance, it's to decode it.
