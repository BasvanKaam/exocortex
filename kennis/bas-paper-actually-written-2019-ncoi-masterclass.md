# Correction: The Cloud-First Paper Dates to 2019, Not 2020

Bas's essay "Cloud only? Nee. Cloud first of hybride? Ja. De enige weg voorwaarts" (reproduced as Chapter 17 of his 2025 book) is recorded in the brain as written "at the end of 2020". The original assignment files correct this: it was the closing survey/essay for the NCOI Masterclass Cloud 2019/2020, submitted under his student number 4752950. At least two graded versions exist, dated 26-11-2019 and 17-12-2019 (both Versie 1.0), written in the cohort that the parallel 2020 Masterclass deck calls "Class of 2020".

The essay is explicitly not written from a single employer or a fixed client assignment: Bas states his views come from dozens of client conversations, assessments, roadmaps and implementations across many clients. Provenance detail worth keeping straight: the thesis is 2019, even though it surfaces in the 2025 book and a 2020 Masterclass.
