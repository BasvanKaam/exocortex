# SCAF Adoption cloud-governance go-live checklist

Governance is begun in the Execution phase and gets its finishing touches in Adoption, with the explicit caveat that governance needs time to grow and is always a process subject to change. The concrete checklist of governance items the template requires to have been brought to attention:

- All used cloud resources and their costs are visible; a cost-aware organization is the ultimate goal; someone (or a team) is made responsible; tooling is in use and mapped; first reports are made.
- Agile/iterative working is applied in practice.
- The main processes - incident, problem and change management - are set up and operational, however lean.
- All teams are onboarded; roles and responsibilities are clear; everyone knows what is expected.
- Newly built and delivered products/services are taken into management; daily operations cover monitoring, alerting, patching, reporting, automation, lifecycle and more.
- A strict security policy is applied, enforced and monitored.
- Ambassadors go 'into the field' to drive further promotion: workshops/trainings are organized, with room for questions and feedback to the user organizations.
- A communication plan is drawn up (possibly by the ambassadors) to regularly communicate which cloud-adoption phase the organization is in and what new products/services are being taken into use.
