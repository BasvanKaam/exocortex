# You must understand traditional solutions to give good cloud advice

Bas's stance on what makes a credible cloud advisor: cloud expertise alone is not enough. If you don't know or understand the 'traditional' (on-premises / datacenter) solutions, you are also less able to give the best advice with an eye on the cloud. In his words: 'Als je de traditionele oplossingen niet kent of begrijpt zul je ook minder goed in staat zijn om het beste advies te geven met het oog op de Cloud.'

The implication is deliberate: he positions his datacenter and traditional-infrastructure knowledge not as legacy baggage but as the foundation that makes his cloud advice better. A consultant who only knows the cloud cannot properly weigh migration, transition or hybrid choices, because those choices are anchored in what the organisation already runs.
