# The content and channel stack behind a community launch

Bas's reusable template for what a practitioner-community invitation actually offers, drawn from the Nerdio Tech Insider launch. The "what's in it for you" stack:

- A **private Slack channel** where customers, partners, enthusiasts, and vendor employees collaborate: questions, use-cases, tips, feature requests, general feedback.
- A steady stream of **training events and webinars** around the product and adjacent technologies (here AVD/Azure).
- **Release notes and helpful material** shared with the group as releases ship.
- Occasional **roadmap discussions** for those interested.
- **Swag** when circumstances allow.
- A clear note that the community does **not replace official support** (email, phone, video, Zendesk); those stay as the first point of reference. Important framing: a community supplements support, it does not become a backdoor support queue.

Channel mechanics he uses: a **Meetup page** as the public home and RSVP point for all future events, a **dated kick-off event** to create a moment, and an **intro blog** published in lockstep with the launch. The invite explicitly asks recipients to **forward to colleagues**, turning each contact into a distribution node.
