# Don't stress so much about the cloud

This is one of the signature positions of Bas's Salomon era, though the exact phrasing "Maak je niet zo druk over de Cloud" is a quote he attributes to his colleague Eric Kuiper (Salomon IT). Bas builds the whole post around it and clearly endorses it as his own stance.

The argument: cloud has grown so big that if you talk IT without naming cloud you are almost laughed at, and IT leaders need to stand firm to push back inside their own organizations. He recounts an IT lead who spent 20+ minutes raving about "de Cloud" and wanted to dive in completely tomorrow, while saner voices at the table advised starting with small steps. Hype travels: people hear enthusiastic stories, repeat them on the work floor, it reaches director level, and suddenly the question is "why aren't we in the cloud yet?"

Bas's answer is deliberately simple: don't stress. The cloud is there; can you go? Maybe. Must you? Perhaps. But first take time to look at what really matters in your business: the people, your users. Let function lead, and if cloud then fills your functional needs, fine. Don't do it the other way around. Don't let yourself be driven crazy, concentrate on the bigger picture.

Attribution note: the headline line is Eric Kuiper's; the framing, endorsement and the function-first elaboration are Bas's.
