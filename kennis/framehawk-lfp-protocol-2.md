# Framehawk's Lightweight Framebuffer Protocol

How it worked then (2014): Citrix re-acquired Framehawk, whose value was its Lightweight Framebuffer Protocol (LFP), a display protocol inspired by the founders' work in spacecraft communications at NASA. LFP was designed to communicate over long-distance, high-latency networks while maintaining security with no or minimal loss of quality, and to deliver a good user experience on mobile devices (touch, gesture mapping, offset mouse, keyboard popup). The bet was that blending it into XenApp/XenDesktop/XenMobile would put Citrix ahead on user experience over bad networks.
