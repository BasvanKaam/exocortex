# SCAF Adoption-phase management summary palette

A supporting-documentation template for the Adoption (Adoptie) phase of the SCAF (Step-Cloud Adoptie Framework). It is a one-page management summary "palette" that captures the state of the platform at handover/adoption against a fixed set of checkpoints:

- Delivery of the cloud platform including all products, services and technologies, with SLAs and DAPs in place.
- Taken into management / handed over, with all documentation, processes and procedures complete.
- Communication model defined and actively executed.
- Costs made transparent, with cost awareness established.
- Skill readiness plan progress.
- Cloud governance fully operational.
- Agile working (Plan-Do-Check-Act) being further fine-tuned.
- Ambassadors, training and support in place.

This is a consulting/training deliverable artifact that operationalizes the SCAF Adoption phase into a reportable checklist; the framework itself is covered in the book.
