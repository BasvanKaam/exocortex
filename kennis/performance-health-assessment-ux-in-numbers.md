# Performance & Health Assessment: user experience in numbers

An assessment that screens the full infrastructure and shows first results within hours. It answers: which systems are loaded hardest and by what, which groups of users suffer (possibly continuously) and why, and whether the cause is the hypervisor, network, storage, insufficient compute/memory, a config error, the browser, other software, or a combination.

Key principle: the whole chain matters because user experience is only as strong as the weakest link. The assessment makes infrastructure performance visible and expresses the end-user experience in objective numbers, then separates improvements into low-hanging fruit versus longer-term items, so appropriate action can follow (by Salomon, own staff, or a partner of choice).

Prevention angle: running the process continuously in the background lets problems be avoided before they occur — 'voorkomen is beter dan genezen'.
