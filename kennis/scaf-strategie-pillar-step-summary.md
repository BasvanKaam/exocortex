# SCAF Strategie Pillar: Summary of Steps

Bas summarises determining the initial cloud strategy as a sequence of steps. He flags that from step 4 (business case) onward, goals and actions are treated in later chapters (Cloud Economy, Inzicht and Uitvoering):

1. **Interviews and Workshops (information gathering):** one-on-one interviews with key stakeholders (CEO, CTO, CIO, IT management, senior architects, CISO); group workshops for shared insight and IT-business synergy; collect existing documents (mission, vision, IT strategies, security policy, running projects); store everything digitally in a shared platform (e.g. Teams or SharePoint).
2. **Analysis and Evaluation (classify and prioritise):** run a SWOT; use a priority matrix ranking drivers/objectives by urgency and impact, distinguishing essential (noodzaak) from desirable (nut); review findings with stakeholders to agree priorities.
3. **Defining the Cloud Strategy:** set primary goals for migration (cost optimisation, flexibility, innovation speed); define measurable KPIs/success criteria; run a gap analysis (current vs desired) for skills, technologies or processes.
4. **Compact Business Case:** cost-benefit analysis using the TCO template (part of the Inzicht phase); risk analysis including impact of running IT projects (Inzicht phase); discuss cloud options public/private/hybrid (prep in Inzicht, decision in Uitvoering).
5. **Roadmap and Planning:** detailed IT roadmap with milestones (evaluate current architecture, run PoCs, migrate first workloads — across Inzicht and Uitvoering); identify people for a CCoE; plan regular evaluation moments.
6. **Communication and Alignment (stakeholder management):** a visual strategy map with goals, KPIs and success factors; periodic progress/risk/results reports.
7. **Initiating the Execution Phase:** start a Proof of Concept (Uitvoering); make the technology/platform choice (e.g. Azure, AWS, Google Cloud) based on the business case (Uitvoering); organise training and upskilling (Inzicht).
