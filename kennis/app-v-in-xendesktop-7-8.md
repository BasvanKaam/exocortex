# App-V management from Citrix Studio (XenDesktop 7.8)

As of XenDesktop 7.8, Citrix Studio can manage distribution of App-V applications to VDI virtual desktops or XenApp servers without requiring a separate App-V server and database infrastructure. This simplifies administration and eliminates additional infrastructure costs. To add App-V packages directly from Studio, you fill in the accompanying UNC path and no further App-V infrastructure components are needed; Ctxappvlauncher.exe handles everything.
