# Community resources: Team RGE and Marius Sandbu's NetScaler e-Book

- Team RGE (Remote Graphics Experts, teamrge.com): a community group of industry experts - Shawn Bass, Ruben Spruijt and Benny Tritsch - focused on remoting graphics for virtual desktops and applications, sharing unbiased, independent knowledge via blog posts, white papers, videos and presentations. They offer a downloadable whitepaper.
- Marius Sandbu's NetScaler e-Book (msandbu.wordpress.com): Marius, a prolific blogger who has authored NetScaler books for Packt Publishing, started a free NetScaler e-Book project because frequent product releases make printed content hard to keep current. The first step focused on Optimizing NetScaler traffic features.
