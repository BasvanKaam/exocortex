# Intel entering vGPU turns the GPU space into a three-way race

Reacting to the HP Moonshot + Intel Xeon E3 / Iris Pro GPU announcement at Synergy 2014, Bas flagged Intel entering the vGPU and 3D graphics space as 'sort of a big thing' and framed it as a market shift: 'the game is on between NVIDIA, AMD and now Intel as well!' He saw the HP Moonshot + Intel + XenApp combination as delivering richer graphics, higher density, lower TCO and better end-user experience. Notable as an early read that GPU-accelerated VDI/published-apps would become a competitive battleground, not a niche.

*Bron: blogpost 'Citrix Synergy 2014 Keynote wrap-up, here's what's new!' (2014-05-07), basvankaam.com.*
