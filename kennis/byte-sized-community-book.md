# Project Byte-Sized: a crowd-authored cloud book for charity

Byte-Sized ("Cloud design principles and architectural recommendations") was an idea Bas had years earlier and pitched to Christiaan Brinkhoff. Distinctive model: they didn't write the bulk themselves; they invited IT people worldwide to submit their best cloud advice (technical, strategic, future-looking, funny), submissions ranging from a few lines to 5-6 pages. Over 140 co-authors. ~700 physical copies sold worldwide plus ~200 distributed otherwise. Proceeds (after income tax, which they partly self-compensated) went to One Laptop Per Child. The book was later released as a free PDF with a PayPal donate button. How it worked then: community books were produced via networks and Amazon KDP for charity.
