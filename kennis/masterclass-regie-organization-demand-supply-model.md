# Masterclass: Demand-Supply regie model for the orchestration organization

In the masterclass module "Van beheer naar regie" (from management to orchestration), Bas teaches the regie (brokerage/orchestration) function as the layer between Business and IT Delivery. The model splits into three columns: Business poses the demand ("Wat?" / what), the regiefunctie translates demand into supply ("Hoe?" / how), and IT Delivery handles realization. The orchestration organization owns the demand-to-supply translation rather than doing the delivery itself.

This is a teaching framing specific to the training; pair it with the supporting toolkit note.
