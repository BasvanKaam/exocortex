# Use-cases for VDI/desktop benchmarking

Bas's list of why you benchmark a (virtual) desktop environment, which holds up beyond any single tool: go-live simulation; maximizing end-user experience; enhanced performance reporting; measuring performance before production; hypervisor concurrent-usage guesstimation; verification of new infrastructure; measuring the impact of changes; testing hotfixes and service packs; burn-in testing of new hardware; performance baselining. The durable principle: measure before production and after changes, and use baselining to know what "workable" looks like.
