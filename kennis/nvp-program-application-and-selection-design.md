# NVP Program: Application and Selection Design

The NVP application form captures how the Nerdio Valued Professional community-advocate program sources and evaluates candidates. It is a reusable blueprint for running a vendor advocate/MVP-style program.

Intake fields collected:
- Name, Company, Role.
- Social channels (Twitter, LinkedIn, personal website, Reddit, etc.).
- Other community titles, if applicable (so existing MVP/community recognition is visible).

The form deliberately points candidates at the introduction blog post announcing the first three NVPs before they sign up, so applicants self-calibrate against the bar already set.

Three selection questions, each chosen to test a different signal:
1. Why do you want to become an NVP? (motivation/fit)
2. Concrete examples of Nerdio-related community (not professional) work, links preferred: product feedback, blog posts, social shares, podcasts, supporting/teaching others, evangelizing Nerdio in general. (evidence of prior, unpaid community contribution)
3. What are your plans once you become an NVP, anything you are working on or planning short term? (forward intent and momentum)

Key design choices worth reusing:
- Explicitly separate community contribution from professional/paid work. The program rewards voluntary advocacy, not day-job deliverables.
- Ask for links/evidence rather than self-assessment.
- Set a service-level promise on the back end: evaluate and respond within 5 working days. Treating applicants with the same responsiveness you would a customer is part of keeping the program credible.
- Use a public reference cohort (the first three NVPs) as the calibration anchor for new applicants.
