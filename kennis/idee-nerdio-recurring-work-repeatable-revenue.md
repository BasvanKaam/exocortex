# Idea: Turn recurring MSP work into repeatable revenue with Nerdio

Article/content concept. Positioning: transform operational tasks into productized, revenue-generating services.

The angle: the MSP margin problem is time-consuming, low-margin work (Intune policy updates, user provisioning, app deployment). Nerdio becomes a packaging engine: use policy baselines, scripted actions, and auto-scaling as the backbone for standardized service tiers (e.g. Silver/Gold/Platinum endpoint care). Concrete example service: monthly policy drift reports plus automated remediation.

The pricing argument: charge per outcome, not per hour (e.g. per secured device per month).

CTA hook: offer a Nerdio-based service-design template or 'packaging canvas'.
