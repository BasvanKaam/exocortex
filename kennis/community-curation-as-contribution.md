# Stance: curating the community's best content is a contribution worth making

In September 2015 Bas launched a monthly 'Community Edition' roundup, hand-picking the best EUC/Citrix blogs, podcasts, tools and news of the month. His view is that the volume of good community content is overwhelming and hard to keep track of, so curation has value in itself. He's deliberately inclusive: he'll share vendor-oriented blogs too 'as long as it is helpful, interesting or entertaining', and openly invites readers to flag anything he missed. He also notes how much work it is: 'this is my first monthly effort and it's harder than you might think. You have to pay attention all the time.' Shows his early role as a community connector/curator, not just a technical author.

*Bron: blogpost 'My personal Community Edition: best of August 2015' (2015-09-01), basvankaam.com.*
