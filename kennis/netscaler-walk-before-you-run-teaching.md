# Teaching stance: master the basics before the advanced NetScaler features

In August 2015, opening a multi-part NetScaler getting-started series, Bas frames the product as interesting and powerful but potentially confusing for newcomers, with its licensing models, many ADC features, and an alphabet soup of abbreviations (NSIP, SNIP, MIP, VIP). His stance: "It's important to understand the basics and work your way up from there. Walk before you run." He deliberately builds a layered series of foundational articles rather than diving into advanced configuration, a recurring teaching instinct in his EUC-era writing.

*Bron: blogpost 'Citrix NetScaler basics: VIPs, service/server objects and monitors' (2015-08-03), basvankaam.com.*
