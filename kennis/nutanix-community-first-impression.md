# Nutanix's community-first posture impressed him

From the Dutch HQ opening (February 2014), Bas's takeaway was as much about culture as product. He was struck that Nutanix (CEO Dheeraj Pandey, Howard Ting, Mark Fisher present) genuinely wanted to know what the community thought of the company, products and marketing, and asked what they could do to make evaluating and testing easier. He read this as a vendor that understood the power of community and was willing to invest in it. Howard Ting's framing that the community helps keep Nutanix honest landed with him: "that's exactly what we toasted to, honesty, cheers!" His verdict, even before deep technical evaluation: "good things are coming."
