# De Cloud Professionals: focused cloud-workspace advisory proposition

Proposition and website copy for 'De Cloud Professionals' (decloudprofessionals.nl), a Microsoft/Windows/Office 365-based cloud-workspace advisory brand. Mission: always go for the best possible advice. The collective claims nearly 100 years of combined experience and positions hybrid as the dominant model.

Scope they advise on:
- Windows Virtual Desktop (then a new Microsoft service).
- Microsoft / Office 365, Intune / Autopilot and related services.
- Citrix (hybrid) cloud, optionally combined with WVD.
- Amazon Workspaces and related services.
- Other cloud services: disaster recovery, backup & restore, workspace aggregation, etc.

Key claim: because of long experience they also retain deep knowledge of traditional on-premises environments, which they argue is a must since most organizations are mid-transition from on-prem to cloud. They work closely with managed service providers, security specialists, and workspace/process experts so the client gets everything under one roof with a single point of contact.

Dated: WVD described as a brand-new, soon-to-be-announced service; positioning is tied to that 2019-2020 cloud-workspace moment.
