# LTSR is the right call, but not for everybody

Bas's take (April 2016): LTSR "isn't for everybody." He frames the choice as fundamentally about the type of organisation. LTSR fits slower-moving companies where innovation and cutting-edge tech aren't a top priority, especially bureaucracy-heavy customers, plus mission-critical and high-security workloads. If you want innovation and the newest features, go CR instead. His personal preference, stated in passing, is the '-1 version approach' on CR: not running the latest-and-greatest all the time, deliberately staying one version behind for stability. Overall verdict on Citrix introducing the options: "definitely a good move from Citrix, the customer has spoken, they listened and this is the result. Having options never hurt anybody right?" His one criticism is communication: Citrix could have handled the messaging around CR better, because people (customers and others) wrongly assumed support had changed when it hadn't, causing avoidable confusion.
