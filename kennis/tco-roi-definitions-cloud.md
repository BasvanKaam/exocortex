# TCO and ROI Defined for Cloud Decisions

**Total Cost of Ownership (TCO):** all direct and indirect costs around the use of a product or service, i.e. the financial impact. Includes hardware, software, management/operations work, training, governance, etc. Apply it to both the 'old' and 'new' situation.

**Return on Investment (ROI):** a financial instrument measuring whether an investment is worthwhile (does it yield enough), i.e. the return.

Formula: ROI = (expected return of the investment minus the one-time cost of the investment) divided by the one-time cost of the investment, expressed as a percentage (x 100%).

**Worked example:** a new cloud service costs 500 euro/month (OpEx). The old situation cost that same service/application 1000 euro/month (initial CapEx divided by months of depreciation). Positive difference: 500 euro x 12 = 6000 euro per year. Transition/adoption costs (design, build, configure, migrate, temporary overlap) = 2000 euro. ROI = 6000 - 2000 = 4000 euro; 4000 / 2000 = 200% in the plus.

**Putting it together (business case):** compare the TCO of the old and new situation (subtract 'new' from 'old') to get the difference, add the one-time/temporary transition costs, and you can fairly easily compute whether it gives the desired result. Then view it over 1, 3 or e.g. 5 years (ROI).

Source: Bas van Kaam, Salomon IT blog, 2019-10-23.
