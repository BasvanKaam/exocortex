# Treating the job as a hobby you'd do anyway

In his 2020 review Bas articulates a core driver: "Even though the above is part of my job, or most of it anyway, it's hobby as well and I love to spend additional time on all this." The whole list of community-building, blogging, podcasting and user-group founding blurs the line between work and passion for him.

Related framing he uses: "Teamwork makes the dream work, right?" and repeated, specific credit to named collaborators (Christiaan Brinkhoff, Stefan Dingemanse, Esther Barthel, Niels Kok, the EUCdigest crew). He is conspicuously generous with attribution - notably flagging that he didn't attend a single EUCdigest recording yet calls the team's work amazing. This community-and-credit stance is a consistent part of his professional identity.
