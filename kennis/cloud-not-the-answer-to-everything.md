# Cloud Is Not the Answer to Everything

Bas explicitly refuses to claim the cloud is the answer to everything; realistically it is not. His position is calibrated: cloud services can in many cases simplify IT, with the side benefit that companies keep a stronger focus on what truly matters (daily operations, their products, services and customers). For that reason 'cloud' should at all times be considered as part of your broader IT strategy, but as one option to weigh, not a default destination. This balanced, anti-hype stance is characteristic of his cloud-era thinking.

Source: Bas van Kaam, Salomon IT blog, 2019-09-17.
