# Opdracht 5: build your own business case (the method)

Bas's fifth assignment asks the reader to attempt a first real business case now that they understand what one should look like. He notes later chapters cover gathering information (Inzicht/insight) to clarify the current situation, but if you already know your current situation and lean towards cloud, this is a valuable exercise ('Oefening baart immers kunst' - practice makes perfect).

The steps:
- Identify the business situation and needs: type of company, number of users, specific IT needs (shared/multi-session desktops, specific applications, security requirements, geographic spread). Sketch which IT infrastructure could fit (e.g. need for high scalability vs full control).
- Analyse the options: compare the four solutions - on-premises, co-location, managed services and cloud - on cost (Capex vs Opex), maintenance requirements, scalability and flexibility. Estimate total cost (TCO) short and long term; reuse data from earlier assignments.
- Assess risks and strategic considerations: risks/benefits regarding technological flexibility, security, regulatory compliance, and impact on organisational culture; consider organisational changes needed for implementation.
- Draft a Capex vs Opex proposal: show how the choice plays out and how it aligns with the company's financial strategy (e.g. limited cashflow vs long-term investments).
- Calculate ROI and TCO over three to five years, backed by costs for maintenance, management and staffing. Bas notes this can be hard at this point and may be skipped.

Bas frames the broader purpose: it develops sharp insight into IT-transformation choices, strengthens the ability to back business decisions with numbers, and (optionally) presenting the proposal builds presentation skills and team collaboration. Even if the current situation isn't fully clear yet, the exercise yields tangible insights.
