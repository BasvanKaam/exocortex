# IoT networks won't be winner-take-all (2017 prediction)

Closing the IoT series in August 2017, Bas declined to pick a single winning IoT network and argued they need not be mutually exclusive: 'there's no real reason why they should be mutual exclusive, they don't have to be.' His reasoning: LTE carriers (Verizon, AT&T) focus on the high end while LPWAN providers (LoRa via IBM/Cisco/Swisscom/KPN) target lower-to-mid sensor markets, leaving room for both, and the picture differs by region (LPWAN well distributed in Europe, NB-IoT/LTE-M ahead in the US). He flagged 5G (standards due 2018, commercial ~2020) as a future factor. Core durable framing: 'In the end, it will all depend on the use-case at hand... the range of (I)IoT is too broad to fit just one or two networks.'
