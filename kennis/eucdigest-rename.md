# EUC-X renamed to EUCdigest (2019)

EUC-X was legally forced to rename because EUC-X was already taken; the backup name EUCdigest became the brand (Twitter handle @EUCdigest). Renaming a registered non-profit foundation was cumbersome: papers to sign, places to physically visit, ~1.5 months lost. The crew (Johan van Amersfoort, Kees Baggerman, Ingmar Verheij, Jits Langedijk, Eric van Klaveren, Bas van Kaam) had microphones and recording software in place and a first podcast recording date planned. How it worked then: even a volunteer community brand carried real legal/bureaucratic overhead in NL.
