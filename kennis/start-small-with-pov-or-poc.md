# Bas: build buy-in with a small PoV or PoC and free resources

Within the Opdracht 5 wrap-up, Bas advises that a temporary Proof of Value (PoV) or Proof of Concept (PoC) can build support ('draagvlak') within the organisation at this stage. His guidance: start small, with initiatives that have direct, immediate utility, and consider using available free options - free cloud resources (subscriptions/trial credits), events, internal trainings, and consulting relevant books and magazines.
