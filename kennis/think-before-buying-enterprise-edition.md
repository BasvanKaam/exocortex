# Don't buy XenMobile Enterprise on impulse, scope your actual needs

Bas pushed back on a pattern he saw in the field (May 2014): companies, often poorly advised and tempted by Citrix's 20% Enterprise-edition discount, implementing XenMobile Enterprise 'without even thinking.' His position: scope first. If you only want to manage hardware (devices) and push some native apps, MDM alone might be enough; you only need App Controller + ShareFile Enterprise if you genuinely need to manage and secure business apps/data separately from personal data. 'it all depends (there it is again ;-)'.

He frames mobility as a strategy decision, not a product purchase: device ownership, BYOD policy, data ownership, security levels, lost/stolen/leaver handling are all 'serious content to consider as part of your mobile IT strategy' that should be settled before buying. Recommends a proper PoC, taking it one step at a time. An early statement of his consultant-not-salesman stance.
