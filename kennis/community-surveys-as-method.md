# Why community surveys are a simple, effective way to gather industry insight

In this Aug 2016 pre-announcement Bas explains why he loves running community surveys: the concept is "dead simple, yet very effective when properly executed" -- a set of questions answered by dozens or hundreds of likeminded IT people worldwide, surfacing valuable information. He cites Project Virtual Reality Check's "State of the VDI and SBC union" as the biggest, best-known example that inspired him. His method notes: cap it (30-35 questions) to avoid overkill, mix multiple-choice with free-text "Other" fields, ensure a core set (~15) applies to everyone, report N-answered-per-question transparently, and go through every individual submission to group likeminded thoughts. Shows his community-research instinct and his transparency about methodology.
