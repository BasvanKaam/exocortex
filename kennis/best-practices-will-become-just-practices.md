# Web-scale will make today's best practices obsolete, change is coming fast

Bas's June 2014 conviction, stated as a prediction: web-scale is here to stay and will eventually take over. Citing Gartner's forecast that web-scale would run in 50% of global enterprises by 2017 (up from <10% in 2013), he argued the hardware-centric, SAN-based way of doing things is on borrowed time: 'The best practices we once knew will soon become obsolete and known as just practices, the way we once did stuff.'

He's careful and self-aware about hype ('I know, I get carried away sometimes, but you have to admit that there's a truth in there somewhere, right?'). His nuance: SANs won't vanish overnight, they're useful and too expensive to dump, and he genuinely likes them ('I do like them, really'). What changes is how things are put together (converged) and managed (software). As a Citrix engineer he welcomes it: 'we need more speed (massive IOPS and less latency), flexibility, scalability and ease of management.' A clear, dated-but-prescient bet on the direction of infrastructure.
