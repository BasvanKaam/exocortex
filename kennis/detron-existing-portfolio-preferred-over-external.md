# Prefer the existing portfolio over external products, even for externally-born ideas

In his Detron proposition-development and inventory/selection processes, Bas takes an explicit stance: when building out a new proposition, candidate products/partners can and often should come from Detron's existing portfolio, even when the idea or customer question originated externally. He notes this frequently has the preference for strategic reasons.

It is a deliberate bias toward leveraging what you already have and your existing partner relationships before reaching outside, rather than defaulting to a new vendor for every new idea.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> Hierbij is het belangrijk om op te merken dat potentiele producten/partners ook vanuit het bestaande Detron portfolio voort kunnen komen - ook al is het idee, of de vraag 'extern' ontstaan. Vaak heeft dit om strategische redenen zelfs de voorkeur.
