# XenDesktop 7.5 added little net-new over XenApp 7.5

Writing in January 2014, Bas was underwhelmed by XenDesktop 7.5 on its own merits. His conclusion: "there isn't that much to add when compared to XenApp 7.5." The biggest genuinely new pieces he saw were Hybrid Cloud Provisioning and improved Remote PC Access (Wake-on-LAN via SCCM); the rest was already present or had arrived with XenApp. He noted the whole 7.5 launch was dominated by the re-introduction of XenApp, with XenDesktop almost an afterthought, and that mobility was pushing the industry to be more about applications than anything else.

*Bron: blogpost 'Citrix XenDesktop 7.5... What's new?!' (2014-01-30), basvankaam.com.*
