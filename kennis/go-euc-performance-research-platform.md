# Go-EUC: independent community EUC performance research

Go-EUC is an open, independent, community platform focused primarily on performance research within the EUC domain. Per the EUCdigest episode featuring Eltjo van Gulik and Ryan Ververs-Bijkerk, its work involves defining what makes up a test run and handling the huge volumes of data generated across many test runs, all in service of understanding end-user experience. Notable as part of the independent-benchmarking culture in the EUC community Bas champions.

*Bron: blogpost 'EUCdigest Podcast EPISODE 4 – Go-EUC The Initiative That Compares' (2020-02-11), basvankaam.com.*
