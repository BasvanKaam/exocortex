# WFH is not going away anytime soon

Writing at the end of 2020 (mid-COVID), Bas frames working from home as a durable shift rather than a temporary measure: "Since WFH isn't going away anytime soon, I decided to again upgrade my WFH setup." This is the worldview behind much of his EUC content in this period: remote work as the new baseline, which is the context for his interest in workspace-aggregation tooling (e.g. the Awingu episode weeks later) and end-user experience.
