# SCAF supporting templates: RACI, Risk Register, Skill Readiness

Bas's Step-Cloud Adoptie Framework (SCAF) ships a toolkit of supporting templates that run alongside the four main phases. Three recurring management instruments:

- **RACI-matrix**: defines responsibilities and roles for team members and stakeholders. RACI = Responsible (does the work), Accountable (end-responsible), Consulted (gives input), Informed (kept up to date on progress and results). In cloud adoption it brings clarity to complex processes like migration, governance and maintenance, preventing miscommunication.
- **Risk Register / risicoregister**: identifies, analyses and manages risks throughout the migration. Per risk it captures the nature, likelihood, expected impact and planned mitigations. It is a *dynamic* document, updated regularly so new risks are added and existing ones adjusted as the project advances.
- **Skill Readiness Planning template**: assesses and plans the skills and knowledge levels of team members during the transition. Columns typically cover required knowledge areas, current skill levels, and gaps to be filled with training or coaching, so organisations can monitor skill-readiness progress.
