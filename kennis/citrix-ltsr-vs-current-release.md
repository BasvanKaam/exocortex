# Citrix LTSR vs Current Release

Beyond the standard lifecycle policy (Mainstream Maintenance, Extended Maintenance, End of Life), Citrix introduced two maintenance tracks: the **Long Term Service Release (LTSR)** and the **Current Release (CR)**.

**LTSR** benefits (per Citrix): Extended Lifecycle — for each LTSR the clock restarts, giving 5 years of mainstream support and 5 years of extended support (separate contract required); Predictable Maintenance — cumulative updates on a regular schedule containing mostly fixes, void of new features; Reduced IT Costs — highest-quality releases with the most predictable schedule, lowering TCO.

**CR** benefits: rapid innovation with the latest security, productivity and collaboration features; ability to address new business challenges and more use cases; faster delivery of enhancement requests via shorter release cycles.

Bas's note: the CR isn't really new — it's basically the way things always were before the LTSR was introduced; Citrix just needed a second name to distinguish the two.
