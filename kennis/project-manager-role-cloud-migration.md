# The project manager's role across migration phases

Bas folds the project manager into his phased migration model as a role that plans, coordinates and guides the migration so it finishes on budget and on time - across the execution and adoption phases, and probably already during the insight phase, since the PM is often responsible for assigning roles and responsibilities.

What a PM does: planning and coordination (migration plan, phases, deadlines, milestones); role and task allocation; risk management (spotting risks early, action plans); communication to IT, business, suppliers and partners; budget control (including finding cheaper alternatives); quality control via tests and audits during and after migration; adoption and training; and post-migration evaluation and optimisation.

Shifting focus per phase - Execution: planning and guiding the technical migration, monitoring progress, minimising risk. Adoption: supporting users, gathering feedback, smoothing the transition. In an ideal world the PM keeps everything running smoothly from first plans to final adoption.
