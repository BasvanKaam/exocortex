# IGEL UD Pocket: a USB stick that turns any PC into a thin client

How it worked then (2017). The IGEL UD Pocket is a small (12.2mm x 22.4mm), hardened, dual-boot 8GB USB device. Boot a workstation, laptop (Mac included) or tablet from it and it loads IGEL Universal Desktop Linux, turning the machine into a full thin client in seconds (Bas measured ~15s from boot menu) without touching the OS already on the internal disk. Minimum requirements: 64-bit CPU, 2GB+ RAM, Intel/AMD/Nvidia graphics, bootable USB 2.0/3.0 port, ethernet or wireless. Supports two-factor auth via smart card readers or USB tokens. The durability (dust/shock/water resistant) and reuse of existing hardware make it a 'bring your own thin client' (BYOTC) play that gives older desktops a second life and cuts replacement costs.
