# Bas's research workflow: pen-and-paper first, then digitalise

His personal method for any new piece of writing: get out the paper notepad and pen first, and write down what he already knows, what he still needs to find out, and (some, not all) new information found during research. Only after the note-taking does he rewrite everything on the laptop, digitalising it and adding extra detail and graphics for a smoother look.

He frames a 'hybrid' fallback for people who won't go full analog: a digital pen such as the Livescribe, which captures handwriting while digitalising the data automatically. Goal of the whole process: 'absorb as much information as I possibly can.'

*Bron: blogpost 'My version of why the pen is mightier than the keyboard' (2015-02-09), basvankaam.com.*
