# Microsoft's WVD power scripts are not real autoscaling

In this August 2020 post Bas draws a hard line between Microsoft's built-in power-management scripts for WVD and Nerdio's autoscaling engine: "Sure, there are some power management scripts available from Microsoft, but that doesn't come close to what we are capable of doing. And even if it did, it wouldn't be compatible with our code."

This is an early Nerdio-era competitive framing: position the platform's value as the autoscaling/cost-savings engine, and dismiss the native option as not in the same league. Written shortly after he joined Nerdio as Field CTO EMEA/UK.
