# Idea: 30-day Nerdio engineer bootcamp framework

Content/onboarding concept. Positioning: fast-track onboarding and internal skills growth. Most MSPs struggle with standardized internal training; Nerdio's scope makes this easier.

Four-week structure:
- Week 1: UI orientation, RBAC, account provisioning.
- Week 2: Intune integration, policy management.
- Week 3: Scripted actions, auto-scaling, alerts.
- Week 4: Real-world scenarios and labs (e.g. customer onboarding).

Add a gamification/certification layer (scorecard or badge system) and use Nerdio's audit logs to review learner performance. CTA: free downloadable bootcamp syllabus.

This is a reusable training-design pattern: orientation first, then integration, then automation, then applied scenarios, with a measurable certification layer on top.
