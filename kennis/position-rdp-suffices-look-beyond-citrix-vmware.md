# Position: RDP Suffices in 70%+ of Cases, So Look Beyond Citrix/VMware

Bas is openly fond of Citrix and VMware ('ik draag ze een warm hart toe'), but argues it never hurts to broaden your horizon because there are multiple roads to Rome. His core argument for considering alternatives:

- Choice is good and competition is good, especially for the end customer.
- Several alternatives are cloud-native, bringing cost, simplicity (including the license model), a low entry point and no caps on the number of licenses.
- The RDP protocol has improved strongly in recent years and suffices in 70% of cases or more.
- The feature set of these solutions is more than enough for the vast majority of companies.

He pairs this with a realism check on SaaS-only: research and thousands of specialists agree that legacy ('pardon, traditional') Windows applications will be with us for many years. With only a browser and no backend (on-prem or cloud) you will not make it for the foreseeable future. Making it feel SaaS-like to end users is a separate story.
