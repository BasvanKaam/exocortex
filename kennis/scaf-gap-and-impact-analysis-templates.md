# SCAF GAP-analysis and Impact-analysis templates

Two analysis templates in Bas's SCAF:

**SCAF GAP-analyse**: compares the current situation (IST) of IT components and processes with the desired situation (SOLL). It identifies specific areas where knowledge, automation, tools or services don't yet meet the desired standards, and indicates where training, outsourcing or other solutions are needed. This helps teams prioritise and plan targeted actions to close identified gaps so the Cloud environment meets governance and operational requirements.

**SCAF Impact analyse**: used to run an impact analysis. It contains a matrix plotting the **likelihood** of an event (low to very high) against its **impact** (also low to very high). This matrix helps identify and prioritise risks arising from Cloud migration or other IT changes, so teams can take targeted measures to control potential problems and minimise disruptions during the implementation and adoption phases.
