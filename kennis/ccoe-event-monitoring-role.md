# CCoE role in event monitoring and incident management

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

The CCoE supports the organization in designing the optimal configuration of tooling or services, helps determine the impact of service-component failure(s) in the cloud, and designs the tooling platform for the use of programmatic interfaces (APIs) for event monitoring.

Responsibility demarcation to settle: who is responsible for full-stack monitoring, who is responsible for monitoring cloud services, and who is responsible for the entire service.

To-do list: create a flowchart showing who is responsible when and who can be consulted during which period; set up a provider dashboard for the support team; train the support team to estimate the impact of cloud service disruptions; and create a RACI model for the support organization during the transition.
