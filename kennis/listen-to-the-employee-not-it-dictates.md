# Listen to the employee, don't let IT dictate how they work

Bas's stance: the bigger issue behind device and lockdown debates is that IT departments too often decide for employees what they get and how they must do their work, when they should be listening to them. He sees employees wanting more freedom than IT grants, and argues that meeting that desire (own device, own apps, work anywhere) improves both happiness and efficiency. IT is a means, not an end, and is wrongly treated as a necessary evil. He explicitly ties this to a Salomon differentiator: they always go into conversation with clients, and the strength is in listening, inventory and measuring ('meten = weten'), attention that he says is too rarely paid elsewhere, thinking from the user's perspective.
