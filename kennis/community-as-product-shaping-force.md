# Community feedback as the force that shapes the product

Launching the Nerdio Tech Insider program (Sept 2020), Bas frames community not as marketing but as the core input to product direction: "it is one of the main reasons why our product, Nerdio Manager for WVD is shaped the way it is."

His stated deal: this is about technology alone, no marketing/sales/leads, members owe "close to nothing" in return beyond honest feedback now and then. The program is explicitly built to learn FROM the community ('This program will be about YOU, and what we can learn from YOU').

This reflects a recurring belief of his: that he gets value by being deep inside the EUC/WVD community, and that product and credibility flow from that proximity. Consistent with his earlier years running community blogs and podcasts.
