# Be a Learn-It-All Organization, Not a Know-It-All One

Bas frames Nerdio's community stance around a clear position: an organization should be a "learn-it-all" rather than a "know-it-all" one. He couples this with belief in the power of community, co-innovation, and driving progress forward together with partners, enterprise customers, fans and peers.

In practice this means: a vendor shouldn't assume it has all the answers; it should build structured channels (programs like NVP, feedback loops, founding-member input) to learn from the field and let outside perspective shape the product and the program. The NVP program is the operational expression of this stance.
