# Bet: WVD third-party vendors are where the action is

In May 2020, before publicly joining Nerdio, Bas revealed he had spent the prior two months 'teaming up with a couple of WVD third party vendors, one of which is Nerdio.' His framing: he was 'especially interested' in Nerdio Manager for WVD specifically, and saw the traction and could 'see why.' Captures the moment his attention shifted from the broad EUC/community-evangelist role toward the WVD-on-Azure tooling layer. The teased 'personal announcement' was the precursor to his Nerdio hire one week later.
