# Blog topic backlog: closing the IT-business gap via cloud adoption

A backlog of blog ideas, all oriented around the non-technical side of cloud and the gap between IT and the business:

- Zoom in on the 'gap' between IT and the business: what we mean exactly, with examples; a strategy map can help (parts 1 and 2).
- Governance: a much-used term, but what does it actually mean? Examples plus visuals; governance grows, so where do we (minimally) start?
- Why strategy matters and how to approach it. Only ask questions if you know what you will do with the answers.
- From reactive to proactive IT, linked to cloud and therefore SCAF.
- The value of a business case and how to tackle one.
- AI does not lead to a 12-hour work week (re: James Ma), but smarter use of technology can win a lot.
- A GAP analysis to map knowledge shortfalls.
- Capturing process: how to do it, applying the KISS principle, with examples.
- Inventorying: explain the 'waves' principle, possibly introduce the 'palet' overviews.
- Strengths and weaknesses: why mapping them pays off (SWOT can help).
- Applying agile and 'lessons learned' during an adoption/implementation track; the plan-do-check-act lifecycle.
- Adoption, communication, and ambassadors: why they matter and how to approach them.
- Using cloud: everything except the technology, and its impact on your organisation.

The through-line: cloud success is mostly an organisational, governance, and adoption problem, not a technical one.
