# Idea: a cross-unit Technology Board

Stand up a Technology Board that spans the business units, optionally split into work domains, chaired by him with members drawn from across the units. Cadence: meet physically once a month, virtually every other week, at a central location, preferably not the office. Agenda covers propositions, marketing, sales, and event planning, with rotating guests.

He ties this to the Detron precedent, where the Technology Board acted as a sounding board and fed the internal knowledge cafe (DiK).
