# BYOD has graduated from hype to something you can't ignore

Writing in 2013, Bas's framing thesis is that BYOD has crossed from buzzword to operational reality: 'the whole bring your own device (BYOD) concept has evolved from being a hype to something we can no longer ignore and must take seriously before it's too late.' He grounds it in tablet-adoption growth figures and frames the real problem as a human tension, not a technical one: locked-down corporate devices keep data safe but make users miserable, while letting personal apps onto corporate devices risks leakage. His stance is that containerization/MAM is the way to resolve that tension, and that getting mobile management right early saves time and money. An early example of Bas spotting a shift in the EUC landscape and pushing readers to act ahead of it.

*Bron: blogpost 'BYOD... Beyond the hype' (2013-08-08), basvankaam.com.*
