# Don't buy a desktop management suite just to lock down a desktop

In this 2014 post Bas notes that on a Hosted Shared Desktop you often lock down the session with RES Workspace Manager or AppSense. His stance: 'these suites can do a lot more, and in my opinion should only be purchased if they are going to be used for a lot more than just locking down your desktop.' An early example of his recurring value-for-money lens: don't license a heavyweight product for a feature you could otherwise handle cheaply.
