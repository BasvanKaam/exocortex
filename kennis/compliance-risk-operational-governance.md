# Compliance and risk as operational governance

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

Compliance and risk are operational governance concerns. The external and internal requirements that guide decisions are laid out earlier, in the vision and strategy phase; compliance and risk is where they are operationalized.

A high level of automation is required to stay in control when transitioning to the cloud, and automation is an important operational governance tool. But automation has to be governed in turn: stay in control of your control by asking whether the automation works as expected, where it can improve, whether proper logging trails are being created, whether you comply to the standards and security levels expected of you, and whether you feel in control of risk.
