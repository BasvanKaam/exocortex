# Community code stays with its author

When a vendor runs a community scripting event, the right model is: the vendor does not become the owner, code stays with its developer, and everyone is credited at the top of every script. Entries are shared publicly on GitHub, free for everyone, and the vendor will not resell or reuse them beyond what is agreed. Plagiarism is the participant's responsibility, not the organizer's; fair play is expected and reusing your own code is fine. The goal is shared, freely available optimizations for the whole AVD/Azure community, not a content grab.
