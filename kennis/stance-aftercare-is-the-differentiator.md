# Stance: aftercare and knowledge transfer are the real differentiator

Bas calls out aftercare as the step that is "vaak vergeten of er wordt te weinig tijd en aandacht aan geschonken." His position is that delivery does not end at go-live: providers should keep supporting staff on the floor, deliver training and workshops, and involve the client's own people during design and implementation to avoid surprises afterward. Combined with personal attention and humor, he frames customer-listening and aftercare, not technology, as what differentiates a provider in a crowded IT market.
