# Bas's GitHub and the AI tooling he builds

In his Learning & Development role at Nerdio, Bas does not just write content, he builds the AI-powered systems and tooling that push the content creation, review and delivery pipeline forward. His public GitHub is where that work lives. This is the hands-on, builder side of his current role and a concrete instance of the "second brain as cognitive infrastructure" idea applied to L&D.

## GitHub
- Profile: [github.com/basvankaam](https://github.com/basvankaam), roughly 11 repositories, Pro account, mostly AI / Nerdio-enablement tooling (built 2025 to 2026).

## Tools he has built (public repos)
- **uniassistant**: a smart, AI-powered gateway to Nerdio University; helps learners find and navigate training content.
- **dash**: an "NME Plans & Features Explorer"; surfaces Nerdio Manager for Enterprise plans and the feature set (related to the 282-feature NME Explorer work).
- **NerdioLab**: lab/enablement tooling.
- **autoscaletrainer**: a trainer/simulator around Nerdio autoscaling (one of his core technical topics).
- **studio**: content/production tooling.

## Why it matters
This connects three threads in the brain: his shift from Field CTO to Principal Learning Architect (the L&D role), his use of AI to scale content production (the EUC News Nuggets "I write, Finn reads" model), and his long-running belief in building knowledge infrastructure. It is also the most concrete starting point if he wants to extend his own second brain into a working tool.
