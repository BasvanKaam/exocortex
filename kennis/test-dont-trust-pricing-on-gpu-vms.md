# Test GPU VMs, don't decide on price alone

Bas's stance: companies over-index on the sticker cost of GPU-enabled VMs and let that hold them back. A GPU benefits ordinary workloads (Office/M365, Windows, browsers), not just graphics-heavy ones, so it is worth evaluating. Decisions should be driven by actual testing rather than price comparisons, because the cloud makes testing cheap and fast. Combined with GPU partitioning and intelligent autoscaling, the economics change materially: he cites average savings up to 75% within days or weeks.
