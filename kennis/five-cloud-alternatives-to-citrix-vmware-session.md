# Session proposition: five (cloud) alternatives to Citrix and VMware

A ~30-minute talk format Bas runs: a fast-paced walkthrough of five relatively unknown (cloud) alternatives to the established ways of delivering (virtual) applications and desktops, i.e. Citrix Virtual Apps and Desktops (formerly XenApp/XenDesktop) and VMware Horizon.

Framing and intent:
- The goal is not to push Citrix and VMware aside. Both are used successfully and regularly, including by the host organisation. The goal is to pick the solution that fits best.
- The promise of the alternatives: help users in the same way (or better) while keeping the required investment and human effort to a minimum.
- Deep technical detail is deliberately out of scope during the session; questions and discussion come afterwards. Audience leaves with a clear picture of the alternatives plus deliberate "homework" and food for thought.

This is a recurring positioning Bas uses: there is no single right way to deliver apps and desktops, and the market over-focuses on the two big incumbents.
