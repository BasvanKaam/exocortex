# Enterprise Architecture (EA) and TOGAF

Enterprise Architecture (EA) is a way of working that helps organizations align their IT systems, processes and services. It can be seen as a blueprint showing how all parts of an organization (IT, processes, tools) reinforce each other to achieve business goals. The goal of EA is to make everything and everyone work together smoothly and to add new technologies to the portfolio in a smart and safe way.

A well-known EA tool is **TOGAF (The Open Group Architecture Framework)**, a collection of methods, techniques and guidelines that helps companies build their architecture step by step. TOGAF focuses on collaboration between departments and ensures all parts of an organization fit well together. Although it offers clear structure, the success of EA always depends on how well it is executed (i.e. followed) and how engaged the organization is.

With a well-set-up EA, companies can work more efficiently, save costs and respond better to change; EA makes it easier to integrate new Cloud technologies and services without rebuilding everything, increasing flexibility and future-proofing.
