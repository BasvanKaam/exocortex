# A candid critique of Citrix support response times

From a real field case (Oct 2014), Bas is openly critical of Citrix support: opening a case "took days, weeks even" to get somewhere, sometimes days for any reply, with an apparent time-zone gap and no follow-the-sun, despite Premier Support 24x7x365. His practical advice: enable CDF tracing, PortICA and VDA logging on all DDCs, StoreFront servers and VDIs before opening a case so you can upload logs immediately, which he reckons saves about a week. "They should make this mandatory."

He closes diplomatically: "I'm not here to bring them down or anything, I just like to keep them on their toes :-)" A characteristic move: honest, specific criticism delivered with warmth and without burning the relationship.
