# Cloud Service Ideas: Cost Optimization and Architecture Review

Bas's product ideas for broadening the Salomon/CGIT portfolio from the cloud angle:

- A service around optimization and cost saving, Azure-specific.
- Reviewing cloud architectures and/or designs: supplying commentary, feedback, improvement points and optimizations.
- A training partnership with AT Computing / Vijfhart opleidingen.

The through-line: turn his cloud expertise into repeatable, reviewable deliverables rather than open-ended consulting.
