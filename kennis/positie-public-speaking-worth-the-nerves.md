# Public speaking: terrified every time, but totally worth it

In his 2015 year-in-review (Dec 2015), Bas frames 2015 as 'the year of public speaking'. He started in 2013, finally 'got over himself' and took the leap, presenting 8 times publicly that year plus internal tech/pizza sessions. His honest stance: he still has lots to learn and is 'nervous as hell' every time he has to get up there, but in the end it's 'totally worth it'. This is a recurring authentic move, admitting the nerves rather than projecting effortless confidence, and a marker of the early arc of his speaking career.
