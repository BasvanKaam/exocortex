# The "I'm no guru" philosophy: blogging as a learning tool, not authority

On 1 January 2014, reflecting on his first year of blogging, Bas articulates the identity that underpins his whole writing voice: he positions himself explicitly as a learner-in-public, not an expert. "I'm no guru, I don't know everything and probably never will, and besides that I probably need to use Google as much as the next person, but who cares?!"

He started the blog to build a personal online knowledge base and share knowledge, and frames the payoff as educational and satisfying rather than authoritative. The first-year numbers he is proud of: 42 articles, 100,000+ words, 72,000+ views, 230 comments, 53 followers, in 12 months.

A second strand: he conquered a genuine fear of public speaking through training, going from avoidance to enjoying presenting (5 talks in six months). Worth noting as a personal-growth marker. His 2014 plan: stay on the Citrix portfolio but zoom into XenDesktop and XenMobile, and give Microsoft and VMware overdue attention.
