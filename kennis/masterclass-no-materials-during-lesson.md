# Withhold slides and hand-outs until the end of the training

In the Cloud Masterclass, Bas deliberately keeps the printed hand-out and any digital material out of attendees' hands until the session ends. His reasoning: giving people the material up front only distracts them and tempts them to page ahead instead of following the live narrative. Course material is published afterward via Avensus Academy, not during the lesson, and uploading slides via Google is rejected on presentation-quality grounds ("ziet er niet uit"). The stance: pacing and attention are facilitator-controlled, and self-paced access belongs after the class, not during it.
