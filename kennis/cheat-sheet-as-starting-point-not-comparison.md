# A cheat sheet should orient, not rank vendors

Bas is explicit (Dec 2018) that his cloud cheat sheet is deliberately NOT a feature-by-feature vendor comparison. The intent is to reduce overwhelm ('hundreds, if not thousands of different cloud services... where to start?') by giving a per-platform 'what to look for to get started' map. He treats the artifact as living and community-corrected: he openly invites feedback, admits 'it's near to impossible to get it all right in the first try', and notes services change continuously (updated after AWS re:Invent). This reflects his content philosophy: reduce noise to a usable one-pager, stay humble about completeness, and credit the community.

*Bron: blogpost 'Cloud Core Services Cheat Sheet. Compute, Networking, Storage, and Databases' (2018-12-04), basvankaam.com.*
