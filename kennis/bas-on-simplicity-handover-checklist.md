# Bas on handover: 'simple is better'

A recurring Bas principle, stated again for the handover (overdracht): "simpel is beter" (simple is better). Use simple overviews so you easily keep track of everything. He acknowledges there are various software packages available for this and for several other things discussed earlier in the book, but notes he has never personally needed them, preferring plain overviews and checklists.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> Ik heb het vaker gezegd, simpel is beter.
>
> ik heb ze nog niet nodig gehad
