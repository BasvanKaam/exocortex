# Working From Home: Routine and Productivity Practices

A practical set of habits for sustainable home working, where technology (a decent device plus the cheapest available cable/fiber internet) is treated as a minor detail and the harder part is behavioral.

**Routine and rhythm:**
- Set your alarm at a fixed time every day. Shower, eat breakfast, brush teeth, exercise/meditate, keep your normal morning ritual; get dressed as if going to the office.
- Take regular short breaks (the home equivalent of walking to the coffee machine), about 10 minutes away from your workspace.
- Get some daylight and go outside once or twice a day for a walk.
- Eat lunch at fixed times.
- Background music in moderation, lowered or off for calls.
- Ask others who already work from home for their tips; use social media for advice.
- Do some exercise, or tackle a long-postponed chore, with the extra time.

**Productivity:**
- Work from a closable room used only for work, so you do not associate the kitchen table or living room with work. Home is home, work is work.
- Write down what you want to achieve that day (a to-do list), preferably pen and paper or a whiteboard.
- Use your calendar (e.g. Outlook) as you normally would; plan, make appointments, take notes.
- Stay in touch with at least one or two colleagues, including non-work chat, which also keeps you 'in beeld' (visible).
- Make clear agreements with manager and clients; result-oriented working matters more than ever.
- Find a balance with partner/children/family about the fact that you are working.
- Use a clean-desk policy; keep only essentials within reach.
- Use noise-cancelling headphones if your surroundings are noisy (brand/type irrelevant, the function is not).
- Mute social media; close WhatsApp and possibly Outlook when doing focused work.
- Use your phone's do-not-disturb mode; after about half an hour, switch it off and respond to people.
- Apply the 5-minute rule: if you can and want to handle it within 5 minutes, do it now; otherwise schedule it immediately for later.

Note: most of these points apply equally if you work from a fixed office location.

Source: Bas van Kaam, Salomon IT blog, 2020-03-18.
