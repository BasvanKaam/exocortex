# State of the SBC/VDI market in 2018 (survey snapshot)

How the market looked then, from the 2018 Login VSI/Frame survey (755 respondents) as read by Bas:

- SBC was still big and even slightly more prevalent than 2017; new SBC deployments declined only ~0.8%. VDI use rose from 77% to 82%, but newly deployed VDI fell from 3% to 1.18%.
- Citrix remained the clear SBC platform/broker leader but declined ~6%; VMware roughly doubled to over 16% share. ~55% of respondents were considering changing their on-prem broker, leaning toward VMware.
- ~27% were still running workloads on Windows Server 2008 (R2), which XenApp/XenDesktop 7.18 (just released) had dropped support for (2012 R2 and 2016 only).
- For cloud SBC/VDI, Citrix Cloud interest (18%+) far outpaced VMware (3.23%). Amazon Workspaces interest fell from 7.17% to ~4%. Frame on AWS/Azure rose to 8.7%, Workspot to 8.71%.
- UEM was led by Citrix, VMware and Microsoft (76.2% combined), largely because UEM was already bundled in the license.
- ~75% of all applications were still traditional Windows applications. 66.59% used no application layering at all (up 7% from 2017); Citrix led layering at ~14%.
- Office 2016/O365 use was over 30% and rising; Office 2010 and 2013 still at 15.18% and 25.3%.

Key survey insight Bas highlighted: the biggest competitor to public-cloud DaaS/remote-app-as-a-service is not Citrix, Frame, Amazon, VMware or Microsoft, it's on-premises VDI/SBC combined with the trend toward more web/SaaS, mobile and PCs.
