# SCAF Adoption result: the end goal of the Cloud transition

In the SCAF Adoption phase all earlier efforts converge into an (as good as) fully operational platform ready for use. For Bas this marks the point where the organisation has not only finished the technical implementation but laid a solid long-term foundation. Success here is about more than technology: it requires integrating communication, governance and cost-awareness across the whole organisation.

Characteristic attention points of the result:
- **Fully operational platform** (Cloud or hybrid) with all Cloud-based services in active use.
- **Management and documentation**: environment officially taken into management or handed over, with fully updated documentation.
- **Communication model** set up, with a constant stream of internal communication via email, intranet portal or meetings.
- **Processes in order**: monitoring, release/change management, maintenance, portfolio/reporting, business continuity, incident and problem management.
- **Cost awareness**: all resources and costs made visible, stimulating a culture that avoids unnecessary spend.
- **Cloud governance**: a base laid for rules, guidelines and responsibilities, that keeps maturing as the organisation gains experience.
- **Lifecycle iterations** based on Plan-Do-Check-Act, possibly part of a broader IT roadmap.
- **Teams and responsibilities** clearly defined.
- **Addressing gaps**: previously identified gaps tackled, with a training plan so each role/team gets attention through the year.
- **Final report (eindrapportage)**: the closing piece and reference work that formally ends the project.
