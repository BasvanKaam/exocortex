# Teaching Stance: Cloud Adoption Is More Than Technology

The Masterclass description encodes several of Bas's teaching positions on how cloud adoption should be taught and approached.

- **Adopting the cloud goes far beyond technology alone.** He deliberately foregrounds the new business reality and the often-forgotten cultural shifts, not just IaaS/PaaS/SaaS mechanics.
- **Vendor-independent on principle.** The whole training, including his adoption framework, is taught fully cloud-independent; he looks at the largest providers and their architectures but does not anchor the method to any one of them.
- **An exit strategy is a necessary evil.** He frames a solid exit strategy as something you must plan for up front, treating provider lock-in as a real governance concern.
- **Learn from each other, hands-on.** Delivery is interactive by design: the whiteboard is out, participants actively take notes, discussion and disagreement are welcomed, and the training is loaded with hands-on tips, examples and use cases rather than slides alone.
- **The community is part of the value.** He explicitly introduces participants to the Cloud and EUC community and how to draw on it, professionally and personally, as part of the curriculum.
