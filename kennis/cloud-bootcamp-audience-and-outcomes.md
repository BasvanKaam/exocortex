# Cloud Bootcamp: target audience and learning outcomes

Target audience for the Salomon Cloud Bootcamp: the ambitious cloud (business) consultant, administrator, or IT Manager/CIO who wants to learn more about the potential impact of cloud on their organization and the added value of (hybrid) cloud technologies in the broadest sense. No prior knowledge required but a plus; the training mixes beginner and advanced material.

Declared outcomes a learner is familiar with after the training: basic and advanced cloud concepts; the new business reality and its often-unexpected cultural shifts; the changing role of the CIO/IT management; the influence end users gain through cloud services; how business and IT can/should grow closer (and who counts as 'the business'); the financial impact of cloud (business case, TCO, ROI); the technical concepts behind IaaS, PaaS, SaaS and DaaS; the cloud architectures of the three largest providers; current and upcoming trends; common objections and provocative statements; data in the cloud handled responsibly; why an exit strategy is a necessary evil and what to watch for; community-driven initiatives and their use.

Note the framing 'exit strategy is a necessary evil' and the recurring emphasis that adopting the cloud goes far beyond technology alone, including the often-forgotten cultural shifts.
