# 'Finally' one bill, but the 25-user minimum rules out small shops

Bas's take, May 2019. He's clearly relieved Citrix finally bundled compute and service into one bill: 'Finally :) This was missing before, one (specified) bill.' But he flags the gatekeeping: the 25-license minimum 'will automatically rule-out smaller shops. I guess there's always WVD to turn to.' He speculates about the architecture's maturity, noting there's 'no word on the Cloudification of all this' (no serverless / Azure Functions replacing the role VMs) and wonders aloud whether that immaturity is 'why the minimum of 25 licenses, perhaps?'. The post also captures him doing live community journalism, quoting a Citrix PM's tweet that partners can aggregate smaller customers below the 25-user floor.
