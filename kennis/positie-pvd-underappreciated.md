# Positie: Personal vDisk is great and underappreciated

In Sept 2013 Bas argued Personal vDisk was an underused, underappreciated technology that more shops should adopt. Across pooled and dedicated, given the storage and image-update trade-offs, his personal default was: 'I would personally prefer Pooled desktops for overall use and only offer Dedicated desktops when really needed' - and PvD as the best-of-both middle ground.

His stance on PvD: 'Personal vDisks are great and it's a shame that they're not implemented more often, an underappreciated product to say the least.' He pushed back on the reputation that it was hard to manage: 'complex to manage? Come on, that can't be (really) true right?!' His pitch was business-led: better VDI scalability, lower storage cost, no extra training or tools, lower cost per user over time. Worth noting in the comments he graciously accepted correction from a reader (Rob) on the profile-management pros/cons, conceding 'my bad :-)' - characteristic of how he handles being corrected in public.
