# Microsoft is suddenly winning everything (2017 observation)

Throughout this 2017 survey commentary Bas repeatedly noted Microsoft's momentum: the new CSP licensing for Windows 10 with virtualization rights (announced at Inspire) made DaaS easier, Citrix's renewed collaboration with Microsoft drove Citrix to the top of preferred DaaS tech, and on EMM 'Microsoft is winning terrain on these grounds, quickly.' His summary line: 'it just seems that everything Microsoft introduces or launches these days turns out to be successful.' An early read on the Microsoft resurgence that would reshape EUC. In replies he also showed a critical edge on aggressive vendor marketing: 'Workspot is doing ok, don't like their aggressive marketing strategy, though.'
