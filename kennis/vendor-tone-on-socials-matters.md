# How a vendor behaves on social channels is a signal

In evaluating Workspot, Bas notes critically that they come across as rather direct and hostile on the socials ('Ze komen nogal direct en vijandig over via de socials'). He treats vendor tone and conduct as part of the evaluation, not just the feature matrix, and contrasts it with Parallels, whom he praises for being very transparent with lots of public information.
