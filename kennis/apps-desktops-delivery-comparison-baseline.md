# Comparison baseline: assume the VDI choice was already sound

Framing device Bas uses across his 'alternatives for delivering apps and desktops' series so he can compare vendors apples-to-apples. He deliberately parks the meta-debate of whether you should even offer a virtual, often-shared desktop or published applications at all, assuming the choice was correct and well-reasoned in roughly 70% of cases ('pak 'm beet 70%'), so the series can focus on comparing the delivery options themselves. He also notes the market context: the Netherlands is a strongly Citrix-oriented country, but VMware and even Microsoft (RDS-only) play a role. Shared starting assumptions are set once in part 1 so the later parts don't repeat them.
