# The Azure calculator is fine, but MSPs need a real one

In this June 2020 post Bas frames the Azure pricing calculator as good enough for rough small-environment estimates and for surfacing the lesser-known benefits of license types, but explicitly inadequate for MSP/CSP precision. He closes by pointing to a dedicated, "award winning" per-user/per-month/per-desktop calculator that accounts for every discount and parameter. His stance: know your licenses, use every benefit (AHU + RI) to your advantage, but reach for purpose-built tooling when accuracy matters.
