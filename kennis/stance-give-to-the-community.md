# The community's success depends on us; give before you take

June 2015. This post is a clear statement of Bas's community ethos, riffing on JFK: "Don't ask what CUGC can do for you, but what you can do for CUGC!" He frames the CUGC's success as collectively owned: "in the end the success of all this will depend on us, the community." He celebrates the platform precisely because Citrix provides the rails but the community supplies the content.

Beyond CUGC he actively promotes peers' initiatives rather than just his own: the Frontline Chatter EUC podcast by Andrew Morgan and Jarian Gibson ("already it is a big hit! And with good reason"), and Eric (xenappblog.com) and his Virtual Expo. This generosity, amplifying other community voices, is a defining trait of his early brand. His enthusiasm for the EUC community as a place that is both serious and fun foreshadows his own later podcast/creator work.
