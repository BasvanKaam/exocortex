# SCAF Insight: Workshop as Starting Point

Bas's experience-based advice for the SCAF Insight phase: just like in the strategy phase, **start with a workshop** to surface the bulk of the needed information as quickly as possible. Information can be gathered via supplied (customer) documentation, inspecting systems (with or without specialist software), interviews and/or workshops; in most cases it's a combination of all of these. All gathered information is documented in the corresponding SCAF template form.

Useful opening workshop questions:
- How do we best arrive together at a full inventory of all applications in use?
- Same question for all physical and virtual systems, network and security components.
- Which overviews currently exist (applications, licenses, datasets, security measures/policy, governance, processes) and where can we find/share them?
- Can we get access to all systems, possibly under supervision?
- Is inventory/monitoring software used; any objection to deploying such software?
- Besides monitoring/inventory tooling, what other software is used for management, process guidance, etc.?
- What is the current knowledge level around Cloud services/products/technologies, and how do we safeguard it?
- Which teams are active and for what purpose (not limited to technical)?
