# SCAF Insight Phase: Stakeholders to Involve

In the SCAF Insight phase, although there is overlap with the strategy phase, additional stakeholders join, specifically technical experts and responsible parties. Bas lists:
- **Database administrator (DBA)** for insight into data management.
- **Network engineer** for network requirements.
- **System and application administrators** for knowledge of daily-used systems and applications.
- **IT manager** for coordination, decision-making and strategic direction.
- **Senior system architect** for overview and knowledge of the IT architecture.
- **CISO** for security and compliance adherence.
- **External parties** (IT partner or software vendor) for insight into Cloud compatibility, licenses and configurations.

Involving this broader group lets you draw up a plan that is both technically feasible and strategically fitting.
