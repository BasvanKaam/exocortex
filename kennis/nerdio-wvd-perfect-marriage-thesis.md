# Thesis: Nerdio on top of WVD is a 'perfect marriage'

June 2020. Bas's core positioning thesis on joining Nerdio: Microsoft owns and keeps improving the WVD core (he cites the 2020 spring edition and MSIX AppAttach progress as evidence Microsoft is 'doing an excellent job'), and Nerdio innovates on top of that core rather than competing with it. He calls it 'a perfect marriage' where both companies work closely together. The value prop he stresses: hiding complexity so an admin or MSP needs no specific Azure, PowerShell or WVD knowledge and is 'good to go within hours instead of day's weeks.' This 'innovate on top of Microsoft, hide the complexity' framing becomes the spine of his Nerdio messaging for years.
