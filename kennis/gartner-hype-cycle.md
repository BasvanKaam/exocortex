# The Gartner Hype Cycle and its five stages

A hype cycle is a graphical model describing the stages new technologies typically pass through from introduction to full maturity and productivity, made famous by research firm Gartner. The stages:

1. **Technology Trigger** — first attention; prototypes and pilots; media interest begins.
2. **Peak of Inflated Expectations** — hype grows, often overly optimistic predictions of impact.
3. **Trough of Disillusionment** — shortcomings become clear, hype fades, early investors may withdraw.
4. **Slope of Enlightenment** — a more realistic picture; persistent firms build practical applications; tech stabilises.
5. **Plateau of Productivity** — broadly accepted and deployed, proven value, mainstream.

For cloud, the model helps firms judge which phase a solution is in (e.g. AIOps, serverless, edge computing) and adopt at the right moment to capture value without being swept up by hype. Bas's insight: hype/innovation does not always equal real value, and some technologies only become effective over time.
