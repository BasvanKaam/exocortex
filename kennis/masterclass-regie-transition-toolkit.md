# Masterclass: toolkit for working out regie processes

For the transition to a regie (orchestration) organization, the masterclass hands learners three concrete instruments:

- **Nine-grid model (negenvlaksmodel)**: capture existing processes, roles, functions and tasks.
- **RACI table**: record responsibilities by attaching roles to process steps.
- **Workshops**: work out the regie processes collaboratively.

These are the hands-on tools the training prescribes for the regie design exercise.
