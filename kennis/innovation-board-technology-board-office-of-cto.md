# Where to 'park' innovation: Innovation/Technology Board, Solutions Board, Office of the CTO

Bas's answer to balancing innovation with stability is to give innovation a dedicated home so the rest of the organisation stays focused on core tasks. He recommends structurally scheduling time to research new tech via an **Innovation Board or Technology Board** — usually a relatively small team that assesses and selectively implements new technologies in a planned, deliberate way.

From personal experience (he has been member, initiator and leader of several such groups) this works excellently. His model in practice split duties: a **Technology Board** meeting explored new developments and trends and how the organisation could turn them into a service or product for customers, while a **Solutions Board** was where those initiatives and plans were presented for a go/no-go decision, budget and time.

An alternative/complement is an **Office of the CTO** — a team supporting the CTO in developing the technological vision and strategy, exploring future trends and innovation while keeping a clear focus on business value and relevance. There is significant overlap between these initiatives. Bas notes that while writing the book he was himself part of such a team, a valuable addition to his personal development and his company's.
