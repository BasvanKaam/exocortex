# CCoE left brain / right brain split across the Cloud Service Lifecycle

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

the framework divides the CCoE into two parts, a left and right side of the brain, mapped onto the Cloud Service Lifecycle (Plan, Build, Run, Retire; source: opengroup.org).

**Left side: Plan and Build.** Supports the Plan and Build phases. The actual transition to cloud is a complex project requiring specific cloud knowledge; exchanging and applying that knowledge is the left brain's main task. They must 'get up on a pulpit and propagate the gospel of cloud' through training and pizza sessions, Knowledge Exchange Fairs, hackathons and the like. The left side maps to the Business / Demand side of the IT value chain: service design, information management, integration solutions, cloud architecture.

**Right side: Run and Retire.** Supports the Run and Retire phases. Here the CCoE redesigns existing IT(IL) service delivery processes for cloud using policy-based automation, blueprints and application scripts for configurations, and supports broader business processes through cloud data analysis surfaced in dashboards and reports. The right side maps to suppliers of services (internal/external), the Delivery side of the IT value chain, where managing external parties and services is an important value-add.
