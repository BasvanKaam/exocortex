# Bas's 2020 WFH / streaming kit-list

In December 2020 Bas documented his upgraded work-from-home setup, oriented toward presenting, webinars and live content creation. Core elements:

- IKEA IDASEN electric sit/stand desk; Secretlab TITAN 2020 chair; Carl Friedrik desk mat.
- Compute: Intel NUC (32 GB RAM, 10th-gen i7 2.5 GHz, Samsung 970 Evo NVMe SSD).
- Displays: Samsung C34H890 34" FHD curved (3440x1440) plus a Samsung 24" FHD secondary.
- Live production: Elgato Stream Deck, Elgato Key Light (x2), Blackmagic Design ATEM Mini HDMI live switcher, Panasonic HC-VX1 4K camera used as a webcam, RODE NT-USB mic on a RODE PSA1 arm.
- Audio: Jabra Evolve2 85 headset, Sonos One SL, Bose Companion 2 speakers.
- Input: Microsoft Surface Precision Mouse, ROCCAT Vulcan 121 Aimo mechanical keyboard (brown switches).

The gear list is dated, but it documents the rig behind his presentations and podcast work in this era. Note the dual-monitor + widescreen setup here is exactly the problem solved in his Stream Deck resolution-switching post three days later.
