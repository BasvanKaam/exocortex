# De Cloud Professionals vision: focus and hybrid as the main act

The stated vision: cloud usage will keep growing strongly over the next five to ten years, and hybrid solutions will play the leading role. As the number of cloud services keeps increasing, it becomes hard to keep overview, let alone hold the right in-house knowledge.

The brand's answer is deliberate focus: focus on a handful of crucial, workspace-related cloud services; keep tracking market developments and certifications up to date while letting practical experience do the talking. Above all, listen to the customer ('where is the need') so they get a bit better with every engagement, a concept they claim to have applied successfully for years. Combined with on-prem expertise, personal attention, and a touch of humor, this is what they say sets them apart in the IT market.
