# NIST and its definition of Cloud computing

NIST (National Institute of Standards and Technology) is a US government agency founded in 1901 that develops technological standards and guidelines. It is internationally known for its influence on technologies including Cloud computing, and was one of the first organisations to develop a general definition of Cloud computing (now more than 15 years ago, published 2011). This definition is widely accepted and provides a structured way to think about the Cloud, used worldwide as a reference model for Cloud implementations to help organisations make the right choices for their needs.

Bas notes that the fundamental Cloud principles he describes are not his own invention - they come from NIST. He also remarks that many of the people he speaks with throughout the year have never heard of NIST.
