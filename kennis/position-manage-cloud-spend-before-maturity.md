# Position: Manage Cloud Spend From Day One, Not After Maturity

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

The framework holds that you should not wait for cloud maturity before managing cloud spend. Even an immature organization must manage its budgets and spend expectations, so a periodic showback giving real-time expense insight should be arranged early.

The underlying stance: data is the enabler and trigger for sound financial management, so reporting deserves its own governance spotlight rather than being treated as a sub-task of cost optimization. Tagging discipline is the precondition that makes meaningful breakdowns possible; without it, reporting cannot do its job as a trigger for the surrounding financial processes.
