# Awingu as an HTML5 workspace aggregator

From the Jan 2021 EUCdigest episode with Arnoud Marliere: Awingu is described as an intuitive, secured, HTML5-based workspace aggregator providing easy and secure access to applications and data. Framed as a remote-work enabler ("key in order to successfully work from home, especially these days"). Dated product positioning, but a marker of the browser-delivered remote-access category that was prominent in the COVID-era EUC space.
