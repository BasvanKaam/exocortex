# Crohn's diagnosis and learning to live with it

In 2016 Bas was diagnosed with Crohn's disease, a chronic autoimmune inflammatory bowel disease that is treatable but non-healable. He started medication a few weeks before writing (early Jan 2017) and notes it will probably hurt his athletic ability, since it may slow red blood cell production. His framing is matter-of-fact and accepting: 'I guess I'll just have to learn to live with it.' Context for understanding his health backdrop from this point onward; he had just run a 41:32 10K (near his 41:22 personal best) at almost 38.
