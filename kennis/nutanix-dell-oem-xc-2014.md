# Nutanix-Dell OEM agreement and the Dell XC appliance line (2014)

How it was announced then (June 2014): Nutanix signed an OEM agreement with Dell to offer a family of converged infrastructure appliances based on Nutanix web-scale software. The result was the Dell XC series, Dell PowerEdge servers with Nutanix software installed on top, available in multiple price/performance variants, targeting VDI, big data and virtualized applications. Shipping in Q4 2014 via Dell sales teams and channel partners.

A notable durable detail of the Nutanix platform mentioned here: the software was compatible with all major hypervisors (VMware, Hyper-V, KVM) and not limited to a single hypervisor within the same environment, letting you mix and match. The specific deal/product is dated, but it's a marker of how hyperconverged software reached mainstream channels via OEM hardware partnerships.
