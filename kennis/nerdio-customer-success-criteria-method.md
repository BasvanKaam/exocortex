# Nerdio customer success criteria method

Bas's method for kicking off a Nerdio engagement (works without Nerdio too). Before (or at) installation of Nerdio Manager for Enterprise, run a meeting to define and document what the customer wants to achieve. Going through the exercise with the right people (those who will actually do the work and have time reserved) surfaces 'critical needs' and 'want-to-haves' the customer hadn't thought of.

This sets a common, documented goal for the 30-day free trial, supported by recurring alignment meetings, architectural reviews and health checks. At the end of the window you review outcomes against the criteria and decide next steps. Establishing how success is measured up front is what makes the trial productive rather than aimless.
