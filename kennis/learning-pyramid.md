# The Learning Pyramid (National Training Laboratories)

The Learning Pyramid, developed by the National Training Laboratories, shows how effective different learning methods are and how much we retain on average. The passive methods sit at the top: reading (10%) and listening (20%). Adding visual elements raises retention: seeing (30%) and seeing-and-hearing combined, e.g. video and demonstrations (50%).

The real power is in the bottom, active layers. By doing it yourself you retain up to 70%, because actively practising, trying things and making mistakes builds understanding, including learning what does not work. The single most effective method is at the very bottom: teaching others (90% retention), because explaining forces you to fully understand, structure and simplify the material, exposing your own gaps.
