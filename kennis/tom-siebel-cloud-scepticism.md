# Tom Siebel's scepticism that the Cloud survived

In the early 2000s, around the dotcom-bubble collapse, there was great scepticism about the viability of Cloud-based businesses. Tom Siebel, founder of Siebel Systems, captured it in an interview: "There's no way that company exists in a year." He was referring to companies dependent on emerging internet technologies. Many indeed went bankrupt, but Microsoft, Amazon and Google survived and grew into Cloud computing powerhouses - their ability to innovate and adapt their infrastructure to a fast-changing digital world was decisive.
