# On the Nutanix NTC program and a community that keeps the company honest

May 2015. Bas's first formal recognition ("the first time my efforts got recognized in such a way") was becoming a Nutanix Technology Champion, comparable to VMware vExpert or Microsoft MVP. His account is warmly positive: the program is "outstanding... top notch," communication is timely and structured, and Nutanix went so far as to run a private one-to-one session (three employees to his one) to keep him in the loop when timezones clashed.

What stands out as his values: he prizes being around people who out-skill him ("it's hard to keep up with some of the guys in there, seriously") because it keeps him learning, and he respects a community that is "being critical when needed, keeping the company honest." He explicitly steps around NDA material and signals it ("you didn't hear it from me"), and he sidelines vendor FUD battles. His relationship with Nutanix traces back to a late-2013/early-2014 blogger session before the Dutch Hoofddorp HQ opening, where he heard the vision from CEO Dheeraj Pandey directly. Note: a personal/community reflection, not technical content.
