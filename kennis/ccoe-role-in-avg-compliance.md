# The CCoE's role in AVG compliance and governance

Bas describes how a Cloud Center of Excellence (CCoE) — a team focused on standardizing, optimizing and promoting best practices for cloud use — can take a leading role in implementing and enforcing security measures and AVG compliance. (See also chapter 5 for the team's composition.)

The CCoE helps with AVG compliance by:
- **Developing policy and standards:** security standards and procedures meeting AVG requirements (encryption, access management, backup policy) so cloud environments are securely set up.
- **Oversight and compliance:** monitoring adherence to security standards, periodic checks like log review and patch updates, to safeguard integrity and spot problems early.
- **Creating awareness and training:** trainings and workshops so employees understand the risks and responsibilities of data protection in the cloud.
- **Automation and monitoring:** automated tools and monitoring to proactively identify security issues; automating log review and access management reduces human error.
- **Risk and impact assessments:** supporting DPIAs for cloud solutions to identify potential risks and take mitigating measures.

Bas sums it up: combining technical and organizational measures in the CCoE's way of working keeps personal data protected and the organization compliant. In other words — governance.
