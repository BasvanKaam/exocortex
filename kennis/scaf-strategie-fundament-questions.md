# SCAF Strategie: The Foundation Questions

The Strategie phase of SCAF starts by building a clear picture of the organisation through targeted questions in workshops and interviews. Bas notes the modern reflex "We moeten naar de Cloud" is often shouted without a real plan or understanding of the consequences — so the choice for cloud must be deliberate, and this is the moment to examine it critically.

As the foundation, you seek well-substantiated answers to questions such as:
- What are the primary drivers for choosing cloud? (Be as specific as possible.)
- What is the current relationship between IT and the business — do they collaborate well, or is there a 'gap'?
- Is anything currently standing in the way of the desired results?
- What is the desired result / business objectives? When is a cloud implementation successful — are there KPIs?
- Any specific wishes/requirements to account for?
- Are projects running whose failure or success would significantly impact the organisation?
- Is there an IT (and possibly separate) security policy? An IT strategy / IT roadmap? A business vision/mission?
- Does innovation play a prominent role? Do you use agile/DevOps-oriented platforms?
- Who are the primary (strategic) partners?
- Can you briefly describe the company culture (norms, values, informal/formal, hierarchical, regieorganisatie)?

Not all questions need answering, and that is fine — together you reach as detailed a description as possible to align the next steps. Findings are then classified and prioritised, distinguishing need (noodzaak) from nice-to-have (nut), and specific knowledge is matched to objectives.
