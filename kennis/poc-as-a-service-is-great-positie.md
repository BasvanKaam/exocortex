# PoC-as-a-Service is genuinely valuable

In April 2019 Bas endorsed Liquidware's fully configured, tested, isolated hosted PoC environment, and grounded the endorsement in 18 months of personally setting up Liquidware PoCs (at the time part of his day job). His argument is the efficiency one: a pre-built, available-today site lets serious evaluators bypass the environment-prep headaches and reuse the configurations and learnings in their own on-site PoC. His closing tell is wry and personal: 'If only they could have come up with all this about 18 months earlier :-)'. The take is positive but earned through hands-on experience, not pure promotion.
