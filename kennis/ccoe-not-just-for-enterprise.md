# A CCoE Is Not Just for Large Enterprises

Bas pushes back hard on a common misconception: that a Cloud Center of Excellence (CCoE) sounds big and is only applicable to large (enterprise) environments. His verdict is blunt: this is WRONG ("ONJUIST"). Whether you're a large multinational or a smaller organisation, a CCoE can play a key role in managing and implementing cloud solutions. His memorable framing: "Je bent nooit te klein voor een CCoE" (you're never too small for a CCoE).

His practical advice: pick a name that fits, the number of people matters little. What matters are the responsibilities and actually applying them. The participants' roles can vary widely depending on the size and impact of the project.

In the SCAF model, setting up a CCoE is an optional part of the strategy phase. The CCoE is a multidisciplinary team that functions as the engine ("de motor") behind the cloud project.

Why the roles matter (Bas's four reasons):
- Collaboration of IT with the business: technical AND business stakeholders ensure the strategy is both technically feasible and aligned with business goals.
- Solid governance: clear guidelines, policy, compliance, cost-efficiency.
- Expertise and knowledge sharing: the CCoE acts as a knowledge hub ("spil") where cloud expertise is consolidated and shared.
- Managing complexity: keeping project management, security, governance and technical aspects seamlessly aligned.
