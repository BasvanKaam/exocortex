# Full Cloud Migration Is Rarely a Good Idea

A core, long-held position of Bas's: moving everything to the cloud is seldom the right choice. He has said this for years and still fully stands behind it (as of 2018).

His reasoning is not anti-cloud and not about provider outages (he grants that no environment is 100% fault-proof, and the big providers like Azure, AWS, and others all had major disruptions in 2017/2018). The point is fit. The average application, and its associated data, is not suitable to be served from a cloud environment, and he expected that to hold for the next 5 to 10 years from 2018. By his own field estimate, in roughly 40-50% of the client cases he saw in a year (excluding obvious SaaS like Office 365), a traditional on-premises or hybrid approach was the better choice.

He frames the cloud as a tool in a toolbox: it offers many advantages and possibilities, no doubt, but you assess per case which solution, or combination of solutions, fits best. Don't overdo it. He offers his own list of realistic limitations, dependencies, pros and cons, in deliberate contrast to the internet's marketing 'reasons to go cloud' lists.
