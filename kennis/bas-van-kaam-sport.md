# Bas van Kaam: sport achievements

The competitive, physical side of Bas, useful context for the human-behind-the-professional. He competes seriously in cue sports and is a serious endurance athlete, and he does all of this while living with Crohn's disease.

## Snooker
- Personal records: highest break 137, highest tournament break 123, 68 century breaks, highest line-up 147 (twice).
- Dutch Team Competition: 1st place in Super League (x3), First Division (x2), and Second Division.
- Dutch Championships (team): Super League 1st (x3), First Division 1st, Second Division 1st.
- Dutch Championships A Ranking: 2nd place (individual).
- Dutch Championships highest break winner (115); Dutch Championships Juniors 5th place (x2).
- The Hague Open (KNBB): 3rd place. Scotch Doubles: 3rd and 8th place across two editions.
- Top 10 Dutch snooker ranking (x4). Summer Cup Snooker League: 1st. 60+ house tournaments won across NL (snooker and pool combined). 10+ open tournament wins.

## Pool
- Dutch Team Competition: 1st in First Division and 1st in Third Division, winning all matches throughout the season in both.
- Dutch Championships First Division Team: 5th. B-Ranking tournament: 1st (x2). Qualification Tournament Dutch Open 9-Ball: 1st. 15 house tournament wins across NL.

## Cycling
- Alpe d'Huez: approximately 40 ascents. Completed an organized time trial up the Alpe within the hour (59 minutes and change).
- Alpe d'HuZes charity event: three editions, 3 consecutive climbs per edition (about 1:10 to 1:15 per climb).
- Longest solo ride: 205 km, 2 km elevation, average 27.8 km/h, non-stop. Most active year: roughly 13,000 km cycling plus 1,000 km running.

## Running
- Burpee-Mile (Death by Burpees, broad jump) completed three times, fastest 1 hour 48 minutes total. Personal bests on Strava. Running alongside cycling and swimming.

## Health context
- He stays this active despite living with Crohn's disease.
