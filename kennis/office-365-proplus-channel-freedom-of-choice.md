# ProPlus channel freedom of choice (ring deployment)

A durable deployment pattern Bas advocates: not all users need to sit on the same update channel. Layer the population in rings:
- The bulk of staff on the Semi-Annual Channel (the default), receiving features every six months.
- A smaller group of compatibility testers on Semi-Annual (Targeted) to vet upcoming Semi-Annual features early.
- An even smaller pilot group on Monthly to test new features the moment they land.

This lets organizations surface teething problems in a limited group before a production rollout. The ring concept outlives the specific channel names.
