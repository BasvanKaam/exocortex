# Reverse Timeline planning around a compelling event

Bas presents the reverse timeline as a planning strategy for cloud/IT projects: you start from a fixed end date or a 'compelling event' (a deadline, launch or mandatory transition date) and work backwards to the start, accounting for the lead time of each component so the project is ready on time.

His observation from practice: license renewal dates running one-to-three years out are nowadays very popular as compelling events. The benefit is that planning in reverse surfaces priorities and bottlenecks early - you see where deadlines are tight and where you need extra capacity or alternative solutions. Because cloud migrations can take quite a bit of time (though not always, he notes), this strategy fits well.

In his XYZ-Care assignment the reverse timeline starts from the 'Go-Live' date and works back through acceptance tests, data validation, training moments and communication events.
