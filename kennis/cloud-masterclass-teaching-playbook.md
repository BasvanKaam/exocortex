# Cloud Masterclass teaching playbook (Bas's delivery method)

Bas's own notes on how he delivers the Cloud Masterclass live, distinct from the course content. The method:

- Open by connecting with the audience: introduce yourself, state what you will do and how, and clarify what participants expect from the training.
- Have the audience introduce themselves when group size allows.
- Set intent and objectives up front; briefly walk the planning.
- Make the ground rules explicit early: when to ask questions, and what happens with questions that cannot be answered on the spot (park them).
- Create recognition: use examples from your own life and practice, and ask participants for similar situations or general recognition.
- Summarize and emphasize pros and cons of each topic.
- Work out a business case together with the group as a shared exercise.
- Frame mistakes as learning: "the more mistakes you make, the more you learn."

This is the facilitation layer that wraps the SCAF/book content during a live masterclass.
