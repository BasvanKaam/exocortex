# WVD will only get easier and cheaper, lowering the barrier

Bas's framing, July 2019. He started with WVD purely out of curiosity, to learn how much work it is and how it performs, and his prediction is that 'things will only get easier (read: automated) from here on, lowering the barrier for even more companies and IT enthusiasts to give it a spin.' He's also pointedly relatable about cost: he pays for his lab out of his own pocket and writes the post specifically because 'many of us have to pay these types of deployments from our own pocket, as do I.' He notes the privileged exits (MVPs get free Azure credits, MSDN or employer-funded business directories), implicitly contrasting his own self-funded tinkering. Captures his hands-on, learn-by-doing, community-first ethos.
