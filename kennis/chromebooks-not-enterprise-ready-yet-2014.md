# Chromebooks valuable but not enterprise-ready (2014)

In February 2014 Bas was genuinely bullish on Chromebooks for the right use cases but judged them "far from enterprise ready" at that moment, and explicitly not because of their lack of local power or offline use (he framed that as part of their strength). The blockers were Citrix-side gaps: no ICA encryption support, HTML5 Receiver often needing a NetScaler, printing broken or awkward, no HD graphics, multimedia issues, lack of committed Citrix roadmap, and uneven global device availability. His prediction: once those issues get addressed (and he was sure they would be), enterprise-oriented Chromebook deployments would grow and prove a valuable asset. "Let's give them some time shall we?" His private reply to a reader added: maybe not at huge scale, but for tens to hundreds of users it could be a fine, innovative solution.
