# Project VRC: independent VDI/SBC research and the State of the Union survey

Project Virtual Reality Check (VRC) was an independent R&D project started in early 2009 by Ruben Spruijt and Jeroen van de Kamp, focused on desktop and application virtualization research. It published white papers with Login VSI test results on hypervisors, Office versions, app-virtualization solutions, server-hosted Windows OSes and antivirus impact. The annual 'State of the VDI and SBC union' community survey (2013/2014 editions drew over 1300 participants) gathered functional and technical data (design goals, storage, VM config). Notable methodology choice: they deliberately avoided iPad/prize giveaways to keep response quality high; instead only participants received the exclusive results report. Historical record of a respected independent industry data source from that era.
