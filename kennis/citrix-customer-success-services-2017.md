# Citrix support model shift to Customer Success Services

How Citrix support and licensing worked, and how it changed at the start of 2017:

Before: to use Citrix support you needed an active Software Maintenance (SM) contract. SM is not the same as Subscription Advantage (SA), which entitles you to install and use the latest released software (XenApp, XenDesktop, StoreFront, etc.). SM included SA as well, and was the more expensive of the two. New product purchases automatically included one year of SA, renewable but not mandatory. Support was tied to a product's Mainstream and Extended Maintenance phases; at End of Life, security hotfixes, support and downloads stopped.

The change (Customer Success Services, launching Jan 1 2017): a new annual subscription named 'Select' replaces both SM and SA, including all SM/SA services plus extras. Two further packages, Priority and Priority Plus, were slated for mid-2017 (no detail yet at time of writing). Legacy contracts (Preferred, Enterprise Support) became non-renewable as of Jan 1 2017; SA's end-of-renewal date was set to July 1 2017. Buying new Citrix software now required also buying a one-year Select subscription, making support mandatory for at least the first year, a shift from the old SA-minimum approach.

Select's listed additions over SA/SM: initial response time cut from 1 hour to 30 minutes; more proactive support; step-by-step / guided installation support; proactive care via on-prem and cloud analytics (e.g. Call Home); unlimited e-learning access; live chat; a Premium Knowledge Center with CTX articles not on the open web; and cloud-based services to accelerate version migrations.
