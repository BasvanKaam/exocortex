# Designing a Community Advocate Program (NVP)

The Nerdio Valued Professional (NVP) program is a community-advocate recognition program modeled on the Microsoft MVP / VMware vExpert pattern, applied to a vendor's own ecosystem. Reusable design thinking for building one:

## Purpose
Recognize and give back to community-minded individuals who voluntarily support the company, its products, and what it stands for. Forms of support that count: social posts, podcasts, webinars, sending leads/deals, and generally spreading positive word of mouth. The point is appreciation, not a transaction.

## Framing the ask
- Lead with appreciation, not obligation: "We don't need anything in return except your continued support and enthusiasm."
- Name why you picked them specifically ("we immediately thought of you").
- Keep the brand simple and acronym-friendly (NVP).

## Launch mechanics
- Start small and curated: launch with a handful of founding members (three) rather than opening the floodgates.
- Introduce members publicly via a blog post where each writes a short self-intro (who they are, their company, day-to-day, why they value the program). This doubles as social proof and seeds the program's reputation.
- Pair recognition with a tangible perk (an invite to the annual conference, all expenses paid) to make membership feel real and aspirational.

This is Bas's own program design at Nerdio; the pattern transfers to any vendor wanting to formalize community advocacy.
