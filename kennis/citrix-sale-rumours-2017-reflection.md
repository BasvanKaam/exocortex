# Framing: reflecting on Citrix's history amid 2017 sale rumours

The occasion for this March 2017 history post was the rumour that Citrix was working with Goldman Sachs on a potential sale process. Rather than speculate on the rumour itself, Bas used it as a prompt to step back and reflect on how Citrix became what it was, ending open-endedly: 'Let's wait and see how this time-line continues to develop going forward.' It captures his instinct, when the industry gets jittery, to reach for history and context instead of hot takes, and his affection for the company and its people (he notes the broad love for Mark Templeton, while regretting he never met him personally).
