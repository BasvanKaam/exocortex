# Genuinely enthusiastic about Citrix mobility, MDX as the standout

In this 2013 piece Bas is openly a Citrix enthusiast and says so upfront, pre-empting the obvious objection: he's not an employee, owns no stock, gains nothing, he is 'just a fan.' That disclaimer is itself a stance: he wants the reader to trust that the enthusiasm is earned, not paid for.

His clearest personal call is on MDX, which he names 'My personal favorite!' among all the CloudGateway components. His framing of MDX is end-user-freedom-first: the point of containerizing business apps in the Vault is to manage only the business apps 'instead of the whole device providing the end users with the freedom they want.' Even this early, his lens is the end-user experience and BYOD freedom, not just IT control, the EUC sensibility that runs through his later work.

He also flags the practical catch others might gloss over: most of the slick security features (MDX, Micro VPN, app policies) only apply on iOS or Android, with HTML5 as the exception, so the BYOD story has device-shaped limits.

*Bron: blogpost 'The Citrix CloudGateway' (2013-02-03), basvankaam.com.*
