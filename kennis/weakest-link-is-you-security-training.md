# The Weakest Link Is You (Security Training War Story)

Bas opens 'De Zwakste Schakel' bluntly: "Dat ben jij!" (That's you!). Over the past ten years he has been on projects where he was required to first complete (and pass) a training before receiving a username and password, i.e. access to the network and the most important systems and data. No exceptions, regardless of background, technical knowledge or years of experience.

These trainings were videos, text, interim questions and a closing exam; fail and you start over, easily taking several hours. He had mixed feelings about it but says the idea behind it is sound. His one critique: they were too generic and missed focus on the specific role you hold in the organisation, which he thinks could be better. He still does at least one such training nearly every year, sometimes several.

His warning: experienced Cloud/IT people too easily think 'pfff, this is obvious, everyone knows this' but that is not true, and you should never make that mistake. He stresses it will surprise you how many colleagues do not know what seems self-evident to you; there is always a win to be had.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)
