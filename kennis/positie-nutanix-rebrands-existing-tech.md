# Nutanix's 'new' platform names are partly rebrands of tech they already had

Stance (September 2015): Bas is bullish on Nutanix's engineering ('innovation is not new to Nutanix') and clearly sees hyperconverged becoming the de facto standard, but he calls out the marketing layer pointedly. He notes the Distributed Storage Fabric is 'basically a rebrand of technology they already had and offered', and that the Extreme Computing Platform is 'another marketing term combining both Nutanix Acropolis and Nutanix Prism.' A genuinely enthusiastic technical advocate who still separates the substance from the branding spin.
