# Shadow IT Examples: Trello/Asana and WhatsApp

Bas illustrates Shadow IT with two everyday examples.

**Project tool** — a marketing team needs a project-management tool fast, and without consulting IT subscribes to a popular cloud tool like Trello or Asana, paying by department credit card. The problem: the tool isn't integrated with company security (single sign-on, two-step verification), so company data is stored outside IT's control — and a comparable tool may already exist in the organisation.

**WhatsApp** — a relatable example (ever been added to chat groups unwanted? Bas bets yes). A sales team uses WhatsApp with clients/colleagues instead of official tools (Microsoft Teams, Slack, email) because it's fast and easy and they find IT's tooling too cumbersome. The risk: conversations happen outside secured systems, WhatsApp isn't under IT supervision and may not meet internal security/privacy protocols. Confidential customer data can be shared or lost; there's no central overview; and if an employee leaves, customer information can become lost or inaccessible. A textbook Shadow IT case. Bas's takeaway: IT must do a much better job promoting existing internal solutions.
