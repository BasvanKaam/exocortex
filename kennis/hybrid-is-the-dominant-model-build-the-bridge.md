# Hybrid Is the Dominant Model, So Build the Best Bridge

Bas's position on where the market is heading (late 2018): the hybrid model will dominate the coming years, and the real competition between cloud providers (Amazon AWS, Microsoft Azure, and others) is now being fought inside our own datacenters via hybrid offerings, not purely in the public cloud.

He reaffirms his consistent advice: go for the solution that best fits the client. Sometimes that is a form of cloud, sometimes not, and he says he genuinely does not understand how anyone can disagree with that, yet it happens. He is provider-agnostic about the outcome: if he or his colleagues conclude 25 times in a row, or even 99 out of 100 times, that a client is better served by cloud, that is fine, they happily advise, guide, build, and maintain it. The point is that the conclusion should follow the case, not a predetermined preference.

His closing thesis: 'Wie de beste brug bouwt, wint!' (whoever builds the best bridge wins). The bridge, the link between on-premises and public cloud, is where the value and the competition now sit. He also notes a tendency among CTOs/CEOs and IT community practitioners ('met de poten in de klei') to describe hybrid/multi-cloud as the only way forward, exceptions aside.
