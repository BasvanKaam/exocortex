# Bas on continuous study and self-investment

Bas loves studying and has earned dozens of certificates over the years — nearly two or more every year. He admits some of those subjects and certificates turned out to be of little or no use to him, yet he believes you learn something from almost everything, as long as it makes some sense.

His argument: an IT leader must understand at least something of the cloud dynamic, just as today's IT'er needs a more strategic mindset. You learn that by doing, but also by studying — self-education, investing in yourself. How you do it doesn't matter, as long as you do it. Think about the topics you'll deal with in the coming year(s), or which direction you most want to go. Thinking ahead has never harmed anyone. Like his earlier advice about ten pages a day, it works step by step, bit by bit.
