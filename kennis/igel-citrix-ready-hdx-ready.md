# IGEL Citrix Ready and HDX Ready certification (2018)

How it stood in 2018. IGEL and Citrix had partnered for ~15-16 years to simplify VDI, DaaS and hosted shared desktop delivery. The full IGEL portfolio was Citrix Ready certified and also HDX Ready certified, meaning it met Citrix's requirements for 3D support (CAD/CAM applications), multimedia streaming, VOIP, multi-monitor, USB and Flash support, and ran the Citrix Receiver / Workspace App. IGEL's Universal Endpoint Management (UEM) Suite, supplied free with IGEL hard- and software, let you manage all physical endpoints (thin/zero clients plus Windows 7 and 10 desktops and laptops) from a single console. The UD Pocket (a USB-stick thin client) provided temporary conversion, with a permanent conversion variant available for older hardware. Specifics (Receiver naming, Windows 7, the certifications as worded) are dated; the partnership pattern endures.
