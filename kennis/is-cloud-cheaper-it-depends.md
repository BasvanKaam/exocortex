# Is the Cloud Cheaper? It Depends

On the perennial debate (one says the cloud is cheaper, another says certainly not always), Bas's position is that both are right: 'het ligt er aan' is the only correct answer. He refuses the binary and insists the answer is conditional on the workload, complexity, duration, goal and other variables. This 'it depends' stance is a signature of his financial and strategy reasoning, and it leads directly into doing the actual TCO/ROI work rather than asserting a blanket claim.

Source: Bas van Kaam, Salomon IT blog, 2019-10-23.
