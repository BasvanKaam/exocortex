# A 3-day course and a cert do not make a specialist

Bas's stance: a standard 3-day NetScaler training (and the certificate it can yield) gives you basic knowledge, some GUI familiarity and awareness of the wizards, but that is not the same as being able to do the work. He is careful not to dismiss it ('I would certainly not call it a bad approach, you have to start somewhere'), but his argument is that real expertise requires more: a strong foundation, self-directed depth, peer learning and genuine hands-on practice over months.

The broader position is that for scarce, complex skills like NetScaler, organisations should invest in a deeper, practice-driven development model rather than treating a short course plus cert as proof of capability.
