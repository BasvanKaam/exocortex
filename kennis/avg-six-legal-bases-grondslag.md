# The six legal bases (grondslagen) for processing under the AVG

Under the AVG, the 'grondslag' is the legal basis required to process personal data. Every organization collecting and processing personal data must have a valid basis. There are six possible legal bases:

1. **Consent of the data subject** (toestemming): explicit consent for a specific purpose.
2. **Necessary to perform a contract** (uitvoering overeenkomst): including pre-contractual measures at the subject's request.
3. **Legal obligation** (wettelijke verplichting): to comply with a legal duty on the controller.
4. **Vital interest** (vitaal belang): to protect the vital interests of the subject or another person, e.g. medical emergencies.
5. **Public-interest task or official authority** (algemeen belang / openbaar gezag): processing for a task in the public interest or exercise of official authority.
6. **Legitimate interest** (gerechtvaardigd belang): for the legitimate interests of the controller or a third party, unless the subject's interests or fundamental rights outweigh them.

Choosing the right basis matters for lawful processing; without a valid basis, processing violates the AVG and can lead to sanctions.
