# Excalibur is XenDesktop on steroids and here to stay (April 2013)

In April 2013, writing about the Tech Preview before the product had even RTM'd, Bas concludes Excalibur is "the next step": "although it's basically XenDesktop on steroids there are some really nice and exciting new features when it comes to the integration of XenApp." He flags openly that some features are missing, but predicts: "With a few adjustments here and there I'm convinced it's here to stay." His reservation is consistent: he personally prefers the old XenApp publishing flow - "I personally like the 'old' XenApp way better but that's probably a bit of getting used to." This is an early, correct directional call (the FMA architecture did become the foundation of XenApp and XenDesktop for the next decade).
