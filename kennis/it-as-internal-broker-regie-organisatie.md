# IT as Internal Broker and the Regie-Organisatie

Bas's vision of IT's ultimate destination: becoming an internal 'broker'. He draws on valuable lessons from being involved in several Shared Service Center (SSC) projects. SSCs are a clear trend; the concept sounds simple in theory but implementation demands considerable adjustment, especially on the process side.

His method — a good first step is to determine which functions are crucial to the organisation's distinctive/differentiating capability and which are more of a commodity. That latter category — services that don't directly contribute to the company's unique character — is very suitable to migrate to the cloud first, giving a low-risk, low-threshold entry.

The level of detail in a service catalog varies per organisation. IT manages this portfolio, works closely with the business to decide which services are offered, and is responsible for maintaining them. Think modern solutions like appstores and general marketplaces (several vendors now offer software for this).

Bas predicts the traditional IT organisation will step by step change into a so-called regie-organisatie (orchestration/governance organisation): the traditional management role becomes a director/orchestration role, and where the focus once lay on the own organisation, it now lies on the customer- and supplier relationship.

Definition: A Shared Service Center (SSC) is a central organisational unit that delivers specific business functions (IT, HR, finance, administration) for multiple departments or business units; municipalities (gemeenten) are a good example.
