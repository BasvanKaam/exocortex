# Take: Blast Extreme is an option, not a PCoIP killer

Writing in Feb 2017 while Blast Extreme was still relatively new, Bas was careful not to overhype it. He flagged it explicitly: 'Blast Extreme is just another protocol option within Horizon / View 7, it is not meant to replace PCoIP.' He also pushed back on letting users pick their own protocol inside the client, calling that 'something I would avoid personally.' The framing is characteristic: enthusiastic about the engineering (H.264, GPU offload, mobile optimisation) but measured about positioning, and opinionated on the admin-control details.
