# A CCoE is not only for enterprises; it's about responsibilities, not headcount

Bas's stance, stated explicitly in the Masterclass: the idea that a Cloud Center of Excellence sounds big and only applies to enterprise environments is wrong. Pick a name that fits, the number of people barely matters, what matters is the responsibilities and actually applying them. He defines a CCoE as a team responsible for leading and guiding colleagues and the organization as a whole in adopting, implementing and managing cloud technologies, and frames success as the moment business units want to work with the CCoE, when "why cloud?" flips to "why not cloud?". He pairs this with a failure-tolerant culture ("if things are not failing, you are not innovating enough").
