# The cheat-sheet method: one A4 to compare a crowded field

A recurring Bas pattern: when a category is busy and confusing, he distills it onto a single comparison sheet versioned with a number (here 'cheat sheet 1.0', matching the v1.0/v2.0 WVD partner sheets). The method is to fix a consistent set of comparison axes across every option (deployment, platform/VDI support, limits, pricing, support) so readers compare apples with apples, and to annotate footnotes for the caveats (license-dependent figures, reseller price variance, 'most expensive license' assumptions). The version number signals that the field moves fast and the sheet is a snapshot, not a permanent truth.
