# Concentrated cloud knowledge in a few seniors is a business risk

Bas's stance: most companies have only a couple of tech-savvy senior sysadmins who can automate and streamline WVD/Azure deployments. Relying on that concentrated knowledge is a real risk, because if those people leave, the capability leaves with them. Companies he talks to explicitly worry about that knowledge leaving the company.

His conclusion: the fix is to make the platform usable by 80%+ of the IT workforce so the organization is not hostage to a few individuals, and so the deep specialists are freed for higher-value work. He frames this as removing a headache and de-risking the practice, not just convenience.
