# SCAF Strategy and CCoE Workshop Method (As Taught)

The Masterclass teaches the SCAF Strategy pillar as a concrete, runnable consulting engagement, not just a model. The taught sequence of the strategy/CCoE phase:

1. Kick-off: explain the approach, determine the involved people and stakeholders, set the teams.
2. Workshop 1: drivers, objectives, and any discussion they surface.
3. Process the data: intake form, SWOT, strategy map.
4. Interviews.
5. Workshop 2: discuss the first outcome.
6. Workshop 3: determine and record the CCoE.
7. Final report for the strategy pillar, optionally presented.

The taught intake is a fixed set of "Startpunt Workshop" questions covering primary drivers for choosing cloud, desired results and business objectives, success criteria/KPIs, specific requirements, in-flight high-impact projects, key stakeholders, the role of innovation, appetite for agile working, existing IT/security policy, current partners and suppliers, strengths/weaknesses, opportunities/threats, prior cloud experience, current IT processes, satisfaction points, business-IT alignment, and who should make up the Cloud Center of Excellence.

Tooling taught per pillar: a fill-in template document per pillar, a SWOT analysis, the Kaplan and Norton strategy map, a CCoE template per pillar (Business, Processen, Mensen, Operatie, Beveiliging, Platform, Volwassenheid), and a final report / management summary. The closing practice note insists a CCoE is not enterprise-only: pick a name that fits, the headcount is irrelevant, what matters is the responsibilities and applying them.
