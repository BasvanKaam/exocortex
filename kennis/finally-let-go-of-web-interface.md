# Time to finally let go of Web Interface and consolidate Receiver

Bas's take (Jan 2015): he is enthusiastic about Receiver X1 and the X1-enabled StoreFront precisely because they mean it is 'finally time to let go of Web-Interface and embrace the new StoreFront architecture.' His self-declared personal favorite is the promise of one-touch, single-interface access to all resources across devices, something he says he was 'actually rooting for last year', ending the fragmentation of Receiver plus Worx Home ('be gone already!'). On Framehawk he is excited but notably skeptical of the hype cycle: he was 'a bit surprised by all the ecstatic reaction' since Citrix had been showing the same demos on YouTube for months. Shows him as an early adopter of consolidation/simplification who still keeps a critical eye on marketing buzz. Caveat: this whole post is sourced from his Twitter feed, not first-hand, since he was not at Summit.
