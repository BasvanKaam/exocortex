# Build model-agnostic, the model is part of your supply chain

The model under your Copilot, Foundry workloads and agents is part of your supply chain now, whether you decided to see it that way or not. Bas's advice: stop counting models and put a thin layer between you and whichever model you call, so you can move from Claude to MAI to OpenAI "without rebuilding the house every time." Resist standardizing too early while the ground shifts this fast.

## Why
- The race between models will swing back and forth for years. "The question is whether you have built your stack so that it does not much matter who wins." Get it right and each new model generation is a gift to choose from; get it wrong and you migrate every six months.
- The model was never the moat. Your data, your workflows and your review process are, "all the boring stuff that everyone keeps treating as plumbing." Frontier Tuning (tuning on your own workflow data, in your own environment) shows where the value is moving.
- Platform vendors keep options open themselves (Microsoft building its own models next to its OpenAI and Anthropic partnerships). The customer should too.

This is his long-standing vendor-independence stance ("don't let any single vendor spook you", plan the exit) applied to AI models.
