# Bas on feedback as a continuous process from the insight phase

Bas argues collecting and using feedback begins in the **insight (inzicht) phase**, continues through execution, and after go-live. His framing across the phases:
- **Insight phase**: investigate users' wishes, needs and pain points; involve users and employees in setting priorities and first plans; listen openly so you don't ignore signals.
- **Execution phase**: feedback stays integral; use it to evaluate progress and adjust; everyone feels free to say what they think.
- **After go-live**: evaluating, learning, developing further; give room to reflect and encourage feedback from personal experience.

**Creating an open environment**: feedback is only valuable when sincere and honest. Bas's concrete techniques:
- **Personal input**: encourage 'Ik vind dat...' / 'Ik denk dat...' (I think / I find) rather than impersonal 'Wij vinden dat...' (we find), which loses nuance.
- **Be open to criticism**: not all feedback is positive, and that's good; critical remarks reveal valuable improvements. Listen without judging.
- **Active listening**: summarise what you heard, ask clarifying questions, thank the speaker regardless of content.
- **Stay objective**: avoid defensive reactions; focus on what the feedback means for the project.

**Responsibility as the key to success**: create ownership (give employees clear responsibility for parts of the process); give users a voice in decisions that affect them; act and be transparent about what you do with input; and apply this across all phases, not just at the end.
