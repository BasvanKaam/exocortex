# On Mark Templeton: it was about the company, not him

At Templeton's final Synergy keynote as Citrix CEO (May 2014), Bas wrote an admiring tribute that reveals what he values in a leader: '...with Mark it's truly about the company, the community, the products and Citrix as a whole, it's not about him!' He called him 'a phenomenal public speaker' and quoted Templeton's closing line approvingly: 'Whatever you do, leave it better than you found it.' This is an early, explicit statement of the community-and-craft-over-ego values that run through Bas's own brand.
