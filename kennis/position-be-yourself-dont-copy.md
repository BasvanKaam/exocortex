# Be yourself, don't copy others, and just ship the idea

Bas's most-valued lesson from working in tech, stated plainly: be yourself and believe in yourself, share your thoughts, ask questions, and don't focus too much on or copy others. He admits he tried copying others years ago and that it 'just doesn't work', and is candid that he is 'quite an insecure person by nature, like many IT professionals are.' His counsel: keep pushing, you'll get there.

Paired stance on execution: getting things done beats talking or dreaming about them. Taking action and putting an idea into practice 'is a great feeling, successful, or not. At least you tried.'
