# On-prem is still king; a cloud 'big bang' is coming, slowly

Bas's read of the 2018 market (June 2018): traditional on-premises SBC/VDI is still king, bigger even than in 2017, with the decline in new deployments 'almost neglectable.' Because these environments run for 4+ years, he predicts this 'might lead to a big bang where older, and larger environments will be cloudified on large scale somewhere in the next few years.' Cloud is gaining ground 'slowly still, but surely', matching what he sees in the field.

Several pointed opinions: (1) He's bullish on VMware's EUC resurgence, praising their 'dream-team' and growing community, and openly doubts the survey's claim that the Citrix community is stronger today. (2) He thinks Amazon Workspaces 'are not getting the love they deserve' and advises a closer look at AWS Workspaces/AppStream. (3) He sees a huge open opportunity in application layering ('the market is still wide open') and does not see SaaS taking over that space anytime soon. (4) On UEM he argues the big three lead not because they're best but because UEM is bundled into the license, and more complete solutions exist elsewhere.
