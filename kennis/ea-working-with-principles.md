# EA: Working with Principles (Top-Down and Bottom-Up Balance)

EA works on the basis of **principles**: basic rules or guidelines that steer decisions in the architecture process, e.g. "security above all" or "simplicity where possible"; more detailed definitions are usually elaborated later. Working with principles lets organizations make consistent, well-considered choices and ensures everyone follows the same direction.

Bas's framing of EA's role in Cloud adoption:
- Pros, cons and opportunities must be discussed as part of a strategic dialogue with the business.
- The EA architect sketches different scenarios for Cloud adoption.
- EA architects combine technical knowledge with business knowledge, strategic goals and IT possibilities.
- **Business objectives are the foundation of Cloud adoption, not the other way around.**
- EA develops and defines design principles, standards and templates for Cloud adoption, but only when there is a business-objective need for it.
- It is possible to work outside the architecture principles.

**Risk of a pure top-down approach**: it is often strongly focused on business results, which can endanger renewal of the rest of the IT and architecture systems. Many innovative developments may not yield immediate visible business benefit but are needed to make the IT environment Cloud-ready; ignoring them causes later problems. Bas advises a **balance** with a bottom-up approach where ideas and needs come from the IT department, within a defined framework so everything still aligns with business goals.
