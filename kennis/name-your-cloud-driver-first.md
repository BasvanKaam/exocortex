# Decide Why You're in the Cloud Before You Migrate

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

His stance: you should be able to answer plainly whether you are investing in cloud for innovation (top line) or to save costs (bottom line) before you start moving workloads. The driver is not a footnote; it is the thing that lets you decide which migrations are tactical and worth prioritizing.

Without an explicit, organization-specific set of strategic drivers tied to the ICT Vision, migration prioritization becomes arbitrary. He treats the mix of top-line versus bottom-line initiatives as a maturity tell: a portfolio still dominated by cost-driven migrations is a traditional, lower-maturity organization.
