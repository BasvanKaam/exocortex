# Stance: take a vendor job but keep it community-first

June 2020. Even while joining a vendor, Bas insists community involvement 'will also play a very important role' because he has his 'roots in various IT communities.' He commits to a Nerdio-backed community program, framed deliberately as start-small-and-shape-it-together: 'We’ll start small, shape it together, and take it from there!' Shows his self-identity is community-evangelist first, vendor-employee second, and his instinct to build with the community rather than broadcast at it. He calls the new gig 'the Formula 1 of Windows Virtual Desktop solution providers.'
