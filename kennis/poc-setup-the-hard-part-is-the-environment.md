# In a PoC the software is easy, the environment is the hard part

A durable consulting observation. Installing PoC software is usually the easy, quick part (Bas once spun up a ProfileUnity PoC in ~25 minutes). The real cost is everything around it: getting an account with proper permissions, sized VMs, test accounts, GPOs that may need reconfiguring, networking, and existing solutions that overlap. These variables are often out of your control and can turn a 30-minute install into a multi-day effort.

His rule: get in contact early, at least two to three weeks ahead, to handle prerequisites and avoid 'surprises'. Even when every box is checked it can still go wrong, because 'IT works in mysterious ways'.

The distinction he draws: a PoC can be effective (you eventually get there) yet deeply inefficient. Hosted/PoC-as-a-Service removes the environment-prep tax so evaluators get hands-on immediately.
