# Weolcan Hybrid Cloud Training (2-day curriculum)

A two-day instructor-led Hybrid Cloud Training (version 2.1.2, dated 22 October 2018, delivered under the Weolcan brand), predating and separate from the later book and the SCAF. It is an earlier piece of Bas's training IP, structured as five modules across two days, each closing with hands-on labs.

**Day 1 (8:30-17:00):**
- **Module 1 - Introduction to Cloud Computing:** Part 1 Definitions (NIST definition of cloud computing, cloud-related definitions); Part 2 Business Drivers and Benefits (benefits of cloud, business drivers behind cloud).
- **Module 2 - Cloud Strategy:** Part 1 Cloud Strategy; Part 2 Cloud Placement Strategy; Part 3 Business Case Model; Part 4 Hybrid IT Strategy.
- **Module 3 - Cloud Governance, Controls and Compliancy:** Part 1 Cloud Governance and Controls (cloud governance and cost management, cloud security); Part 2 Cloud Exit Strategy; Part 3 Measures for Organizations.

**Day 2 (8:00-15:30):**
- **Module 4 - Cloud Architecture:** Part 1 Cloud Architecture; Part 2 Cloud Components; Part 3 Cloud Migration; Part 4 Configuration Management.
- **Module 5 - Cloud Trends:** Part 1 Continuous Delivery; Part 2 Cloud Brokering; Part 3 Amazon, Azure and Google Cloud; Part 4 Micro-Services and Containers and Micro-OS.

Lunch breaks separate the morning and afternoon blocks on both days, and each day ends with hands-on labs. The recurring themes (placement strategy, business-case model, governance and cost management, cloud exit strategy, hybrid IT) are early versions of the structures that later mature into the SCAF and the book.
