# The pandemic compressed VDI/DaaS adoption from months to weeks

From Bas's IGEL Disrupt VDI/DaaS Think Tank panel (with Microsoft and VMware): the panel agreed the most striking EUC shift of the pandemic was the pace of adoption. Organizations large and small adopted a VDI/DaaS strategy in months, sometimes weeks, where it normally took 6 to 12 months. The driver was a pressing need to enable secure work from home, which evolved into work from anywhere on any device. Remote/flexible work had been debated for years, but the pandemic removed the appetite for endless reviews: it simply needed to be done, now. Bas frames the agility and speed of design-and-implementation as the headline takeaway.
