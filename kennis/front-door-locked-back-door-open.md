# Front door locked, back door wide open

Bas's stance: security is universal across every sector (retail, housing market, manufacturing) and dependence on IT, with its attendant risk, has never been greater. Yet many firms have no good picture of how security is actually arranged internally.

His signature framing is 'schijn veiligheid' (false/apparent security): the front door firmly locked while the back door stands wide open, a pattern he says he encounters all too often. Even when you are fairly sure you have thought of everything, an independent second opinion is the prudent move. The cost of getting this wrong is not only financial.
