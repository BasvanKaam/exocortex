# Self-publish first, for the freedom

In March 2016, announcing his first book (Inside Citrix - The Flex Management Architecture), Bas explains he decided years earlier that if he ever wrote a book he would self-publish, or at least try that before anything else. His reasoning is about freedom, not economics: no hard deadlines, no editors breathing down his neck for three to six months. He concedes self-publishing is probably a lot more work, especially the first time, but frames learning something new as a default-good ("learning something new hasn't gone wrong for me yet"). He still paid out of pocket for professional proofreading and copy editing, not because his English is bad but because he wants to deliver the best result his limited resources allow. This is an early, clear statement of a recurring stance: own the work end to end, accept more effort in exchange for control and quality.
