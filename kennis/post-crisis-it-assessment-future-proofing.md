# Post-Crisis IT Assessment and Future-Proofing

After a crisis forces ad-hoc IT decisions (Zoom/Teams adoption, laptops and phones bought, cloud-based workplaces requested, remote login to the corporate network), the work is not done: review what was set in motion and what impact it has or could have. Mistakes and oversights in such situations are normal; what matters is examining them in the weeks/months after.

**Goal (future proof):** ensure the steps taken under duress fit the organization's strategy and policy, identify where adjustments are necessary, minimize business risks today and tomorrow, and secure more control for a next crisis scenario. Properly handled lessons learned make you future proof.

**Assessment questions to create insight:**
- Which products are in use and do they meet GDPR/AVG regulation and company policy?
- Do people know enough to use the adopted products (Teams, SharePoint, Zoom), or is training needed?
- What is possible under the current license form?
- Which home workstations (including peripherals) are in use, are they safe, and where are the risks?
- Is company data sufficiently shielded from risk and how is its integrity guarded?
- Which critical business processes exist, which are essential to safeguard continuity, and which technical resources are needed to support them?
