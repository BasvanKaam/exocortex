# Windows 365 Reserve (Cloud PC panic button)

Explainer angle Bas drafted around Microsoft's Windows 365 Reserve: a temporary, fully pre-configured Cloud PC you can spin up in minutes when your endpoint is lost, stolen or broken. Preloaded with your apps, policies, OneDrive files and security baselines. Up to 10 Reserve days per year, launched from any other device via a browser or the Windows App.

His framing addresses the obvious objection ("if my laptop was stolen, how do I access it?"): you don't need your own device, you need any device (loaner, colleague's machine, tablet, home desktop, hotel business center, thin client) plus an internet connection and credentials. It is not meant to replace your endpoint; it keeps you working when things go wrong.

Why it's clean operationally: no infrastructure to maintain (no host pools, scaling logic or patching), one-click access for the user, fully preconfigured, and no manual cleanup because Reserve Cloud PCs auto-expire and auto-disappear.

Dated: tied to the W365 Reserve preview timing and Microsoft's stated limits (10 days/year). Verify current availability and limits before reuse.
