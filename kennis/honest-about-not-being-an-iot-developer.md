# Honest he's not an IoT developer, but curious about the trajectory

Closing his Octoblu breakdown (May 2017), Bas openly admits 'I'm not that big on IoT developing/programming myself', but says he finds it genuinely interesting to watch how IoT as a concept keeps evolving at a rapid pace. This is a characteristic move: he marks the edge of his own expertise rather than bluffing past it, while still backing his curiosity by doing the architectural homework (he explicitly built the post off a SlideShare deck and a repost from mycugc.org). The stance, intellectual honesty about depth limits plus a forward-looking interest in where a technology is heading, recurs across his work.
