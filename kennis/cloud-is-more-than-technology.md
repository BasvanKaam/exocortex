# Cloud is about far more than technology

Working on the Salomon Cloud Adoption Framework during summer 2019 crystallized a durable position: the technology is the smallest piece of the puzzle. The potential impact on an organisation can be huge and the main focus belongs on the business side. Things to prepare for before diving in headfirst include cultural change, internal processes, the shift from a Capex to an Opex model, end-user acceptance and training, and communication.

This is a recurring theme across his 2019-2020 writing and it carries into how he advises companies.
