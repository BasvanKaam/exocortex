# XenApp folded into the FMA: the Excalibur / XenDesktop 7.0 moment

Bas recounts the pivotal moment of 26 June 2013, when Citrix launched XenDesktop 7.0 (code name Excalibur) and folded XenApp into it. From that date XenApp would no longer be sold as a separate product and became part of XenDesktop, built on the FMA (FlexCast Management Architecture) rather than the IMA (Independent Management Architecture).

His framing of the consequences:
- All further XenApp 6.5 / IMA development stopped (one exception): no more feature packs or enhancements. As a final gesture Feature Pack 2 for XenApp 6.5 shipped alongside XenDesktop 7.
- The announcement got 'a LOT of attention and unfortunately for Citrix not in a good way.'
- A silver lining: it forced Citrix-minded IT folk to realise there was more than the IMA, giving the FMA much more attention.
- For diehard XenApp 6.5 admins who never touched XenDesktop, the FMA was 'unknown territory'; configuring/administering XenApp in 6.5 is completely different from XenApp under the FMA in 7.x. The IMA-to-FMA transition was 'just the first hurdle; unfortunately, more would follow.'
