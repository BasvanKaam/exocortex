# Office 365 ProPlus update channels

ProPlus delivers new features on a chosen channel; organizations control who gets what feature when. Three channels (circa 2019 naming):
- Monthly Channel: newest features immediately, generally monthly.
- Semi-Annual Channel: new features twice a year (January and July). This is the ProPlus default.
- Semi-Annual Channel (Targeted): for pilot users and compatibility testers, twice a year (March and September), four months ahead of the matching Semi-Annual release, fully identical and 100% supported.

Security and quality updates ship monthly for every channel regardless. Channel choice depends on the number and type of apps, add-ins and plug-ins that must be compatibility-tested. Each Semi-Annual feature update is supported for up to 18 months. Switching channels is done via the Office Deployment Tool or Group Policy (config.office.com).
