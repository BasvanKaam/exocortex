# SCAF Guidelines Handbook: Internal, Reusable Source Document

The "SCAF Richtlijnen handboek" is the internal Salomon playbook for running a Salomon Cloud Adoptie Framework engagement. It is explicitly written for internal use, but its texts and overviews are designed to be reused as building blocks for client reports, quotes/offers, blogs and other publications. Per pillar there is a matching set of templates and supporting documents (templates, supporting images/overviews, per-process reports and the final report) that are intended to be shared with the client.

The handbook is structured: H1 why SCAF helps, H2 introduction and differentiators, H4 from first conversation to execution, H5 the four pillars in detail, H6 general assumptions, H7 the end goal, H8 glossary. The stated end goal: each pillar produces one filled-in report against a template, merged into a single final report of no more than 20 to 25 pages plus an executive summary, with evaluation and presentation moments scheduled throughout so the engagement can be steered at any point.

This note records the handbook's nature and reuse model; the per-pillar method, waves, RACI/GAP and templates are distilled in the dedicated SCAF notes.
