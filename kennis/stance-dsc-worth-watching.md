# Citrix's XenDesktop DSC Tech Preview is one to watch

April 2015, part of his -1000 blog series. Bas flagged the newly released Citrix XenDesktop Desired State Configuration resource provider Tech Preview as something to keep an eye on even though it wasn't a hot topic: "Although it may not sound very sexy and it isn't a hot topic at this time it is definitely something to keep an eye on and perhaps worth investing some time in." His prediction: "I'm pretty sure that we will see and hear a lot more on this in the months / years to come." This shows his pattern of spotting infrastructure-as-code / automation trends early and telling his EUC audience to invest learning time before it becomes mainstream.
