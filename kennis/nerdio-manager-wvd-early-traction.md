# Nerdio Manager for WVD launched March 2020 with fast traction

How it was at the time: Nerdio released Nerdio Manager for Windows Virtual Desktop in March 2020. By late May 2020 Bas described the traction it was getting as 'enormous'. This is the product that pulled him toward Nerdio before he formally joined. Useful as a timestamp for when NMW (later NMM / Nerdio Manager) entered the market and started gaining momentum in the WVD third-party tooling space.
