# SCAF timeline: phases, deliverables and gates

The visual that lays the SCAF phases out on a timeline and pins each artifact and decision moment to a phase. Sequence:

1. Project initiation (Project initiatie)
2. Strategy phase (Strategie fase)
3. Insight phase (Inzicht fase)
4. Execution phase (Uitvoering fase)
5. Adoption phase (Adoptie fase)

Mapped onto this line:
- Business case is produced early (around Strategy/Insight).
- A Go/No-Go decision gate sits before committing to Execution.
- Progress reporting (Voortgangsrapportage) runs through Execution.
- A final report (Eindrapportage) closes the engagement.
- Measurement (Meten) is added in the Adoption phase.

Key framing he attaches: the early phases run in weeks, the Adoption phase in roughly a year, and durations are variable and agreed with the client rather than fixed. The timeline is the one-page picture that shows clients where the gates and deliverables fall across an engagement.
