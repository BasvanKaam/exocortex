# Position: No Information, No Control, No Trust

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

The framework frames communication as the linchpin of good governance, not an afterthought. His chain of reasoning: if you don't have information you will never feel in control; without that feeling of control it is impossible to establish trust; and trust is exactly what is needed to empower an increasingly self-managed enterprise.

The stance reframes governance reporting as the enabler of organizational autonomy rather than a bureaucratic burden: you communicate results up the chain precisely so that you can safely decentralize and self-manage.
