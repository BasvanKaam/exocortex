# Citrix Lifecycle Management Services and blueprints

Citrix Lifecycle Management is a CWC cloud-based service to accelerate, automate and simplify the design, deployment and ongoing management of Citrix workloads, including enterprise applications. Using predefined, Citrix-certified blueprints, customers can roll out complete Citrix-based environments fully automated onto their Hypervisor or cloud platform of choice (their Resource Locations).

Depending on subscription (Deploy; Design and Deploy; Design, Deploy and Manage) you can design your own blueprints, edit existing ones, and access the Citrix library of certified/predefined blueprints (by Citrix or third-party partners). Options include alerting, monitoring, disaster recovery, a fully automated upgrade from XenApp 6.5 (including application and policy configurations), and more.

FMA fact: Citrix offers out-of-the-box blueprints for XenDesktop, XenApp, XenMobile, NetScaler and the Workspace Suite. The Cloud Connector connects to your Resource Location (including AD), and blueprint-based machine provisioning/configuration also leverages the Connector. CWC Resource Locations include on-premises/your own datacentres, Azure, AWS, and the Citrix CloudPlatform.
