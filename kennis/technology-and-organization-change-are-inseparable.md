# Technology choices and organizational change are inseparable

Bas's stance underpinning the roadmap listicle: too often it is forgotten that technology choices and organization-driven adjustments are two interconnected sides of a successful IT organization. You cannot treat the tech track and the people/process track separately. Precisely in an era where solutions are more and more cloud-based, the priority is to anchor change within the IT team itself, not just to pick tools.

The roadmap is his instrument for that: it is dynamic not static, exposes weak links wherever they sit (organizational, technical, process), and forces business and technology to grow closer and understand each other. His pragmatic note keeps it un-dogmatic: not every company is ready for a multi-year roadmap, so start small, keep a finger on the pulse, and take appropriate action along the way.
