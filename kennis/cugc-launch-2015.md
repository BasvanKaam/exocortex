# The Citrix User Group Community (CUGC) launched at Synergy 2015

How it worked then (2015). The Citrix User Group Community (CUGC) was announced and officially launched at Citrix Synergy 2015. Over 3,200 people signed up during Synergy alone. The concept: Citrix provides a global, free platform, but the community runs it and fills it with content (with sponsors as needed). Structure included a Blog section (CTPs and Citrix-minded pros), a Files section for shared material, a Community section listing Citrix User Groups (CUGs) by region/country, and planned Special Interest Groups (SIGs). Members could form their own local CUGs ('Build/Bring Your Own CUG'), guided by a Code of Conduct and Content Principles. Signup was simplified and free of charge.
