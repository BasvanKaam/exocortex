# Why keeping only strictly relevant services pays off

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

Decommissioning unused or obsolete services so only strictly relevant services remain yields three concrete benefits:

- No unnecessary cost
- Reduced attack surface
- Administrative ease

This ties continuous improvement directly to cost control, security posture, and operational simplicity, rather than treating service hygiene as optional housekeeping.
