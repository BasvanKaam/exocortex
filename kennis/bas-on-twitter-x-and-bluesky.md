# Bas's take on Twitter/X and Bluesky for following experts

Bas closes his community chapter with a list of Twitter handles, noting as a characteristic aside that he dislikes how 'X' sounds ("sorry, ik vind X niet erg prettig klinken"). He gives no guarantee all handles are still active, but suggests that if you have a spare half hour and an account, following a few is a good idea.

His advice: the list contains some very well-known names but almost certainly also names you've never heard of, and those unknown ones are where he'd start if he were you. As always the list is not complete, just meant to get you going. He also notes that lately quite a few people have moved to Bluesky, a similar platform.
