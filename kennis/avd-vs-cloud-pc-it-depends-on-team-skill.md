# AVD vs Cloud PC is a control-vs-simplicity trade-off keyed to your team's Azure skill

Bas's stance on choosing between AVD and Windows 365 Cloud PC: it comes down to your team's Azure/Intune expertise and how much flexibility and cost-optimization you want. If simplicity is the priority, the Business Cloud PC SKU is the first choice (no Intune, Microsoft-managed network, click-and-go). If you want cost control and have the know-how, AVD is worth it because you control when machines boot, shut down, build and remove, can optimize the underlying infrastructure, and can pick from hundreds of VM types, so there's no use case it can't handle. AVD is effectively free if you already hold the right Microsoft 365 licensing (e.g. Business Premium); you pay only for compute and storage. He observes many companies combine the two: they started on AVD, and once Cloud PC existed it became the better fit for some use cases. The consultant's honest answer remains 'it depends'.
