# EUC News Nuggets: site structure, cast and catalogue

Snapshot of eucnewsnuggets.com as verified on 2 October 2026. The site is Bas's personal microlearning platform, hosted on GitHub Pages, with one content index (`content-index.js`) feeding every page. Use this note for exact titles and dates; the platform's origin and reasoning live in the pivot note.

## Structure: four places
- **Library** (`index.html`): PDF microlessons and cheat sheets.
- **Nuggets** (`nuggets.html`): the audio side, a podcast on Spotify, five to ten minutes per episode.
- **Press** (`press.html`): longer blog posts.
- **Cast** (`the-cast.html`): who writes and who reads.

Plus a personal profile page (the "Hall of Fame" dashboard).

## The cast: "I write, Finn reads"
- **Bas van Kaam**, Principal Learning Architect. Writes every lesson, picks the topics, decides what goes in and what gets cut. Joins the audio on intros and the occasional episode, no fixed rule.
- **Finn Morgan**, credited as "Senior Technical Trainer". AI-generated voice, disclosed openly, no narrator-persona theatre. Does most of the reading because it is clear, consistent and fast, which lets more episodes ship without studio time.

**Nerdio line.** The site states that the library is personal: his time, name and opinions, no internal review, no corporate hat. Nerdio knows it exists and the two agreed where the line runs. Nerdio topics show up heavily because that is his day job.
