# Run Community Events Outside the Marketing Funnel

From Bas's Hackathon retrospective: operational community communication should not run through the marketing automation platform. If every message goes out via HubSpot it starts looking like a campaign, open rates fall, and that fails precisely at the critical moments (getting NDAs signed, sending the kickoff). His position: event organizers need the ability to email contestants directly, outside the marketing system, and need self-service access to export their own contact lists rather than depending on a single gatekeeper. Operations and marketing are different jobs and should not share one pipe.
