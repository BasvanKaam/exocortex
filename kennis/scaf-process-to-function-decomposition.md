# SCAF Process-to-Function Decomposition

The handbook defines the structural logic of how the SCAF is built, crediting the original process-and-function concept to Gert Jan Schop. A process (one of the four pillars) is a coherent whole of activities, people and resources that produces one or more products or services. The decomposition is layered:

- A process breaks down into activities (the steps per pillar).
- An activity breaks down into handelingen (the actual actions you carry out, plan and work out).
- Handelingen are bundled into an activity; multiple activities are grouped into a taak (task); multiple tasks form a functie (a role/function description).
- The various functions (roles) in turn make up one or more (often agile) teams, who together carry out the planned tasks.

The four pillars (Strategie, Inzicht, Uitvoering, Adoptie) together form a process chain (procesketen). This decomposition is what lets the SCAF translate cleanly into RACI-assigned teams and roles rather than staying an abstract diagram.
