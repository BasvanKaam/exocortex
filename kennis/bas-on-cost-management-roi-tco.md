# Bas on cost management: cost vs. value and ROI/TCO

Bas opens his cost-management section with a wry observation: everything stands or falls with the budget, or so it often seems. People generally prefer to look at what something *costs* rather than what it *delivers* ("helaas"). That, he argues, is exactly why an ROI/TCO calculation is so important: something can look expensive on paper while its added value turns out far greater, and the reverse also happens, looking rosy on paper while costs in reality spiral out of control ("de kosten de pan uit rijzen").

The Cloud offers many advantages but without good overview and a solid plan costs rise quickly, so during the adoption phase you must learn to watch and preferably limit spend; it may already be one of your KPIs from the business case. Practical levers he lists:
- Use the cost-management tools of Azure, AWS and Google Cloud to map cost per service and see where to save.
- Set **budgets and alerts** so you're warned before overspending and can intervene (or adjust the budget).
- **Automatically switch off unused services**, e.g. VMs outside working hours; for DaaS there are various third-party platforms, and DevOps can help (assuming you know what you're doing).
- Treat cost management as a **shared responsibility**: teams must understand how their choices affect costs, fostered by regularly sharing cost reports and brainstorming savings.
