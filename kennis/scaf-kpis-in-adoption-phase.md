# KPIs in the SCAF Adoption phase: making success measurable

The adoption phase marks the transition to an operational platform, and Bas insists success must be made measurable here (you may have started during execution, e.g. as part of your business case). KPIs are tools to evaluate and steer the Cloud environment. Bas notes it also helps, especially early on, to share certain successes.

Example KPIs he gives:
- **Uptime and availability** (e.g. a target of 99.9% or higher).
- **Performance and speed**: latency (response time), throughput, transactions per second.
- **Cost optimisation**: cost per user, cost per resource, percentage of unused/idle resources, to foster a culture of cost-awareness (often a substantial part of the business case).
- **End-user satisfaction**: he suggests using DEX (Digital Employee Experience) to measure employee satisfaction on a Cloud platform, but warns strongly not to forget to actually talk to your staff/customer: technology tells only part of the story.
- **Security incidents and compliance challenges**.
- **IT-process efficiency**: mean time to repair, time-to-approval of changes, number of automated tasks.

Example benchmarks: 99.9% uptime, average response time under 200 ms, and a cost-saving target of 15% versus traditional IT spend. Monitoring tools and dashboards track KPIs in real time; periodic (monthly/quarterly) reports to stakeholders build trust and acceptance.
