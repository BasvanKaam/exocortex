# The CCoE at the heart of the Target Operating Model

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

The Target Operating Model (TOM) at the heart of the Cloud Governance framework is centered on the Cloud Center of Expertise. The CCoE is nestled between the Demand and Delivery parties at both ends of the IT value chain, and its main purpose inside the organization is to increase the organization's cloud maturity.

The CCoE focuses on knowledge development across the entire organization on the topic of cloud. Both the role of the business itself and well-thought-out demand management and supply management are crucial; internal management is often insufficiently developed and adjusted when organizations opt for cloud.

The prefix 'Target' signals a Future Mode of Operations: it assumes you already have an IT operating model in place. The TOM is a way to mature an IT organization incrementally; not everything has to be a 5 out of 5 from the start. Choosing to set up a competence center for cloud can also be a good kick in the backside to start training and/or recruiting the people who build the necessary knowledge.

The model is built from 'tiles' (tasks or activities), grouped into Capabilities, with grey interface columns on both sides representing roles and processes that are closely aligned. Each tile is sometimes an existing IT(IL) process to redesign, sometimes a task supporting other roles or teams.
