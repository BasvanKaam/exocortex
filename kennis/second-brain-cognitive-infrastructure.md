# Second brain als cognitieve infrastructuur

A blog by Brian (VP and Futurist at Citrix), early 2026, on running your knowledge as a folder of plain text files that an AI continuously reads, writes and maintains. The thesis matters because it reshapes how this exocortex should work, not just what is in it.

## The usable ideas

- **Flip the loop.** Instead of re-uploading files into each AI conversation, put everything in a folder and instruct the AI: everything you need is here, read and update it. The knowledge management stops living in your head.
- **Self-updating and compounding.** The AI writes changes back during every conversation. It notices when thinking shifts, reconciles contradictions, weaves new insights in. You do not file or tag; you think and talk, the system maintains the record.
- **Connections are the value.** A strategy talk on Monday, a positioning meeting on Wednesday, a half-formed idea on Friday: the system connects all three and files the idea where it links to both, unprompted. The graph beats the pile.
- **It writes from your foundation, not your "style".** With hundreds of files of your frameworks, positions and edits, the output is yours because it draws on the same material you would, not because it mimics a voice. It learns from your edits and pushback.
- **The invisible 80%.** Visible work (emails, docs, formatting) is ~20% and is where corporate AI copilots operate. The real knowledge work, ~80% (strategy, judgment, pattern recognition, tacit expertise, connecting ideas), has always lived only in your head. A second brain is the first tool that reaches that 80%.
- **It is just files.** Plain text in git. That makes it shareable and connectable: pull in a colleague's perspective, subscribe to an expert's frameworks, wire brains together via MCP, automate ingestion (RSS, podcasts) with GitHub Actions overnight. Cognitive infrastructure across people, with open questions on ownership, provenance and the economics of expertise.
- **Not magic.** The AI has no good ideas for you; it develops your ideas faster and keeps nothing lost. Garbage in, well-organized nothing out. You still have to think.

## Why it matters for this brein

Confirms our foundation (files in git, instructions-for-myself in the repo, learning from edits) and exposes our two missing layers: a connection layer between notes and proactive, unprompted maintenance. Both are now encoded in [brein-onderhoud](../standaarden/brein-onderhoud.md). It also pushes us to capture Bas's positions and tacit 80%, not only book facts, and points at a later automation/sharing track (GitHub Actions, MCP).
