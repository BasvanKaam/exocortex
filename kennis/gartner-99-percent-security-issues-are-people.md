# Gartner: 99% of security issues are caused by people

An interesting statistic Bas cites: according to Gartner, 99 percent of security issues are caused by people — i.e. your own staff. This can be as simple as opening an email that appears to come from a trusted source. Bas uses this to set up the case for a Zero Trust approach and the importance of awareness.
