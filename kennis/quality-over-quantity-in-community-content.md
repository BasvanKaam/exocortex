# Quality Over Quantity in Community Content

In the Come Get IT roadmap Bas takes an explicit position: focus on quality instead of quantity. The practical expression is cutting events from three to two a year while increasing consistent serial output (monthly-plus podcasts and articles) and cross-pollinating with adjacent communities like EUCdigest rather than going it alone. The argument is that a smaller number of well-produced things beats a larger number of stretched ones, and that partnering compounds reach instead of fragmenting effort.
