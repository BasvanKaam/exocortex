# Cloud Masterclass delivery setup and materials checklist

The in-person Cloud Masterclass / bootcamp runs as a facilitated classroom day with a fixed logistics setup. Required materials and arrangements:

- Laminated red and green cards (used as interactive response/voting tools during the session).
- A whiteboard (preferred) with working markers, or a flip-over.
- Coffee, tea and soft drinks all day, an afternoon treat (e.g. gevulde koeken), and a few trays of cold Red Bull.
- Catered lunch on-site, deliberately not walking somewhere off-site.
- Everyone seated at a table so they can bring a laptop; enough power outlets for laptops and phones.
- A printed hand-out, but only handed out at the end of the training (so attendees do not page ahead, and to cover anyone absent on day two).
- A Byte-Sized book to give away, a notepad and pen laid out per seat, and a Salomon Cloud Masterclass sticker.
- A completion certificate for every attendee.

Follow-up after the class: a Slack group per class/training, and course material made available via Avensus Academy (deliberately not during the lesson, since access mid-session only distracts and tempts people to page ahead).

This is a delivery/facilitation artifact specific to the training, not book content.
