# Old School: why Bas insists on pen and paper in a cloud book

In a book about innovative technology like the cloud, Bas deliberately asks the reader to work as much as possible with pen and paper, calling the combination of new technology and old-school techniques "verrassend krachtig" (surprisingly powerful), "geloof me, ik kan het weten."

His reasoning: writing by hand stimulates active, deep processing in the brain. You do not just put words down, you think, summarise and structure, which sticks better. He cites a study by Princeton University and UCLA (one of many) showing that people who take notes by hand understand and remember concepts better than those who type; writing slows the process, forces reflection and creates a stronger link between what you read and what you remember.

The important questions, assignments and AANTEKENINGEN (note) spaces in the book are built to make you think and translate theory directly to your own practice. For those who find pen and paper too traditional, he suggests a reMarkable or a tablet with a stylus, and apps like OneNote, Notability or GoodNotes, which keep the handwriting benefit plus digital storage and sharing.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> de combinatie van nieuwe technologieën en old-school technieken een verrassend krachtige aanpak kan zijn, geloof me, ik kan het weten.
