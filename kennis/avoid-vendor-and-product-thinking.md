# Don't think in brands or product boxes

Bas's stance: he does not like to think in boxes, specific brands or products. A vendor end-of-life (here Citrix XenApp 6.5) is merely an example trigger, not a reason to pick a successor product. The right question is not "which upgrade?" but "which route fits you best?" There are always multiple roads to Rome, including but not limited to the cloud.

Underlying argument: it is always wise to periodically ask whether earlier choices are still the right ones and whether you are ready for what is coming. Look further and think differently rather than defaulting to the familiar product.
