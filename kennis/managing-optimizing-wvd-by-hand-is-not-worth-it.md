# Hand-rolling WVD management with PowerShell/DevOps isn't worth the risk and time

Bas's recurring argument for Nerdio: managing, fine-tuning and optimizing WVD deployments is no easy task no matter how good your PowerShell or DevOps automation skills are, and doing it by hand carries real risk and consumes serious time. That, in his telling, is why NMW was a hit from day one and the same logic carries into NMM. The platform's value is removing the steep learning curve so the bulk of an IT workforce can build and manage Azure/WVD environments daily rather than a handful of automation specialists.
