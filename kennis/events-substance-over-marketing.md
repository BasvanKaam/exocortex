# Stance: community sessions should be technical substance, not marketing

When curating Come Get IT, Bas deliberately picks speakers and sessions that go deep on the technology and leave the vendor marketing aside. He frames a NVIDIA session as worthwhile precisely because it covers the markets and tech "waarbij we de marketing praat even laten voor wat het is" (where we leave the marketing talk for what it is). His implicit standard for a good community session: real practitioners, real architecture and tips, bulletpoint-light, humour welcome, hype not.
