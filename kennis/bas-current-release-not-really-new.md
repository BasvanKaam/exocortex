# Bas's take: the Current Release isn't really new

Bas reframes the marketing around Citrix's Current Release (CR). While the CR is presented as a new offering, he points out it really isn't: "CR is basically the way it has always been before the LTSR was introduced. They just had to come up with a second name to make a clear distinction between the two."

He wrote a more detailed blog post on what is and isn't new about the LTSR vs CR at basvankaam.com (April 2016). The takeaway he wants readers to keep: don't be fooled by the rebranding — the genuine innovation is the LTSR track (5+5 years of support, predictable fix-only updates), not the CR label slapped on the old default behaviour.
