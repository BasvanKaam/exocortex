# Bas's paper: Cloud Only? No. Cloud First or Hybrid? Yes.

Chapter 17 reproduces a paper Bas wrote for a Cloud Masterclass at the end of 2020, graded an 8 (he says "9+, well, an 8"). The lower mark was deliberate: he knowingly left out some mandatory sections, confident he'd still pass comfortably, and it saved him a full day of work — "Work smarter, not harder."

Bas adds an honest meta-reflection: rereading it years later he no longer fully agrees with some of his own statements — things change fast and that's fine; you're allowed to change your mind and move with the times ("if you do what you always did, you get what you always got, and that's no progress"). He also finds the paper somewhat negatively written and rather direct/blunt — clear, but not a communication style everyone handles well — something he's worked on in the years since.

The paper's core thesis: the cloud is not a silver bullet. A Cloud-only world is a Utopia, at least for the coming 15-20 years, possibly longer. The only valid path forward is **Cloud first or hybrid** — use cloud services where it makes sense and adds something. Step by step is the only correct approach. The paper covers: cloud characteristics (the promise), the human factor, traditional (Windows) applications, workspace aggregators, physical and software limitations, and the lack of overview/knowledge/governance.
