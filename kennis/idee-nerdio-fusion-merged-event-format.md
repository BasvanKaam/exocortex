# Idea: Nerdio Fusion - a merged event format

Bas's proposed event format combining the Innovation Day (vision, value, prospects, co-hosted with Microsoft) and the Enterprise Training Camp (hands-on technical enablement for existing customers) into one experience. Working names: 'Nerdio Fusion' or 'Nerdio 360'. Theme line he drafted: 'Where Strategy Meets Skill - Connect the vision. Master the tools. Prove your expertise.'

Structure: 2.5 days total.
- Evening before (18:00-20:00): informal welcome drinks, networking, snacks, brief intro to the two days.
- Day 1 (Vision & Value + Technical Kick-off): Microsoft and Nerdio vision/roadmap/alignment, breakout rounds, a demo (could be picked on the fly based on the room), then lab onboarding plus two warm-up starter labs (Workspace and Image source VM) so day 2 starts faster. Closes with an Ask Me Anything (include Microsoft) and optional dinner.
- Day 2 (Build & Certify): the full ETC program, from zero to a functioning PoV/PoC environment in a day, ending in a mini-certification track ('NME-201 Express', built from existing modules like W365, Intune, AVD autoscale, max ~30 min) or a Master Microhack with prizes.

Design principles he flags:
- Pre-event questionnaire (current tools, goals, blockers, pain points, knowledge gaps) to tailor a session around common themes; this can replace a generic breakout and is 'a good fit for day 1.'
- He is wary of separate parallel tracks: they complicate logistics on the day.
- Wants strong joint marketing around the combined 'best of both worlds with Microsoft' story.
- And, recurring: 'we need stickers'.
