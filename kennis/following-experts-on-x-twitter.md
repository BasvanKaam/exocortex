# Following Cloud and EUC experts on X (Twitter)

Bas has for years followed industry experts on Twitter (now X) and lays out why it differs from podcasts. On X you post short, to-the-point messages (paid subscribers can post longer texts). Benefits he highlights:
- Real-time access to updates and insights, with the ability to react directly with your own thoughts - he has more than once seen discussions arise this way that ran on for days. Experts post live reactions to tech developments, product announcements or conferences, often sitting in the room during a keynote, so followers learn of changes without waiting for a longer format like a podcast or blog post.
- Diverse perspectives from a broad network across the industry, not limited to a single source, helping form a more complete picture.
- Short, valuable tips and links to resources experts share (new whitepapers, cases, tools, technical articles), often directly usable in daily practice.
- Insight into the personal side of experts: their daily thoughts, opinions and experiences reveal their thought processes and priorities, which can inspire and create a deeper bond than formal content. Followers also learn of events, webinars and exclusive sessions early, often first via social media, helping them jump on networking or learning opportunities.
