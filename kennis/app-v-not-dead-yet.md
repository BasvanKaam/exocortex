# App-V is not dead yet, despite MSIX

Bas's measured stance against the hype that MSIX/AppAttach instantly kills App-V: "While this won't happen overnight, and App-V is still very popular, and probably will be for years to come, it is clear that Microsoft is slowly moving away from this approach."

The direction of travel is clear (MSIX/AppAttach is the future), but he refuses to write off the incumbent prematurely. Characteristic of his pragmatic, non-hype EUC positioning.
