# Stance: we sell a vision, not hard or software

'Wij verkopen een visie, geen hard of software.' The advisory relationship is the product. Together with the client and, where useful, partners, the goal is a more cost-efficient way of working and readiness for what is coming, not a box.

He couples this with a self-description that reads as a working method: 'Ik denk anders en out of the box, moderne technologieen/oplossingen kennen moderne uitdagingen, iets wat vaak over het hoofd wordt gezien of simpelweg niet bekend is bij de betrokken partijen.' The value he claims is seeing the modern challenges that others miss, not reselling a vendor's catalogue.
