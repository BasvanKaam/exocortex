# Bas van Kaam: credentials, awards and community programs

Consolidated reference of recognition, titles and the programmes he founded or led. 26 titles and programmes in total on his own count.

## Industry awards and recognition
- Turning Complexity Into Clarity Award (Nerdio), 2026.
- Microsoft MVP, Enterprise Mobility category (WVD, RDS, etc.), 2020 to 2023. Now MVP Alumni.
- Citrix Technology Advocate (CTA), Class of 2019.
- Citrix Technology Professional (CTP), 2016 and 2017, described as 1 of 50 worldwide.
- IGEL Tech Community Insider (ITCI).
- Nutanix Technology Champion (NTC), 2014 to 2017.
- Atlantis Community Experts (ACE).
- CUGC Top Forum Poster Award 2016; CUGC Member of the Month June 2015.
- 60+ professional certifications.

## Programmes founded and led
- Nerdio Tech Insider (NTI), founder and manager.
- Nerdio Valued Professional (NVP), founder and manager.
- Liquidware Tech Insider NL (LTI), founder and manager.
- EUCdigest, co-founder (registered non-profit, KvK), with Eric van Klaveren and Jits Langedijk.
- Project Byte-Sized / ProByteSized, co-founder (with Christiaan Brinkhoff).
- Come Get IT (CGIT), co-organizer.
- FMA Internals Co-op, co-founder with Mick Glover.
- Board Member of the Global AVD Community and founder of the Dutch AVD User Group.

## Community roles and platform contributions
- CUGC Forum Moderator and Content Working Group, founding member.
- WhatMatrix contributor (App Layering and DaaS).
- Featured Author on DABCC, 2014 to 2017.
- Inside Track for EUC/VDI VIPs.
- Citrix Facebook Group moderator ("Citrix Certified").
- International Speaker: Citrix Synergy, E2EVC, VCNRW, Virtual Expo, plus IGEL DISRUPT, AVD TechFest, NerdioCon and 12+ Innovation Days (Oslo, London, Munich, Brussels, Helsinki).

Note: his finance venture (Nooit Meer Blut / Altijd in Bèta / basvankaamacademy.nl) is a separate project tracked outside this brain.
