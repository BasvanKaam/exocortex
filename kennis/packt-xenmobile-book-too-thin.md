# Verdict: the Packt XenMobile book is too thin to teach

In his first-ever book review (March 2014), Bas was honest and mostly critical of Packt's Citrix XenMobile MDM book by Akash Phoenix. His core charge: at 112 pages it left too much out, and was advertised as more than it delivered, that was the "wrong turn." He read it in just over an hour. He estimated 30 to 35 percent was download/license instructions and click-next-click-finish steps with a screenshot on every step, which he found too easy. He praised the format (easy to read, plain English, step by step), the NetScaler VPX configuration section, and policies like Jailbreak detection and Passcode, and he respected the author's expertise and the sheer effort of the project. But his bottom line was that it reads like an advanced installation guide rather than teaching the power and technology of XenMobile, and that recommending the year-old 8.5 bits in 2014 made little sense. Throughout he balanced sharp criticism with explicit respect for the work involved.

*Bron: blogpost 'Book review! Citrix XenMobile Mobile Device Management, published by Packt.' (2014-03-16), basvankaam.com.*
