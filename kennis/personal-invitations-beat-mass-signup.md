# Personal Invitations Beat Mass Sign-Up

From running Come Get IT, Bas takes the stance that personal, individually addressed invitations meaningfully outperform impersonal mass sign-ups for community events. Attendees specifically told him they valued being invited in person, and he started deliberately collecting names to make that possible at scale. The argument: for a community event, the felt sense of being personally wanted is part of the product, not a nicety. Operationalize it (maintain a names list) rather than leaving it to chance.
