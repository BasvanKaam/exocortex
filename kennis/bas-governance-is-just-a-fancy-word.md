# Bas's reframe: governance is just a fancy word

Bas insists that, just as with setting up a CCoE, governance is not reserved only for (large) enterprise organizations even if it sounds that way. You can set up a CCoE and governance as large, small or specific as you want — there are no rules. The principle: use only what applies to your company and project, nothing more, nothing less. Years ago he created a mindmap covering governance and all the aspects that could come into play; it shows governance can span many topics and departments.

His signature deflation of the buzzword: 'governance is niets meer dan een duur woord voor alles gewoon goed in de gaten houden en aansturing geven waar en wanneer nodig' — governance is nothing more than an expensive word for simply keeping a good eye on everything and giving direction where and when needed.

> En vergeet niet… governance is niets meer dan een duur woord voor alles gewoon goed in de gaten houden en aansturing geven waar en wanneer nodig.
