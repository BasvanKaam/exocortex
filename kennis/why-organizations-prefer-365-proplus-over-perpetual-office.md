# Why ProPlus/365 is usually preferred over perpetual Office

Bas's read of the market: for many organizations Office 365/ProPlus gets the nod over the traditional editions like Office 2019. The deciding factor is the flexibility you bring in-house, the offered functionality, additional services and update options, compared with Office 2019. The consumption (subscription) model also plays an important role.

His practical follow-on point: precisely because of this you need to know what Microsoft plans regarding support of ProPlus/365 on operating systems like Server 2016, 2019, Windows 10 and future versions, especially when designing a new workplace concept, cloud or on-prem. Know the lifecycle before you commit. This is the same "don't guess, verify the dependencies" instinct that runs through his roadmap and assessment work.
