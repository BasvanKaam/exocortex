# Don't be fooled by DaaS marketing

Bas's core stance (Nov 2013): "Don't be fooled by smart marketing." Vendors make DaaS sound like you're renting a fully functional Windows client desktop when you're usually getting a hosted-shared server-OS desktop. He extends the critique to the Citrix/Microsoft XenDesktop 7 on Azure announcement, which "again making it sound like a client desktop OS solution, but it's clearly not."

Yet he is genuinely a fan of the concept: on WorkSpaces, "for me it's the thought and concept that counts, so yes, you could say that I'm a fan (already)." His prediction: with Amazon's size and influence DaaS will move closer to mainstream, and a lot hinges on Microsoft's client-OS licensing and the secret 'Mohoro' DaaS project, plus VMware's Desktone acquisition. The position is consistent: love the idea, distrust the headline, read past it.
