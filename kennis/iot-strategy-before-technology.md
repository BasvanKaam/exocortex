# IoT is a mindset shift; answer the business questions before the tech

Bas's core argument (Feb 2017): adopting IoT 'goes way beyond the technology and tools involved' and for most organisations 'requires a different mindset and a certain degree of resourcefulness as well as creativity.' He insists you answer the business questions first (advisor vs integrator? enterprise vs consumer? which vertical? what added value?) and involve all stakeholders, including non-IT teams, before touching architecture, or the strategy 'will die an early, slow and painful death.' He believes IoT will be huge (citing Gartner) and that 2017 was the time to invest in IoT knowledge and re-educate existing admins into IoT architects: 'Don't do like a lot did with mobile and cloud' (i.e. don't get caught flat-footed again). He warns IoT may introduce more complexity than almost any other IT service, 'there are just too many things to consider, literally.'
