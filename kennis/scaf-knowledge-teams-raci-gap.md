# SCAF: Knowledge, Teams, Roles and Responsibilities

During the SCAF Insight phase you form a first picture of the new Cloud-based environment, which requires a certain knowledge level in the organization. By having conversations, you create an overview of available knowledge, the roles it's anchored in, and the associated responsibilities. Simultaneously you estimate the knowledge needed for what's coming and surface 'gaps', captured in a **GAP analysis** (in the template, an 'x' can be replaced with a (team) name).

Knowledge, roles and responsibilities come together in **teams**. With Cloud-based products you'll deal with teams such as governance, implementation/adoption, migration, and management, mapped in a **RACI model/matrix**. Using a standard RACI template, the existing and new/needed situation can be mapped in minutes. This is the moment to take an early (pencil-draft) shot at the execution phase's team composition. Names and contact details go in the template; the **CCoE** plays an overarching role. Also consider who manages contracts and SLAs, whether there's a security officer, who is responsible for data and what may be moved where.

**Team-composition questions**: who is responsible for executing technical tasks during adoption/migration; who is ultimately responsible for infrastructure/product changes; who is responsible for implementing Cloud governance and defining governance controls; who are the process owners; who acts as **ambassadors** promoting the upcoming Cloud services (collecting/answering questions, organizing group trainings); who handles the actual implementation/migration; what gets outsourced; and who is responsible for management including daily 'run' activities.

**Skill Readiness**: no decision needed yet, but a first proposal of trainings to follow can be summarized in tables.
