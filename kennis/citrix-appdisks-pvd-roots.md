# Citrix AppDisks was built on PvD and shipped machine-only first

How it worked in 2015: Citrix AppDisks (application layering) was based on the existing Personal vDisk (PvD) technology, with AppDNA folded in. Because it sat on PvD, the initial release could only assign AppDisks based on Delivery Groups, meaning at the machine level, and only for virtual machines, not physical ones. User and user-group assignment plus physical-machine support were expected in later releases. The product manager at the time was David Wagner.

This matters as context for how Citrix's app-layering strategy started: it grew out of PvD rather than being a clean-sheet design, while competitors were already filling the app-layering space fast.
