# Consumer IoT vs Industrial IoT (OT meets IT)

Consumer IoT connects smart consumer devices to improve user experience and save time/money. Industrial IoT (I)IoT, also called Industry 4.0, inter-connects and collects data on industrial machinery: PLCs (programmable logic controllers), RTUs (remote terminal units), HMI machines. A PLC receives (input) and transmits (output) electrical/electronic signals to control mechanical and electrical systems. Combined with the communication infrastructure this forms a **SCADA** system running on a supervisory computer.

In the industrial world this is **OT (Operational Technology)**: hardware/software dedicated to detecting or causing changes in physical processes via direct monitoring/control of valves, pumps etc. The biggest challenge for industrial companies is **merging OT and IT**: different protocols, big data, remote access, and security.

Big data plays a much bigger role in (I)IoT than in consumer/Workspace IoT.
