# Your suppliers' compliance is your responsibility to verify

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

the framework takes the stance that outsourcing to third parties and MSPs does not outsource accountability. It remains your responsibility to check suppliers' compliance to your standards, demand audit reports, certifications and security scans, and make sure that when you engage through an MSP everything is on the up-and-up. Consuming a service does not transfer the obligation to be in control.
