# Nerdio's real payoff is reclaimed engineer time, not just Azure cost

Bas frames the value of automating AVD management primarily as reclaimed human time and avoided error, not raw Azure savings. His argument: routine work (monthly patch/image management, parallel re-imaging on maintenance windows, billing reports, host availability, performance-trend analysis and resize/optimize, capacity planning, daily FSLogix profile work, optional Intune device/app/policy management, HA/failover) is exactly the work automation should absorb so teams can move to strategic projects ("Co-Pilot, anyone?!").

He leans on a recurring rhetorical move: reducing human error cuts incidents (he cites a 15-20% incident decrease) and lowers helpdesk tickets, while usage-pattern analysis cuts resource wastage. The point is that uniform, consistent automated workflows beat manual, error-prone management.

Note: the per-desktop/per-month hour figures and dollar amounts in this piece come from a Nerdio "operational savings analysis" used as marketing; treat the specific numbers as employer marketing claims rather than independent fact.
