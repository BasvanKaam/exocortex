# Cloud-Specific Target Operating Model

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

A Target Operating Model (TOM) reflects the strategic choices an organization makes to achieve its goals; it makes clear what the organization should look like and how it should function. In the Cloud Governance Framework it underpins the IT value chain, so demand parties and suppliers can have meaningful discussions about the effectiveness of collaboration.

The TOM at the heart of the framework is centered around a Cloud Team named the Cloud Center of Expertise (CCoE). It:

- Describes the contact points between Cloud IT and the Demand and Delivery parties at both ends of the IT value chain.
- Defines the interaction between roles, with other processes, and the use of technology.

It is combined with three governance processes (communication, compliance, dispensation) to keep the proper checks and balances in place. The success or failure of a digital transformation is largely determined by alignment between business and IT (internal and/or external) and by process maturity. The TOM, also framed as a Future Mode of Operations, gives direction to the IT organization, aids decision-making, and ensures adequate business involvement during the transition.
