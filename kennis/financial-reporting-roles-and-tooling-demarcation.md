# Financial Reporting: Role Demarcation and Tooling

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

Demarcation of responsibilities for the reporting and forecasting domain:
- Portfolio Manager is ultimately responsible for the P&L of the services.
- CCoE supports the business through tooling and reports.

The CCoE helps design, select and configure reporting tools, or alternatively designs and configures vendor-native reporting methods. It supports setting up requirements, validating designs and assisting the acquisition of a financial reporting solution.

Domain to-do: select tooling for cost optimization and reporting, then implement it.
