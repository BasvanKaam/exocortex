# Technology Still Matters — and Is Getting More Complex, Not Less

Bas pushes back hard on the popular consulting line that technology is a side issue, that "everyone can do it by now," and that the real value sits in bridging the gap between business and IT or in process optimization.

His stance: that is simply not true. Technology is becoming more complex, not less. He believes the field is heading the wrong way by downplaying it — technology is at least as important as a few years ago, if not more so. People want to know more about it and talk about it; the business-IT-gap and process-optimization stories land less well, and in the "new normal" nobody will spend money on them.

His resolution: sit in the middle — business continuity, everything you run into there, which includes a large chunk of technology. And no, not everyone can do or understands that.
