# CGIT Podcast Strategy and Formats

Bas's working strategy for the CGIT podcast.

Goals: generate more content (podcast and blog); publish a podcast biweekly; get production-ready faster (good is good enough, don't polish to perfection — it costs too much time and nobody notices); bring in more external guests instead of too much internal "ons-kent-ons / wc-eend"; approach better-known community members to grow name recognition; write catchy titles and descriptions; occasionally record an "extra" episode to publish in between; and alternate technical content with organizational and more personal soft-skills topics.

Formats discussed:
- Normal: one or two guests, a topic, questions, discussion — 30 to 45 minutes.
- Pro/con debate: a proponent and an opponent who agree the topic and questions up front, then defend their positions — 30 to 45 minutes.
- Mobile podcast taken to an event — 30 to 45 minutes.
- Extra broadcasts: shorter, can be recorded solo — 5 to 10, max 15 minutes.

Challenges flagged: too little time (invest first, show results, then ask for more time); episodes linger too long on a topic (inefficient); editing depends on a single person — a single point of failure to fix.
