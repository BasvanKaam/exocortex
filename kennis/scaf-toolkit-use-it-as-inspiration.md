# Bas: use the SCAF toolkit as inspiration, not a checklist

Bas closes the SCAF template catalogue with a deliberately low-pressure message: you may already have built your own documentation for certain steps, or you may not see the added value of some of these — that is all fine, do whatever benefits you. The list is meant as *inspiration*. For many projects you will only need a small subset of the documents. This reflects his recurring pragmatic stance: the framework serves the project, not the other way around.

## Bron-citaten (NL, verbatim)

> Gebruik deze lijst ter inspiratie. Voor veel projecten heb je misschien maar een kleine subset aan documenten nodig.
