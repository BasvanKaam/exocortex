# Position: candid, named vendor opinions in the comment section

Most of Bas's blog replies are pleasantries, but on two occasions he stated a genuine personal opinion about a named product/vendor, which is characteristic of his willingness to take a position rather than stay neutral.

1. On Citrix application Streaming (2017, Unidesk acquisition thread), verbatim: "Citrix Streaming? Did one project with it myself, didn't like it very much to be honest." A first-hand, unhedged negative verdict grounded in his own project experience.

2. On Workspot (2017, State of VDI/SBC thread), verbatim: "Workspot is doing ok, don't like their aggressive marketing strategy, though. They're not the only ones taking a similar approach, I know." He criticises a competitor's go-to-market while being fair about the product itself and acknowledging others do the same.

These show the engagement pattern: he is friendly and diplomatic by default, but will give an honest opinion when asked directly, name the vendor, and ground it in his own experience rather than hiding behind neutrality.
