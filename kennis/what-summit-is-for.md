# What a partner conference is actually for (not deep technical depth)

Writing ahead of his first Citrix Summit (December 2013), Bas sets out a clear stance on what to expect from a vendor conference: not deep dives or real technical detail, but "basic product information combined with the latest (or improved) features" plus roadmap and vision for the year. He deliberately skipped the multi-hour hands-on Learning Labs in favor of three back-to-back break-out sessions, reasoning that the labs eat too much of a 2.5-day event.

He is explicit that the value is also human: "It's not all about the bits and bytes." Collaborating, exchanging business cards, having a beer with peers. As a relative newcomer he frames it as making new friends rather than reuniting with old ones. His prediction for the year ahead: "My prime focus is on mobility and cloud / DaaS, I think that's what 2014 will be all about." This managed-expectations view recurs and is fully vindicated in his Summit recap (post 43).
