# SCAF Key Takeaways - The Four Phases and Strategy

Bas's Key Takeaways for Chapter 5. The Step-Cloud Adoptie Framework (SCAF) is his own framework giving organisations a basis to realise cloud transformation. It is built around four essential phases: **Strategie, Inzicht, Uitvoering en Adoptie** (Strategy, Insight, Execution and Adoption). These phases work together to guide companies on their journey to the cloud, from setting goals to actually implementing and leveraging cloud technology.

On the first step, the strategy phase: the success of cloud adoption depends on aligning the cloud strategy with business goals — organisations must look not only at technical benefits but at how the cloud contributes to business objectives. Data-driven decision-making is preferred; a thorough analysis of existing data and IT processes helps make well-considered choices and a strategy fitting an organisation's unique needs.

Stakeholder involvement is part of the strategy phase: by involving directors, IT managers and other key people early, broad support (draagvlak) emerges. Strategy workshops and interviews are central — they gather insights AND get IT and business to develop a shared vision, bridging (or at least narrowing) the IT/business gap discussed in the previous chapter.

SCAF also offers an extensive set of ready-made templates so information is captured and shared in a structured, results-oriented way. And crucially: the strategy phase is not an endpoint — cloud adoption requires ongoing evaluation and continuous monitoring/optimisation, so the cloud is not just a technological choice but a strategic instrument.
