# Bas's war story: SAN/NAS data migrations were weeks of nightmares

Storage 'flavours' (NAS, SAN, fileservers/clusters) are not cloud-specific and are also used on-prem, but the cloud changes how storage is offered. Instead of investing in big storage units that must be expanded when nearly full, you can take storage from the cloud in small pieces and pay only for what you need and use - far more efficient.

Bas recounts the pain of his early years: enormous time spent (temporarily) moving and re-placing data, which he still has nightmares about. It was literally weeks of work with no time for anything else; upgrading certain SAN systems took almost an entire weekend. He's not nostalgic about the overtime. Besides the SAN/NAS - both of which gave him headaches - you also had to deal with the fileservers (and clusters) that made it possible to do something with the data. He notes that 20+ years on, the technology has of course moved well beyond the picture he sketches.
