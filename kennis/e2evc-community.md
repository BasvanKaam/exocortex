# E2EVC (Expert to Expert Virtualization Congress)

E2EVC stands for Expert to Expert Virtualization Congress. Bas describes it as a community he values: each year the organiser runs three to four events of maximum two hundred participants, and they fill up fast.

What he likes: it is deliberately kept small even though it is popular enough to grow much larger, because the organiser prioritises the networking part - you must be able to find each other easily, start discussions, ask questions, form groups that go out to dinner. The organiser lets everyone present, regardless of whether you've been presenting for ten years or ten days, as long as you register your session on time, giving people a chance to show themselves and improve before a relaxed, helpful audience. It's always at a nice European metropolis (there's also a non-European version), and partly over a weekend, which helps to get time off from your boss or, as a freelancer, not give up too much work time. The knowledge level of attendees is generally well above average - a real community.

He also mentions the smaller, very informal vBeers gatherings, where five or six people stand around with a beer and a snack discussing business, products and careers.
