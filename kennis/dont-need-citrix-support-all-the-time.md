# You don't need Citrix Support all the time - learn to troubleshoot the FMA yourself

Stance (March 2015), the thesis of his first-ever talk titled 'Why we don't need Citrix Support (all the time): Troubleshooting the FMA - A breakdown.' His position is deliberately not anti-Citrix: 'it has nothing to do with Citrix Support being rubbish or anything, they have their good and bad days just like anyone of us.' The argument is about admin self-reliance: understand the FMA architecture and use the available tools (his top 5, centered on Citrix Scout and CDF Control) so you can troubleshoot bigger issues yourself, and even work in parallel with CTX Support when they are engaged.

He also frames support as a two-way efficiency relationship: know how to assist CTX Support by giving them the specific, detailed information they ask for, which makes the whole process more efficient. This 'understand what happens under the covers' ethos recurs across his work.
