# Stance: hybrid will play the leading role in cloud workspaces

Bas argues that over the next five to ten years cloud usage keeps growing and that hybrid solutions specifically will play the leading role, with on-prem knowledge remaining essential because most organizations sit mid-transition. The corollary stance: the proliferation of cloud services makes overview and in-house expertise hard to maintain, so the right answer for a provider is deliberate focus on a few crucial workspace-related services rather than trying to cover everything.
