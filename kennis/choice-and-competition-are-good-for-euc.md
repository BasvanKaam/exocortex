# Choice and competition are good in the EUC market

Bas frames the existence of credible Citrix alternatives as a positive: 'Keuze is fijn en concurrentie is goed' (choice is nice and competition is good). Rather than treating Citrix as the default, he actively evaluates lighter, more automated, cloud-native alternatives and recommends them on fit, not brand loyalty.
