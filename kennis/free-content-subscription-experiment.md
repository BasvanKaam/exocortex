# The email-gated guide as a deliberate experiment

In Sept 2014 Bas explains he initially gated his XenMobile survival guide (80+ notes, 16 pages, 5600+ words) behind an email subscription as "sort of a test for me personally." Over 200 people subscribed; he judged the experiment a success and then released the guide fully free, no subscription needed.

His stance: build audience deliberately, but default to giving the content away free in the end ("of course it is still a 100% free"). An early, self-aware take on the lead-magnet versus open-access tension that runs through his whole creator career.
