# Organizations care about cost, efficiency and compliance but miss how they connect

> Referentie uit het Weolcan Cloud Governance Framework (WCGF). Niet Bas zijn eigen IP, hier bewaard als naslag.

the framework notes that plenty of organizations do not employ charge-back or show-back, so IT spend is just a lump sum on the overall budget, leaving little incentive to do the considerable work of administering assets and items. His stance: almost every organization does care about IT cost, efficiency and regulatory compliance; what is missing is understanding of the relationship between them. That is why extra, deliberate effort is required to make asset and configuration administration a priority when going cloud, rather than letting it lapse because the payoff is not immediately visible.
