# Salomon Cloud Masterclass: Two-Day Course Structure

Bas's two-day vendor-independent Cloud Masterclass (also positioned as a "bootcamp"). The promise is to move from "the very beginning to the cloud of today" in two days: history, underlying principles and technologies, cloud economics, standout trends, the new business reality, and the often-forgotten cultural shifts. The running thread through the whole training is his own independent cloud adoption framework, taught step by step as a pragmatic, manageable approach that works for very small and very large organizations alike.

**Two-day agenda:**
- Day 1: Once upon a time... / Cloud principles / The cloud anno 2020 / The new business reality / Framework: Strategy / Cloud economics / Architectures and services / Summary.
- Day 2: Framework: Insight / Data and Governance / Security / Framework: Execution / Exit strategy / Community and practical tips / Framework: Adoption / Future and trends.

The framework is delivered in four passes spread across both days (Strategy, Insight, Execution, Adoption), interleaved with supporting topics rather than taught as one block.

**Learning outcomes** include: basic and advanced cloud concepts; the new business reality and its cultural shifts; the changing role of the CIO / IT management; the influence end users gain through cloud services; how business and IT should grow closer (and who counts as "the business"); the financial impact of cloud (business case, TCO, ROI); the technology behind IaaS, PaaS, SaaS and DaaS; the cloud architectures of the three largest providers; current and upcoming trends; common objections and provocative statements; data in the cloud and the GDPR/AVG; why an exit strategy is a necessary evil and what to watch for; and community-driven initiatives and their value.

**Audience:** the ambitious cloud (business) consultant, administrator, or IT Manager / CIO who wants to understand the potential impact of (hybrid) cloud on their organization. No prior knowledge required but a plus; the material spans beginner and advanced.
