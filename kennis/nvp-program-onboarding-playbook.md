# NVP Program: How Bas Onboards Community Members

Bas runs Nerdio's NVP (Nerdio Valued / Valuable Professional) program, a Microsoft-MVP-style recognition initiative for people who voluntarily support Nerdio, its products and community. His onboarding play, reusable for any community/advocacy program:

- **Personal acceptance note**, not a form letter. Congratulate, thank, and frame the relationship as two-way ("helping Nerdio become an even better solution").
- **Public introduction**: each new member fills in a standard "NVP introduction" template (short self-written bio) that gets published on the marketing/social calendar.
- **Identity assets**: a dated digital badge members can share on socials, tied to a program hashtag (#NerdioNVP) so contributions are trackable.
- **Private channel**: a dedicated Slack channel for the cohort to share ideas, questions and feedback.
- **Welcome kit**: a bundle of perks assembled and approved before rollout.

The through-line: make members feel seen, give them shareable status, and open a direct feedback loop back into the product.
