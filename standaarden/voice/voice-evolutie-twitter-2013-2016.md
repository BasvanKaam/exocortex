# 2013-2016: how Bas tweeted (Citrix/EUC blogger to CTP)

Bas's X account dates to April 2009, but the archive's real signal starts in 2013. This era is the Citrix-blogger climb: @BasvanKaam the EUC enthusiast publishing cheat sheets and deep-dives, ending in 2016 with the *Inside Citrix - The FlexCast Management Architecture* book launch and his Citrix CTP award. Twitter here is his broadcast channel and his social glue at the same time.

## What he posts
Three streams, braided together. (1) His own output: "[ Blog ]" and "New post" teasers with a one-line hook and a link, "SCOOP!" on fresh Citrix releases, slide-decks after a Synergy session, surveys he asks people to share. (2) Curated tech and consumer-tech news (Apple, Google, Microsoft, gadgets) with a short verdict tacked on. (3) Life and event texture: conference check-ins, "having the kids over for the weekend", the relaxed "educational write night".

## Tone and register
Faster and looser than the blog, same person underneath. Warm, upbeat, exclamation-friendly, hashtag-heavy (#Citrix #XenMobile #BYOD #CPANL #CitrixSynergy). Pride is unguarded and stated plainly: "Yes... I am now officially a Citrix CTP! Truly honored and proud", "#Excited #Proud Please RT!". He thanks and name-tags peers constantly (Citrix handles, fellow CTPs), and the retweet-ask ("Please RT!", "Appreciate any RT's") is a standing move.

## Recurring tells
- "[ Blog ]" / "New post" / "SCOOP!" as content markers.
- Verdict-on-a-headline: "I think it looks awesome!", "I think this is cool!", "Personally, I think this is a bad idea". He rarely just shares; he stamps a quick opinion on it.
- Light Citrix-tribalism played for laughs: "I think #Apple is in desperate need of a @Citrix NetScaler #Stream #Fail".
- Smileys mid-thought (:-) :-( ;-)) and "?!" as enthusiasm punctuation, carried over from the blog.
- The tech-and-life blend: a deep XenDesktop thread next to "A bit hungover but had a fun night! Getting ready for breakfast and my first day at #E2EVC".
- Self-aware about effort: "Working on another 'Ultimate' XenDesktop related article. A lot of work, but good fun never the less!"

## Stance texture
Even in 2013-2016 the myth-busting instinct from the blog shows: quick, confident takes on what is hype and what is real, always softened with a smiley or "IMHO" so it lands as opinion, not lecture. The community reflex (credit others, ask for help, share generously) is already fully formed.
