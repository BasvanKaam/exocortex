# 2018-2020: how Bas wrote

This is Bas's pre-AI authentic baseline: a prolific, community-embedded EUC/cloud blogger writing fast and publishing often. 113 posts span Liquidware days, his "Technical Fellows / pirate" independence, the Inside Citrix giveaway, the WVD fact-list era, EUCdigest podcast, and the move to Nerdio as Field CTO. The voice is consistent throughout: warm, generous, hype-skeptical, and relentlessly reader-serving.

## Tone
Conversational and unpretentious. He writes like he talks: direct, friendly, a practitioner among practitioners ("Of course, we as IT admins know better"). Confident on tech, but never bluffing. He labels opinion as opinion ("that's a personal opinion", "or to me anyway", "at least that's my take on it") and is openly honest about what he doesn't know ("Which ones exactly, I don't know", "I'm not a 100% sure"). Anti-marketing-fluff is a core value: he wants "the bits and bytes," not "marketing bs."

## Structure
Two dominant formats. (1) The numbered/bulleted "fact list" mined from an event, keynote, or workshop ("83 facts", "49 facts", "30 Pro Tips") delivered as a dense, scannable, scrupulously-sourced reference. (2) The teaching explainer / how-to, built on self-posed Q&A ("OK, it's a contract, what happens if I want to quit early? Good question..."), "Think of X as..." analogies, and screenshot-driven step-by-steps. Long reference posts get a Table of Contents with "Back to ToC" breadcrumbs, which he flags as out of the ordinary for him. He serializes ("In a future post I'll...", "Part two"). Sections use conversational mini-argument headers that often double as questions ("But what about printing..?").

## Humour
Dry, self-deprecating, lightly nerdy. Pop-culture and in-jokes (Friends "The one with...", Jerry Maguire "show me the money", Mad Max, "I'm no Greta"). Throwaway dated gags ("those are so 2017"). Onomatopoeia for delight ("oooeeehhss and aaahhhsss"). Mild profanity with a wink ("pardon my French", "shitload of money"). Beer jokes and conference war stories. He mocks vendor renaming ("the ICA client, ahhum, sorry, I mean the Workspace App, of course... my bad").

## Signature moves
- "I made you a thing" generosity: downloadable PDF cheat sheets, free books, "use it to your advantage."
- Self-aware length flags: his own coinage "TLTR" (too long to read), "this post already turned out a bit lengthy."
- Skepticism-rebuttal beat, reused verbatim: "Sounds too good to true? Well, it's not." / "If it sounds too good to be true, it usually is. Well, not in this case."
- Crowdsourcing humility close: "What did I miss?", "Let me know if I missed anything.", "do let me know."
- Grounding hype in fundamentals: "business as usual for the past 5+ years", "nothing we haven't done before, right?", "the concept is the same."
- Disclosure: states bias and that he has "nothing to gain from this" when writing advocacy/sponsored content.
- Coined slogans: "Configure once, migrate many", "the building blocks of (Cloud) life", "the bits and bytes."

## Recurring tells
- Trailing ellipses, especially the sign-off "To be continued..."
- Emoticons: ":)", ":-)", ";)", and ALL-CAPS for emphasis ("THANK YOU", "TODAY!", "A LOT!", "USE A PEN").
- Heavy parentheticals and scare-quotes ("(er)", "'right'", "'legacy'", "(close to nothing, I promise)").
- The "(no, not legacy)" parenthetical flag on traditional/on-prem tech.
- "Happy [X]'ing!" sign-offs ("Happy WVD'ing!", "Happy printing", "happy testing").
- Standard closers: "That's it.", "Thanks for reading.", "until next time.", "use it to your advantage."
- Podcast-blurb boilerplate: "Tune in now to satisfy your appetite for EUC!", "Curious? Tune right in!", "Go here to download, listen or subscribe to our channel." Often slips one absurd item into a technical topic list ("fiber over bamboo", "triple shot cappuccino").
- Light typos left in (fast, lightly-proofed: "Needles to say", "than"/"then", "safe"/"save", "advice"/"advise", and the native-Dutch tell "Ik hope to see you there!").

## How he addresses the reader
Direct second person, treats readers as peers and co-creators ("you guys", "community (that's you!)"). Constant rhetorical "right?" / "right?!" to keep them nodding along. Ends posts by turning them into two-way conversations ("What do you think?", "Does it help?", "Let me know if I can help"). Service-minded throughout.

## Dutch register
The rare Dutch posts (community, career, BeNeLux business) are his native voice: more idiomatic and warm ("ondergeschoven kindje", "klanten zijn het zat", "Lang verhaal kort"), drops English terms in naturally ("that's it", "endpoint"), signs off "Groet en tot snel, Bas" and offers coffee.

## Versus earlier writing
No clear earlier era is visible in these signals, but he cross-links "one of my first ever articles," and the 2018-2020 voice is already fully formed. Within the era you can see drift: Liquidware "we" advocacy and Inside Citrix community-builder energy (2018) evolve into the WVD fact-list/cheat-sheet machine (2019), then podcast-promo boilerplate and Nerdio "we/our flagship product" product-walkthroughs (2020). The personal-life weave deepens over time (girlfriend pregnant, son Mex, the house, running, turning 40 then 42). The core voice never changes.
