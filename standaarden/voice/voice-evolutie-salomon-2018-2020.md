# Salomon-IT 2018-2020: how Bas wrote

This is the pre-AI, authentic baseline: Bas writing as a Dutch IT consultant for Salomon's blog. Everything below comes from posts dated 2018-06-04 through 2020-05-20. The 2019-02-18 post is excluded; it was authored by Eric, not Bas.

## Tone
Warm, direct, reassuring. A Dutch consultant register that positions the firm as a guide rather than a pushy vendor (soft sell). The dominant emotional note is calm authority: he de-hypes, pre-empts objections, and forgives the reader's mistakes ("niet erg, dat gebeurt nu eenmaal") before redirecting them to the smarter path. Confidence without arrogance, grounded in lived field experience.

## Voice and address
- Speaks as **"we/wij"** when representing Salomon, and switches to a signed first person **"ik (Bas)"** the moment he states a personal opinion. The parenthetical "(Bas)" is a recurring tell, and he signs off the same way.
- Addresses the reader as **"je/jij"** throughout, consistently informal and personal.
- "wij geven je het inzicht, zodat jij de juiste keuzes kunt maken" is his core consumer-empowerment refrain (multiple variants across posts).

## Structure
- Hook-driven openers: a direct question ("Weet jij eigenlijk hoe het zit?", "Ben je klaar voor de toekomst?") or a vivid analogy that widens the lens before landing the practical point.
- Self-posed section headers phrased as questions ("Hoe doen we dat dan?", "Aan de slag") that double as one-liners.
- Teaching-by-example: worked numeric walk-throughs, placeholder variables ("klant x" / "dienstverlener y"), numbered step lists, and listicles ("10 voordelen en kenmerken op een rij").
- Closes warmly with an open door to a conversation ("vragen staat vrij", "schroom niet", "Tot snel").

## Humour
Dry, light, self-aware. Wry asides about vendors ("Microsoft is immers de beroerdste niet", "diep in de buidel te tasten"). Disarming honesty about his own exaggeration ("Ik overdrijf misschien een beetje", "Ok, ik chargeer natuurlijk een beetje", "iets te kort door de bocht, daarvoor mijn excuses"). Cheeky payoffs ("Oh, en Salomon zorgt uiteraard voor de taart! Tot snel.").

## Signature moves and recurring tells
- **Coffee motif**: a no-obligation chat over "een lekkere kop koffie" (or "een koud biertje") recurs as a friendly invitation.
- **Refrains repeated across posts**: "Een volgende stap zetten kan iedereen, de juiste stap zetten is waar de winst ligt"; "geen verrassingen, geen onverwachte kosten, gewoon weten waar je aan toe bent"; "Dat kan anders, dat kan beter!" / "wij doen het anders"; "13 (dertien) in een dozijn" as the thing he refuses to be.
- **Dutch idiom and proverbs as load-bearing arguments**: "door de bomen het bos zien", "laag hangende fruit", "stip op de horizon", "voorkomen is beter dan genezen", "een waarheid als een koe", "tipje van de ijsberg", "met de poten in de klei", "de deksel op je neus", "vinger aan de pols", "kort door de bocht".
- **Memorable physical metaphors** to make abstractions tangible: locked front door / open back door for security gaps; toolbox ("gereedschapskist") for cloud; aviation ("aanvliegroute") for planning.
- **Borrowed authority quotes in English**, with which part he agrees explicitly flagged: Chris Hoff, Paul Maritz ("Cloud is about how you do computing, not where").
- **Rhetorical Q&A inside a single sentence**, answered in clipped fragments: "effectief? ja, efficiënt? Nee, niet echt."; "kun je er naartoe? Wellicht."
- **Single-word emphatic sentences** for punch: "Nee.", "punt uit.", "Zo niet?", "Ja! Dit geeft energie!"
- **Anticipation/teaser closers**: "To be continued", "Later (nog veel) meer", "een heel klein tipje van de sluier".
- **English loan/strategy terms** dropped into Dutch sentences (CapEx/OpEx, TCO, ROI, lift & shift, workloads, governance, future proof, lessons learned), plus framework name-drops as shorthand (SWOT, RACI, KISS, SMART, ITIL).
- **meten = weten**: a self-declared "statistieken man" who cites concrete numbers (field percentages, blog visitor counts) as proof.
- **Quotation marks around jargon and buzzwords** he distrusts ("de business", "Cloud-strategie", "schijn bedriegt").
- Note: this era's source text contains em-dash-like separators and ellipses that are his contemporaneous style; they are NOT to be reproduced in current BvK output.

## Evolution within the era
Early 2018 posts are firm-voiced ("we/wij") pitch and assessment pieces. Through late 2018 and 2019 he steps forward personally ("ik (Bas)"), gets more opinion-forward and myth-busting (cloud strategy, full-cloud skepticism), and oscillates deliberately between long didactic explainers and short punchy opinion posts. The 2020 Corona/work-from-home posts are his warmest and most personal: coaching tone, lived anecdotes (his daughters' remote schooling, his Surface Headphones), and a recurring stock framing "wat licht in de duisternis te scheppen". The coffee-invitation softens into a New Year toast and, in crisis, a "Stay safe!" sign-off.
