# 2020-2023: how Bas tweeted (Nerdio Field CTO, community builder)

June 2020 Bas joins Nerdio as Field CTO for EMEA, and the feed re-centers around it: WVD/AVD, Windows 365 Cloud PC, Nerdio Manager releases, and above all community-building (Nerdio Tech Insider sessions, the AVD/WVD User Groups, the Nerdio Valued Professional program). This is also his most personal era on X: COVID, his wedding, his birthday rituals, snooker and pool, F1, vinyl, running. The professional and the personal sit side by side without apology.

## What changes
Format gets cleaner and more spaced-out (line breaks between every beat, scannable bullet lists of features). The role shifts him from "here's my content" to "here's what we're building and who's joining us": product-release rundowns, welcome posts for new hires and NVPs, event invites. The note-tweet (long-form) appears in 2023 and he uses it for gratitude essays and the AVD Security cheat-sheet relaunch.

## Tone and register
Warmest and most us-focused of all the eras. Big stretched-caps excitement is still there ("HUGE news", "HUGE announcement", "BOOM") but it's almost always about the team, the partner, the community, rarely just himself. Gratitude is the dominant note: the three-year-anniversary post ("the best move I have ever made professionally... I've learned a ton... made a lot of new friends. Thank you all"). Personal milestones get the same wholehearted voice as product news: "in exactly two weeks from today, I will be MARRIED", "It's my birthday. That means... I have a day off, I'm sleeping in, will go out for a run, play some pool / snooker, and finish off with a (giant) steak dinner."

## Recurring tells
- Spaced, scannable layout; ">>>" and "?" arrows pushing the reader to the link or the next beat.
- Feature lists as bullets ("Start, stop, restart personal desktops / Manage current sessions / Resize/Re-image own VM").
- "let's be honest" as his honesty-flag before a contrarian aside: "#CloudPC VM's run 24 X 7, which, let's be honest, nobody really needs".
- "if you ask me", "Nothing short of amazing", "This is going to be epic!" as earnest hype, never ironic.
- COVID-era care language: "STAY SAFE everyone!", "Stay safe!", "in these challenging times", "#CelebrateLife".
- Self-deprecating Dutch tells leaking through: "all in de = all in the. My Dutch took over there :)".
- Community as the through-line: "This community thing I keep talking about, around @GetNerdio I mean, will evolve going forward", spinning up six WVD User Groups in a day ("WHO IS NEXT?"), starting an IT Strava club because "IT and sports go hand in hand, it keeps you sane and healthy".

## Stance texture
The opinions here are product-and-market: Cloud PC vs AVD trade-offs (the cheat-sheet wars), what customers actually need vs what's marketed, cost-saving angles ("nobody really needs" 24x7), and a recurring belief that ease-of-use and time-saved are the real selling points. Personal-tech verdicts still land flat and honest: after four months on Android, "I picked up another iPhone X today. Sooo much better. Really." On COVID he's analytical, not panicked: "the (financial) impact might be much bigger than some think... habits are hard to break."
